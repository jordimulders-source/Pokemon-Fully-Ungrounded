STYLE AND FORMAT
- 64x64 pixel sprite, max 16 colors, 1 px dark outline, light from top left, clean shading with large calm areas like official Gen 3 sprites, no noise or stray pixels.

AVOID
- Not a raccoon, red panda, Zigzagoon, Linoone or Furret. No plush toy, chibi or mascot look.
