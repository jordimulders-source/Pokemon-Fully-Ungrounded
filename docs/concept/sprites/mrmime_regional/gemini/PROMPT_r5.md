# Regionale Mr. Mime (jiujitsu) – Gemini-startprompt r5 (22 nov, NIEUW gesprek, geen bijlage)
Zelfde als r4, alleen de pose (en kijkhoek) veel concreter: r1–r4 lagen allemaal op de zij.

```
Design a brand-new regional form of Mr. Mime: a Brazilian jiu-jitsu fighter. Type Fighting/Psychic.
Draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): pixel art, 64×64, max 16 colors, dark selout outline, plain white background.

WHAT IT IS
A really different design from Kanto Mr. Mime, just as Galarian Mr. Mime is a completely new design (but do NOT copy Galarian Mr. Mime: no tap shoes, no ice, no cane). It is a Pokémon, not a human in a gi.
NO pink balls anywhere.

COLORS
- Skin: the normal Mr. Mime skin tone (pale pinkish), the head too.
- Fingertips and feet: light blue.
- Cheeks: yellow.
- Two jagged purple spikes ("ears") on the sides of the head, like a jester's hair.
- A BLUE jiu-jitsu gi with a BLACK belt (not white).

POSE (most important)
It lies FLAT ON ITS BACK, like a turtle on its back: its whole back touches the floor, its chest, belly and black belt face UP to the sky. It is NOT lying on its side.
Both legs are lifted up in the air, knees bent, legs open, the soles of both feet pointing at the viewer.
Both hands are raised in front of its chest, waving the viewer in: "get into my guard".
Its head rests on the floor behind its body, chin tucked, looking at the viewer between its knees.

VIEW
The viewer is the opponent, standing at its feet and looking down at it, slightly from the left (three-quarter).
So we see the feet and knees in front, the belly and belt in the middle, and the head behind them.
Compact: the whole Pokémon fits inside a square, not a long horizontal figure.

DO NOT
Not lying on its side, not a side view, not jumping, not falling.
No ground, no mat, no shadow under it. No glow, no aura, no effects, no motion lines.
No opponent, nothing else in the image.
No plush/Neopets look, no chibi mascot, not a realistic human.
```
