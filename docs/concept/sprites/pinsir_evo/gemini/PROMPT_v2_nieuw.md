# Pinsir-evolutie – startprompt v2 (25 nov, nieuw gesprek, geen bijlage)
Jordi: "ja doe A, nieuwe prompt" (nieuwe startprompt met alle besluiten + punten bij r1/r2).
Ten opzichte van r1: kleur van r2 vastgelegd; hoorns met één haakpunt; lijf glad schild i.p.v. rotsgolem; klauwen i.p.v. vuist; keverkop i.p.v. kattenkop. Voorstel Claude: brede kaak met kleine tandjes (zoals Pinsirs mond), klauwen met 2–3 punten.

```
Design a brand-new Pokémon: a huge beetle kaiju. Type Bug/Ground.
Draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): pixel art, 64×64, max 16 colors, dark selout outline, plain white background.

WHAT IT IS
A massive, heavy beetle monster that leans forward, like a classic kaiju: a strong, simple hybrid of a beetle and a monster.
It stands on two thick legs and has two strong arms (four limbs in total). The legs and arms are beetle legs, but thick and sturdy: longer and more jointed than stubby monster legs, but NOT long and thin like a real beetle's legs.
The body is one big, simple, solid shape with a clear silhouette: a smooth, glossy beetle shell with a big rounded shell on the back (the wing covers). Simple shapes, not detailed.

HEAD AND FACE
An insect head, not a mammal: no snout, no nose, no cat or dog face. A flat, hard beetle face with a wide jaw lined with small teeth across the mouth, and small fierce eyes.

HORNS
Two horns grow out of the top of its HEAD (not out of the back or chest). Both horns have exactly the same shape, like the horn of a Golofa rhinoceros beetle: each horn rises upward in one smooth curve and ends in ONE single sharp hook that bends FORWARD at the tip, like the handle of a walking cane. Each horn has only one point. A fringe of short orange-brown hair runs along the back edge of each horn.

ARMS
The arms end in short, pointed beetle claws (2-3 sharp points), not hands with fingers. No fist.

COLORS
The body is a warm, rich brown with clear light and shadow, so it looks like a hard, shiny shell. The horns are grey-white (dark brown only at the base), so they stand out against the body. Orange-brown hair on the horns, grey claw tips.

POSE
Leaning forward, heavy and powerful, both arms slightly raised and both visible, ready to grab and crush. It looks much stronger and heavier than an ordinary beetle Pokémon.

VIEW
Three-quarter view facing LEFT, like every Gen 3 front sprite: not straight to the front, not facing right.
Compact: the whole Pokémon fits inside a square, limbs close to the body.

DO NOT
No forked or split horn tips, no pincer or antler tips, no straight horns, no extra horns.
No rock texture, no stone cracks, no muscles, no complex patterns, no spikes all over the body, no separate armor plates.
No fingers, no fists. No mammal face.
Not a realistic insect painting. No ground, no rocks, no shadow, no dust. No glow, no aura, no effects, no motion lines.
No plush/Neopets look, no chibi mascot.
```
