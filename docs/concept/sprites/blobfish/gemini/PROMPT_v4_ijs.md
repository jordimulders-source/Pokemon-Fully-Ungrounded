## Prompt

```
The attached image is a Pokémon sprite draft. KEEP ITS EXACT SHAPE, SILHOUETTE AND POSE. Change only the material and the face.

MATERIAL
- It is no longer wood: it is frozen ice cream, like an ice cream cake from a mold. Pure Water type, the first stage of a two-stage line.
- Strawberry-pink ice cream, matte. A thin layer of white frost on the upper edges. A few soft scoop ridges on the body. Frozen solid: not melting, no drips.
- Remove everything wooden: no wood grain, no worn paint, no metal ring on the back.
- Fins are part of the same ice cream, with simple pressed grooves as fin rays.

FACE (change this, except keep the big, thick lips)
- Slightly derpy and happy, as if it is glad to see you: a goofy smile with those big lips.
- THE NOSE DOES NOT HANG: no drooping or sagging nose over the mouth.

STYLE AND FORMAT
- Official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style), 64x64, max 16 colors, 1 px dark outline, light from top left, flat Gen 3 shading with 3 to 4 tones per color, no dithering.
- Plain white background. NO floor, cone, stick, plate, shadow, sprinkles, effects. One single creature.

AVOID
- No cone, no wafer, no sprinkles, no ice cubes. Not jelly, pudding or slime. No glossy 3D render.
```

## Tips
- Wordt het een ijsje met hoorn of een ijsblok: "ice cream cake shaped like a fish, no cone" bovenaan.
- Leest niet als ijs: frost-laag dikker maken ("white frosty edges like ice cream straight from the freezer").
