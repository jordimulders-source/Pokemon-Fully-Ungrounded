# Zandloper-lijn fase 3 – Gemini-prompts

Doel (Jordi, 26 nov): "doe eens een prompt voor fase 3, kan het nog niet goed visualiseren". Volgorde nog niet gekozen, dus twee prompts: A = volgorde 1 (systeem), B = volgorde 2 (zand vrij). Lijf met twee armen en zonder benen is een invulling van Claude om het te kunnen visualiseren (les GEMINI.md: voorwerp zonder anatomie = mensje in kostuum), geen besluit. Type Ground/Psychic komt uit de pitch.

## r1 A – systeem
```
Design an original Pokémon as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style), 64x64 pixels, max 16 colors, white background, facing 3/4 to the left.

Concept: the final evolution of an hourglass Pokémon (Ground/Psychic). The sand has left the hourglass and became the creature's body: a large, heavy, upright creature made of flowing golden sand, with two thick arms and no legs (the lower body pours down into a mound of sand). The original hourglass is now inside its chest as its core/heart, visible through the sand.
Around and through its body runs a whole system of glass: several smaller glass bulbs, tubes and funnels connected like clockwork or a chemistry setup, with sand trickling from one bulb into the next. The glass parts ARE its body (shoulders, back, crown), not objects it carries.
Face: simple Pokémon face on the sand head, calm and ancient, eyes that feel wise and patient.
Compact silhouette that fits a square, limbs close to the body.

No effects, no glow, no sparkles, no ground or shadow under it. AVOID: a realistic hourglass with a face, a human in a costume, a plush toy, Neopets style.
```

## r1 B – zand vrij
```
Design an original Pokémon as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style), 64x64 pixels, max 16 colors, white background, facing 3/4 to the left.

Concept: the final evolution of an hourglass Pokémon (Ground/Psychic). Its glass has shattered and the sand is finally free: a large creature made of flowing golden sand, with two thick arms and no legs (the lower body pours down into a mound of sand). The shards of the broken hourglass are stuck in its sand body like armor plates on the shoulders, chest and head, and the two wooden or metal end caps of the hourglass have become part of its body (for example a crown and a base).
Face: simple Pokémon face in the sand, between the shards, eyes that feel old and powerful.
Compact silhouette that fits a square, limbs close to the body.

No effects, no glow, no sparkles, no ground or shadow under it. AVOID: a realistic hourglass with a face, a human in a costume, a plush toy, Neopets style.

## r2 A – minder menselijk, minder detail (NIET gebruikt; Jordi 26 nov: "wacht even met een nieuwe prompt, eerst sparren")
Zelfde prompt als r1 A; alleen het gezicht/de handen en de hoeveelheid glas veranderd. "Geen vingers" en "2–3 bollen" zijn invulling van Claude.
```
Design an original Pokémon as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style), 64x64 pixels, max 16 colors, white background, facing 3/4 to the left.

Concept: the final evolution of an hourglass Pokémon (Ground/Psychic). The sand has left the hourglass and became the creature's body: a large, heavy, upright creature made of flowing golden sand, with two thick arms and no legs (the lower body pours down into a mound of sand). The original hourglass is now inside its chest as its core/heart, visible through the sand.
Around its body runs a simple system of glass: only 2 or 3 small glass bulbs connected by one tube, with sand trickling from one bulb into the next. The glass parts ARE its body (shoulders, back, crown), not objects it carries.
It must NOT look human: no human face, no nose, no human head shape, no human hands or fists. The head is a rounded mound of sand with simple Pokémon eyes and a small mouth; the arms end in rounded sand stumps without fingers.
Keep it simple and readable at 64x64: big clear shapes, few details, like a real Gen 3 sprite.
Compact silhouette that fits a square, limbs close to the body.

No effects, no glow, no sparkles, no ground or shadow under it. AVOID: a realistic hourglass with a face, a human in a costume, a plush toy, Neopets style.

## r3 – na het sparren (26 nov), varianten brein als hoofd (A) / brein in de core (B)

Invulling Claude: twee armen met ronde stompen zonder vingers, 2–3 grote stukken glas.
```

Concept: a sand djinn (Ground/Psychic) that has escaped from an hourglass. At the bottom, an hourglass lies on its side, broken open in the middle (at the narrow waist). Sand pours out of that opening and rises up into the djinn: its lower body is a sand tail that stays connected to the hourglass, like a genie rising from its lamp. The hourglass is the source.
Upper body: made of golden sand, with two arms that end in rounded sand stumps without fingers.
[A] Its head IS a brain made of sand, with simple Pokémon eyes; show the brain folds with only a few clear lines, no fine detail.
[B] Its head is a rounded mound of sand with simple Pokémon eyes; in its chest sits a brain made of sand as its core, shown with only a few clear lines, no fine detail.
Around its body: glass science apparatus (flasks, tubes, funnels) that is part of its body, not carried. Only 2 or 3 pieces, each large and clearly defined, nothing tiny.
It must NOT look human: no human face, no nose, no human hands.
Keep it simple and readable at 64x64: big clear shapes, like a real Gen 3 sprite. Compact silhouette that fits a square.

No effects, no glow, no sparkles, no shadow; draw no ground under the hourglass. AVOID: a realistic hourglass with a face, a human in a costume, a plush toy, Neopets style.
```
