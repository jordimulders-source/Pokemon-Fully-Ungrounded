# Gemini-prompt Regicosmo r1 (nieuw gesprek, geen bijlage)

Eigen invulling van Claude (schrappen als Jordi het niet wil): aantal bollen (7), rangschikking per lichaamsdeel, "zwakke paarse/blauwe tinten" niet genoemd (kleur aan Gemini gelaten).

```
Design a new legendary Pokémon as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): pixel art, 64x64, max 16 colors, dark outline, light from top-left, plain white background, no ground, no shadow, no effects.

WHAT IT IS: a new member of the Regi family of golem legendaries. Like every Regi it is a faceless golem with no eyes and no mouth; its only "face" is a pattern of glowing dots in the middle of its body. It must clearly look like it belongs next to the other Regis (blocky golem build, wide shoulders, arms, no separate head), but it is a new design, not a copy of any of them.

BODY: the golem is built from separate planets and celestial bodies (small rocky planets, a moon, a ringed planet, etc.) that do NOT touch each other: there are visible gaps between every part. Together they still clearly form a golem silhouette: a big central sphere is the torso, smaller bodies float where the shoulders, arms, hands and lower body would be. Compact, fits in a square, limbs close to the body.

THE DOTS (most important detail): the central sphere has the Regi dot pattern. The dots are a map of its own body: each dot sits in the position that matches one of the floating planets around it, so the dot pattern looks like a tiny star chart / orrery diagram of the golem itself. The dots glow.

POSE: 3/4 view facing left, standing upright and imposing like a Regi.

DO NOT: starfield or galaxy texture on the body, nebula, sparkles, eyes, mouth, separate head, planets touching each other, orbit lines, effects.
```
