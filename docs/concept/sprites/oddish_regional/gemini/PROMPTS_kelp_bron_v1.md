## Oddish
```
Design a regional form of the Pokémon Oddish and draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style pixel art).

CONCEPT
- Oddish from the deep kelp forests of the Galápagos, based on the Galápagos kelp (Eisenia galapagensis). Type: Water/Grass.
- Body, feet, face and pose are exactly those of Oddish's own Gen 3 front sprite: the small round body, the two stubby feet in a walking pose, tiny red dot eyes and a small open red mouth. No big eyes, no smile.
- Body color: dark basalt grey, like the volcanic rock of the Galápagos seafloor. Not blue.
- On top: instead of Oddish's five leaves, five kelp blades in exactly the places, sizes and directions of Oddish's leaves, fanning out from one point on top of the head, mostly up and to the right. The blades are olive to golden brown, leathery, with wrinkled surfaces and finely toothed, wavy edges, like real kelp.

POSE
- Three-quarter view facing left, the classic Oddish walking pose.

STYLE AND FORMAT
- 64x64 canvas, max 16 colors, 1 px dark outline, light from top left, flat Gen 3 shading, no dithering.
- Plain white background. No ground, water, bubbles, shadow or extra effects. One single creature, no extra views.

AVOID
- No green land-plant leaves, no blue body, no flower, no fire or smoke.
```

## Gloom
```
Design a regional form of the Pokémon Gloom and draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style pixel art).

CONCEPT
- Gloom from the deep-sea hydrothermal vents near the Galápagos. Type: Water/Fire. It evolves from an Oddish with five olive-brown kelp blades on its head, after sinking into the dark deep sea.
- Body, face and pose of Gloom's Gen 3 front sprite: the round body, the short arms held out, the short legs, the squinting closed eyes and the wide open drooling mouth.
- Body color: dark basalt grey.
- On top: a young hydrothermal vent forming. A big lumpy, irregular mound of mineral crust grows on the head, built from stacked rough layers of black and dark grey sulfide with patches of pale yellow sulfur and rusty orange crust. It is uneven and knobbly, like a rock that grew, not a smooth or segmented bud. Jagged cracks run through the crust and glow orange-red from the heat inside, like cooling lava.
- A few wilted kelp blades, faded to a dull brown, hang limply down from under the mound on the left and right. Their tips are scorched black by the heat, with a thin glowing orange edge, like burning paper.
- The drool is glowing orange-hot vent water, and the inside of the open mouth glows like a furnace.

POSE
- Three-quarter view facing left, the classic Gloom pose.

STYLE AND FORMAT
- 64x64 canvas, max 16 colors, 1 px dark outline, light from top left, flat Gen 3 shading, no dithering.
- Plain white background. No ground, water, bubbles, smoke or extra effects. One single creature, no extra views.

AVOID
- No smooth round bud, no bud made of petals or segments, no spots pattern like Gloom's bud, no green, no flower, no flames.
```

## Vileplume
```
Design a regional form of the Pokémon Vileplume and draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style pixel art).

CONCEPT
- Vileplume from the deep-sea hydrothermal vents near the Galápagos. Type: Water/Fire. Its line starts as Oddish with kelp on its head; Gloom grows a mineral mound; Vileplume has become a living hot vent with sea anemones living around it.
- Body and pose of Vileplume's Gen 3 front sprite: the small body under a large, wide top, short arms held out, short legs, the face mostly hidden under the top.
- Body color: dark basalt grey.
- On top: in the center, where Vileplume's flower center is, a short, wide vent crater of black mineral crust that glows orange-hot inside. Around it, in place of Vileplume's five petals, five big, plump, open sea anemones sit in a ring, each a fleshy round cushion with a ring of short, thick tentacles, in soft pink and peach colors. Together they form one wide, flat top as wide as Vileplume's flower.

POSE
- Three-quarter view facing left, the classic Vileplume pose.

STYLE AND FORMAT
- 64x64 canvas, max 16 colors, 1 px dark outline, light from top left, flat Gen 3 shading, no dithering.
- Plain white background. No ground, water, bubbles, smoke or extra effects. No fish, crabs or other creatures. One single creature, no extra views.

AVOID
- No petals, no flower, no green, no leaves, no tall chimney, no flames.
```
