# Steenmozaïek-jaguar: Gemini-prompts en rondes

Volgorde (FAKEMON_DESIGN: begin bij de eindvorm): eerst fase 2 (volwassen), dan fase 1 (welp) in een **nieuw gesprek** met de gekozen fase 2 als tweede bijlage. Bij elke prompt de foto `../referenties/beeldje_jordi_mexico.png` meesturen.

## Prompt fase 2 (volwassen)
```
Attached is a photo of a stone figurine from Mexico: a jaguar head with an open mouth and white fangs, made as a mosaic of angular inlaid stone pieces in blue/lilac, reddish-brown, black and cream, separated by thin seams, with a round black eye.

Design an original Pokémon based on this figurine: a pure Rock-type jaguar, the fully evolved (second and final) stage of a two-stage line. The whole creature, head and body, is a stone mosaic inspired by the figurine. As the final stage it is more beautiful and more advanced in material and colour than its pre-evolution. The head is inspired by the figurine: open mouth, white fangs, round black eye. Anatomy of a real adult jaguar.

Pose: standing on all four legs, body in 3/4 view facing left, head turned slightly more towards the viewer than the body. Compact composition that fits in a square, legs close to the body.

Style: an official Gen 3 Pokémon front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style), 64x64 pixels, at most 16 colours, 1px dark outline, light from the top left. Plain white background. No ground, no rocks, no shadow, no glow or effects.

AVOID: plush/Neopets look, realistic painting, extra rocks or crystals growing from the body, a statue on a pedestal.
```
