# Bokser-Mr. Mime – startprompt r10 (23 nov, NIEUW gesprek, GEEN bijlage)
"Mr. Mime" niet meer noemen (les Banette-evolutie: Gemini valt terug op de genoemde soort; r9 = palette swap). Kop in woorden.

```
Design a brand-new Pokémon: an old-time GENTLEMAN boxer, like the bare-knuckle boxers in 1880s black-and-white portrait photos. Fighting type.
Draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): pixel art, 64×64, max 16 colors, dark selout outline, Gen 3 shading with 3–4 tones per color, plain white background.
Draw ONE single Pokémon.

HEAD
A big round, bald head with a white mime face: two small black eyes, a small calm closed mouth, round grey cheek circles.
Two jagged black hair spikes grow from the sides of the head and are slicked far BACK, close along the head (not sticking out sideways like horns).

BODY
Short and stocky like a little heavyweight: no neck, heavy rounded shoulders, a barrel chest, short thick arms, big bare fists, short thick legs, simple dark shoes.
Its high-waisted old-fashioned dark boxing trunks are a pattern of its own body, with a lighter waistband.
No balls, spheres or round pads on the shoulders, hands or knees. No boxing gloves.

COLORS
Black-and-white palette, like an old photo and like a classic mime: white, greys and black.

POSE (most important)
It is NOT punching and NOT in a fighting stance: it stands still and proud, posing for an old portrait photo.
- Legs straight and close together, feet almost touching. No bent knees, no wide stance.
- The far arm is held out forward to the left, the elbow slightly bent, the fist raised a little above shoulder height and held still.
- The near arm is bent with the elbow out to the side, its fist resting against the middle of the chest.
- Chest out, chin up.

VIEW
Three-quarter view facing left, like every Gen 3 front sprite. Compact.

DO NOT
No ground, no ring, no shadow, no glow, no effects, no motion lines.
No cartoon line art, no plush/Neopets look, no chibi mascot, not a realistic human.
```
