# Ruimtestation (fase 3), Gemini-prompt r3 – NIEUW gesprek, geen bijlage
Jordi (25 nov, na r2-prompt): "de bal moet geen losse bal meer zijn maar onderdeel van een groter geheel" · "en misschien moeten we meerdere ballen doen".
Basis = r2. Wijzigingen: kern weer een bal (Jordi zegt "bal"), half verzonken in het station; drie ballen (Claude koos het aantal; "misschien" van Jordi → zo geprobeerd). Ogen op de andere ballen niet genoemd (Jordi: eerst de vorm zien). Ring/panelen zoals r2.

```
Design a Pokémon: an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style), 64×64 pixels, max 16 colors, clean dark outline, plain white background, no ground, no shadow, no effects or glow.

WHAT IT IS: a living space station. It is a pure machine with its own personality, the way Magneton, Metagross and Porygon are Pokémon: no animal parts, no arms, no legs, no mouth. The station itself is the creature's body. At a glance it must read as a SPACE STATION: several modules docked together with big solar panel wings.

POSE AND ANGLE (most important): Gen 3 front-sprite 3/4 view facing the viewer's LEFT, seen slightly from above. Everything is drawn in this one perspective. The creature is big and fills most of the 64×64 frame, but stays compact in a square.

THE SPHERES: the station is built around SEVERAL round white spheres (three), set INTO the station's structure like its main modules. They are not loose balls floating in front of it: each sphere is half sunk into the station, joined to the modules by purple docking collars, so the whole thing reads as one solid machine.
THE MAIN SPHERE (center front, the clear focus) holds one big camera-lens eye: from outside to inside a thin dark line, a white ring, a purple ring, a dark ring, a turquoise iris and a dark pupil, flat colors. Two long purple antennae (not hair-thin) with a round purple ball on each tip, rising from the top of the main sphere and curving backward.

THE STATION: short white cylinder modules connect the spheres to each other with purple ring-shaped docking collars, forming one connected body. No part floats loose.

SOLAR PANELS: two big flat rectangular solar panel wings, dark navy-purple with a lighter purple grid of a few large cells, one on each side, attached with short purple rods. They are large, like on a satellite, and both lie in the same 3/4 perspective.

RING: one SMALL, thin white ring partly behind the station, like a background detail. It must NOT be the main shape and must not frame the creature.

DETAIL LEVEL: it must translate well to a 64×64 sprite: big readable shapes, thick parts, medium detail, nothing thinner than the antennae. No rivets, no text, no logos, no tiny windows or greebles.

COLORS: pearly white with lavender shading, purple accents (joints, collars, rods, antennae), dark navy-purple solar panels with lighter purple grid lines, turquoise iris as the only accent. Light from the upper left. Nothing else.

DO NOT: a loose ball in front of a station, a big ring or wheel as the main shape, a steering wheel or lifebuoy look, a planet with a Saturn ring, starfield or galaxy patterns, stars or Earth around it, a flat front view or side view, realistic NASA/ISS hardware, a robot with a face screen, arms or legs, cute plush/Neopets style.
```
