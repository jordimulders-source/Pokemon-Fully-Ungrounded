Nieuw gesprek. Bijlage: de Shuppeteer-afbeelding (fan-fakemon, Jordi slaat hem zelf op), alleen als maat voor bouw en massa. Jordi: "die shuppeteer is al iets beter" / "maar hij moet nog groter en imposanter". Verder alle besluiten uit SPEC.md.

## Keuzes Claude (niet besproken; schrappen mag)
- Eerst artwork, daarna pas de sprite (zoals v6).
- Expliciet niet overnemen van Shuppeteer: rood-zwarte helften, belletjes, Shuppets als handpoppen, ritsgrijns (wijken af van Jordi's besluiten).

```
Create official Pokémon concept artwork in Ken Sugimori's Gen 3 style: clean lines, flat cel shading, plain white background.

This is the evolution of Banette, Ghost/Dark: an evil ghost puppet master / joker.

The attached image is a fan design on the right ("Shuppeteer"). Use it ONLY as a reference for size and build: my Pokémon must be EVEN BIGGER, broader and more imposing than that one. Do NOT copy its colours, its bells, its zipper mouth or its hand puppets.

DESIGN
- Banette's zipper mouth was opened and this new creature climbed out of it. Banette's former body now hangs around his waist like a garment, the opened zipper around his waist. It is just clothing now: Banette's eyes and face are not on it. His torso rises directly out of the opened zipper.
- His own body and head are much bigger than Banette's: massive, broad, heavy, towering.
- His head has two long points like the points of a jester's hat.
- A very sinister grin: a zigzag mouth with teeth, the teeth the same colour as his head. No zipper in his mouth.
- Eyes similar to Banette's, but bigger.
- Both arms held up high. His hands are two marionette control crosses, not wood: made of the same dark ghostly substance as his body, growing directly from his arms.
- From the crosses hang very thin threads; on them hang two mini Shuppets, which he controls as puppets.
- He floats, with two short undefined stubby legs, like Banette.
- Colours: like Banette, a bit darker.
- Pose: 3/4 view facing left, seen slightly from above.

AVOID
- Not thin, not gaunt, not small. No glow, aura or effects. One creature plus its two Shuppet puppets, nothing else.
```
