# Kricketune-evolutie – volledig nieuwe startprompt (22 nov, nieuw gesprek, geen bijlage)
Na r3: "hij verandert niks, volledig nieuwe prompt" (Jordi).

```
Design a brand-new Pokémon: the final evolution of Kricketune (Kricketot → Kricketune → this). Type Bug/Fairy.
Draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): pixel art, 64×64, max 16 colors, dark selout outline, plain white background.

WHAT IT IS
A cricket that is an orchestra conductor: a real maestro, a professional who masterfully commands a concert hall and an orchestra.
It must clearly read as the third and final stage: bigger and grander than Kricketune. Grander means a true maestro, NOT bulkier or more muscular.
It must clearly read as Kricketune's evolution: Kricketune's red body, its curled moustache, its dark arms. But it is a new creature, not a copy of Kricketune.

BATON
One finger of one hand is a long, thin, pointed baton that grows out of the hand. It is the conductor's baton and also a weapon, like a crab that has one claw bigger than the other. There is no separate stick.

TAILCOAT
The wing covers hang down BEHIND the body like the two tails of a tailcoat. They do not stand out to the sides or forward like a cape or skirt.

POSE
Standing upright, both arms raised, in the middle of conducting a bar of music.

VIEW
Three-quarter view facing left, like every Gen 3 front sprite: NOT straight to the front.
Compact: the whole Pokémon fits inside a square.

DO NOT
No separate baton held in a hand. Not bulky, not muscular, no lobster or crab claws for arms.
No music notes, no sheet music, no podium, no ground, no shadow. No glow, no aura, no effects, no motion lines.
No plush/Neopets look, no chibi mascot, not a realistic insect painting.
```
