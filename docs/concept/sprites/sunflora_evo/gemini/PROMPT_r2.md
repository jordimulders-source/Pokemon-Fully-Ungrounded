Design a brand-new Pokémon, drawn as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style). Grass/Cosmic type.

What it is: a sunflower Pokémon that went so hard on photosynthesis that it gained cosmic powers. It is truly halfway between a plant and a star. It may look very cosmic and "unlocked", but its body still clearly looks like a plant. It floats in the air instead of standing.

The head is where the big change is: the flower has become much more a star. Not a five-pointed star shape, but a cosmic-looking star.

Face (in the middle of the head): a content, very, very happy face.

Sprite rules:
- 64x64 pixels, max 16 colors, 1 px dark outline in Gen 3 style, soft Gen 3 shading.
- 3/4 view facing left, compact so it fills a square.
- Plain white background; no ground, no shadow, no rock.
- No glow, no sparkles, no light rays, no loose effects.
- NO starfield, galaxy or night-sky pattern anywhere on the body.
AVOID: plush/toy look, Neopets style, chibi mascot, realistic sunflower painting, a human in a costume, a five-pointed star.
