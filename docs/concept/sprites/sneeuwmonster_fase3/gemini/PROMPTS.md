## r1 (nieuw gesprek, zonder bijlage)
```
Design an original Pokemon. Type: Ice/Fire. Final stage of a three-stage line.

POSE (most important): it SITS, very relaxed, leaning back, legs in front of it, like someone sitting on a sauna bench. Both arms are spread out to the sides and slightly back, hanging loosely as if draped over the backrest of a bench (do not draw the bench; no objects, no ground). Facing 3/4 to the left.

Concept: a big yeti made of snow and red-hot stones. Its build looks huge and dangerous: massive shoulders, a broad heavy body, thick arms, rough shaggy snow fur. But its personality is very friendly: as long as it can enjoy its sauna it is completely relaxed and content. Expression: relaxed, content and friendly.

Head: an old wooden sauna bucket still sits upside down on its head, but it has grown together with the head and become part of it, like part of its skull: the wooden staves and the dark metal band blend into the snow fur, so it is no longer purely a bucket.

Stones: several stones are part of its body: rough, irregular, lumpy rocks (not round, not smooth, not gems, not crystals, not shiny): dark charcoal-black volcanic rock, heated until they glow, with bright orange and yellow glowing cracks running through them and glowing orange edges, like lava rock or red-hot sauna stones. Where: one big stone on each shoulder; two big stones on the belly, one above the other in a vertical line, like the buttons of a snowman; and if it fits, one stone on each knee. Only the stones themselves glow; no halo, no light rays.

Colors: snow in white with cool light blue and grey-blue shading. Bucket in warm brown wood with a dark grey band. Stones in dark charcoal with orange and yellow glowing cracks.

Style: official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style), 64x64, max 16 colors, dark outline, compact composition that fills a square, the arms kept within the square. White background, no ground, no shadow, no bench, no effects, no steam, no fire, no falling snowflakes.

AVOID: the Flurry from Mario, Abomasnow, Snorunt, Vanillite, Bigfoot photo realism, a classic three-ball snowman, a carrot nose, a scarf, a towel, an angry or roaring face, sharp fangs showing, plush toy / Neopets look, flames.
```

```
Design an original Pokemon. Type: Ice/Fire. Final stage of a three-stage line.

Each is a rough, irregular, lumpy piece of dark charcoal-black volcanic rock, heated until it glows, with bright orange and yellow glowing cracks running through it and glowing orange edges, like lava rock or red-hot sauna stones. Style: official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style), 64x64, max 16 colors, dark outline, compact composition that fills a square, the arms kept within the square. White background, no ground, no shadow, no bench, no effects, no steam, no fire, no falling snowflakes.

AVOID: the Flurry from Mario, Abomasnow, Snorunt, Vanillite, a rock or golem creature, grey or black rock armor, a big dark belly, a plain bucket on the head, a grin or laughing face, stiff outstretched arms, a fat round body, a classic three-ball snowman, a carrot nose, a scarf, a towel, sharp fangs showing, plush toy / Neopets look, flames.
```
