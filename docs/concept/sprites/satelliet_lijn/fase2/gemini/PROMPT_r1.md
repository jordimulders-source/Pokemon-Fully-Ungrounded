# Satelliet-lijn fase 2 – startprompt r1 (23 nov), NIEUW gesprek, geen bijlage
Basis (SPEC): satelliet met zonnepanelen; fase 1 (witte bol met één robotoog, paarse naad, paarse antennes met bolvoetjes) is onderdeel van fase 2 en wordt een kleiner deel van het geheel (Jordi). Pure machine met eigen persoonlijkheid, 1 oog, wit/paars à la Mewtwo, cyber. Fase 1 NIET bij naam/als bijlage (GEMINI.md: pre-evolutie trekt Gemini terug). Telescoopoog uit het eerdere voorstel vervangen door het oog van fase 1 (fase 1 is onderdeel); voorleggen aan Jordi.

```
Design a new, original Pokémon.
Draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): pixel art, 64×64, max 16 colors, dark selout outline all around, plain white background.

WHAT IT IS
A living machine: a satellite. It is a pure machine with its own personality, the way Magneton, Metang and Porygon are Pokémon: no animal parts, no arms, no legs.
Its core is a small pearly white sphere with one curved purple seam and exactly ONE eye: a robotic camera lens of concentric purple rings around a teal iris with a dark pupil (flat colors, no glossy shine). This sphere is now only a SMALL part of the whole body: the satellite has grown around it.
Around the core: a sleek satellite body with two large SOLAR PANEL wings (one on each side, the far one partly hidden), plus a few purple antennas sweeping back. Bigger and more complex than a simple ball, but still clean and readable: big shapes, medium detail, no tiny greebles.

DETAIL LEVEL
Medium. Solar panels: a simple grid of a few large cells (not dozens of tiny ones). No rivets, no text, no logos, no tiny buttons.

COLORS
Pearly white body with purple accents, close to Mewtwo's colors; solar panels in a deep purple/indigo with lighter purple cell lines. Smooth, sleek, cyber look. Light from the upper left; shadow side in a clearly deeper lavender-purple. Antennas and their ball sockets purple.

POSE
It floats in the air, solar panels spread like wings, slightly tilted as if gliding.

VIEW
True three-quarter view facing LEFT, seen slightly from above, like every Gen 3 front sprite: the eye on the front-left, the near solar panel in front, the far panel smaller and partly behind the body.
It is clearly bigger than a first-stage Pokémon: it fills most of the 64×64 frame.

DO NOT
No effects, no glow, no electricity, no stars, planets or Earth around it.
No ground, no shadow.
Not Magneton, not Metang, not a robot with a face screen, not a real-world satellite copy.
No plush/Neopets look.
```
