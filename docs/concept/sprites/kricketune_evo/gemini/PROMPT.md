# Kricketune-evolutie – Gemini-startprompt r1 (22 nov, nieuw gesprek, geen bijlage)

```
Design a brand-new Pokémon: the evolution of Kricketune. Type Bug/Fairy. It is an orchestra CONDUCTOR. It must clearly read as Kricketune's evolution (same colors, same curled moustache-like antennae, same cricket build), but it is a new creature, not a copy of Kricketune.
It must clearly read as a THIRD (final) stage: Kricketot → Kricketune → this. Bigger and grander than Kricketune.
Draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): pixel art, 64×64, max 16 colors, dark selout outline, plain white background.

WHAT IT IS
A cricket that is a conductor.
One forearm ends in a thin conductor's baton: organic, part of its body, the way Kricketune's arms are blades.
Its wing covers hang down behind it like the two tails of a tailcoat.

POSE
Standing upright, both arms raised, in the middle of conducting a bar of music.

VIEW
Three-quarter view facing left, like every Gen 3 front sprite.
Compact: the whole Pokémon fits inside a square.

DO NOT
No separate baton held in a hand: the baton IS the forearm.
No music notes, no sheet music, no podium, no ground, no shadow. No glow, no aura, no effects, no motion lines.
No plush/Neopets look, no chibi mascot, not a realistic insect painting.
```
