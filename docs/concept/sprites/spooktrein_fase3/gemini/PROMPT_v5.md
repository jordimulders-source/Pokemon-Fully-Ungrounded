Dier als skelet afgewezen: "nee dit is een trein, geen dier".
Aanpak v5 (Claude): Gemini valt bij pixelart steeds terug op een echte loc; eerst een ontwerp in officiële Pokémon-artworkstijl (daar kent Gemini de vormtaal beter), het gekozen ontwerp daarna in een nieuw gesprek als bijlage omzetten naar een Gen 3-sprite (stap 2 volgt). Roest als textuur, niet als kleur. Geen bijlage.

## Stap 1: ontwerp

```
Design an original Pokémon in the official Ken Sugimori Pokémon artwork style (clean lines, flat cel shading, plain white background).

TYPE: Steel / Ghost. Final stage of its line.

CONCEPT: a ghost that has possessed an old steam locomotive and turned it into its body. It is a train Pokémon, designed with Pokémon shape language like Revavroom or Klinklang: a few bold, stylized train parts, not a realistic locomotive.

THE TRAIN PARTS ARE ITS FACE AND BODY
- The front of the locomotive is its head. The cowcatcher at the front opens like a mouth.
- Two identical ghost eyes on the front of the head. The headlight is NOT an eye.
- The face is NOT human: no nose, no eyebrows, no lips, no cheeks, no human face on a round plate.
- Few parts: a long low boiler, a short chimney, the cowcatcher mouth, two big front wheels. No cab, no windows, no rods, no pipes.
- Long and low silhouette, the head pushed forward.

EXPRESSION
- Cheeky and mischievous: it is visibly having fun being a ghost and enjoys going so fast. Not angry, not scary.

GHOST SMOKE
- Behind the front wheels, the back of the train dissolves into green ghost smoke, with faint ghostly back wheels inside the smoke.

COLORS
- Dark grey old iron covered in rust patches and rust streaks (rusty, but still a working train).
- Green for the ghost (eyes, smoke, ghostly wheels).

FULL SPEED
- The whole body leans forward, charging ahead; the front wheels are drawn in motion like in a comic book. No speed lines or rails.

VIEW: slightly more frontal than a three-quarter view, facing left.

AVOID: no human face, not Thomas the Tank Engine or any children's TV train, no realistic locomotive, no cab, no wagons, no skull or horror mask, no conductor, no small ghosts in the smoke.
```
