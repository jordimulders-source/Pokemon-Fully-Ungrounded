v4 + Jordi over r4: "iets te realistisch en iets te weinig pokemon", klauwen "volledig van steel", poten: een paar dikke banden, kuif "meer een harpy look" en "meer overdreven feature".

```
Design a new Pokémon based on the HARPY EAGLE of the Amazon rainforest. The attached photo is a real harpy eagle: it is the source for the crest. Its type is Steel/Flying.
Draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): pixel art, 64×64, max 16 colors, dark selout outline, plain white background.

WHAT IT IS
Anatomy of a real harpy eagle: a big, powerful eagle with a large rounded head, a hooked beak, and the harpy's double crest on top of its head. The crest is more stylized than on the real bird: on a real harpy eagle the crest feathers are a bit scattered; here they are not. The double crest is its iconic signature feature and is exaggerated: bigger and more striking than on the real bird, with even more of the harpy eagle's crest look, so it becomes a Pokémon feature. Pale grey head, a dark band across the upper chest, white belly, dark slate-grey back and wings, legs with only 2–3 thick bands (no thin stripes; they must read at 64×64).
Its Steel typing is ONLY in its claws and its beak: the claws are completely steel: the whole feet, toes and talons, steel-grey with metal sheen, NOT yellow anywhere. The hooked beak is steel too. Still part of the bird's body. No metal anywhere else, no armor plates.

BUILD
Not compact and not stocky. Not too buff either.

POSE
It sits upright, perched, calm and imposing, with its double crest raised high. The crest must be clearly visible and readable. The huge steel claws are clearly visible in front at the bottom. 2 wings in total (near and far), folded against the body.

VIEW
A true three-quarter view facing left, like every Gen 3 front sprite: we see the chest and face from the front-left, not the bird in side profile. The far wing and far foot are on the left (narrower, partly hidden), the near ones on the right. The head turns a bit more toward the viewer than the body.

STYLE
Less realistic, more Pokémon: stylized like an official Pokémon design, not a realistic bird drawn as pixel art.

DO NOT
No branch, no perch, no ground, no shadow under it.
No yellow feet. No armor plates, no metal on the body or wings (only claws and beak), no glow, no effects.
No plush/Neopets look, no chibi mascot, not a realistic photo-like bird: the photo is only a source, the result is a Pokémon sprite.
```
