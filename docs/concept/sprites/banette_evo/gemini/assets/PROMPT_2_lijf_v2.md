Attached is the finished HEAD of my original Pokémon, a Ghost/Dark evil puppet master / jester. Keep this head EXACTLY as it is (shape, points, bells, eyes, mouth) and draw the rest of the Pokémon below it. DO NOT DRAW BANETTE.

BODY
- Big and tall, like Dusknoir or Grimmsnarl are big: a large, towering build with broad shoulders and long arms. Use those two only for SIZE and PROPORTIONS, nothing else from them.
- NOT fat, NOT muscular. No belly, no muscles.
- Smooth, in the same dark colour as the face. The head sits directly on the body.

HE CLIMBED OUT OF A DOLL
- He used to be a grey cloth doll whose mouth was a zipper. That zipper mouth opened and he climbed out of it.
- The doll's empty body is now worn around his waist and hips like a costume he has half stepped out of: grey cloth with patches and stitches, its two limp little arms hanging at the sides, its limp tail with a yellow tip hanging at the back. The doll has no face.
- The opened zipper runs all the way around his waist: his own body rises out of that open zipper. The doll is NOT a small separate thing hanging from him, and NOT underwear.
- Under the doll costume, two short stubby legs without detail. He floats.

HANDS
- He has no normal hands. Each arm ends in a marionette control cross instead of a hand: two straight bars crossing at right angles like a + sign, held horizontally, in the same dark colour as his body, grown from the arm. NOT a star, NOT wood, NOT held.

PUPPETS
- From the ends of each cross hang threads, 1 pixel thick.
- On the threads hangs one Shuppet under each hand, controlled as a puppet, big enough to read clearly.

STYLE AND FORMAT
- Official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style), 64x64, max 16 colours, 1 px dark outline, flat shading, no dithering, plain white background, 3/4 view facing left, seen slightly from above. The whole Pokémon fills almost the whole 64x64 square.
