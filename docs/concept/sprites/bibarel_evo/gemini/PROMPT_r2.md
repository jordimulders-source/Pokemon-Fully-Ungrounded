# Bibarel-evolutie – Gemini-startprompt r2 (23 nov, NIEUW gesprek, geen bijlage)

Jordi bij r1: "het is nog iets teveel bidoof, niet eens bibarel, moet echt een fase 3 zijn, zoals alphyena dat ook is". Alleen dat veranderd (fase 3-blok + DO NOT); rest gelijk aan r1. Vertaling naar volwassen verhoudingen (kleinere kop t.o.v. lijf, zwaar, niet schattig) is van Claude; "niet gespierd" blijft (Jordi).

```
Design a brand-new Pokémon. Type Water/Fighting. It is a hard-working builder beaver: a busy boss, working very hard, like real beavers do. The "construction worker" idea is shown ONLY through its body, its colors and what it is doing, NEVER through clothing.
Draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): pixel art, 64×64, max 16 colors, dark selout outline, plain white background.

FINAL EVOLUTION (most important)
This is the THIRD and final stage of its line, not a baby and not a middle stage. It must look big, heavy, mature and imposing, like a fully-grown boss, the way final evolutions do.
Adult proportions: the head is small compared to the large, heavy, barrel-shaped body; a big, broad, powerful frame. A serious, determined adult face, not cute, not round and chubby-cheeked.
Big and powerful through SIZE and WEIGHT, NOT through muscles: no muscle definition, no bodybuilder arms.

WHAT IT IS
Animal anatomy: a real BEAVER, standing upright on its hind legs.
Brown fur with a lighter tan muzzle.
Big ORANGE chisel-like front teeth (real beaver teeth are orange), clearly visible.
A big, broad, flat paddle tail, shaped a bit like a mason's trowel, with one or two BROAD bands in a bright warning color (safety yellow or orange) like a road-work sign.

POSE
Busy at work: carrying a heavy log on its shoulder (or under one arm), mid-stride, determined and in a hurry.

VIEW
Three-quarter view facing left, like every Gen 3 front sprite: the far arm and leg on the left (partly hidden), the near ones on the right.
Compact: the whole Pokémon including the log fits inside a square.

KEEP IT SIMPLE
Large, clear shapes that read at 64×64. No small details: no wood chips, no twigs, no tool belts, no claws drawn in detail, no thin stripes.

DO NOT
Not a small, cute, baby-like beaver; not a first-stage Pokémon.
No clothing: no hard hat, no helmet, no safety vest, no overalls, no belt, no gloves, no tools held in hand.
Not muscular, no bodybuilder arms.
No ground, no water, no rock, no shadow under it. No glow, no aura, no effects, no motion lines, no sweat drops.
No plush/Neopets look, no chibi mascot, not a realistic animal painting.
```
