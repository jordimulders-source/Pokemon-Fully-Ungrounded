# Guaraná fase 1 – startprompt r3 (25 nov), NIEUW gesprek, geen bijlage
Basis: PROMPT_r2 (r2 = beste tot nu toe); alleen veranderd wat Jordi akkoord gaf: groene buigzame rank i.p.v. hout, grijpranken i.p.v. stokpootjes, gladde vruchten zonder stekeltjes/raster. Eigen toevoegingen: zie r2, plus ogen iets groter (5-6 px).

```
Design a new, original Pokémon.
Draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): pixel art, 64×64, max 16 colors, dark selout outline, plain white background.

WHAT IT IS
A Grass/Ghost Pokémon based on the guaraná plant from the Amazon.
Its body IS a creeping vine: a living branch that crawls over the ground like an animal. It has no animal or human body parts at all; the ONLY animal-like features are its eyes. That is what makes it creepy.
At the front end of the vine grows its head: a tight cluster of about six closed guaraná fruits.

THE FRUITS (must clearly read as guaraná, not a rose, raspberry or grapes)
Each fruit is a separate small, round, smooth and glossy red-orange capsule with ONE thin seam line down the middle and a small rounded tip, all attached to one short stalk. No black grid lines between the fruits, no spikes or black points on the tips.
Two or three fruits are split open a little along their seam, and from each split a large white eye with a black pupil stares out at you. The eyes are clearly visible at sprite size (about 5-6 pixels wide), looking straight at the viewer.
Mood: unsettling. The eyes stare and follow you, but it is still a Pokémon you would want on your team. Not gory.
It is the first stage of an evolution line: in the next stage all fruits burst open, covered in staring eyes.

THE VINE (compact, reads well as a sprite)
One clear, readable vine body, not a tangle. It is a GREEN, soft, flexible climbing vine (a liana), not brown wood, not a tree branch or trunk. It curves in an S-bend so the whole Pokémon fits in a square, compact shape.
It moves with three or four thin curling tendrils that coil around and grip the ground, close to the body. These are plant tendrils, NOT legs: no stick legs, no feet. Only a few leaves.

POSE
It crawls toward the viewer, the fruit cluster raised and turned to you, as if it just noticed you.

VIEW
Three-quarter view facing left, like every Gen 3 front sprite.
It fills about half to two thirds of the 64×64 frame.

DO NOT
No glow, no effects, no ghostly aura, no floating particles.
No ground, no shadow, no pot.
No mouth, no teeth, no blood, no face other than the eyes.
Not a human or child figure, not a snake, not a snail, not a seahorse, not a four-legged animal, not a flower, not a bunch of grapes or raspberry.
No plush/Neopets look.
```
