# Mr. Prime – startprompt r2 (23 nov, NIEUW gesprek, GEEN bijlage)
Jordi bij r1: niet genoeg evolutie, meer naar Mr. Rime (ook de hoed), oren/vorm te veel Mr. Mime, stok niet in gevechtshouding, niet kaarsrecht.

```
Design a brand-new regional Pokémon: the final evolution of a Mr. Mime line, an OLD ENGLISH GENTLEMAN who fights with his walking cane (bartitsu, the Victorian gentleman's martial art). Fighting/Psychic type.
Draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): pixel art, 64×64, max 16 colors, dark selout outline, Gen 3 shading with 3–4 tones per color, plain white background.
Draw ONE single Pokémon.

WHAT IT IS
It is a real evolution, a big step up like Mr. Rime is from Mr. Mime. Follow MR. RIME for the body shape, the proportions and the hat: taller, with Mr. Rime's kind of body and Mr. Rime's kind of hat as part of its head. It must NOT look like Mr. Mime.
- HAIR: two hair spikes slicked flat backwards along the back of the head, pointing backward. NOT Mr. Mime's spikes that stick out to the sides.
- A moustache, a bit bigger than a very small pencil moustache.
- A MONOCLE over one eye.
- SUIT: the suit is NOT clothing. It is simply the shape and pattern of its own body.
- It holds a walking cane.

COLORS
Black-and-white palette, like an old photo and like a classic mime: white, greys and black.

POSE (most important)
- Standing perfectly upright, straight as a rod: back straight, chin up. Legs straight and close together, feet almost touching. No bent knees, no slouch.
- The cane is held up like a weapon, ready to fight: raised in front of the body, diagonal. It does NOT touch the ground and it does NOT lean on it.
- COMPACT and NOT WIDE: arms and cane stay close to the body, nothing sticks far out to the sides.

VIEW
Three-quarter view facing left, like every Gen 3 front sprite. Compact, fits a square.

DO NOT
No ground, no shadow, no glow, no effects, no motion lines.
No cartoon line art, no plush/Neopets look, no chibi mascot, not a realistic human.
```
