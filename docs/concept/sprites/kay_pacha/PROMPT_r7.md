# Gemini-prompt Kay Pacha r7 (nieuw gesprek, geen bijlage)

Basis r3 (Jordi: "als deze een masker krijgt en de versieringen ... simplistischer"), plus: mos ipv blaadjes, masker bedekt minder dan r6, "nog steeds teveel kleine details", "die kruizen wil ik weg", "nieuwe prompt from scratch, maar wees specifiek".
Eigen invulling van Claude: masker = alleen voorhoofd + rond de ogen (snuit, neus, mond, oren vrij), geen mensengezicht; versiering uit r3 per stuk beschreven; aantallen (4 punten) en kleurtellingen.

```
Design a new legendary Pokémon as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): pixel art, 64x64, max 16 colors, dark outline, light from top-left, plain white background, no ground, no shadow, no effects. Big, simple shapes only: every part must still be readable at 64x64.

WHAT IT IS: a Grass/Psychic legendary puma, guardian of "Kay Pacha" (the world of the living in Inca belief), part of a legendary trio like Entei and Raikou. Must not look like any existing Pokémon.

BODY: puma build: slim, muscular, four long legs, long thick tail curling up at the end, short rounded ears. Stylized like a Pokémon with a BIG head. Coat: green (one light green, one dark green for shading, darker green on the far legs). Eyes: purple. No spots, no stripes.

DECORATION: Inca gold, exactly these four pieces and nothing else:
1. MASK: a gold half-mask that covers ONLY the forehead and the area around the eyes, like a domino mask. The purple eyes are visible through it. The muzzle, nose, mouth, cheeks and ears are NOT covered and stay green puma. It is not a human face.
2. HEADPIECE: one tall gold blade on top of the mask, shaped like a tumi (Inca ceremonial knife with a half-moon blade), leaning slightly back.
3. BACK CREST: one big gold crest standing up along the middle of the back, in a stepped zigzag shape with 4 points (like the zigzag walls of Sacsayhuamán).
4. HIP PLATE: one large smooth gold plate covering the hip and upper back leg, attached to the crest.
All gold is plain and smooth with simple shading (3 gold tones). NO engravings, NO lines or patterns on the gold, NO spirals, NO crosses, NO faces, NO studs, NO small plates, NO straps or bands.

MOSS: a few big, solid patches of dark moss green lying on top of the gold pieces (on the crest and the hip plate). Simple flat shapes, not speckles or texture. No leaves, no vines.

POSE: 3/4 view facing left, standing on four legs, head raised, proud and calm.

DO NOT: leaves, vines, crosses, chest plates, shoulder plates, collar, bracelets, jewelry, small details, glowing aura, magic effects, rocks, background.
```
