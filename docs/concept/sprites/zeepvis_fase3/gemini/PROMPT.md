# Gemini-prompt: zeep-vislijn fase 3 (21 nov)

Jordi (20 nov): fase 3 = "een kleurrijke kempvis waar de vinnen gemaakt zijn van zeepschuim/zeepsop". Lijn: clownfish in roze zeepbel → discusvis als zeepje (schuim uit de kieuwen) → deze. Water/Fairy. Geen bijlagen.

## Prompt v1
```
Design a new Pokémon and draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style pixel art).

THE CREATURE
- A Water/Fairy Pokémon based on a betta fish (Siamese fighting fish, Betta splendens), with real betta anatomy: compact body, upturned mouth, long flowing fins and tail.
- The body is colorful.
- Its fins and tail ARE made of soap foam / soapy suds: they are not normal fins with bubbles on them, the long flowing betta fins themselves consist of foam. Not carried, not attached: the foam is the fin.
- It is the final evolution of a small clownfish inside a pink soap bubble.

POSE AND VIEW
- Swimming, facing left in a 3/4 view (far side on the left and smaller, near side on the right), fins and tail spread out the way a betta displays them.
- Compact composition that fits a square, like official Gen 3 sprites; fins and tail flow around the body instead of stretching far out.

STYLE AND FORMAT
- 64x64 pixel sprite, max 16 colors, 1 px dark outline around everything (also around the foam, so it stays readable on white), light from top left, clean shading with large calm areas like official Gen 3 sprites, no noise.
- Plain white background. No water, ground, rock, shadow or base. No glow, sparkles or effects. One single creature.

AVOID
- Not Milotic, Feebas, Goldeen, Alomomola or Finizen. No plush toy, chibi or mascot look, no oversized sparkly eyes, no human features.
```

## Prompt v2 (nieuw gesprek, geen bijlage)
```
Design a new Pokémon and draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style pixel art).

THE CREATURE
- A Water/Fairy Pokémon based on a betta fish (Siamese fighting fish, Betta splendens), with real betta anatomy: compact sturdy body, upturned mouth, long flowing fins and tail.
- It is a FINAL evolution and must look fully grown and mature: an adult fish with presence, not a baby or a cute small fish. Not angrier, just more grown-up.
- Colors: the body is blue on the head, shifting to purple towards the tail, with a visible scale pattern; pink lips. The fins and tail are creamy white soap foam with a faint pink tint, with thin blue fin rays running through them.
- Its fins and tail ARE made of soap foam / soapy suds: the long flowing betta fins themselves consist of foam, with round bubbles along their edges. Not normal fins with bubbles on them, not carried, not attached: the foam is the fin.
- Nothing on top of the head: no bubble, bump, crest or growth on the head.
- The body is large and clearly the main part of the creature; the foam fins are big but do not dwarf the body (roughly: body and head take up about half of the whole figure).

POSE AND VIEW
- Swimming, facing left in a real 3/4 view, not a side profile: the body is turned towards the viewer so we see the front of the head and some of the chest/belly, the far side is on the left and smaller, the near side on the right. Both eyes may be visible, the far one smaller.
- Fins and tail spread out the way a betta displays them, flowing around the body.
- Compact composition that fits a square, like official Gen 3 sprites.

STYLE AND FORMAT
- 64x64 pixel sprite, max 16 colors, 1 px dark outline around everything (also around the foam, so it stays readable on white), light from top left, clean shading with large calm areas like official Gen 3 sprites, no noise.
- Plain white background. No water, ground, rock, shadow or base. No glow, sparkles or effects. One single creature.

AVOID
- Not Milotic, Feebas, Goldeen, Alomomola or Finizen. No plush toy, chibi or mascot look, no oversized sparkly eyes, no human features.
```
