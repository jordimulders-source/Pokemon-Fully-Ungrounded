Design a new Pokémon: the final evolution of Emolga (Electric/Flying flying-squirrel Pokémon). It must look like a real evolution — a clearly bigger, more mature creature with its own design, NOT Emolga with longer wings.

Concept: an adrenaline-junkie flying squirrel based on WINGSUIT FLYING (people jumping from cliffs in a suit with large fabric wings between arms and body and between the legs). Its gliding membrane is a SUPER LARGE wingsuit: huge taut panels from wrist to ankle on both sides plus a big panel between the legs. The body is long and elongated and grown into the wings — the membrane starts directly along the whole side of the body, so body and wings form one streamlined shape. Aerodynamic look overall.

Head: ears longer and more pointed than Emolga's. No yellow cheek spots; instead a visor-coloured area around the eyes, like the tinted visor of a helmet (it is part of its fur/skin, not an object).

Limbs: longer legs than Emolga, stretched out within the wingsuit. Exactly two arms and two legs.

Belly: a cool aerodynamic pattern on its belly.

Colour pattern (Emolga's real pattern): white face and white belly/underside of the body; black on top of the head, the back and the upper side of the wing membrane; bright yellow on the underside of the wing membrane.

Pose: diving steeply downward like a real wingsuit flyer — head down and forward, body pointing down-left, arms and legs spread wide so the whole wingsuit is stretched open, tail trailing behind. The near wing panel is fully visible, the far wing panel partly behind the body. Confident daredevil expression. No helmet, no goggles, no clothing or accessories.

Style: official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style), 64×64 pixels, max 16 colours, dark outline, 3/4 view facing left, compact composition filling the square. No electricity, sparks, speed lines, glow or effects; no ground, cloud, rock or shadow. Plain white background.

AVOID: plush/Neopets look, bat or dragon wings, bird feathers, a human in a costume, a parachute, a simply bigger Emolga, round cheek spots.
