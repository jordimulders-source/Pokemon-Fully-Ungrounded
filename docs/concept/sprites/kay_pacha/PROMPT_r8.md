# Gemini-prompt Kay Pacha r8 (nieuw gesprek, geen bijlage)

Jordi: "laten we een grass/psychic poema maken die daar tussen zou passen" + "akkoord" op Claudes voorstel (zie SPEC). Alle onderdelen zijn Claudes voorstel.

```
Design a new legendary Pokémon as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): pixel art, 64x64, max 16 colors, dark outline, light from top-left, plain white background, no ground, no shadow, no effects. Big, simple shapes that read at 64x64.

WHAT IT IS: a Grass/Psychic legendary puma that fits right next to Entei, Suicune and Raikou, as if it were a fourth legendary beast by the same designers. It is the living land of the Andes in the body of a big cat: NOT an animal wearing accessories. All its special features are part of its body, the way Raikou's cloud, Entei's mane and Suicune's ribbons are. Its own design, not a copy of any existing Pokémon.

BODY: stylized fantasy big cat with puma features (slim, powerful, long legs, short rounded ears, big head), green coat.
FACE PLATE: like Entei's and Raikou's face plates, a hard plate that is part of its face, shaped like a stylized Inca puma head. Purple eyes.
MANTLE: its biggest feature, like Raikou's cloud or Entei's mane: a large mantle of living green plant growth from the neck to the hips, stepping down in broad layers like Inca farming terraces on a mountainside.
CREST: one big, simple stepped (staircase-shaped) Inca crest on the forehead, like Suicune's crest.
MARKINGS: bold purple stepped stripes on the legs and flanks, like Raikou's stripes but in an Inca staircase shape.
TAIL: two long tails like vines, like Suicune's ribbons.

POSE: 3/4 view facing left, standing on four legs, proud and powerful, like the Gen 3 sprites of the legendary beasts.

DO NOT: jewelry, gold ornaments, armor, collars, bracelets, clothing, glowing aura, magic effects, rocks, ground, background.
```
