# Gemini-prompt: Banette-evolutie, v1 (17 nov)

Nieuw gesprek. Bijlagen: `bijlage_banette.png` en `bijlage_shuppet.png` (Gen 3-sprites, 4× vergroot).
Gebaseerd op SPEC.md; geen eigen toevoegingen behalve de vaste Gen 3-eisen (GEMINI.md). Kleurregel staat open tot Jordi antwoordt.

```
Design an original Pokémon: the evolution of BANETTE (Ghost/Dark). Attached: the Gen 3 sprite of BANETTE (its pre-evolution) and the Gen 3 sprite of SHUPPET (Banette's own pre-evolution).

CONCEPT
- An evil ghost puppet master. The zipper mouth of Banette has been opened and this new creature has climbed out of it.
- What Banette used to be now hangs around him as a garment, with the open zipper around his waist. It is truly a piece of clothing now: Banette's eyes and face are NOT on it.
- He still looks somewhat like Banette, but bigger and more imposing.

BODY
- Floating. Two short stubby legs, not defined, like Banette's.
- His hands are two marionette control crosses (the crossbars a puppeteer holds), NOT made of wood: they are made of the same dark ghostly substance as his body.
- He holds both arms up high.

FACE
- A very sinister grin. The mouth is NOT a zipper: a zigzag mouth with teeth, the teeth the same colour as his head.
- Eyes similar to Banette's eyes, but larger.

PUPPETS
- From his hand-crosses hang threads, each thread only 1 pixel thick.
- On the threads hang two mini SHUPPETs (small versions of the attached Shuppet), which he controls as puppets.

STYLE AND FORMAT
- Official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style), 64x64, max 16 colours, 1 px dark outline, light from top left, flat Gen 3 shading with 3 to 4 tones per colour, no dithering.
- 3/4 view facing left, with a perspective as if seen slightly from above.
- Compact: the whole figure including the two Shuppets fits in a square.
- Plain white background. NO floor, shadow, glow, aura or effects.

AVOID
- No zipper mouth. No wooden crosses. Banette's face must not appear on the garment. Not a plush toy, no glossy 3D render.
```
