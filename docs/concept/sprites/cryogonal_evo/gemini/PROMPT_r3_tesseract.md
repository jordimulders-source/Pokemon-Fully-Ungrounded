# Gemini-prompt r3: tesseract uit Cryogonal (25 nov)

r2 (`PROMPT_r2_tesseract.md`): Jordi "vorm ok, maar het moet veel meer cryogonal zijn" / "hij is totaal niet herkenbaar toch? Ik wilde van cryogonal een complexere vorm maken". Fout van Claude: r2 beschreef een losse ijskubus; Cryogonal zelf zat er niet in. Nu wordt Cryogonal wél genoemd (herkenning is hier het doel). Plakken in een nieuw Gemini-gesprek, zonder bijlage.

Notitie, eigen invulling van Claude: Cryogonal als kern in de binnenste kubus; ribben = Cryogonals kristalarmen; kettingen weggelaten.

```
Design the evolution of the Pokémon Cryogonal and draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): 64x64 pixels, max 16 colors, clean dark outline, flat cel shading, plain white background. Compact, fits in a square. No ground, no shadow, no glow, no sparkles, no special effects.

IDEA (most important): Cryogonal has grown from a flat snowflake into a much more complex geometric form: a tesseract (a 4D hypercube, the classic "cube inside a cube" drawing). It must be instantly recognizable as Cryogonal's evolution: same ice crystal material, same colors, same face, same crystal style. It is NOT an animal and NOT a humanoid: no limbs, no body parts. It floats.

STRUCTURE:
- Cryogonal's own hexagonal crystal body with its face sits at the center, inside the inner cube, and is the core of the creature.
- The edges of the inner cube and the outer cube, and the 8 diagonal edges connecting their corners, are all made of Cryogonal's long faceted ice-crystal arms (the pointed, gem-like crystal spikes of Cryogonal), joined at the corners.
- Seen in 3/4 view facing left, so both cubes and their depth read clearly.
- Strict geometry: straight crystal edges, sharp facets, sharp corners. The overall silhouette is the tesseract, not a flat snowflake.

FACE: exactly Cryogonal's frozen, aggressive face on the central crystal body, big enough to read at 64x64.

COLORS: Cryogonal's colors: glossy translucent icy blue with white highlights and darker blue shading.

DO NOT: make it a plain ice cube or a glass box; put the face on a flat window; add arms, legs, a crown or a cape; add a starfield or space pattern; add snow particles or a glow aura.
```
