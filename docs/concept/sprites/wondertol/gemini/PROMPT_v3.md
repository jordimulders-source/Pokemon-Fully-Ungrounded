## Prompt

```
Design an original Pokémon and draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style).

CONCEPT
- Fire/Fairy type, the middle stage of a firework evolution line: a SALAMANDER MADE OF FIRE WHOSE LOWER BODY IS A SPINNING TOP.
- Attachment 1 (fire salamander) is only for the head and legs. Attachment 2 (a spinning top) is only for the shape of the lower body.
- THE WHOLE CREATURE IS MADE OF FIRE: no skin, living flame with flickering flame tongues along the edges and flame streaks through it. Only the eyes and the mouth are drawn into the flame.
- UPPER HALF = SALAMANDER: a FLAT, WIDE salamander head held low and forward, with small eyes sitting on top of the head and a wide mouth line. The two front legs are spread wide to the sides, gripping the rim of the top, with small splayed toes. They are legs, not arms.
- LOWER HALF = THE SPINNING TOP, MADE FROM ITS OWN BODY: from the waist down, its fiery body flares out into a wide round rim (the widest part of the whole creature), then narrows into a cone that ends in one sharp point touching the ground. The tail is the cone and the point. Spiral flame bands wrap around the cone, showing that it is spinning. It is NOT standing on legs, it is NOT lying or sitting on a separate top: the top IS its body. No hind legs.
- From the point, short solid jets of sparks spray out sideways close to the ground.
- FIREWORK COLORS WITH GREEN AND BLUE: emerald green and turquoise blue flames, glittering gold-yellow spots like a fire salamander, a few small touches of soft pink. Sparks in the same colors.
- FAIRY: playful and mischievous, a few small four-pointed twinkling stars in the flames.

STYLE AND FORMAT
- Official Gen 3 front sprite, 64x64, max 16 colors, 1 px dark outline, light from top left, flat Gen 3 shading with 3 to 4 tones per color, no dithering.
- Solid shapes with clean outlines that read as a clear silhouette at 64x64; the top shape must be obvious in the silhouette.
- Three-quarter view facing left. Compact composition that fits a square.
- Plain white background. NO floor, shadow, smoke or motion lines. One single creature.

AVOID
- NOT CHARMANDER: no big round head, no big cute eyes, no upright standing body, no belly, no arms, no hind legs, no flame on the tail. No dinosaur or dragon look, no horns or spikes.
- No separate top, disc or object. No smooth skin. No coiled body, no snake shape. No orange. No firework explosions or burst shapes. No rocket, no stick. Not Wooper, not Hitmontop. No hearts. No text. No glossy 3D render.
```
