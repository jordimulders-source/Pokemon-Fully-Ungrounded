# Regional Claydol – Gemini r10: volledig nieuwe startprompt (nieuw gesprek, GEEN bijlage)
Jordi: "misschien een algeheel nieuw prompt voor gemini". Les GEMINI.md: zonder bijlage werkt Gemini beter; alle besluiten in de tekst.
Besluiten erin: Tiwanaku/Zonnepoort-Stafgod, Claydol-kop met krans van oogjes (. - . -), 2 grote ogen (Jordi: 3 is van voren gek), rode lijn boog-V-boog → band om de zijkant (r8-concept), rustige ronde kop met een paar cracks (Jordi: niet rommelig, geen ander patroon), lijf en losse staven van r3, warme steen (palet B).

```
Design a regional form of the Pokémon CLAYDOL, type Rock/Psychic, inspired by the ancient stone carvings of TIWANAKU (Bolivia), especially the Staff God of the Gate of the Sun. It evolves from a Tiwanaku-style Baltoy that has two round eyes with a red painted line above them. It must clearly read as Claydol and as a living Pokémon, not a statue.
Draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): pixel art, 64×64, max 16 colors, dark selout outline, plain white background, three-quarter view facing left. Clean, readable pixel clusters, no noise.

HEAD (the biggest part, like Claydol)
- A big, round, carved stone head, lit from the top left and gradually darker towards the bottom right, like a ball. Calm surface with only a few thin carved cracks. No other patterns, no patches.
- A short stone point on top of the head.
- Around the top and sides of the head, a CROWN of about ten small round stone eyes (like Claydol's ring of eyes). Their pupils alternate: a dot, a short line, a dot, a short line.

FACE
- Exactly TWO big round eyes: cream white, a dark ring around each, one short horizontal line in the middle. No pupils, no mouth.
- A clear, straight stone nose ridge between the two eyes.
- Because of the three-quarter view, the two eyes and the nose sit a little to the LEFT of the middle of the head; the right part of the head is the side of the head, turning away.
- A RED painted line: one arch above each eye, meeting in a V above the nose; after the arch above the right eye it turns smoothly into a horizontal band that goes around the right side of the head, following its round shape.

BODY
- A small, sturdy stone body under the head with two short stubby arms and short stubby feet.
- On the chest: a few carved stepped lines and two red stepped hook motifs (Tiwanaku style).

STAFFS
- Two separate stone staffs float on the left and right side, not touching the body, each with a knobbed top and red bands wrapped around it, like the two staffs of the Staff God.

COLORS
- Warm beige-grey stone, cream eyes, deep red paint. Nothing else.

DO NOT
No cartoon face, no pupils in the big eyes, no mouth, no glow or aura, no ground or shadow, no human proportions, no smooth high-resolution art.
```
