# Bibarel-evolutie – Gemini-startprompt r1 (23 nov, nieuw gesprek, geen bijlage)

Bibarel bewust niet bij naam (Dundragoon-les: basisnaam = kopie). Bruine vacht met lichte snuit is een voorstel van Claude; Jordi heeft nog niet gezegd of hij herkenbaar Bibarel moet blijven.

```
Design a brand-new Pokémon. Type Water/Fighting. It is a hard-working builder beaver: a busy little boss, working very hard, like real beavers do. The "construction worker" idea is shown ONLY through its body, its colors and what it is doing, NEVER through clothing.
Draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): pixel art, 64×64, max 16 colors, dark selout outline, plain white background.

WHAT IT IS
Animal anatomy: a real BEAVER, standing upright on its hind legs. Round, solid body, NOT muscular.
Brown fur with a lighter tan muzzle.
Big ORANGE chisel-like front teeth (real beaver teeth are orange), clearly visible.
A big, broad, flat paddle tail, shaped a bit like a mason's trowel, with one or two BROAD bands in a bright warning color (safety yellow or orange) like a road-work sign.

POSE
Busy at work: carrying a log on its shoulder (or under one arm), mid-stride, determined and in a hurry.

VIEW
Three-quarter view facing left, like every Gen 3 front sprite: the far arm and leg on the left (partly hidden), the near ones on the right.
Compact: the whole Pokémon including the log fits inside a square.

KEEP IT SIMPLE
Large, clear shapes that read at 64×64. No small details: no wood chips, no twigs, no tool belts, no claws drawn in detail, no thin stripes.

DO NOT
No clothing: no hard hat, no helmet, no safety vest, no overalls, no belt, no gloves, no tools held in hand.
Not muscular, no bodybuilder arms.
No ground, no water, no rock, no shadow under it. No glow, no aura, no effects, no motion lines, no sweat drops.
No plush/Neopets look, no chibi mascot, not a realistic animal painting.
```
