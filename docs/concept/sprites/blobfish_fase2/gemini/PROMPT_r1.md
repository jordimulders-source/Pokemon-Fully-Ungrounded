## Prompt

```
Design a new Pokémon and draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): 64x64 pixel art, at most 16 colours, light from the top left, plain white background, no ground, no shadow, no glow, no sparkles, no effects outside the creature.

The Pokémon is based on the BLOBFISH (Psychrolutes marcidus), see the attached photo: a deep-sea fish that, once pulled up to the surface, collapses into a sagging, gelatinous blob with a big drooping nose and a downturned, grumpy mouth. This Pokémon is the evolved form: the fish after it has been brought up and has collapsed.

BODY: the body is shaped like a PUDDING turned out of its mould that is sagging under its own weight: a soft, wobbly mound, wider at the bottom, with a slightly slumped top. The blobfish face sits on the front of this pudding body: the large soft drooping nose hanging over the mouth, the downturned mouth, small dark eyes. Small fins lie limp against the sides near the bottom; a short limp tail at the back.
COLOUR: the real pale pink to greyish pink of a blobfish out of water, slightly glossy-wet, darker pink in the folds and at the bottom.

POSE: a three-quarter view facing LEFT, resting slumped on itself. Compact so everything fits in a square; the body fills about two thirds of the width, centred, sitting low in the frame.

AVOID: a cute plush, chibi or Neopets look, big shiny cartoon eyes, a happy smile, a dish or plate under it, a spoon, whipped cream, a cherry or other dessert decorations, water or bubbles, a normal fish shape with a pointed body, arms or legs, a glossy 3D render.
```

## Tips
- Wordt het een dessert met gezicht in plaats van een vis: nieuw gesprek, bovenaan "it is a blobfish whose collapsed body has the shape of a pudding, not a dessert".
- Niet langer dan 1–2 keer bijsturen in hetzelfde gesprek (GEMINI.md).
