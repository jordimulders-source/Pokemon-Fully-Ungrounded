2. Dier en vuur één geheel.
3. Draait rond op de vuurbuik, niet staand op de poten.

## Prompt

```
The attached image is a draft of my original Pokémon: a Fire/Fairy salamander whose body is a burning ground-spinner firework. Redraw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style). KEEP the blinding white-gold fire in its body, the flat salamander head and face, and the colors. Change only these three things:

1. REMOVE ALL THE SPARK TRAILS ABOVE ITS BACK. No fountain, no streaks, no effects around it at all.
2. MAKE IT ONE WHOLE: the creature and the fire are one body. The blinding fire is its belly and core, and the same fire flows into its head, legs and tail: hot white-gold in the middle, hot pink and magenta toward the edges, so nothing looks pasted on.
3. IT SPINS ON ITS FIERY BELLY, NOT STANDING ON ITS LEGS: the blazing belly is the only part touching the ground, like a spinning top on its point. The four legs are lifted off the ground and stretched out sideways, the tail swings around the body in the direction of the spin, and the body is slightly tilted, as if caught mid-spin.

STYLE AND FORMAT
- Official Gen 3 front sprite, 64x64, max 16 colors, 1 px dark outline, light from top left, flat Gen 3 shading with 3 to 4 tones per color, no dithering. Clear silhouette.
- Three-quarter view facing left. Compact, fits a square.
- Plain white background. NO floor, shadow, smoke, motion lines or spark effects. One single creature.

AVOID
- No sparks or streaks above or around it. No standing on its legs. No orange. No green. Not Charmander. No coiled snake body. No text. No glossy 3D render.
```
