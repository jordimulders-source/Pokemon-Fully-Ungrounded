# Satelliet-lijn fase 1 – startprompt r4 (23 nov), NIEUW gesprek, geen bijlage
= r3 + Jordi's punten (zie SPEC): antennes gelijk en naar achteren, meer contrast, oog zonder glans.

```
Design a new, original Pokémon.
Draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): pixel art, 64×64, max 16 colors, dark selout outline, plain white background.

WHAT IT IS
A small living machine based on Sputnik 1, the first satellite: a polished metal sphere with four long antennas. The Sputnik inspiration must be clearly recognizable.
It is a pure machine with its own personality, the way Klink, Magnemite and Porygon are Pokémon: no animal parts, no arms, no legs.
It has exactly ONE eye: a robotic camera lens in the front of the sphere, made of a few concentric rings (purple rings around a small cyan center). Flat pixel-art colors only: NO glossy reflection, no shine or highlight crescent on the lens.

DETAIL LEVEL
Medium: one seam around the middle of the sphere (like Sputnik's two halves), and a small purple socket where each antenna attaches. Nothing else: no rivets, no panel grid, no spots, no dishes or knobs on the antennas.

ANTENNAS (like the real Sputnik)
Exactly four, all IDENTICAL: same thickness, same color, same socket. All four attach at the BACK of the sphere and sweep straight BACKWARD (to the right), in two pairs, slightly spread like a swept-back tail. None point forward, up or down.
Thick enough to read clearly in the sprite: at least 2–3 pixels wide, tapering slightly toward the tip. Never 1-pixel lines.

COLORS
White and purple, close to Mewtwo's colors: pearly white sphere, purple accents (seam, sockets, eye rings). Smooth, sleek, cyber/high-tech look.
Strong contrast like a Gen 3 sprite: light from the upper left, the shadow side of the sphere and antennas in a clearly DEEPER purple (not just pale lavender), so it does not fade into the white background.

POSE
It floats in the air, clearly turned in three-quarter view: we see the front of the sphere with the eye AND part of its left side. Tilted slightly as if looking up curiously.

VIEW
Three-quarter view facing LEFT, like every Gen 3 front sprite: the eye is on the left half of the sphere, the antennas trail to the right.
It is small: it fills about half of the 64×64 frame.

DO NOT
No effects, no glow, no electricity, no stars or planets around it.
No ground, no shadow.
Not Magnemite, not Voltorb, not a Poké Ball, not a robot with a face screen.
No plush/Neopets look.
```
