# Gemini-prompts: Banette-evolutie, v13 (17 nov)

Twee varianten, elk in een **nieuw gesprek**. Zelfde ontwerptekst (alle besluiten uit SPEC.md, plus "niet gespierd": Jordi "het moet geen muscledude worden").

## A: met vormschets
Bijlage: `bijlage_vormschets.png` (Claude, ruwe collage van r7-hoofd + r8, lijf 1,3× breder; alleen invoer voor Gemini, niet de sprite).

```
Attached is a rough shape sketch of my Pokémon, made by cutting and pasting. Use it for the SHAPE and COMPOSITION: the head shape, the broad body, the raised arms with the crosses, the two hanging Shuppets and the garment. Redraw it cleanly as one coherent creature; the rough edges and mixed styles of the sketch are not part of the design.

DESIGN (follow this where it differs from the attachment)
- The evolution of Banette, Ghost/Dark: an evil ghost puppet master / joker. Big, broad and imposing, but NOT muscular: a heavy, wide body shape, no muscles.
- Banette's zipper mouth was opened and he climbed out. Banette's former empty body hangs around his waist like clothing, the opened zipper around his waist: grey cloth with a patch, Banette's limp little arms and its tail with the yellow tip. Banette's face is not on it. His own smooth ghost torso rises directly out of it.
- His head has two long points like a jester's hat.
- A very sinister zigzag grin with teeth the same colour as his head. No zipper in his mouth.
- Eyes like Banette's, but bigger.
- Both arms held up high. His hands ARE marionette control crosses, made of his smooth dark ghost substance, growing from the arms. Not wood.
- From each cross hang threads, 1 pixel thick; on them hangs one Shuppet each, controlled as a puppet.
- Floating, two short undefined stubby legs. Colours like Banette, a bit darker.

FORMAT
- Official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style), 64x64, max 16 colours, 1 px dark outline, flat shading, no dithering, plain white background, 3/4 view facing left, seen slightly from above. Fills almost the whole 64x64 square. NOT illustration style.
```
