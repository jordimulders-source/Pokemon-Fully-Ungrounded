## Prompt

```
The attached image is Gnometrotter, Cave Form (Fairy/Rock): an old garden-gnome Pokémon whose whole body has turned to stone, built from chunky pieces fitted together, keeping its color pattern (pink nose, pink body and arms, sky-blue legs and feet).

Draw the SAME Pokémon in a different form: Ice Form (Fairy/Ice), after ages walking through glaciers and snow. This is like Wormadam's forms: the body stays exactly the same, only the material changes.

KEEP EXACTLY THE SAME AS THE ATTACHED IMAGE
- The same pose, the same three-quarter view facing left, the same size and position in the frame.
- The same body shape and proportions: the same round body, the same size of the round pink nose, the same arms and round hands, the same legs and feet, the same thick soles, the same belt ring, the same stick in the same hand (the front hand, on the left of the image), the same cap shape and size over the eyes, the same beard shape and size, the same ridge on the back.
- The same color pattern: pink nose, pink body and arms, sky-blue legs and feet.
- The same construction: the body built from chunky pieces with visible seams between them.
- The same Gen 3 sprite style, 64x64, max 16 colors, 1 px dark outline, plain white background.

CHANGE ONLY THE MATERIAL
- The pink and sky-blue stone pieces become pink and sky-blue ICE blocks (same colors, icy, with flat faces).
- The cap becomes solid, pale icy-white ice, with a few icicles hanging from its lower rim at the sides (not in front of the nose).
- The beard and mustache become a heavy cluster of white icicles hanging down, in the same overall beard shape.
- The hair tufts become white frost and snow in hard, spiky shapes.
- The belt ring and back ridge become white snow-crusted ice; the back ridge gets a few sharp ice shards.
- The soles become hard white packed snow.
- The stick becomes one long icicle, in the same place and at the same angle.

AVOID
- Do not change the pose, the size, the proportions or the outline. No visible eyes, no dark band under the cap, no visible mouth. No glow, no effects, no background.
```
