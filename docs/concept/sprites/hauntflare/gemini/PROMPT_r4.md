## Prompt

```
Design a new Pokémon and draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): 64x64 pixel art, at most 16 colours, 1-pixel dark outline around every part, light from the top left, plain white background, no ground, no shadow, no glow, no sparkles, no motion lines, no effects outside the creature.

The Pokémon is "Hauntflare", a Cosmic/Poison type based on a PROTOSTAR: a newborn star with a disc of dust around it that shoots out jets of gas (Herbig-Haro jets). It is NOT a ghost and NOT related to Haunter; it only looks similar by coincidence (convergent evolution, like Wiglett and Diglett).

FACE: the attached sprite is HAUNTER. The eyes and the open grin with teeth must be exactly Haunter's from this attachment (same shape, same size), not Gengar's: Haunter's slanted eyes and Haunter's open mouth with a few teeth, not Gengar's wide closed grin. The face sits on the upper LEFT part of the ball, above the ring, because the Pokémon looks to the left.

BODY: a round ball of hot GAS (the young star): bright pink with a lighter pinkish-white centre and a big soft highlight at the top left. It must read as gas, not as a solid egg or a plastic ball: give it soft, slightly swirling cloud-like shading inside, like glowing gas, with no hard shine.
RING: a dark reddish-brown ring of dust around the ball, tilted, passing behind the ball at the back and in front of the lower half of the ball at the front. The ring does NOT touch the ball: there is a clear gap of white background between the ring and the ball all around, as if the ring floats around it. The ring is a solid band of medium thickness.
JETS: the upper jet comes out of the top-back of the ball as two or three sharp flame-like spikes pointing up and back (to the right), giving the head its pointed shape. The lower jet comes out of the bottom-back as one long, tapering, wavy tail pointing down and back (to the right), ending in a point. Red and rose-pink with orange edges.
HANDS: exactly two hands, shaped like Haunter's hands (three clawed fingers), in the SAME red and rose-pink with orange edges as the jets (not purple, not grey). They float free, with a clear gap of white background between each hand and the body: the near hand at the lower left, bigger and in front; the far hand at the right, smaller and partly behind.

POSE: a true three-quarter view facing LEFT, floating in space. Compact so everything fits in a square; the ball fills about half the width of the image, centred.

AVOID: Gengar's face or Gengar's grin, purple or grey hands, a face low on the ball under the ring, facing right, a ring that touches or is glued to the ball, motion lines or swirls around the ring, a glossy plastic or egg-like ball, a ghost look, a purple colour, legs or feet, arms connected to the body, extra hands, stars or sparkles around it, glow effects, messy smoke strands, a cute plush, chibi or Neopets look, a glossy 3D render.
```
