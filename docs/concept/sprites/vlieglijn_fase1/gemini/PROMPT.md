# Vlieglijn fase 1 (fruitvlieg) – Gemini-startprompt (r1, 20 nov)

Besluiten: lijn fruitvlieg → huisvlieg → mug (Jordi, 20 nov). Alleen de echte fruitvlieg als anatomie; geen eigen kenmerken toegevoegd. Geen bijlagen.

```
Design a new Pokémon: the first stage of a three-stage line (fruit fly → house fly → mosquito).
Draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): pixel art, 64×64, max 16 colors, dark selout outline, plain white background.

WHAT IT IS
A fruit fly (Drosophila melanogaster). Anatomy of the real insect: small rounded body; large bright red compound eyes; tan/yellow-brown thorax; abdomen with dark bands; two clear, rounded wings (2 wings in total, near and far); six thin legs; short antennae with a feathery bristle (arista).
It is a small, early Pokémon: the base stage of its line.

VIEW
Three-quarter view facing left, like every Gen 3 front sprite: the far wing and far legs on the left (narrower, partly hidden), the near ones on the right.
Compact: the whole Pokémon fits inside a square, limbs close to the body.

DO NOT
No glow, no electricity, no effects, no motion lines.
No fruit, no ground, no shadow under it.
No clothing or accessories. No plush/Neopets look, no chibi mascot. Do not make it look like an existing Pokémon (not Yanma, not Venonat, not Ninjask, not Combee).
```
