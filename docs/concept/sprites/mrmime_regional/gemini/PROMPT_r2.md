# Regionale Mr. Mime (jiujitsu) – Gemini-startprompt r2 (22 nov, NIEUW gesprek, geen bijlage)

```
Design a brand-new regional form of Mr. Mime: a Brazilian jiu-jitsu fighter. Type Fighting/Psychic.
Draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): pixel art, 64×64, max 16 colors, dark selout outline, plain white background.

HOW DIFFERENT
This regional form is a really different design from Kanto Mr. Mime, just as Galarian Mr. Mime is a completely new design with its own body shape and colors (but do NOT copy Galarian Mr. Mime: no tap shoes, no ice, no cane).
Only the mime-like face with red cheeks tells you it is related to Mr. Mime.
NO pink balls anywhere: not on the head, shoulders, hands, knees or anywhere else.

WHAT IT IS
A jiu-jitsu fighter in a BLUE jiu-jitsu gi with a belt. The gi is part of its body design, not a costume on a clown.
It is a Pokémon, not a human in a gi.

POSE
Lying with its BACK flat on the ground (not on its side), legs up in an open guard, inviting the opponent in ("get into my guard").

VIEW
Three-quarter view facing left, like every Gen 3 front sprite.
Compact: the whole Pokémon fits inside a square, not a long horizontal figure.

DO NOT
No ground, no mat, no shadow under it. No glow, no aura, no effects, no motion lines.
No opponent, nothing else in the image.
No plush/Neopets look, no chibi mascot, not a realistic human.
```
