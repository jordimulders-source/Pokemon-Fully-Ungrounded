# Guaraná fase 1 – startprompt r1 (25 nov), NIEUW gesprek, geen bijlage
Basis: SPEC.md. Eigen toevoegingen van Claude (schrappen bij twijfel): bladeren aan de rank, "half a dozen" vruchten, twee tot drie barstjes.

```
Design a new, original Pokémon.
Draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): pixel art, 64×64, max 16 colors, dark selout outline, plain white background.

WHAT IT IS
A Grass/Ghost Pokémon based on the guaraná plant from the Amazon: a climbing vine with clusters of small round red fruit capsules.
Its body IS a creeping vine: a short, thick vine stem with curling tendrils that it uses as limbs to crawl and grip, and a few leaves on it.
Its head IS a closed cluster of about half a dozen red guaraná fruits growing at the front end of the vine.
The fruits are still closed, but two or three of them have a small crack, and through each crack a white eye with a black pupil peeks out and stares at you.
Mood: unsettling. The eyes stare and follow you, but it is still a Pokémon you would want on your team. Not gory, not a horror monster.
It is the first stage of an evolution line: in the next stage the fruits burst fully open and are covered in staring eyes. Keep this one small and simple.

POSE
It crawls low over the ground on its tendrils, the fruit cluster raised slightly and turned toward the viewer, as if it just noticed you.

VIEW
Three-quarter view facing left, like every Gen 3 front sprite.
It is small: it fills about half of the 64×64 frame. Compact, tendrils close to the body.

DO NOT
No glow, no effects, no ghostly aura, no floating particles.
No ground, no shadow, no pot.
No mouth full of teeth, no blood.
Not a human or child figure, not a snake, not a grape bunch with a cartoon face.
No plush/Neopets look.
```
