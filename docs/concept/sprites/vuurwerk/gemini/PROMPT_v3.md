## Prompt

```
Design an original Pokémon and draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style).

CONCEPT
- Fire/Fairy type. A PORCUPINE WHOSE SHAPE IS MADE OF FIRE AND FIREWORKS. The animal's silhouette is drawn by flames and firework light: you recognize a porcupine (rounded body, short snout, small feet), but it is formed by fire and fireworks, not by fur or skin.
- The quills are only a SUGGESTION: flame tongues and trails of sparks sweeping back over the head and back. No literal sticks or straight lines.
- ITS HEAD IS A FIREWORK EXPLOSION: one clear, star-shaped burst, with a small readable face (eyes and snout) in the center.
- Colors: YELLOW, RED AND PINK, ordered like a real firework: bright yellow-white near the face and core, red further out, pink at the tips.
- Exactly TWO FRONT LEGS; from the elbow down each front leg IS a firework rocket (casing in red and pink with yellow, pointed tip forward like a fist, a small lit fuse at the back). Its arms are rockets; it does not hold them.
- It chases away evil spirits: a bold, defiant, protective stance.

STYLE AND FORMAT
- Official Gen 3 front sprite, 64x64, max 16 colors, 1 px dark outline, light from top left, flat Gen 3 shading with 3 to 4 tones per color, no dithering.
- Three-quarter view facing left. Compact composition that fits a square; limbs close to the body.
- Plain white background. NO floor, shadow, smoke or loose sparks outside the creature. One single creature.

AVOID
- Not a rainbow, no green or blue. Not a broom of straight colored lines. Not a humanoid, no hat. Not a dragon. Not Sonic. No text. No glossy 3D render.
```

## Tips
- Te veel vuur, geen dier: "the porcupine shape must be readable at a glance".
- Te veel dier: "the body itself is flame, not fur".
