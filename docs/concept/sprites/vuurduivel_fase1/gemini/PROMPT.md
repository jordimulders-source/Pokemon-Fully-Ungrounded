# Gemini-prompt: vuurduivel fase 1, v1 (22 nov)

Nieuw gesprek, geen bijlage.

## Jordi
- "een soort wezentje als het duiveltje van ashley in wario ware, niet precies hetzelfde design, maar zelfde idee. Het is een duiveltje die ongein veroorzaakt en dat lachen vindt."
- Basis sater ("daar was ik een beetje naar op zoek"); Fire/Dark; 2 fases, eindvorm "de wat volgroeidere bokkenduivel look".
- Keuzes: vuur als kuif tussen de hoorns + staartpunt (+ "iets anders", nog niet gespecificeerd); donker + fel vuur; "2 poten maar niet per se mollig"; brede grijns; "hij mag er best cartoony uit zien", "maar wel pokemon cartoony he".

## Prompt

```
Design an original Pokémon and draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style).

CONCEPT
- Fire/Dark type, the small first stage of a two-stage evolution line.
- A little devil imp based on a young SATYR: a baby goat creature that stands upright on two goat legs with small cloven hooves. Not chubby; light and springy, ready to run off after a prank.
- It loves causing mischief and finds it hilarious: a BIG, WIDE, MISCHIEVOUS GRIN.
- Goat features: small curved goat horns, goat ears, goat muzzle.
- FIRE: a small flame tuft of hair between the horns, and a thin devil tail ending in a small flame. No other fire.
- COLORS: dark body (black and dark brown) with bright, vivid orange-yellow fire. The fire is the brightest thing in the sprite.
- Cartoony, but in the Pokémon way: simple bold shapes, a clear readable silhouette, expressive face, like an official Ken Sugimori design.

STYLE AND FORMAT
- Official Gen 3 front sprite, 64x64, max 16 colors, 1 px dark outline, light from top left, flat Gen 3 shading with 3 to 4 tones per color, no dithering.
- Three-quarter view facing left. The creature is small, well under 64 px tall.
- Plain white background. NO floor, shadow, smoke or effects. One single creature.

AVOID
- Not Impidimp, Grimmsnarl, Houndour or Skiddo. No pitchfork, no props, no clothing, no wings. No red body. No pentagram or symbols. No text. No western-cartoon or plush look. No glossy 3D render.
```
