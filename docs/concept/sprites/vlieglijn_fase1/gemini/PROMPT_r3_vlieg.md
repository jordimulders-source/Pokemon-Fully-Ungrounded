# Vlieglijn fase 1 (vlieg) – r3, nieuw gesprek zonder bijlage (20 nov)

Besluiten (Jordi, 20 nov): 2 fases vlieg → mug, reguliere vlieg "met wat meer zwart"; poten als stroomkabels; ogen met LED-lampjes, de brandende vormen een blije expressie (^ ^), lampjes paars; lijf amber + zwart; vleugels plat naar achteren.

```
Design a new Pokémon: the first stage of a two-stage line (fly → mosquito).
Draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): pixel art, 64×64, max 16 colors, dark selout outline, plain white background.

WHAT IT IS
A common fly (house fly, Musca domestica), standing on its legs like the real insect. Anatomy of the real insect: stocky bristly body; very large compound eyes that cover most of the head; thorax; rounded abdomen; two clear wings (2 wings in total, near and far); six legs; short antennae.
Body colors: amber and black.
Wings: lying flat and folded backwards over its back, like a resting fly, reaching past the end of the abdomen. They do NOT stand up.
Legs: its legs give off electric shocks, so its six legs look a bit more like electric power cables.
Eyes: the eyes are like an LED screen: dark eyes made of a grid of a few large LED lights. Some LEDs are lit bright purple, the rest are off (dark). The lit LEDs form a HAPPY expression, like happy robot eyes on a screen (^ ^).
It is a small, early Pokémon: the base stage of its line.

VIEW
Three-quarter view facing left, like every Gen 3 front sprite: the far wing and far legs on the left (narrower, partly hidden), the near ones on the right.
Compact: the whole Pokémon fits inside a square, limbs close to the body.

DO NOT
No sparks, no glow, no electricity effects, no motion lines.
No ground, no shadow under it.
No clothing or accessories. No plush/Neopets look, no chibi mascot, not a robot.
Do not make it look like Beedrill or any bee or wasp: no yellow-and-black stripes, no stinger, no upright wings. Also not Yanma, Venonat, Ninjask or Combee.
```
