Asset voor asset (zoals vuurwerk fase 1): eerst het ontwerp in een eenvoudige staande pose; stap B = in hetzelfde gesprek alleen de pose bijsturen (geen bijlagen, Jordi).

```
Design a brand-new regional form of Mr. Mime: a Brazilian jiu-jitsu fighter. Type Fighting/Psychic.
Draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): pixel art, 64×64, max 16 colors, dark selout outline, plain white background.
Draw ONE single Pokémon.

THE BODY IS THE GI
Just like Kanto Mr. Mime's clown outfit and Mr. Rime's tuxedo are really their own body, this Pokémon does NOT wear clothes: its round torso IS a jiu-jitsu gi.
- The torso is blue skin shaped like a gi jacket: the two lapels are folds of its own skin crossing over the chest.
- A black band around its waist is a natural body marking (like a black belt), not a piece of cloth.
- No sleeves, no trousers, no fabric: arms and legs are bare Pokémon limbs.
- It is a Pokémon with Mr. Mime's proportions, not a human. NO pink balls anywhere.

COLORS
- Skin of head, arms and legs: the normal Mr. Mime skin tone (pale pinkish).
- Fingertips and feet: light blue.
- Cheeks: yellow.
- Two jagged purple spikes ("ears") on the sides of the head, like a jester's hair.
- Torso: blue, with the black waist band.

POSE
Standing, in a simple calm fighting stance. (The pose will be changed later; now only the design matters.)

VIEW
Three-quarter view facing left, like every Gen 3 front sprite. Compact, fits inside a square.

DO NOT
No clothes, no fabric folds, no sleeves, no trousers.
No ground, no shadow, no glow, no effects. Only one Pokémon, nothing else in the image.
No plush/Neopets look, no chibi mascot.
```
