# Gemini-prompt: Banette-evolutie, v2 (17 nov)

**Nieuw gesprek** (4 wijzigingen tegelijk: met r1 als bijlage negeert Gemini die, GEMINI.md). Bijlagen: `bijlage_banette.png` en `bijlage_shuppet.png`, níet r1.
Wijzigingen ten opzichte van v1 (Jordi over r1): wat donkerder, groter/imposanter, jas meer Banettes voormalige lichaam, nieuw lijf + hoofd groter, twee hoofdaanhangsels (puppetmaster/joker), richting 64×64 vullen.
Vertaling "hoofdaanhangsels" door Claude: "like the single pointed spike on top of Banette's head". Klopt dat niet, dan aanpassen.

```
Design an original Pokémon: the evolution of BANETTE (Ghost/Dark). Attached: the Gen 3 sprite of BANETTE (its pre-evolution) and the Gen 3 sprite of SHUPPET (Banette's own pre-evolution).

CONCEPT
- An evil ghost puppet master, big and imposing. The zipper mouth of Banette has been opened and this new creature has climbed out of it.
- He is much bigger than Banette: his new body and his new head are clearly larger than Banette's old body.
- Banette's former body now hangs around his waist like a garment, with the open zipper around his waist. The garment must clearly read as Banette's old body (its shape, its little arms, its tail with the yellow tip), worn like an empty costume. Banette's eyes and face are NOT on it.

HEAD
- Two head appendages, like the pointed spike on top of Banette's head, so he looks even more like an evil puppet master / joker.
- A very sinister grin. The mouth is NOT a zipper: a zigzag mouth with teeth, the teeth the same colour as his head.
- Eyes similar to Banette's eyes, but larger.

BODY
- Floating. Two short stubby legs, not defined, like Banette's.
- His hands are two marionette control crosses (the crossbars a puppeteer holds), NOT made of wood: they are made of the same dark ghostly substance as his body.
- He holds both arms up high.

PUPPETS
- From his hand-crosses hang threads, each thread only 1 pixel thick.
- On the threads hang two mini SHUPPETs (small versions of the attached Shuppet), which he controls as puppets.

COLOURS
- Like Banette, but a bit darker.

STYLE AND FORMAT
- Official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style), 64x64, max 16 colours, 1 px dark outline, light from top left, flat Gen 3 shading with 3 to 4 tones per colour, no dithering.
- 3/4 view facing left, with a perspective as if seen slightly from above.
- Large: the figure, including the two Shuppets, fills almost the whole 64x64 square.
- Plain white background. NO floor, shadow, glow, aura or effects.

AVOID
- No zipper mouth. No wooden crosses. Banette's face must not appear on the garment. Not a small Banette with a costume: he is a bigger, new creature. Not a plush toy, no glossy 3D render.
```
