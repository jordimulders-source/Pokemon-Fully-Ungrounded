Nieuw gesprek, bijlage: Shuppeteer (Jordi). Kledingstuk/rits nu weggelaten (lijf grotendeels geschrapt); kleuren niet genoemd.

```
Design an original Pokémon, Ghost/Dark: an evil puppet master / jester. DO NOT DRAW BANETTE.

COMPOSITION
- The body is almost entirely left out: we see only a HUGE head and two arms, with at most a small remnant of body under the head.
- The head is enormous: about two thirds of the width of the sprite and most of its height. It overlaps the arms and threads, which is fine.
- The arms come out of the left and right sides of the head, at the height of the mouth, and rise up and outward to the crosses.

HEAD
- Use the attached fan design as the reference for the head: the jester's cap shape with its points as part of the head, the eyes and the overall look.
- The mouth is NOT a zipper: a mouth in the style of Spiritomb's mouth, a wide, crooked, jagged zigzag grin. Take only the mouth style from Spiritomb.

ARMS AND HANDS
- Each arm ends in a marionette control cross instead of a hand: two straight bars crossing at right angles like a + sign, held horizontally, grown from the arm. Not wood, not held.

PUPPETS
- From the ends of each cross hang threads, 1 pixel thick.
- On the threads hangs one Shuppet under each hand, controlled as a puppet, big enough to read clearly. Not hand puppets.

STYLE AND FORMAT
- Official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style), 64x64, max 16 colours, 1 px dark outline, flat shading, no dithering, plain white background, 3/4 view facing left, seen slightly from above. Fills almost the whole 64x64 square.
```
