# Gemini-prompt Pachamama r8 (nieuw gesprek, GEEN bijlage, from scratch)
Jordi (27 nov): "nee je kan het gezicht niet veranderen en dan de rest niks"; "probeer nog een prompt, maar prompt wat slimmer, ik heb ook niet meer tijd om nog meer te doen"; "gewoon from scratch".
Alle bevroren besluiten uit r1–r4c erin; nieuw: verhoudingen in concrete maten (groot gezicht) en de gezichtslijnen die Jordi belangrijk vond.

```
Design a new legendary Pokémon as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): pixel art, 64x64, max 16 colors, dark pixel outline (no smooth vector lines), light from top-left, plain white background, no ground, no shadow, no effects.

WHAT IT IS: Mother Earth, Ground/Fairy type: a living mountain shaped like a mother wrapped in a wide mantle, with a snow hood. Sacred, calm, like a carved stone idol that is alive.

PROPORTIONS (most important, the face must be readable at 64x64): a BIG head. The face is about one third of the total width of the Pokémon, and the head with the hood is about 45% of the total height. The mantle below is compact and wide, like a short mountain.

HEAD: a hood of snow: white snow on top, cream-colored sides, draping naturally onto the shoulders. Inside the hood a grey-brown stone face made of a few big shapes and bold dark lines: two large eyes (white with a brown iris) in clearly outlined eye sockets, a straight nose between them with a dark line at the bottom, a mouth shaped like an upside-down U under the nose, and one line on each cheek running down to the chin. No other lines or cracks in the face.

BODY: the mantle is a mountain of rock painted in broad horizontal bands, from top to bottom: red, pink, ochre yellow, turquoise (the colors of Rainbow Mountain, Vinicunca, Peru). Two rocky mountain ridges come forward from the mantle as arms, held out like a mother, with a little snow on their tips. A few small tufts of plants on the lower slopes.

POSE: 3/4 view facing left.

DO NOT: small face, big scarf or collar, realistic human, beard texture, many cracks, thin stripes, hands, fingers, villages, animals, offerings, gems, glowing, background.
```
