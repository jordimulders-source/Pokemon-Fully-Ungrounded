# Hoatzin fase 2: Gemini-prompts v1 (19 nov)

Besluit Jordi (19 nov): "2 fases, poison/dark. Fase 1 doen we een kuiken en gaan we iets meer overdrijven met die klauwen en die smerige look. fase 2 wordt een volgroeide hoatzin die er eng en geniepig uit ziet". Vraag: hoe de nadruk leggen op poison/stank door zelf te fermenteren. Twee concepten om visueel te kunnen reageren. Bijlage: een foto van een volwassen hoatzin.

## Concept A: de krop als gistzak
```
Design an original Pokémon based on the real HOATZIN bird (photo attached; follow its real anatomy: small head, spiky ragged crest, bare blue face skin, red eyes, long neck, heavy body, long broad tail, chestnut wings).
Type: Poison/Dark. Fully grown, creepy and sneaky.
Core idea: it poisons by fermenting its own food. Its crop (the throat/chest pouch where real hoatzins ferment leaves) is hugely swollen into a taut, slightly translucent sac on its chest; you can see chewed leaves and bubbles of gas inside. A half-chewed leaf hangs from its beak.
Pose: perched hunched forward, head low and pushed out, staring sideways with a sly, scheming look, like it is waiting for you to come closer. Claws grip an implied perch but draw NO branch.
Style: official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style), 64x64, max 16 colors, 3/4 view facing left, compact composition with wings and tail close to the body, clean dark outline, white background.
No effects: no gas clouds, no glow, no stink lines, no ground, no shadow, no branch.
AVOID: cute plush/Neopets look, turkey, chicken, Archeops, Honchkrow, Murkrow, Toucannon.
```

## Concept B: de krop als gistkruik (chicha)
```
Design an original Pokémon based on the real HOATZIN bird (photo attached; follow its real anatomy: small head, spiky ragged crest, bare blue face skin, red eyes, long neck, heavy body, long broad tail, chestnut wings).
Type: Poison/Dark. Fully grown, creepy and sneaky.
Core idea: it poisons by fermenting its own food. Its bulging crop is shaped like a round clay fermenting jug (as used for chicha), with ring-like bands of tough skin around it and a vent-like opening at the top where the stench escapes. Sticky, stained feathers below the beak and around the crop. A large bare, rough callus patch on the breast where it rests on branches while digesting.
Pose: low, crouched and still, body leaning on the breast callus, wings half spread like a cloak, red eyes narrowed, beak slightly open in a smug, menacing grin.
Style: official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style), 64x64, max 16 colors, 3/4 view facing left, compact composition with wings and tail close to the body, clean dark outline, white background.
No effects: no gas clouds, no glow, no stink lines, no ground, no shadow, no branch.
AVOID: cute plush/Neopets look, turkey, chicken, Archeops, Honchkrow, Murkrow, Toucannon, pottery or a jar the bird is holding (the jug shape IS its own body).
```
