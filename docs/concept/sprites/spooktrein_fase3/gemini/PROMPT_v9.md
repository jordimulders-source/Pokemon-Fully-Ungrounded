Claude: neus weg (kwam uit "bullet-like nose" in v8), ladder weg. Spiritomb niet bij naam (Gemini kopieert genoemde gezichten); de zigzag-grijns beschreven. Nieuw gesprek, geen bijlage (zelfde opbouw als v8).

```
Design an original Pokémon in the official Ken Sugimori Pokémon artwork style (clean lines, flat cel shading, plain white background).

TYPE: Steel / Ghost. Final stage of its line.

CONCEPT: a piece of rusty railway scrap that would miserably fall apart without the ghost inside it. A whole army of spirits holds it together and has turned it into a furiously fast vehicle, and they are having an enormous amount of fun, a bit like Rotom. It is ONE creature, not a train with a ghost added.

HELD TOGETHER BY THE GHOST
- The body is made of loose, battered pieces of rusty iron (bent plates, a dented chimney, a broken cowcatcher) that do not really fit together.
- Green ghost energy fills the cracks and gaps between the pieces and holds them in place, like glue made of ghost.
- Many pieces of ghost come out of it: green ghostly arms reaching out of the gaps and gripping the iron, and an extra small ghost face peeking out of a crack, as if a whole army of spirits is working on this train. They are all part of the same green ghost mass.

SHAPE (follow this closely)
- Overall: a teardrop or tadpole shape lying on its side, thick and round at the front, narrowing towards the back. Long and low, fitting a square. The whole body tilts forward and down, as if diving ahead at furious speed.
- Head (front half): one big rounded iron block. NO nose of any kind. No flat round door, no plate with a face on it.
- Eyes: two identical, big, crooked green eyes high on the front, glowing out of cracks in the iron, mischievous.
- Mouth: one huge, wide, crooked zigzag grin torn across the front of the head, jagged like a crack, glowing green inside. The cowcatcher sits under it as a lower jaw.
- Chimney: short, wide and dented, bent backwards as if by the wind, with a wisp of green ghost smoke.
- Body (back half): the iron narrows and ends in a ragged, broken edge. Out of the open back, the ghost streams out as a green smoke tail.
- Wheels: no iron wheels. Many wheels made of green ghost energy underneath, at least four, all spinning furiously in motion like in a comic book, and more faint ghostly wheels in the smoke tail.

COLORS: rusty iron (rust brown with a bit of dark grey); everything of the ghost is green.

EXPRESSION: cheeky and mischievous, clearly enjoying the speed. Not angry, not scary.

VIEW: slightly more frontal than three-quarter, facing left.

AVOID: not a realistic or complete locomotive, no cab, no windows, no rods, no pipes, no wagons, no headlight, no ladder, no human face (no nose, no eyebrows, no lips), not Thomas the Tank Engine, no skull, no conductor, no separate ghost characters, no rails, no speed lines.
```
