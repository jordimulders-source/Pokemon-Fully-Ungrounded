# Gemini-prompt: spooktrein fase 3 (Steel/Ghost), v6 (15 nov)

Jordi over v5: "het moet gewoon nog minder een trein zijn". Eerder: "nee dit is een trein, geen dier".
Twee richtingen (Claude), elk een nieuw gesprek, geen bijlage. Daarna stap 2 (sprite) zoals in `PROMPT_v5.md`.

## Variant A: de geest is het lijf, de trein zijn wat hij draagt

```
Design an original Pokémon in the official Ken Sugimori Pokémon artwork style (clean lines, flat cel shading, plain white background).

TYPE: Steel / Ghost. Final stage of its line.

CONCEPT: a green ghost Pokémon that has possessed a steam train. The ghost is the body; only a few train pieces are part of it.
- The body is a green, smoky ghost shape, long and low, leaning forward as if racing ahead. Its tail end dissolves into ghost smoke.
- Train pieces it has taken over and wears: the front of a steam locomotive as its head or mask, with a short chimney on top, a cowcatcher at the front like a jaw, and two spinning train wheels underneath (drawn in motion like in a comic book). Faint ghostly wheels in the smoke behind.
- That is all of the train: no boiler body, no cab, no windows, no rods, no wagons.
- The iron pieces are dark grey old iron with rust patches and rust streaks.
- Two identical ghost eyes, not human: no nose, no eyebrows, no lips. The headlight is not an eye.
- Expression: cheeky and mischievous, visibly having fun being a ghost and going so fast.
- View: slightly more frontal than three-quarter, facing left.

AVOID: no human face, not Thomas the Tank Engine, no realistic locomotive, no skull or horror mask, no conductor, no small ghosts, no speed lines or rails.
```
