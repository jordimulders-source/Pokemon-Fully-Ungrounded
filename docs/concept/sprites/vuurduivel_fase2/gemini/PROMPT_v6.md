## Prompt

```
Design an original Pokémon and draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style).

CONCEPT
- Fire/Dark type, the final (second) stage of a two-stage evolution line.
- The first stage is a small, cute, laughing satyr devil imp: black body, hair made of flames (dark red at the roots, orange, yellow at the tips), two small red devil horns, big pointed ears, shaggy brown goat legs with cloven hooves, a thin black tail with a fire-colored tip.
- This evolution is the grown-up version: a mature GOAT DEVIL. Bigger and stronger, with powerful shaggy goat legs with cloven hooves. The goat side is now much stronger: a goat-like devil head.
- HORNS: two thick, ridged, COILED goat horns, rolled up like a big ram's horns. Both horns are the same: each one starts at the top of the head, sweeps back, then curls down and forward around the ear into a full round coil tight against the side of the head. Not straight, not pointing up, not horizontal, not spread wide. Plain horns with no fire or flame pattern. They are the most striking part of the silhouette.
- POSE: a Baphomet-like seated pose. It sits upright with its shaggy goat legs crossed in front of it, hooves visible. Exactly two arms. ARMS: no pointing gestures (not one hand up and one hand down); instead one or both hands are held open, palm up, with a small flame burning in the palm. Upright, calm and confident, like an idol. NOT a sideways pose: the body faces the viewer like the classic Baphomet image, only turned slightly to the left.
- It keeps the same family features: black body, the flame hair (now a bigger mane of fire), the shaggy brown goat fur on the legs, the tail with the fire-colored tip.
- A serious expression, no grin. Imposing, but still a Pokémon, not a horror monster.
- Not cartoony: only the first stage was cartoony; this evolution is a more serious design. Clear readable silhouette, like an official Ken Sugimori design.

STYLE AND FORMAT
- It must look like a real in-game sprite from Pokémon Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald, NOT a pixel-art illustration. Draw it at true sprite resolution: the whole creature fits inside 64x64 pixels, with big visible pixels when shown enlarged.
- Sprite simplicity: simple bold shapes, large flat color areas, only the details that still read at 64x64 (no fine fur strands, no thin flame wisps, no tiny facial details). Max 16 colors, 1 px dark outline around the whole creature, light from top left, flat Gen 3 shading with 3 to 4 tones per color, no dithering, no anti-aliasing.
- Compact Gen 3 composition: limbs, horns and tail kept close to the body so it fits a square frame.
- Mostly front-facing, turned only slightly to the left (not a side view). A large final-stage Pokémon that fills most of the 64x64 frame.
- Plain white background. NO floor, rock, throne, shadow, smoke or effects (the flames in the palm, the flame hair and the tail flame are the only fire). One single creature.

AVOID
- No pentagram, no symbols, no text, no occult signs, no torch between the horns. No clothing, no props, no pitchfork, no wings. Not Houndoom, Grimmsnarl, Infernape or Krampus. No muscles or abs. No red body. No cartoony, western-cartoon or plush look. No grin. No glossy 3D render. No high-resolution pixel-art illustration.
```
