```
Design a regional form of Kadabra and draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style pixel art).

Attachment 1 is the original Kadabra: use it for the body and anatomy. This must clearly be KADABRA (its head shape, big ears, short mustache, long tail, body proportions), not Alakazam.
Attachment 2 is its evolution, the regional form of Alakazam, a sneaky card-swindler illusionist (Dark/Psychic). Use attachment 2 ONLY for the colors and markings, translated onto Kadabra's body: grey body with a light violet sheen in the shadows, black shoulder plates, black back and sides, grey upper arms and black forearms, a white chest plate, a small red dewlap under the chin, playing cards with black spades. Do not copy its pose or its shapes.

CONCEPT
- The pre-evolution: a charlatan's apprentice. A card swindler too, but not yet very experienced; still learning the trade.
- A red heart mark on its forehead, in the place of the star of the original Kadabra.
- It holds exactly ONE playing card.

POSE (most important)
- An "is this your card?" pose: it holds the one card up in front of it with the card face turned straight toward the viewer, clearly visible and frontal, as if showing it to the audience.
- Its body faces the viewer more than in a side view, and its head and eyes look a bit toward the LEFT.
- A different pose than its evolution: not turned sideways, no hand behind its back.

STYLE AND FORMAT
- 64x64 pixel sprite, max 16 colors, 1 px dark outline, light from top left, clean shading like official Game Freak Gen 3 sprites.
- Plain white background. NO floor, rock, shadow, base, motion lines, glow, sparkles or effects. One single creature, no extra views.

AVOID
- Not Alakazam, not a copy of attachment 2.
- No clothing: no jacket, cape, sleeves, top hat or gloves; no bow tie as a separate object.
- Not a human magician. No plush toy, chibi or mascot look.
- Exactly one card, nothing else in its hands.
```
