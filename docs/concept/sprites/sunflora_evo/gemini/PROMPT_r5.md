# r5: nieuwe startprompt, nieuw gesprek, geen bijlage.

Design a brand-new Pokémon, drawn as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style). Grass/Cosmic type. It is the evolution of Sunflora.

What it is: a sunflower Pokémon that went so hard on photosynthesis that it gained cosmic powers. It is truly halfway between a plant and a star. It floats in the air instead of standing.

HEAD (the big change): the flower has become a star that is truly a cosmic entity. A star with many points (NOT five points), with flame-like edges around it. The head is BIG and exaggerated: at least as big as the whole body below it. Colors of the head: golden yellow, orange and dark navy blue, with few shades: big clear color areas like a real Gen 3 sprite.

FACE (in the middle of the star): a content, very, very happy face: eyes closed in happy upward curves, wide open smile.

BODY: the body of Sunflora: two big leaf arms spread out to the sides, a small green leaf-shaped torso, and two leaf feet. Only the body is like Sunflora, NOT its head. Because it floats, show how the leaf feet hang or rest in the air.

Sprite rules:
- 64x64 pixels, max 16 colors, 1 px dark outline in Gen 3 style, soft Gen 3 shading.
- 3/4 view facing left, compact so it fills a square.
- Plain white background; no ground, no shadow, no rock.
- No glow, no sparkles, no light rays, no loose effects.
- No starfield or galaxy pattern on the green body.
AVOID: plush/toy look, Neopets style, chibi mascot, realistic sunflower painting, a human in a costume, a five-pointed star.
