```
Design a Pokémon: an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style), 64×64 pixels, max 16 colors, clean dark outline, plain white background, no ground, no shadow, no effects or glow.

WHAT IT IS: a living satellite. The satellite itself is the creature's body, not a costume or a machine with a face stuck on. Its one eye is a camera lens.

POSE AND ANGLE (most important): Gen 3 front-sprite 3/4 view, facing the viewer's LEFT. The body is a capsule whose long axis runs diagonally: the FRONT end with the lens-eye is at the lower left and points toward the viewer and slightly left; the BACK end is at the upper right, further away. We see the lens almost from the front and the right side of the capsule. Everything (body and both panels) is drawn in this one perspective.

PROPORTIONS (important): the body is BIG: it takes up about 40% of the total width of the creature and is the clear center of the image. Each solar panel is about 1.3 times as tall as the body. The whole creature fits in a square.

BODY: a white capsule, slightly bullet/acorn-shaped: rounded and a bit narrower at the back, wider at the front, with soft lavender shading on the underside. The front ends in a big white dome. In the middle of the dome is the eye: a camera lens with, from outside to inside, a thin dark line, a white ring, a purple ring, a dark ring, a turquoise iris, a dark pupil and one small white shine. Around the dome runs one thin dark line and one lavender line, like the rim of a lens housing.
JOINTS: round purple ball joints: two on top of the body (each is the base of an antenna), one at the upper back, one at each side where a panel is attached.
ANTENNAE: exactly two long, thin purple antennae with a round purple ball on each tip, rising from the two top joints and curving up and backward like insect feelers. No third antenna.

SOLAR PANELS: exactly two flat rectangular panels, dark navy-purple with a lighter purple grid (2 columns × 4 rows of cells), a dark outline and a faint lighter shine band. Each panel is straight and flat (not bent), attached with a short purple rod to a ball joint at the MIDDLE HEIGHT of the body, one on each side, sticking out sideways. Both panels lie in the same flat plane and have the same tilt, as on a real satellite. Because of the 3/4 view: the near panel (right, at the back half of the body) looks larger; the far panel (left) is foreshortened and partly hidden behind the body.

COLORS: white body with lavender shading, purple joints, antennae and rods, dark navy-purple panels with lighter purple grid lines, turquoise iris as the only accent.

DO NOT: facing right, a side view, a flat front view, panels in different perspectives or tilts, bent or curved panels, small panels, panels attached low on the body, a small body, more than two antennae, a mouth, arms or legs, starfield or galaxy patterns, realistic NASA hardware, cute plush/Neopets style.
```
