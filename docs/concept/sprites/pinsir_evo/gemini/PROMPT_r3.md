# Pinsir-evolutie – Gemini-startprompt r3 (22 nov, nieuw gesprek, geen bijlage)
Basis = prompt r1 (concept "prima"), alleen Jordi's punten erbij; Pinsir-identiteit teruggebracht tot mond, scharen en stekels (r2 "teveel pinsir").

```
Design a brand-new Pokémon: the evolution of Pinsir. Type Bug/Ground. It is a new creature that evolved from Pinsir; it must NOT look like a recolored or bigger Pinsir.
Draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): pixel art, 64×64, max 16 colors, dark selout outline, plain white background.

WHAT IT IS
A huge beetle-kaiju hybrid, based on the SABERTOOTH LONGHORN BEETLE (Macrodontia cervicornis) from the Amazon. Its body shape, head shape and markings come from the sabertooth longhorn and the kaiju, not from Pinsir.
Only two things are inherited from Pinsir: its mouth (Pinsir's vertical mouth with rows of teeth on both sides, but bigger; this is the only mouth, no snout, nose, grin or second mouth) and its spiked pincers, which have become the sabertooth longhorn's enormous, long, curved, saw-toothed mandibles.
From the sabertooth longhorn: a broad flat head shield (pronotum) with spikes on its edges, long wing cases with a bold bark-like pattern. NO antennae.

COLOR
Not monotone and not Pinsir's brown: the colors of a real sabertooth longhorn, with clear contrast between the parts (dark body, wing cases with a bold mottled bark pattern in several browns and tans, reddish mandibles with dark tips).

KAIJU
It stands UPRIGHT ON TWO heavy, thick legs like a classic movie kaiju, with a thick heavy tail, rows of big sharp spikes running down its back and tail, a hunched, massive, towering posture.
VERY SPIKY: much spikier than Pinsir, every spike big enough to read at 64×64 (no tiny 1-pixel spikes).
Head and body big in the frame.

VIEW
Three-quarter view facing left, like every Gen 3 front sprite: the far limbs on the left (partly hidden), the near ones on the right.
Compact: the whole Pokémon fits inside a square; tail and limbs close to the body.

DO NOT
Not Pinsir with a tail, not a dinosaur with a beetle head, not a realistic beetle on its hind legs: one creature that is both beetle and kaiju.
No separate armour plates or robot parts. No ground, no rock, no shadow under it. No glow, no aura, no breath attack, no effects, no motion lines.
No plush/Neopets look, no chibi mascot, not a realistic painting.
```
