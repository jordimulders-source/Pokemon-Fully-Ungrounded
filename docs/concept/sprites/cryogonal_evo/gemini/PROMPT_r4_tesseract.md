# Gemini-prompt r4: tesseract van Cryogonals stukken (25 nov)

r3: Jordi "neeeeee dat bedoelde ik niet, de kubus is opgebouwd uit de stukjes die cryogonal nu de huidige vorm geven, dit is gewoon een kubus met een cryogonal erin". r4: geen Cryogonal in het midden; de ribben zijn Cryogonals eigen stukken (brede platte kristalplaten met een gevorkte V-punt, zie `graphics/pokemon/cryogonal/anim_front.png`), het facetkristal met het gezicht is zelf een stuk van de constructie. Kleuren naar de echte sprite (periwinkle/koningsblauw, niet bleek).

Notitie, eigen invulling van Claude: het gezichtskristal als voorvlak van de binnenste kubus (Jordi zei niet waar het gezicht komt); kettingen/ijsbolletjes weggelaten.

```
Design the evolution of the Pokémon Cryogonal and draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): 64x64 pixels, max 16 colors, clean black outline, flat cel shading, plain white background. Compact, fits in a square. No ground, no shadow, no glow, no sparkles, no special effects.

IDEA (most important): Cryogonal's body is made of separate crystal pieces: broad, flat, faceted ice-crystal slabs, each ending in a forked V-shaped notched tip (like a swallowtail or chevron), arranged around a faceted gem-cut crystal that carries its face. In this evolution those SAME pieces have rearranged themselves into a tesseract (4D hypercube, the "cube inside a cube" drawing). The tesseract is BUILT FROM Cryogonal's pieces. There is NO Cryogonal inside the cube and no snowflake anywhere.

STRUCTURE:
- Every edge of the outer cube, the inner cube, and the 8 diagonal edges between their corners is one of Cryogonal's flat faceted crystal slabs. Where edges meet at a corner, the forked V-tips of the slabs interlock.
- The faceted gem-cut crystal with the face is itself a piece of the structure: it forms the front face of the inner cube.
- Open frame: you can see through the cubes between the slabs.
- Seen in 3/4 view facing left, so both cubes and their depth read clearly.
- Strict geometry: straight slabs, sharp facets, sharp corners. It floats. Not an animal, not a humanoid, no limbs.

FACE: Cryogonal's frozen, aggressive face (dark slit-like eye band with light cyan eyes, a dark mouth below) on the faceted crystal, big enough to read at 64x64.

COLORS: exactly Cryogonal's: deep periwinkle and royal blue faceted crystal, white highlights on the facets, light cyan accents, black outline. Not pale grey-white.

DO NOT: draw Cryogonal or a snowflake inside or behind the cube; make it a plain ice cube or glass box; use round bars; add arms, legs, a crown or a cape; add a starfield or space pattern; add snow particles or a glow aura.
```
