# Gemini-prompt r2: tesseract (25 nov)

Jordi koos de tesseract uit `../schetsen_vormen2.png` (nr. 3). Plakken in een nieuw Gemini-gesprek, zonder bijlage.

Notitie, eigen toevoegingen van Claude (niet door Jordi gezegd; schrappen bij twijfel): het gezicht op de binnenste kubus en hoe dat eruitziet (barsten zoals Cryogonal), ijs als materiaal en de ijsblauwe kleuren, de ijsbalken (dik in plaats van draad), de hoekbollen weggelaten.

```
Design an original Pokémon and draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): 64x64 pixels, max 16 colors, clean dark outline, flat cel shading, plain white background. Compact, fits in a square. No ground, no shadow, no glow, no sparkles, no special effects.

WHAT IT IS (most important): a living tesseract (a 4D hypercube) made of ice. It is NOT an animal and NOT a humanoid: no limbs, no tail, no wings, no body parts. It is a floating geometric object; the tesseract itself is the creature.

SHAPE:
- A large outer cube built only from thick, straight bars of clear ice (an open frame, you can see through it).
- Inside it floats a smaller inner cube, also an open frame of ice bars.
- Each of the 8 corners of the inner cube is connected to the matching corner of the outer cube by a straight diagonal ice bar, exactly like the classic tesseract drawing (cube inside a cube).
- Seen in 3/4 view facing left, so the depth of both cubes reads clearly.
- Strict geometry: perfectly straight bars, sharp corners, nothing rounded, bent or organic. Not a snowflake.
- The bars are thick enough to read at 64x64, with a light face and a dark face on each bar.
- It floats in the air.

FACE: only on the front face of the inner cube, inside the frame. The face is made of two horizontal cracks in a thin pane of ice: the upper crack holds two small eyes, the lower wider crack is a jagged mouth. Frozen, still, aggressive expression.

COLORS: icy pale blue and white with shiny highlights, darker steel-blue on the shaded sides of the bars, eyes in one solid color. Cold and glossy like polished ice.

DO NOT: add arms, legs, a body, a crown, spikes or a cape; draw a realistic glass cube, a gem, a dice or a box; add a starfield, galaxy or space pattern; add snow particles, a glow aura or light beams; make it round, cute or plush.
```
