Besluiten en citaten: `PROMPT.md` (v1) en `docs/concept/IDEEEN.md`. Ronde 1: `r1/` (Jordi koos het idee van b: wagons lossen op in rook; "het is nog geen pokemon").
Jordi (15 nov) eens met de analyse van Claude: (1) echte locomotief met gezicht erop, te veel onderdelen; (2) treinverhoudingen i.p.v. gedrongen; (3) pompoen-/horrorgrijns; (4) niets leeft, de geest is alleen rook. "maak een nieuwe prompt, ik ben het met je eens".
Geen bijlage: de opbouw moet anders, en een bijlage wordt gekopieerd (GEMINI.md). Geest-vorm niet gekozen: vier GEEST-blokken, per blok een nieuw gesprek.

## Prompt (basis; plak er één GEEST-blok onder)

```
Design an original Pokémon and draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style pixel art).

TYPE: Steel / Ghost. Final stage of its line.

IT IS A POKÉMON, NOT A MACHINE WITH A FACE
- A spirit has taken over an old steam locomotive, a bit like how Rotom possesses machines: the ghost is the creature, the locomotive is its shell.
- Simplify the locomotive into a few big, bold shapes, like Revavroom, Klinklang or Magnezone are simplified machines. Few separate parts: no cab windows, no rows of rivets, no rods and pipes everywhere.
- Stocky Pokémon proportions: a short, round, chunky locomotive. The round smokebox front is a big head, the short boiler behind it is a small body. A short chimney on top.
- At most 2 wheels visible on the near side, big and simple.

FACE
- The ghost looks out through the round front of the locomotive: real eyes and a mouth with an expression, like a Ghost-type Pokémon (Gengar, Drifblim, Haunter). Mischievous and alive.
- NOT a carved jack-o'-lantern grin, NOT a horror mask, NOT a skull, no jagged rows of teeth.

COLORS
- The iron is warm old iron with tints of rust: the train is functional again, not a wreck.
- The ghost has a nice contrasting greenish color against the iron.

FULL SPEED (this must be obvious)
- The whole locomotive leans forward, charging ahead.
- The wheels are drawn in motion like in a comic book.
- Behind the locomotive there are no wagons: the train dissolves into a greenish trail of ghostly smoke.

VIEW
- Semi-frontal: slightly more frontal than a three-quarter view, facing left. The front of the locomotive is closest to the viewer.

STYLE AND FORMAT
- 64x64 pixel sprite, the creature filling most of the canvas (final stage), compact silhouette that fits a square, max 16 colors, 1 px dark outline, light from top left, flat Gen 3 shading with 3 to 4 tones per color, no dithering, few small details.
- Plain white background. NO rails, floor, shadow, glow, speed lines, sparkles or effects outside the creature. One single creature, no extra views.

AVOID
- No realistic locomotive, no long train, no wagons, no cab with windows.
- No ghost conductor or separate ghost characters, no small ghosts in the smoke.
- Not a cartoon / rubber-hose style, not a children's TV train with a friendly human face, not a plush toy, chibi or Neopets mascot. No glossy 3D render.
```

### GEEST-blok a (kuif/oren van rook)
```
GHOST: the greenish smoke from the chimney forms the ghost's crest or ears, part of the creature's head.
```

### GEEST-blok b (spookstaart)
```
GHOST: the greenish smoke trail behind the locomotive ends in a ghost tail, like Haunter's or Drifblim's lower body.
```

### GEEST-blok c (handen)
```
GHOST: the ghost reaches out of the locomotive with two greenish ghostly hands, like Rotom's plasma arms; the hands are close to the body, not stretched wide.
```

### GEEST-blok d (alleen het gezicht)
```
GHOST: the ghost only shows in the face and in the greenish smoke trail; no other ghost body parts.
```

## Tips
- Weer een realistische trein: zet "IT IS A POKÉMON, NOT A MACHINE WITH A FACE" ook als eerste regel, nieuw gesprek.
- Weer een horrorgrijns: "cute but mischievous ghost face, small mouth" proberen.
- Te breed: "compact, fits a square 64x64 sprite, the smoke trail is short".
