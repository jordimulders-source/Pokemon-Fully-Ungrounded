# Pinsir-evolutie – Gemini-startprompt r1 (22 nov, nieuw gesprek, geen bijlage)

```
Design a brand-new Pokémon: the evolution of Pinsir. Type Bug/Ground. It must clearly read as Pinsir's evolution, but it is a new creature, not a copy of Pinsir.
Draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): pixel art, 64×64, max 16 colors, dark selout outline, plain white background.

WHAT IT IS
A huge beetle-kaiju hybrid, based on the SABERTOOTH LONGHORN BEETLE (Macrodontia cervicornis) from the Amazon.
Keep Pinsir's identity: the brown beetle body, Pinsir's face with its wide mouth lined with teeth, and the two big spiked pincers on top of the head. Here those pincers become the sabertooth longhorn's enormous, long, curved, saw-toothed mandibles, even bigger and with MORE spikes than Pinsir's.
From the sabertooth longhorn: bark-like mottled pattern on the back and wing cases, long segmented antennae.

KAIJU
It stands UPRIGHT ON TWO heavy, thick legs like a classic movie kaiju, with a thick heavy tail dragging behind it, a row of sharp spikes running down its back and tail, a hunched, massive, towering posture. Short strong arms like Pinsir's. Many more spikes than Pinsir overall.

VIEW
Three-quarter view facing left, like every Gen 3 front sprite: the far limbs on the left (partly hidden), the near ones on the right.
Compact: the whole Pokémon fits inside a square; tail and limbs close to the body.

DO NOT
Not a dinosaur with a beetle head, not a realistic beetle on its hind legs: one creature that is both Pinsir-beetle and kaiju.
No separate armour plates or robot parts. No ground, no rock, no shadow under it. No glow, no aura, no breath attack, no effects, no motion lines.
No plush/Neopets look, no chibi mascot, not a realistic painting.
```
