# Gemini-prompt: spooktrein fase 3 (Steel/Ghost), v10 (15 nov)

Ronde 7 (`r7/v9.png`, prompt v9): Jordi: "teveel wielen en teveel armen nu. De blik is nu wel wat beter. laten we weer afwijken van sugimori en meer naar pokemon emerald sprite gaan, want dat is makkelijker na te bouwen, er is nu teveel detail. Laten we het beperken naar 1 geest die uit de schoorsteen komt met 1 arm die zo'n pose maakt met zijn elleboog naar beneden en zijn vuist omhoog alsof die aan het touw trekt dat de trein snel laat gaan. deze geest heeft ook een miniatuur locomotief hoofd (de voorkant)".
Keuzes Claude (Jordi zei hier niets over): 2 geestwielen + vage wielen in de staart (zoals v8); groene "lijm" in de kieren blijft; gezicht van het lijf als in v9 ("blik beter"); gezicht van de kleine geest niet ingevuld. Nieuw gesprek, geen bijlage.

```
Design an original Pokémon and draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Pokémon Emerald style pixel art).

TYPE: Steel / Ghost. Final stage of its line.

CONCEPT: a piece of rusty railway scrap that would miserably fall apart without the ghost inside it. The ghost holds it together and has turned it into a furiously fast vehicle, and it is having an enormous amount of fun, a bit like Rotom. It is ONE creature, not a train with a ghost added.

BODY
- Overall: a teardrop shape lying on its side, thick and round at the front, narrowing towards the back. Long and low, fitting a square. The whole body tilts forward and down, as if diving ahead at furious speed.
- Made of a few big, battered pieces of rusty iron that do not really fit together; green ghost glow fills the cracks between them and holds them in place.
- Head (front half): one big rounded iron block. No nose. No flat round door, no plate with a face on it.
- Eyes: two identical, big, crooked green eyes high on the front, glowing out of cracks in the iron, mischievous.
- Mouth: one huge, wide, crooked zigzag grin torn across the front of the head, glowing green inside. The cowcatcher sits under it as a lower jaw.
- Back: the iron ends in a ragged, broken edge; out of the open back the ghost streams out as a short green smoke tail.
- Wheels: no iron wheels. Two big wheels made of green ghost energy underneath, spinning in motion like in a comic book, and one or two faint ghostly wheels in the smoke tail.

THE GHOST IN THE CHIMNEY
- Exactly ONE small green ghost comes up out of the dented chimney, its lower body flowing into the chimney.
- Its head is a miniature locomotive front.
- It has ONE arm, in the pose of pulling a train whistle cord: elbow down, fist up, like pumping the whistle to make the train go faster.
- No other arms, ghosts or faces anywhere.

COLORS: rusty iron (rust brown with a bit of dark grey); everything of the ghost is green.

STYLE AND FORMAT
- 64x64 pixel sprite, the creature filling most of the canvas (final stage), compact silhouette, max 16 colors, 1 px dark outline, light from top left, flat Gen 3 shading with 3 to 4 tones per color, no dithering.
- Gen 3 level of detail: a few big readable shapes, very few small details.
- Plain white background. NO rails, floor, shadow, glow, speed lines, sparkles or effects outside the creature. One single creature, no extra views.

VIEW: slightly more frontal than three-quarter, facing left.

AVOID: not a realistic or complete locomotive, no cab, no windows, no rods, no pipes, no wagons, no headlight, no ladder, no human face, not Thomas the Tank Engine, no skull, no conductor, no extra arms or ghosts. Not a plush toy, chibi or Neopets mascot. No glossy 3D render.
```
