# Gemini-prompt: Banette-evolutie, v5 stap 1 (17 nov)

Nieuw gesprek, **geen bijlagen**. Narrenmuts bovenaan en concreet (Jordi over r4: "hij doet niet eens de narrenmuts"). Alleen de nieuwe puppetmaster zelf; geen pop, rits of Shuppets (die halen Banette terug).
Stap 2 (later, met de beste stap-1-versie als bijlage): het kledingstuk om zijn middel. Stap 3: de Shuppets aan de draden.

```
Design an original Pokémon: a sinister ghost puppet master, Ghost/Dark type.

MOST IMPORTANT: THE HEAD SHAPE IS A JESTER'S HAT. The top of his head splits into TWO long points that curve outward to the left and right, like the two points of a jester's cap. Both points are part of his own head (same dark substance), not a separate hat. The head must NOT have one central point or spike.

- Imposing and genuinely scary: a villain, not cute.
- A very sinister wide grin: a zigzag mouth with sharp teeth, the teeth the same dark grey as his head.
- Large slanted eyes: red with a yellow centre.
- Floating; the lower body is simple, with two short stubby undefined legs.
- Both arms held up high. His hands ARE marionette control crosses: each arm flows directly into its cross in one continuous shape. Not a cross held in a hand. Not wood: the same dark ghostly substance as his body.
- From each cross hang a few empty threads, each only 1 pixel thick.
- Colours: dark grey-black body, red-yellow eyes.

STYLE AND FORMAT
- Official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style), 64x64, max 16 colours, 1 px dark outline, light from top left, flat Gen 3 shading with 3 to 4 tones per colour, no dithering.
- 3/4 view facing left, with a perspective as if seen slightly from above.
- Large: fills almost the whole 64x64 square.
- Plain white background. NO floor, shadow, glow, aura or effects.

AVOID
- No zipper anywhere. No single horn or single central point on the head. No wooden crosses. Not cute, not chibi. Not a plush toy, no glossy 3D render.
```
