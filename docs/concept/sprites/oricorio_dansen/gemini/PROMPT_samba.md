# Oricorio Samba – Gemini-startprompt (r2, nieuw gesprek)

Bijlagen: `../bestaande_vormen.png` en `../koppen.png` (r2: oog beschreven, r1 gaf 3× een spleetoog met wit). Studie en wat van Jordi is: `../STUDIE.md`.

```
Design a new form of the Pokémon ORICORIO: SAMBA STYLE, from the carnival of Rio de Janeiro. Its type is Grass/Flying.
It is a new dance style of ORICORIO. The attached image shows the 4 existing Oricorio styles (Baile, Pom-Pom, Pa'u, Sensu): keep exactly the same small songbird body, short neck, pink beak, thin pink legs with toes and the same Oricorio eye.
THE EYE (most important, this is what makes it Oricorio): a big, solid black oval eye with one small white shine dot, sitting just above the base of the beak. NO white around the eye, it is not a narrow slit and not a sleepy, droopy eye. Over the top of the black eye lies a thick dark eyelid line (like Baile, Pa'u and Sensu in the attached image), which gives a calm, cool look. The second attached image is a close-up of the 4 existing heads: copy that eye. Only the plumage changes: the feathers themselves ARE the dance costume (like Baile's feathers are a flamenco dress), not clothing worn over the bird. Do not copy one of the 4 existing styles.

WHAT IT IS
A samba carnival dancer made of feathers: green and yellow carnival feathers, MAINLY GREEN (the Grass type color); yellow only as accent. A huge fan of long plumes rising behind it like a carnival backpiece, and a tall feathered headdress as its crest. The plumes look like tropical leaves, because it is Grass type. The body feathers are short and shiny like a sequined costume.

POSE
Samba step: standing on the toes of one foot, the other foot lifted, wings raised and spread, the backpiece fanned open.

Draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): pixel art, 64×64, max 16 colors, dark selout outline, plain white background. Three-quarter view facing left, like every Gen 3 front sprite: far wing and far leg on the left, near ones on the right. 2 wings and 2 legs in total. Compact, fits in a square.
DO NOT
No ground, no shadow, no stage, no glow, no music notes, no effects. No human body parts (no arms, hands, hair): only feathers. No plush/Neopets look.
```
