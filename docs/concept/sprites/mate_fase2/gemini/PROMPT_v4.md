## Analyse Claude
Rand (zilver) en mond (klein) zijn opgelost. Maar het ventje houdt de bombilla's rechtop als vorken en de rechter hangt als haakje over de rand: er wordt niet geroeid. Prompt v4 beschrijft alleen de roeibeweging (gedrag als posebron, les GEMINI.md).

## Prompt

```
The attached image is a Pokémon sprite draft. KEEP EVERYTHING EXACTLY AS IT IS: the gourd canoe, its silver rim, carved eyes and nose, the leaf heap at the front, the hot water at the back, the leaf man's head, face and colors.

CHANGE ONLY THE POSE OF THE LEAF MAN: he is clearly ROWING.
- He leans forward and holds ONE long bombilla with both hands, like a kayak paddle, slanted diagonally across his body.
- The lower end of the bombilla (the flat round filter, like a small spoon) dips into the water beside the canoe, near the back. The upper end (the mouthpiece) points up and back over his shoulder.
- The bombilla is a thin straight silver metal straw, long enough to reach the water.
- A second bombilla lies across the canoe behind him, or is held the same way in the other hand on the far side, so both ends are clearly in rowing position. Nothing hangs over the rim like a hook.

STYLE AND FORMAT
- Official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style), 64x64, max 16 colors, 1 px dark outline, light from top left, flat Gen 3 shading, no dithering.
- Plain white background. NO river, NO floor, NO shadow, NO effects.

AVOID
- No other changes. No oars, no forks, no extra arms.
```

## Tips
- Twee rietjes blijven lastig: "one bombilla in each hand, both slanted the same way, both lower ends in the water, like a rowing boat seen from the front".
