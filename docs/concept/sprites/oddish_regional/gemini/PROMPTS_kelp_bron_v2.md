## Oddish
```
Design a regional form of the Pokémon Oddish and draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style pixel art).

CONCEPT
- Oddish from the sunlit kelp forests of the Galápagos, based on the Galápagos kelp (Eisenia galapagensis). Type: Water/Grass.
- Body, face and pose of Oddish's own Gen 3 front sprite: the small round body in a walking pose, tiny red dot eyes and a small open red mouth. No big eyes, no smile.
- Body color: sea green / turquoise, with a lighter belly, like a fish countershaded in sunlit water. Not blue, not grey.
- Feet: instead of Oddish's two stubby feet, two small kelp holdfasts: short, branching, root-like grips, still placed where Oddish's feet are, in the same walking stance.
- On top: instead of Oddish's five leaves, five kelp blades in exactly the places, sizes and directions of Oddish's leaves, fanning out from one point on top of the head, mostly up and to the right. Olive to golden brown, slightly glossy, with ruffled, wavy, crinkled edges and a wrinkled surface. Kelp has NO veins: no midrib, no side veins.

POSE
- Three-quarter view facing left, the classic Oddish walking pose.

STYLE AND FORMAT
- 64x64 canvas, max 16 colors, 1 px dark outline, light from top left, flat Gen 3 shading, no dithering.
- Plain white background. No ground, water, bubbles, shadow or extra effects. One single creature, no extra views.

AVOID
- No leaf veins, no dry or autumn leaves, no green land-plant leaves, no flower, no fire or smoke.
```

## Gloom
```
Design a regional form of the Pokémon Gloom and draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style pixel art).

CONCEPT
- Gloom from the dark deep sea near the Galápagos hydrothermal vents. Type: Water/Fire. It evolves from a sea-green Oddish with kelp on its head, after sinking into the deep.
- Face and pose of Gloom's Gen 3 front sprite: the squinting closed eyes, the wide open drooling mouth, the short legs.
- Body: soft and gelatinous like a deep-sea animal, slightly sagging under the pressure, rounder at the bottom. Instead of arms, two small fins held out to the sides.
- Body color: deep red / burgundy, like deep-sea animals (red is invisible in the deep).
- On top: a young hydrothermal vent forming: a lumpy, irregular, knobbly mound of stacked rough mineral crust, black and dark grey with a few patches of pale yellow sulfur. Not divided into lobes or segments, no spots. Jagged cracks run through it and glow orange-red from the heat inside, like cooling lava.
- A few wilted kelp blades, faded to dull brown, hang limply down from under the mound on the left and right, broad and ruffled (not thin strands). Their tips are scorched black, with a thin glowing orange edge, like burning paper.
- The drool is glowing orange-hot vent water, and the inside of the open mouth glows like a furnace.

POSE
- Three-quarter view facing left, the classic Gloom pose.

STYLE AND FORMAT
- 64x64 canvas, max 16 colors, 1 px dark outline, light from top left, flat Gen 3 shading, no dithering.
- Plain white background. No ground, water, bubbles, smoke or extra effects. One single creature, no extra views.

AVOID
- No round bud divided into lobes, no spots pattern like Gloom's bud, no hair or dreadlocks, no arms, no green, no flower, no flames.
```

## Vileplume
```
Design a regional form of the Pokémon Vileplume and draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style pixel art).

CONCEPT
- Vileplume from the pitch-dark hydrothermal vents of the Galápagos Rift, 2500 m deep. Type: Water/Fire. Its line: a sea-green Oddish with kelp, a deep-red Gloom with a mineral mound, and now a living hot vent with sea anemones around it.
- Body: the small body under a large, wide top, like Vileplume, with short arms held out. Instead of short legs, two long, thin stilt legs like the fin rays of a deep-sea tripod fish, standing it high above the seafloor.
- Body color: ghostly white / pearly, like the pale animals living at vents.
- On top: in the center, where Vileplume's flower center is, a short, wide vent crater of black mineral crust that glows orange-hot inside. Around it, in place of Vileplume's five petals, five big open sea anemones in a ring, in bright red and orange. Each anemone is a fleshy cushion whose top is a wide crown of thick tentacles pointing up and outward around a small central mouth. The tentacles are on top, not hanging down. Together they form one wide top, as wide as Vileplume's flower.

POSE
- Three-quarter view facing left, standing on the two stilt legs.

STYLE AND FORMAT
- 64x64 canvas, max 16 colors, 1 px dark outline, light from top left, flat Gen 3 shading, no dithering.
- Plain white background. No ground, water, bubbles, smoke or extra effects. No fish, crabs or other creatures. One single creature, no extra views.

AVOID
- No mushroom caps, no cupcake or fringe shapes, no tentacles hanging down, no petals, no flower, no green, no grey body, no tall chimney, no flames.
```
