# Gemini-prompt: regionale Tangela (Medusa), startprompt v1 (27 nov)

Regels: `docs/lessen/GEMINI.md`. Bijlage: `bijlage_tangela.png` (Tangela's Gen 3-sprite, 8×; alleen voor de herkenbaarheid). Besluiten: `docs/concept/IDEEEN.md` → "Regionale Tangela: Medusa".

Jordi's woorden: "regional Tangela maken medusa inspired"; "de vines worden slangen en het midden blijft zwart met ogen, maar de ogen iets anders". Type staat nog open en zit daarom niet in de prompt. Hoe de ogen anders worden, zei Jordi niet; Gemini mag dat ontwerpen.

```
Design a regional form of the Pokémon TANGELA and draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style pixel art).

THE ATTACHED SPRITE IS THE NORMAL TANGELA. Keep it clearly recognizable as Tangela: a round tangled ball as the body, a black center inside the tangle with two eyes looking out, and the two feet underneath.

CONCEPT
- Inspired by Medusa from Greek mythology.
- The vines are snakes: every strand of the tangle is a snake, with its own small snake head. Together they still form Tangela's round tangled ball.

DESIGN
- The center stays black, with the eyes in it.
- The eyes are a bit different from the normal Tangela's eyes.

POSE
- Three-quarter view facing left, like official Gen 3 front sprites.

STYLE AND FORMAT
- 64x64 pixel sprite, max 16 colors, 1 px dark outline, clean shading like official Game Freak Gen 3 sprites.
- Compact, fits in a square.
- Plain white background. No floor, rock, shadow, glow, sparkles or effects. One single creature, no extra views.

AVOID
- No human face, no woman, no human features.
- No Greek clothing, crown or accessories.
- No glossy 3D render, no plush or mascot look.
```
