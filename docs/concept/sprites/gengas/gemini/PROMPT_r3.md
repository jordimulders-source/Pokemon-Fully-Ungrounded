Nieuw gesprek, ALLEEN de Gengar-sprite als bijlage (geen vorige Gengas: dan kopieert Gemini hem). Kleurregel: Jordi kiest.

```
Design a new Pokémon and draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): 64x64 pixel art, at most 16 colours, 1-pixel dark outline around every part, light from the top left, plain white background, no ground, no shadow, no sparkles.

The Pokémon is "Gengas", a Cosmic/Poison type based on a BROWN DWARF (a failed star): a small ball of gas, like a tiny gas giant planet. It is NOT a ghost and NOT related to Gengar; it only looks similar by coincidence (convergent evolution, like Wiglett and Diglett).

FACE: use the attached Gengar sprite. The eyes and the wide grin must be exactly Gengar's: same shape, angle, red eyes, white teeth, size and position.

BODY: a round ball that clearly reads as GAS, not rock: soft swirling cloud bands like Jupiter, with wavy, flowing edges between the bands, one small swirling storm vortex on the side, smooth soft shading and a soft highlight at the top left. No cracks, no craters, no stone texture on the body.
EARS: two solid ears with exactly the shape of Gengar's ears (wide base, sharp point, on top of the head), made of the same swirling gas as the body; the tips trail off slightly like smoke.
LIMBS: exactly four limbs, two hands and two feet, shaped like Gengar's stubby hands (three blunt fingers) and feet, but they are small rocky MOONS with craters, the only solid parts. All four float free around the ball with a clear gap of white background between each limb and the body: hands at the left and right sides, feet below the ball. Nothing touches or overlaps the ball.
COLOUR: [KLEURREGEL]
POSE: front-facing like the attached Gengar, compact so everything fits in a square.

AVOID: rock or stone texture on the body, a tabby cat look, Gengar's blue-purple colour, spikes, limbs touching the body, extra limbs, hollow loop ears, rings like Saturn, stars, a cute plush or candy look.
```
