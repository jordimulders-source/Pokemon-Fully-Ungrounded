# Ledian-evolutie – Gemini-prompt r2 (27 nov)

Op r1 (`r1.png`), Jordi: "het helpt misschien wel om ledian te benoemen, want deze wijkt veel af en is niet per se een superheld/tokusatsu vorm ervan". Pose: "Vliegen met zijn arm voorwaarts zou een prima pose zijn".
Nieuw gesprek, geen bijlage.

```
Design an original Pokémon: the evolution of Ledian (Gen 2). Draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): 64x64 pixels, at most 16 colors, dark outline, white background, no ground, no shadow, no glow, no energy or special effects.

What it is: Ledian evolved into a full superhero / tokusatsu hero version of itself, a space superhero that flies through space. Bug type and Cosmic type. Keep Ledian's design and push its superhero and tokusatsu inspiration further, so it is clearly recognisable as Ledian's evolution.

Anatomy (from Ledian): upright ladybug body, red ladybug shell with black spots on its back, four arms with white round hands, two long antennae, Ledian's large eyes.

Pose: flying like a superhero with one arm stretched forward. 3/4 view facing left (far arm and far antenna on the left side, near ones on the right). Compact Gen 3 composition that fits a square.

AVOID: a human in a costume, a plush toy, Neopets style, chibi proportions, Gen 5 style, realistic insect rendering, extra limbs beyond four arms and two legs.
```
