# Sneeuwmonster fase 1 (Ice/Fire-lijn, fase 1 puur Ice): Gemini-prompts
Besluiten en citaten: `docs/concept/IDEEEN.md` → "Extra Ice-lijn: Ice/Fire".
Vast (Jordi): Ice → Ice/Fire → Ice/Fire; fase 1 "puur ice type ... geinspireerd op de flurry uit mario", "gewoon een wandelend sneeuwmannetje, maar moet wel iets afwijken van mario"; knopen = "koude donkere steentjes" (worden per fase grotere gloeiende stenen); emmer pas vanaf fase 2; early game.
Eigen invulling Claude (niet door Jordi gezegd, schrappen bij twijfel): ogen als ingedrukte steentjes, twee knopen, peervormig en klonterig, pluk ruige sneeuw op de kop, geen armen, uitstraling nieuwsgierig/onschuldig. Flurry niet bij naam genoemd (een naam in de prompt = Gemini kopieert het), wel in AVOID.

## r1 (nieuw gesprek, zonder bijlage)
```
Design an original Pokemon. Type: Ice. First stage of a three-stage line.

Concept: a small living snowman that walks around. Its whole body is made of packed snow: one single rounded body (no separate head ball, no stacked snowballs), slightly pear-shaped and a little lumpy, like snow that was pressed together by hand. Two short stubby feet of snow underneath, no arms. A small tuft of rough, uneven snow on top of its head.

Face: its two eyes are small dark pebbles pressed into the snow, like the coal eyes of a snowman, with a tiny highlight. No mouth or a very small simple mouth. Personality: innocent and curious.

Buttons: on its belly, in a vertical row, two cold dark grey pebbles pressed into the snow, like the buttons of a snowman. They are plain cold stones, matte, not glowing, not metal, not gold.

Colors: snow in white with cool light blue and grey-blue shading. Eyes and pebbles in dark charcoal grey. No other colors.

Style: official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style), 64x64, max 16 colors, dark outline, facing 3/4 to the left, compact composition that fills a square. White background, no ground, no shadow, no effects, no glow, no falling snowflakes.

AVOID: the Flurry from Mario, Shy Guy, Snorunt, Vanillite, a classic three-ball snowman, a carrot nose, a hat or scarf, stick arms, big black oval hole eyes, gold or shiny buttons, plush toy / Neopets look, glowing or warm colors.
```
