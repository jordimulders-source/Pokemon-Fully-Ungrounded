# Gemini-prompt: vuurduivel fase 1, v2 (22 nov)

Nieuw gesprek, geen bijlage. r1: `../r1/gemini_r1.png`.

## Jordi over r1
"het is nog teveel bok en te weinig satyr, en het vuur is er gewoon op geplakt"

## Prompt

```
Design an original Pokémon and draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style).

CONCEPT
- Fire/Dark type, the small first stage of a two-stage evolution line.
- A little devil imp based on a SATYR (faun), NOT on a goat. Satyr anatomy: the upper body is that of a small, impish child-like creature: a round impish face with a small snub nose (no goat muzzle), pointed ears, two tiny horn buds on the forehead, slim arms with small hands. From the hips down it has shaggy goat legs bent backwards like a goat's hind legs, ending in small cloven hooves.
- Not chubby; light and springy, ready to run off after a prank.
- It loves causing mischief and finds it hilarious: a BIG, WIDE, MISCHIEVOUS GRIN.
- THE FIRE GROWS OUT OF ITS BODY, it is not placed on top:
  - Its hair IS fire: the dark hair on its head turns into flames towards the tips, dark red at the roots, then orange, yellow at the tips. One shape, no separate flame on top of the head.
  - It has a thin devil tail; the tuft of fur at the end of the tail gradually turns into a small flame, the same way.
  - No other fire.
- COLORS: dark body (black and dark brown, shaggy darker legs) with bright, vivid red-orange-yellow fire. The fire is the brightest thing in the sprite.
- Cartoony, but in the Pokémon way: simple bold shapes, a clear readable silhouette, expressive face, like an official Ken Sugimori design.

STYLE AND FORMAT
- Official Gen 3 front sprite, 64x64, max 16 colors, 1 px dark outline, light from top left, flat Gen 3 shading with 3 to 4 tones per color, no dithering.
- Three-quarter view facing left. The creature is small, well under 64 px tall.
- Plain white background. NO floor, shadow, smoke or effects. One single creature.

AVOID
- No goat head, no goat muzzle, no goat body. Not Chimchar, Monferno, Impidimp, Grimmsnarl, Houndour or Skiddo. No pitchfork, no props, no clothing, no wings. No red body. No pentagram or symbols. No text. No western-cartoon or plush look. No glossy 3D render.
```
