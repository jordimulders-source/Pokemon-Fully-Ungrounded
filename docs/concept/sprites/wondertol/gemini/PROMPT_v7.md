```

- Plain white background. NO floor, shadow, smoke, motion lines or spark effects. One single creature.

AVOID
- No separate stump or foot under the body. No lines dividing head, legs and body. No sparks or streaks around it. No orange. No green. Not Charmander. No coiled snake body. No text. No glossy 3D render.
```
