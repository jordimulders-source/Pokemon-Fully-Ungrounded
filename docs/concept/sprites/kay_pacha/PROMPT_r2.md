# Gemini-prompt Kay Pacha r2 (nieuw gesprek, geen bijlage)

Jordi na r1: "lijkt ook veel te veel op een normale poema", "maak maar groen", "groter hoofd", "en meer versiering met inca verwijzingen, mogelijk wat bladeren ook", "ogen paars prima", "versiering is niet een paar juwelen".
Eigen invulling van Claude (schrappen als Jordi het niet wil): groen = de vacht; de voorbeelden van Inca-versiering (tumi, zigzag van Sacsayhuamán, gouden platen, trapeziumvorm); pose zoals r1.

```
Design a new legendary Pokémon as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): pixel art, 64x64, max 16 colors, dark outline, light from top-left, plain white background, no ground, no shadow, no effects.

WHAT IT IS: a Grass/Psychic legendary beast based on a puma, the guardian of "Kay Pacha", the world of the living in Inca belief. It belongs to a legendary trio, like Entei, Raikou and Suicune: it must read immediately as a legendary Pokémon, NOT as a normal animal wearing accessories. It must not look like Entei, Raikou, Suicune, Leafeon, Meowscarada or any other existing Pokémon.

BODY: puma build (slim, muscular, long thick tail, short rounded ears), but stylized like a Pokémon, with a BIG head (clearly larger than a real puma's head). The coat is GREEN. The eyes are PURPLE. No spots, no stripes.

ORNAMENTS: the decoration is NOT a few pieces of jewelry. Lots of Inca-inspired gold decoration that is part of the creature and changes its silhouette, the way Entei's mane and Raikou's back cloud do. Inca references, for example: a gold headpiece shaped like a tumi (Inca ceremonial knife with a half-moon blade), a crest along the back in the stepped zigzag shape of the Inca walls of Sacsayhuamán, large gold plates like fitted Inca stones, trapezoid shapes like Inca doorways. Some leaves may be part of the decoration. Keep every ornament a clear, bold shape that reads at 64x64 (no tiny patterns, no thin lines, no symbols).

POSE: 3/4 view facing left, standing on four legs, head raised, proud and calm like a legendary beast.

DO NOT: a normal-looking puma, a collar, bracelets, jewelry chains, spots, stripes, wings, a human, glowing aura, magic effects, rocks, grass on the ground, background.
```
