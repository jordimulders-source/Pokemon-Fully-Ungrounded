```
Design a brand-new Pokémon: a lazy, goofy dragon. Type Dragon/Normal. It is the evolution of Dunsparce, but it must NOT look like a copy of Dunsparce: it is a new creature.
Draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): pixel art, 64×64, max 16 colors, dark selout outline, plain white background.

WHAT IT IS
A big, long, round-bodied dragon with a lazy, goofy attitude.
Head: a real dragon head with a LONGER SNOUT and small HORNS. The only thing kept from Dunsparce's face is the blue ring around the eye. Expression lazy and goofy (half-closed eye, relaxed), not fierce.
Body: long, round and chubby, with blue bands on a pale yellow body.
Legs: 2 short, stubby legs. No arms.
Tail: the tail flows seamlessly into a drill tip, no seam or break between tail and drill.
Wings: 2 wings (near and far) based on Dunsparce's small pale blue-white wings, but somewhat bigger. They must be quite BIZARRE and unusual in shape, NOT typical dragon wings (no bat wings).

POSE
The body runs DIAGONALLY across the sprite: head at the upper left, drill tail at the lower right.

VIEW
Three-quarter view facing left, like every Gen 3 front sprite: the far wing and far leg are on the left (narrower, partly hidden), the near ones on the right.
Compact: the whole Pokémon fits inside a square.

DO NOT
Do not reuse Dunsparce's round head or its mouth; do not draw a coiled worm.
No bat wings, no typical dragon wings. No beard, no whiskers.
No ground, no rock, no shadow under it. No glow, no effects.
No plush/Neopets look, no chibi mascot, not a Western knight dragon, not a realistic reptile.
```
