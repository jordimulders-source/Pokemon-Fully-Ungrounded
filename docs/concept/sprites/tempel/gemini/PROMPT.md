# Gemini-prompt v1 (23 nov)

Create an original Pokémon as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style), 64×64 pixels, max 16 colors, clean dark outline, plain white background.

The Pokémon IS an ancient Inca temple that has come to life after centuries. It is not a creature wearing a temple, and it is not a creature standing next to one: the building itself is the whole body. Ghost/Rock type.

Body: a compact, massive block of Inca masonry: large, tightly fitted, slightly rounded stone blocks with clean joints (typical Inca stonework, no mortar), walls that lean slightly inward toward the top. It is huge and heavy, like Snorlax in weight and presence, filling most of the 64×64 frame in a compact, square composition.

Face: the classic Inca TRAPEZOID doorway (narrower at the top than at the bottom) in the front wall is its mouth: dark inside, like an entrance you would walk into. Above the doorway, two trapezoid niches/holes in the wall are its eyes: the eyes are light shining out from inside these holes in the stonework, the only sign that something lives inside. No other facial features. The idea: you think it is just the entrance to a temple, until you notice it is looking at you.

No limbs at all: no arms, no legs, no feet, no hands. It rests directly on its own base. Viewed in 3/4 view facing left (front wall and one side wall visible).

Colors: weathered grey/grey-brown andesite stone of Inca walls, darker in the joints and the doorway.

No glow, no aura, no light rays, no particles, no effects. No ground, no rock, no shadow under it.

AVOID: Aztec or Maya step pyramids, gold, carvings of gods or faces in the stone, a mummy or ghost figure inside the doorway, cute plush style, a person or creature in a costume, a realistic architectural drawing.
