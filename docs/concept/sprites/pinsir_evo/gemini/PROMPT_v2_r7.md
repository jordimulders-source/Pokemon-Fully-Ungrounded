Design a brand-new Pokémon: the evolution of Pinsir. Type Bug/Ground. It must clearly read as Pinsir's evolution: a towering, massive, monstrous KAIJU version of Pinsir, much bigger and heavier, with Pinsir's huge pincers grown into enormous mandibles. A new creature, not a recolored Pinsir.
Draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): pixel art, 64×64, max 16 colors, plain white background. Design it FOR 64×64: big, simple, chunky shapes that stay readable at that size, like Tyranitar, Aggron or Pinsir in Gen 3. No tiny details.

WHAT IT IS
A beetle-kaiju based on the SABERTOOTH LONGHORN BEETLE (Macrodontia cervicornis) from the Amazon, standing UPRIGHT ON TWO legs like a classic movie kaiju (think Godzilla): a massive, heavy, towering body with broad shoulders, thick hips and thighs, a slightly forward-leaning menacing stance, and a long, thick, heavy tail. The head is small compared to the huge body.
- Head: a broad, flat, slightly domed head shield on top with 3 big spikes. Under it Pinsir's own mouth (the vertical mouth with rows of teeth on both sides), somewhat bigger; only that one mouth, no snout or nose. Two angry, slanted, light eyes under the edge of the shield, forming an angry V.
- Mandibles: the sabertooth longhorn's mandibles GROW OUT OF THE HEAD (thick at the base, rooted at the two front corners of the head beside the mouth), point forward and curve toward each other at the tips. They are the BIGGEST, most eye-catching feature, just like Pinsir's pincers: very large, thick and powerful, sweeping forward and slightly up, still within the square. Each has only 2 or 3 BIG teeth on the inside edge. Dark reddish-brown with darker tips.
- Body: big, heavy, simple. Medium brown with a lighter cream-tan belly plate in a few large segments (like Pinsir's belly). NOT black: there must be clear light and shadow on every part.
- Arms: two short, thick arms held close to the body, each ending in 3 big claws. Simple shapes, no separate plates per joint.
- Legs: two heavy, thick kaiju legs, simple big shapes with 3 big claws per foot.
- Back: its closed beetle wing cases have fused into ONE big, domed, SPIKY SHELL that covers the whole back, like the back shell of Turtonator or Drednaw (shell shape and perspective only, not their colors or faces). In this front three-quarter view the shell is seen from the side-front exactly as in Turtonator's front sprite: it sits behind the body, its rim follows the shoulders and runs down to the base of the tail, and it is part of the body (not a separate disc, not a backpack). The shell is ochre/tan with a few (4 or 5) large dark bark-like blotches, simple shading (light upper-left, shadow lower-right), and a row of 5 or 6 BIG spikes along its rim and top that continue into the tail spikes.
- Tail: a long, thick, heavy kaiju tail continuing the row of BIG dorsal spikes.
- Spikes overall: few and big (head, back, tail), each a clean triangle. No rows of tiny spikes.

VIEW
Three-quarter view facing left, like every Gen 3 front sprite: the far limbs on the left (partly hidden), the near ones on the right.
Compact: the whole Pokémon fits inside a square; body large in the frame, mandibles, tail and limbs close to the body.

DO NOT
No separate oval disc on the shoulder. No tiny spikes, saw-teeth, cones or textures. No thick black lines dividing the body into many small plates. No antennae. Not a dinosaur with a beetle head, not a realistic beetle.
No ground, no rock, no shadow under it. No glow, no aura, no effects, no motion lines.
No plush/Neopets look, no chibi mascot, not a realistic painting.
