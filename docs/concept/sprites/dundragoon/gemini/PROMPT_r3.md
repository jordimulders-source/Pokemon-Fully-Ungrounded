```
Design a brand-new Pokémon: a lazy, goofy dragon. Type Dragon/Normal. It is the evolution of Dunsparce, but it is a new creature, not a copy of Dunsparce and NOT a Dragonite.
Draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): pixel art, 64×64, max 16 colors, dark selout outline, plain white background.

WHAT IT IS
A big, long, serpent-like dragon. The body may be bulky and heavy, but it is NOT an upright standing biped: it is a long, thick body that lies and stretches diagonally, like a huge lazy snake-dragon.
Head: a dragon head with a LONGER SNOUT and small straight HORNS. The only thing kept from Dunsparce's face is the blue ring around the eye. Expression lazy and goofy, not fierce.
Body: long, thick and round, pale yellow with blue bands across the back. No light belly plate, no belly stripes.
Legs: 2 short, stubby legs under the long body. NO arms, no hands.
Tail: the tail ends in a CLEAR DRILL: a spiral, cone-shaped drill tip, flowing seamlessly from the tail, no seam between tail and drill.
Wings: 2 wings (near and far) based on Dunsparce's small pale blue-white wings, but somewhat bigger. They must be quite BIZARRE and unusual in shape, NOT typical dragon wings (no bat wings).

POSE
The long body runs DIAGONALLY across the sprite: head at the upper left, drill tail at the lower right.

VIEW
Three-quarter view facing left, like every Gen 3 front sprite: the far wing and far leg are on the left (narrower, partly hidden), the near ones on the right.
Compact: the whole Pokémon fits inside a square.

DO NOT
Not Dragonite: no upright standing pose, no arms, no pale striped belly, no curled antennae, no round Dragonite muzzle.
Do not reuse Dunsparce's round head or mouth; not a coiled worm.
No bat wings, no typical dragon wings. No beard, no whiskers.
No ground, no rock, no shadow under it. No glow, no effects.
No plush/Neopets look, no chibi mascot, not a Western knight dragon, not a realistic reptile.
```
