## Prompt

```
Design an original Pokémon and draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style).

CONCEPT
- Fairy/Rock type, the final stage of a three-stage evolution line. Its name is Gnometrotter: a gnome Pokémon that has walked so far for so long that its body has fully adapted to its environment. This is its Cave Form: after ages in caves, rock has become part of its body. A rock gnome: an old garden gnome slowly turning into rock.
- It must still clearly be the evolution of a cartoony garden-gnome Pokémon: a big round pink nose, a droopy gnome cap over the eyes, a round pink belly, a sky-blue lower half, rounded blue feet and a belt.

THE GNOME PART (keep)
- A big, perfectly ROUND, ball-shaped pink nose, like a round ball. Not oval, not long.
- A soft, round, chubby pink body, like a ball. NOT muscular: no big biceps, no broad chest, no bodybuilder shape. Its heaviness comes from the rock on it and its old posture, not from muscles.
- The lower half of the body including the legs is sky blue, like a garden gnome's trousers, with a thin dark-blue line on the border. This is NOT clothing, just a second body color.
- Feet: simple, rounded, sky-blue feet, a bit big. NOT made of stone, no toes.
- A simple dark belt around the waist with a small square buckle.
- Its free arm (the one without the stick) is a thin, short pink oval arm ending in a round, chubby ball-shaped pink hand. Not muscular.

THE ROCK PART
- Posture: hunched forward with age, taking a slow, heavy step, leaning on its stick. An old, seasoned hiker.
- Stick arm: one arm has turned to stone from the shoulder down, and its fist has grown into a long rock walking stick. Fist and stick are ONE single piece of rock: there is no hand gripping a stick, no fingers, no visible border between fist and stick; the stone forearm simply continues and grows down into the stick.
- Cap and back are one rock mass: the cap has the droopy pointed shape of a soft gnome cap (NOT tall, NOT straight up), but is made of dark rough rock; its tip hangs down behind and fuses with a rocky ridge growing out of its shoulders and back.
- The cap comes down low over the face: its lower edge overlaps and covers the top of the nose. The cap touches the nose directly, so there is NO dark band, gap, slit or shadow between cap and face, and nothing that could read as eyes.
- Where pink skin meets rock (shoulders, back, the stone arm) the border is jagged, with cracks running into the skin. The rock grows out of it, nothing is stuck on.
- Beard like a rockslide: one heavy beard of light grey angular rock layers, from under the nose down to the belt, with a broken, angular bottom edge. Mustache part of it. NO mouth visible.
- Hair: rough light grey rock tufts sticking out sideways from under the cap.
- Two kinds of rock: the cap, the back ridge and the stone arm are dark, angular grey rock. The beard and hair tufts are a lighter, grainy grey rock.

STYLE AND FORMAT
- Official Gen 3 front sprite, 64x64, max 16 colors, 1 px dark outline, light from top left, flat Gen 3 shading with 3 to 4 tones per color, no dithering.
- Keep the rock readable at sprite size: big shapes and a few cracks, no fine texture or speckles.
- Three-quarter view facing left. A final-stage Pokémon, about 60 px tall, compact, centered in the frame.
- Plain white background. NO floor, shadow, loose rocks on the ground, cave background or effects. One single creature.
- Cartoony, but in the Pokémon way: bold shapes, a clear and cool silhouette, like an official Ken Sugimori design.

AVOID
- No visible eyes, no dark band under the cap, no visible mouth. No muscles, no buff arms. No stone feet, no toes. No hand holding a separate stick. No oval or long nose. No crystals, no glow. No pebbles stuck onto the skin. No human proportions, no little man in a costume. Not Golem, not Graveler, not Impidimp. No western-cartoon or plush look. No glossy 3D render.
```
