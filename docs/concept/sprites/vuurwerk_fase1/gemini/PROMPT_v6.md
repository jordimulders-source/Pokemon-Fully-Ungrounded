## Prompt (A)

```
Design an original Pokémon as an official Gen 3 pixel-art front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style).

WHAT IT IS
This Pokémon comes out of a firework rocket. The rocket stands upright. The fire that shoots out of the open BOTTOM of the rocket creates a small firework creature: the fire itself becomes the creature. Nothing of it exists outside that fire: no legs, no tail, no second body.

HOW IT IS BUILT, IN THIS ORDER
1. Fire comes out of the open bottom of the rocket.
2. That fire becomes the creature's body. The body rises up close behind the rocket, partly hidden by it.
3. Out of this fiery body come TWO LONG ARMS, thin like ribbons of fire.
4. Both arms wind TWO FULL TURNS AROUND THE ROCKET, spiraling upward from the bottom to the top, like the two snakes winding around the staff of a caduceus (but these are arms: no snakes, no wings). You see each arm pass in front of the rocket twice.
5. At the TOP of the rocket, just under the pointed tip, the two small hands grab the rocket.
6. The head sits right there, just above the hands, peeking over the tip.

DETAILS
- Head of a fire salamander: flat, broad, rounded snout, small black bulging eyes on top of the head, swollen glands behind the eyes. Calm, curious. Cute because it is small and clings to its rocket.
- Made of fire: flame edges, flame streaks, glitter specks, a few four-pointed twinkles. Gold-white at the head, hot pink in the middle, magenta where the fire leaves the rocket. No orange.
- Rocket: cardboard tube with horizontal pink, magenta and yellow rings, pointed pink tip. The bottom end is simply open, the fire comes straight out of it. No stick.

FORMAT
64x64, max 16 colors, 1 px dark outline, flat Gen 3 shading, no dithering, three-quarter view facing left, plain white background, no shadow. No firework explosions. No text, no 3D render.
```
