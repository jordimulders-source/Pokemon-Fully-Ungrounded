# Bokser-Mr. Mime – Gemini-startprompt r1 (22 nov, nieuw gesprek, geen bijlage)

```
Design a brand-new regional form of Mr. Mime: an old-time boxer, like the heavyweights in black-and-white boxing photos. Fighting type.
Draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): pixel art, 64×64, max 16 colors, dark selout outline, plain white background.
Draw ONE single Pokémon.

WHAT IT IS
A mime that became a boxer: shadowboxing is miming a fight against an invisible opponent.
The BODY SHAPE is completely different from Kanto Mr. Mime (just like Galarian Mr. Mime has its own body): not slim with thin limbs, but short and stocky, with a thick neck, heavy shoulders, a barrel chest and short, thick forearms.
On top of that body: Mr. Mime's head and face, with its red cheeks and blue hair spikes (slicked back), in the normal Mr. Mime skin tone.
It wears NO clothes: like Mr. Rime's tuxedo, its high-waisted dark old-fashioned boxing trunks are a pattern of its own body.
Hands: NO boxing gloves. Bare fists in Mr. Mime's white hands (or wrapped in tape).
NO pink balls anywhere.

POSE
The old-time crouch: low and hunched, chin tucked, shoulders up, the near fist cocked back for an overhand punch, the far fist guarding the face.

VIEW
Three-quarter view facing left, like every Gen 3 front sprite. Compact: fits inside a square.

DO NOT
No boxing gloves, no skirt, no tunic, no robe, no fabric.
No ground, no ring, no shadow, no glow, no effects, no motion lines. Only one Pokémon.
No plush/Neopets look, no chibi mascot, not a realistic human.
```
