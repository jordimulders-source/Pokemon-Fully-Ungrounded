# Gemini-prompt v5 (23 nov)

Create an original Pokémon as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style), 64×64 pixels, max 16 colors, clean dark outline, plain white background.

The Pokémon IS an ancient Inca temple that has come to life after centuries. It is not a creature wearing a temple, and it is not a creature standing next to one: the building itself is the whole body. Ghost/Rock type. It is huge and heavy, like Snorlax in weight and presence. The body FILLS the 64×64 frame almost edge to edge, in a compact, square composition.

It looks like a REAL Inca temple, as found at Machu Picchu or Pisac: a single-storey temple building standing on top of a few stone agricultural terraces (andenes). Make it interesting through big, readable shapes (not through small detail):
- The terraces: two or three LONG, LOW, narrow terrace walls that are much wider than the temple and gently CURVE around the hillside, like the agricultural terraces of Pisac and Machu Picchu. NO central staircase: at most a few small single stones sticking out of a terrace wall as steps.
- The temple on top: ONE storey only, a rectangular building with walls that lean inward toward the top, made of a few LARGE, irregular polygonal stone blocks fitted seamlessly together (like the Inca walls of Cuzco), not a regular brick grid. Plain flat-topped walls, no overhanging ledges or cornices.

Face: the front wall has a large Inca DOUBLE-JAMB TRAPEZOID doorway (a trapezoid door set inside a slightly bigger trapezoid frame, narrower at the top than at the bottom): this doorway is its mouth, dark inside, like an entrance you would walk into. Above it, two trapezoid niches/holes in the wall are its eyes: the eyes are light shining out from inside these holes in the stonework, the only sign that something lives inside. No other facial features. The idea: you think it is just the entrance to a temple, until you notice it is looking at you.

No limbs at all: no arms, no legs, no feet, no hands. Viewed in 3/4 view facing left (front wall and one side wall visible).

Colors: weathered grey/grey-brown andesite stone of Inca walls, darker in the joints and the doorway.

No glow, no aura, no light rays, no particles, no effects. No ground, no rock, no shadow under it.

AVOID: a Maya or Aztec pyramid with a central staircase, a temple on top of a stepped pyramid, a tower of stacked storeys, overhanging ledges or cornices, a ziggurat or fantasy temple, a single box with a door, a regular brick pattern, an igloo or hut shape, Aztec or Maya step pyramids, gold, carvings of gods or faces in the stone, a mummy or ghost figure inside the doorway, cute plush style, a person or creature in a costume, a realistic architectural drawing, lots of tiny detail.
