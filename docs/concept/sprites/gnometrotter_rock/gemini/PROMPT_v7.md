# Gemini-prompt: Gnometrotter (Rock-vorm), v7 (23 nov)

Nieuw gesprek, geen bijlage. r6 (prompt v6, vanaf nul geschreven): "muts is nu weg en hij is niet duidelijk van steen". v7 = prompt v5 (gaf r5: muts en steen goed) met alleen het idee "helemaal van stenen, kleurenpatronen behouden" erin: roze en blauwe delen worden steen met barsten en afgebrokkelde randen.

## Prompt

```
Design an original Pokémon and draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style).

CONCEPT
- Fairy/Rock type, the final stage of a three-stage evolution line. Its name is Gnometrotter: a gnome Pokémon that has walked so far for so long that its body has fully adapted to its environment. This is its Cave Form: after ages in caves, its whole body has turned to stone. A rock gnome: an old garden gnome that has become living stone, but every part keeps its original color.
- It must still clearly be the evolution of a cartoony garden-gnome Pokémon: a big round pink nose, a droopy gnome cap over the eyes, a round pink belly, a sky-blue lower half and rounded blue feet.

THE GNOME PART (keep)
- A big, perfectly ROUND, ball-shaped nose of pink stone, like a round pink pebble. Not oval, not long.
- A round, chubby body, like a ball, made of PINK STONE: the same pink as before, but clearly stone, with a few cracks running over it and a few chipped, angular edges on its outline. NOT muscular: no big biceps, no broad chest.
- The lower half of the body including the legs is SKY-BLUE STONE, like a garden gnome's trousers carved from blue stone, with a few cracks and chipped edges. NOT clothing.
- Feet: simple, rounded feet of sky-blue stone, a bit big. Under each foot is a thick, chunky slab of grey rock as a sole, clearly visible: noticeably thick, sticking out a little beyond the foot at the front and back, with a rough, chipped edge, in the same grey rock as the belt ring. The foot itself stays blue and round; only the sole is rock. No toes.
- Its free arm (the one without the stick) is a thin, short oval arm of pink stone ending in a round, chubby ball-shaped pink stone hand, with a crack or two.

THE ROCK PART
- Posture: hunched forward with age, taking a slow, heavy step, leaning on its stick. An old, seasoned hiker.
- Stick arm: one arm has turned to stone from the shoulder down, and its fist has grown into a long rock walking stick. Fist and stick are ONE single piece of rock: no hand gripping a stick, no fingers, no border between fist and stick; the stone forearm simply continues and grows down into the stick.
- Cap: the droopy pointed shape of a soft gnome cap (NOT tall, NOT straight up), made of dark rough rock; its tip hangs down behind and fuses with a rocky ridge growing out of its shoulders and back.
- The cap comes down low over the face: its lower edge overlaps and covers the top of the nose. The cap touches the nose directly, so there is NO dark band, gap, slit or shadow between cap and face, and nothing that could read as eyes.
- Belt section all stone: the whole belt section around the waist, between the pink belly and the blue lower half, is a thick ring of solid rock that grows out of the body. The belt itself is stone, buckle included. This rock ring connects to the rock ridge on its back: back and belt are one rock mass.
- Beard and hair are SOLID STONE, not hair: the mustache and beard are one heavy block of light grey rock with hard, angular, chipped edges and flat broken faces, like a beard carved out of stone, hanging from under the nose down to the belt ring. The hair tufts sticking out sideways from under the cap are also hard, angular chunks of light grey rock. NO soft strands, NO fluffy or wispy edges, NO fur. NO mouth visible.
- Everything is stone, so there is no skin: the pink stone, blue stone and grey rock parts are all one stone body, nothing is stuck on.
- Two kinds of rock: the cap, the back ridge, the belt ring and the stone arm are dark, angular grey rock. The beard and hair are a lighter grey rock.

STYLE AND FORMAT
- Official Gen 3 front sprite, 64x64, max 16 colors, 1 px dark outline, light from top left, flat Gen 3 shading with 3 to 4 tones per color, no dithering.
- Keep the rock readable at sprite size: big shapes and a few cracks, no fine texture or speckles.
- Three-quarter view facing left. A final-stage Pokémon, about 60 px tall, compact, centered in the frame.
- Plain white background. NO floor, shadow, loose rocks on the ground, cave background or effects. One single creature.
- Cartoony, but in the Pokémon way: bold shapes, a clear and cool silhouette, like an official Ken Sugimori design.

AVOID
- No visible eyes, no dark band under the cap, no visible mouth. No soft skin anywhere, no smooth plastic look. No soft or fluffy beard or hair. No flat cap or beret: it must be a long, droopy, pointed gnome cap. No leather belt. No muscles. No feet made entirely of stone, no toes. No hand holding a separate stick. No oval or long nose. No crystals, no glow. No pebbles stuck onto the skin. No human proportions, no little man in a costume. Not Golem, not Graveler, not Impidimp. No western-cartoon or plush look. No glossy 3D render.

```
