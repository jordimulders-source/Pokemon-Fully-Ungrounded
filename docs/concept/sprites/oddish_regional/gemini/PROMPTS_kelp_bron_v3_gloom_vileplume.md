Reparaties (verkleintest `kelp_r2/test_verkleind.png`): Gloom: bol weer in lobben met gele vlekken; kieren en kwijl verdwijnen in rood/zwart → feller en meer contrast. Vileplume: tentakels te fijn (rode ruis), krater verdwijnt → minder, dikkere tentakels, rand per anemoon, krater met contrast.

## Gloom
```
Design a regional form of the Pokémon Gloom and draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style pixel art) that stays readable at real 64x64 size.

CONCEPT
- Gloom from the dark deep sea near the Galápagos hydrothermal vents. Type: Water/Fire. It evolves from a sea-green Oddish with kelp on its head, after sinking into the deep.
- Face and pose of Gloom's Gen 3 front sprite: the squinting closed eyes, the wide open drooling mouth, the short legs.
- Body: soft and gelatinous like a deep-sea animal, sagging under the pressure, wider and rounder at the bottom. Instead of arms, two small fins held out to the sides.
- Body color: deep red / burgundy.
- On top: ONE single solid lump of black volcanic mineral rock, like a chunk of cooling lava: an irregular, asymmetric, craggy shape with a rough surface. It is NOT made of separate round lobes or bubbles, and has NO yellow spots. A few thick, jagged cracks run through it that glow bright orange-yellow, clearly brighter than the red body.
- A few wilted kelp blades, dull brown, hang limply down from under the rock on the left and right, broad and ruffled. Their tips are scorched black with a bright orange glowing edge.
- The drool is glowing bright orange-yellow vent water, and the inside of the open mouth glows bright orange like a furnace, clearly standing out against the red body.

POSE
- Three-quarter view facing left, the classic Gloom pose.

STYLE AND FORMAT
- 64x64 canvas, max 16 colors, 1 px dark outline, light from top left, flat Gen 3 shading, no dithering. Use bright orange-yellow only for the glowing parts, so they pop.
- Plain white background. No ground, water, bubbles, smoke or extra effects. One single creature, no extra views.

AVOID
- No round lobes, no bubbles, no segmented bud, no spots, no hair or dreadlocks, no arms, no green, no flower, no flames.
```

## Vileplume
```
Design a regional form of the Pokémon Vileplume and draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style pixel art) that stays readable at real 64x64 size.

CONCEPT
- Vileplume from the pitch-dark hydrothermal vents of the Galápagos Rift. Type: Water/Fire. Its line: a sea-green Oddish with kelp, a deep-red Gloom with a lava rock on its head, and now a living hot vent with sea anemones around it.
- Body: the small body under a large, wide top, like Vileplume, with short arms held out, standing on two long, thin stilt legs like the fin rays of a deep-sea tripod fish.
- Body color: ghostly white / pearly.
- On top: in the center a wide vent crater with a thick black rim, glowing bright orange-yellow inside, clearly visible between the anemones. Around it, in place of Vileplume's five petals, five big open sea anemones in a ring, in red and orange. Each anemone is a fleshy cushion with its own dark outline, topped by only 6 to 8 THICK, short, rounded tentacles pointing up and outward, like fat fingers, around a small central mouth. Big simple shapes, not fine detail.

POSE
- Three-quarter view facing left, standing on the two stilt legs.

STYLE AND FORMAT
- 64x64 canvas, max 16 colors, 1 px dark outline, light from top left, flat Gen 3 shading, no dithering.
- Plain white background. No ground, water, bubbles, smoke or extra effects. No fish, crabs or other creatures. One single creature, no extra views.

AVOID
- No thin or many tentacles, no fringe, no coral or cauliflower texture, no tentacles hanging down, no mushroom caps, no petals, no flower, no green, no grey body, no tall chimney, no flames.
```
