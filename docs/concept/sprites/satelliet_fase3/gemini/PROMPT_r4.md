# Ruimtestation (fase 3), Gemini-prompt r4 – NIEUW gesprek, geen bijlage
r3 (`r3a.png`, `r3b.png`), Jordi (25 nov): "allebei niet groot genoeg, het leest niet als een evolutie".
Diagnose Claude: beide zijn even groot en even complex als fase 2 (één bal + 2 panelen). Eerder besloten: "groter en complexer" (zoals Magneton) en fase 1 wordt "een steeds kleiner onderdeel van het geheel". r4: hoofdbal met oog is klein t.o.v. het geheel, een lange centrale balk met meerdere bollen/modules, vier panelen (2 paren), vult het hele vak. Ring weggelaten (Jordi: "te prominent"; als achtergronddetail gaf hij alleen dunne lijnen). Ogen op de andere bollen nog steeds niet genoemd. Aantallen (3 bollen, 4 panelen) = keuze Claude.

```
Design a Pokémon: an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style), 64×64 pixels, max 16 colors, clean dark outline, plain white background, no ground, no shadow, no effects or glow.

WHAT IT IS: a big living space station, the final evolution of a small one-eyed satellite. It is a pure machine with its own personality, the way Magneton, Metagross and Porygon are Pokémon: no animal parts, no arms, no legs, no mouth.

SIZE AND COMPLEXITY (most important): this is a LARGE, heavy, final-stage Pokémon, much bigger and more complex than a single satellite. It fills the WHOLE 64×64 frame from edge to edge. Its eye-sphere is only a SMALL part of the whole creature (about one fifth of its width).

POSE AND ANGLE: Gen 3 front-sprite 3/4 view facing the viewer's LEFT, seen slightly from above. Everything in this one perspective. Compact in a square.

BUILD: a long white central spine (a thick cylinder) runs diagonally through the creature. Along it, THREE round white spheres are built in, joined to the spine by purple ring-shaped docking collars; between them sit short white cylinder modules. Nothing floats loose: it is one solid connected machine.
THE EYE-SPHERE (front, lower left, the focus point): one big camera-lens eye: from outside to inside a thin dark line, a white ring, a purple ring, a dark ring, a turquoise iris and a dark pupil, flat colors. Two long purple antennae (not hair-thin) with a round purple ball on each tip rise from it and curve backward.

SOLAR PANELS: FOUR big flat rectangular solar panel wings in two pairs, attached with purple rods to the spine (one pair near the front, one pair near the back), dark navy-purple with a lighter purple grid of a few large cells. All in the same 3/4 perspective; the far ones smaller and partly hidden.

DETAIL LEVEL: it must translate well to a 64×64 sprite: big readable shapes, thick parts, medium detail, nothing thinner than the antennae. No rivets, no text, no logos, no tiny windows or greebles.

COLORS: pearly white with lavender shading, purple accents (collars, rods, antennae), dark navy-purple solar panels with lighter purple grid lines, turquoise iris as the only accent. Light from the upper left. Nothing else.

DO NOT: a single satellite with two panels, a small creature with empty space around it, a ring or wheel, thin orbit lines, starfield or galaxy patterns, stars or Earth around it, a flat front view or side view, realistic NASA/ISS hardware, a robot with a face screen, arms or legs, cute plush/Neopets style.
```
