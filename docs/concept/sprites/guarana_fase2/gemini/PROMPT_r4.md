# Guaraná fase 2 – startprompt r4 (26 nov), NIEUW gesprek, geen bijlage
Jordi over r3: "ok dan doet die niks, doe maar het idee van minder ogen in een volledig nieuwe prompt" (optie a: ±4–5 ogen per tros, ogen ±5–6 px). Verder alles uit r1 + de bijsturing van r2 (getwijnde liaan, echt guaraná-blad).

```
Design a new, original Pokémon.
Draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): pixel art, 64×64, max 16 colors, dark selout outline, plain white background.

WHAT IT IS
A Grass/Ghost Pokémon based on the guaraná plant from the Amazon.
Its body IS a creeping vine: a living green vine that crawls over the ground like an animal. It has no animal or human body parts at all; the ONLY animal-like features are its eyes. That is what makes it creepy.
The vine branches: from one thick main vine, three branches rise up, and each branch carries its own cluster of guaraná fruits, like several heads.

THE FRUITS AND EYES (must clearly read as guaraná, not a rose, raspberry or grapes)
Each cluster has only four or five fruits, so every fruit is big.
Every fruit has burst open. Each fruit is a round, smooth, glossy red capsule that has split open along its seam; the red skin peels back like two lips around what is inside.
Inside each open fruit sits one big eye: a white ball with a big black pupil, exactly like a real ripe guaraná fruit (black seed in white flesh). The red split skin frames every eye.
The eyes are big: each eye is about 5-6 pixels wide in the 64×64 sprite, so every eye clearly reads as a white eye with a black pupil at real sprite size. They all stare straight at the viewer.
Mood: unsettling. The eyes stare and follow you, but it is still a Pokémon you would want on your team. Not gory.

THE VINE (reads well as a sprite)
Smooth green vine, not woody, not brown. The main vine is two or three vine strands twisted around each other like a rope, as lianas do in the jungle. At the top the strands separate into the three branches that carry the fruit clusters. One clear body, not a tangle.
A few simple tendrils grip the ground like crawling limbs, close to the body. No decorative curls or spirals.
Leaves: real guaraná leaves, each a compound leaf of five long, pointed leaflets with lightly toothed edges. Only a few leaves, but big enough to read clearly at sprite size.
Compact: the whole Pokémon fits in a square shape and fills almost the whole 64×64 frame.

POSE
It crawls toward the viewer, the fruit clusters raised and turned to you, as if they all just noticed you.

VIEW
Three-quarter view facing left, like every Gen 3 front sprite.

DO NOT
No glow, no effects, no ghostly aura, no floating particles.
No ground, no shadow, no pot.
No mouth, no teeth, no blood, no face other than the eyes.
Not a human or child figure, not a snake, not a flower, not a bunch of grapes, not a tree.
No plush/Neopets look.
```
