# Nazca-astronaut – Gemini-startprompt (r1, nieuw gesprek, geen bijlage)

Besluiten: `../SPEC.md`. Jordi: Cosmic/Normal, counterpart van Sigilyph, "de manier waarop Sigilyph ontworpen is mag lijken op het astronaut ontwerp", "een echt wezen".
Voorstellen Claude (niet van Jordi): pose van de geoglief (een arm omhoog, een arm omlaag); Sigilyph's ontwerptaal en palet in woorden; staand in plaats van zwevend; geen bijlage (GEMINI.md: geen extra referenties).

```
Design a new Pokémon based on "El Astronauta" (also called the Owlman), the Nazca geoglyph on a hillside in Peru: a figure with a big round head with two huge round eyes, one arm raised and one arm pointing down, and short legs with feet.
Its type is Cosmic/Normal. It is the counterpart of the Pokémon SIGILYPH (which is based on the Nazca bird geoglyphs): use the same design language as Sigilyph, but it must be its own creature, not Sigilyph.
It is a real living creature, not a flat line drawing on the ground.
Draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): pixel art, 64×64, max 16 colors, dark selout outline, plain white background.

WHAT IT IS
A small humanoid figure with the silhouette of the geoglyph: a very big round head (owl-like) with two huge round eyes, a short body, two arms and two short legs with feet. 2 arms in total, 2 legs in total.

DESIGN LANGUAGE (like Sigilyph)
Black as the main body color, with bright accents in yellow, red, blue and green; geometric, ancient-looking patterns (zigzag bands, eye-like spots) on the body; stylized and symmetrical-feeling, like a Nazca/Paracas artwork that came to life.

POSE
The pose of the geoglyph: one arm raised high (like waving or pointing at the sky), the other arm pointing down at the ground. It stands on its feet.

VIEW
Three-quarter view facing left, like every Gen 3 front sprite. Compact, limbs close to the body, it must fit a square.

STYLE
Stylized like an official Pokémon design.

DO NOT
No ground, no lines on the ground, no hill, no shadow under it.
No glow, no stars, no effects.
Not a human in a space suit, no helmet, not a robot.
Not Sigilyph with arms: a different creature in the same style.
No plush/Neopets look.
```
