```
Design a new regional form of the Pokémon BRAVIARY, based on the HARPY EAGLE of the Amazon rainforest. Its type is Steel/Flying.
Draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): pixel art, 64×64, max 16 colors, dark selout outline, plain white background.

WHAT IT IS
Anatomy of a real harpy eagle: a big, heavy, powerful eagle with a large rounded head, a hooked beak, and the harpy's double crest on top of its head. The crest is more stylized than on the real bird: on a real harpy eagle the crest feathers are a bit scattered; here they are not. Pale grey head, a dark band across the upper chest, white belly, dark slate-grey back and wings, barred legs.
Its Steel typing is ONLY in its talons and its beak: the huge talons and the hooked beak are hard as steel (the texture and sheen of metal in the talons and beak themselves, still part of the bird's body). No metal anywhere else, no armor plates.
It is a Braviary form: same art style and same proud, heroic attitude as Braviary, but the bird is clearly a harpy eagle.

POSE
It sits upright, perched, calm and imposing, with its double crest raised high. The huge steel talons are clearly visible in front at the bottom. 2 wings in total (near and far), folded against the body.

VIEW
Three-quarter view facing left, like every Gen 3 front sprite: the far wing and far foot are on the left (narrower, partly hidden), the near ones on the right. The head turns a bit more toward the viewer than the body.
Compact: the whole Pokémon fits inside a square, limbs close to the body.

DO NOT
No branch, no perch, no ground, no shadow under it.
No armor plates, no metal on the body or wings (only talons and beak), no glow, no effects.
No plush/Neopets look, no chibi mascot, not a realistic photo-like bird.
```
