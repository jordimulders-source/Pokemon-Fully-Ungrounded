# Ruimtestation (fase 3), Gemini-prompt r6 – NIEUW gesprek, geen bijlage
r5 (`r5.png`), Jordi: "ok dit is qua vorm prima". Daarna: "antennes weg, ogen op alle vier, schrijf r6, het oog kleiner is niet erg, maar dan moeten we gewoon zorgen dat we slim designen, dus met wat minder detail dan fase 2 bijv".
Basis = prompt r5, alleen gewijzigd: antennes weg; vier even grote bollen met elk één oog aan het eind van de vier armen (achterste: oog niet zichtbaar); oog eenvoudiger (paarse ring, turquoise iris, donkere pupil; geen extra ringen/glans) = keuze Claude bij "minder detail dan fase 2"; Jordi: "iris en pupil blijven wel". Paneelraster ±2×3 vakken.

```
Design a Pokémon: an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style), 64×64 pixels, max 16 colors, clean dark outline, plain white background, no ground, no shadow, no effects or glow.

WHAT IT IS: a big living space station, the final evolution of a small one-eyed satellite. It is a pure machine with its own personality, the way Magneton, Metagross and Porygon are Pokémon: no animal parts, no arms, no legs, no mouth.

SIZE AND COMPLEXITY (most important): this is a LARGE, heavy, final-stage Pokémon, clearly more complex than a single satellite or a straight line of modules. It fills the WHOLE 64×64 frame from edge to edge. Each eye-sphere is only a small part of the whole creature.

POSE AND ANGLE: Gen 3 front-sprite 3/4 view facing the viewer's LEFT, seen slightly from above. Everything in this one perspective. Compact in a square.

BUILD (a cross-shaped station): in the middle is a round white node sphere. From it, white cylinder modules branch out in FOUR directions like a cross: a long main arm toward the front-left, a long arm toward the back-right, and two shorter side arms. The modules have different sizes (some wide and short, some long and slim) and each has one or two dark purple bands around it. All joints are purple ring-shaped docking collars. Nothing floats loose: it is one solid connected machine.
FOUR EYE-SPHERES: at the end of EACH of the four arms sits a round white sphere of the same size, each with one eye pointing outward along its arm, like four satellites joined into one station. So we see the eye of the front-left sphere from the front, the eyes of the two side spheres at an angle, and the back-right sphere only from behind (its eye is hidden). Each eye is SIMPLE so it reads at small size, but always with a turquoise iris and a dark pupil: one purple ring, a turquoise iris and a dark pupil, flat colors, no extra rings, no shine.
NO ANTENNAE anywhere.

SOLAR PANELS: THREE pairs of long, narrow, flat rectangular solar panel wings sticking out at right angles from the arms (one pair near the front, one pair at the node, one pair at the back), dark navy-purple with a lighter purple grid of only a few large cells (about 2 by 3). Long like on a real space station, but each panel still clearly wide (never thin strips). All in the same 3/4 perspective; the far ones smaller and partly hidden.

DETAIL LEVEL: it must translate well to a 64×64 sprite: big readable shapes, thick parts, medium detail, no thin parts (every part at least a few pixels wide). No rivets, no text, no logos, no flags, no tiny windows, dishes or greebles.

COLORS: pearly white with lavender shading, purple accents (collars, bands, rods, eye rings), dark navy-purple solar panels with lighter purple grid lines, turquoise iris as the only accent. Light from the upper left. Nothing else.

DO NOT: antennae, more or fewer than four eye-spheres, eyes with many rings, a single satellite with two panels, one straight line of modules, a small creature with empty space around it, a ring or wheel, thin orbit lines, starfield or galaxy patterns, stars or Earth around it, a space shuttle, a flat front view or side view, realistic NASA/ISS hardware, a robot with a face screen, arms or legs, cute plush/Neopets style.
```
