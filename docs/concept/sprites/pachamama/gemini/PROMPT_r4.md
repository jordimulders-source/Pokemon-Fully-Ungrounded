# Gemini-prompt Pachamama r4 (nieuw gesprek, geen bijlage)

```
Design a new legendary Pokémon as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): pixel art, 64x64, max 16 colors, dark outline, light from top-left, plain white background, no ground, no shadow, no effects.

WHAT IT IS: Mother Earth as a living legendary, Ground/Fairy type. A mountain that is also a mother wrapped in a wide mantle. Not a golem, not a robot, not a doll, not a human: a gentle, serene, sacred creature, like a worshipped idol that is alive.

BODY: a tall mountain silhouette: pointed snowy summit, slopes widening down like a long mantle or cloak that reaches the bottom of the sprite, with a few large rock planes (not many cracks). The summit is the head, the snow covers it like a hood. Two mountain ridges come forward from the sides of the mantle as arms, held out gently like a mother; the arms are part of the mantle and end in rounded rock, with NO hands, no fingers, no fists. No shoulders or muscles.

FACE: a simple Pokémon face set in the rock under the snow hood: two large calm eyes and a tiny mouth, smooth and stylized like a carved idol. Beautiful and gentle, not a realistic human face: no nose detail, no beard, no wrinkles, no eyebrows.

COLORS: the colors of Rainbow Mountain (Vinicunca, Peru): 3-4 broad bands of red/terracotta, pink, ochre yellow and turquoise green that follow the slopes like rock layers. White snow on the summit.

POSE: 3/4 view facing left, upright like a legendary.

DO NOT: golem, robot, hands, fists, fingers, muscles, realistic human face, beard, soft blob, cloth, doll, many small cracks, thin stripes, plants, animals, offerings, gems, glowing, background.
```
