# Gemini-prompt: wondertol, v4 (16 nov)

Nieuw gesprek, geen eerdere versies als bijlage. Bijlagen: (1) foto vuursalamander (kop en poten), (2) **foto of filmstill van een brandende wondertol** (draaiende vonkenwerveling); als Jordi een eigen referentie heeft, die gebruiken (bij fase 3 werkte Jordi's referentie beter dan tekst).

## Jordi over v3 (`r3/gemini_v3.png`, 16 nov)
"het is nog steeds geen wondertol"
(Claude: Gemini maakte van de vuurbal een dichte bol met vlekken = schild; het dier leest als schildpad. Een brandende wondertol is een werveling: fel midden, gebogen armen van vuur en vonksporen in één draairichting.)

## Kleur (Jordi, 16 nov)
"en hoe meer ik zie hoe minder ik overtuigd raak van het groen"; uit A (blauw/turkoois zonder groen), B (fase 3-kleuren goud/roze/magenta met blauw/turkoois accent), C: **"B"**. Waar het turkoois zit (punten van de armen, vonksporen) = keuze Claude.

## Wijzigingen t.o.v. v3 (keuzes Claude, schrappen mag)

## Prompt

```
Design an original Pokémon and draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style).

CONCEPT
- Fire/Fairy type, the middle stage of a firework evolution line: a SALAMANDER WHOSE BODY IS A SPINNING GROUND-SPINNER FIREWORK, A WHIRLING BALL OF FIRE.

- THE BODY IS THE FIREWORK WHILE IT SPINS: not a solid ball, not a shell. It is a WHIRL OF FIRE seen at the moment of spinning: a small white-hot core, and around it several thick, curved arms of flame and spark trails that all sweep in the same direction, like a pinwheel of fire, together forming a round spinning disc of light. The edges of the whirl are ragged with flame tongues. There is no separate body behind or under it: the whirl IS the body.
- THE HEAD sticks out of the front of the whirl: a FLAT, WIDE salamander head, held low and forward, with small eyes sitting on top of the head and a wide mouth line. The head is made of the same fire as the whirl; only the eyes and the mouth are drawn into the flame.
- FOUR SHORT SALAMANDER LEGS made of the same fire stick out sideways from the whirl, with small splayed toes. They are legs, not arms.
- The tail is one of the flame arms of the whirl.
- Short solid spark trails fly off the ends of the flame arms, continuing the spin.
- FIREWORK COLORS, NOT CAMPFIRE COLORS: vivid, saturated firework colors: gold-white near the core, hot pink and magenta in the flame arms, deep crimson at the outer edges, with ONE ACCENT of turquoise blue (for example the tips of the flame arms and the spark trails). Gold-yellow spots like a fire salamander on the head. No green.
- FAIRY: playful and mischievous expression.

STYLE AND FORMAT
- Official Gen 3 front sprite, 64x64, max 16 colors, 1 px dark outline, light from top left, flat Gen 3 shading with 3 to 4 tones per color, no dithering.
- Solid shapes with clean outlines that read as a clear silhouette at 64x64; the round spinning whirl must be obvious in the silhouette.
- Three-quarter view facing left. Compact composition that fits a square.
- Plain white background. NO floor, shadow, smoke or motion lines. One single creature.

AVOID
- NOT CHARMANDER: no big round head, no big cute eyes, no upright standing body, no belly, no arms, no flame on the tail tip. No dinosaur or dragon look, no horns or spikes.
- NOT A SHELL, NOT A TURTLE, NOT A SOLID BALL WITH PATCHES. No cone, no separate top, disc or object; it does not lie or sit on anything. No smooth skin. No coiled body, no snake shape. No glitter, no sparkle specks, no twinkling stars. No orange. No firework explosions or burst shapes. No rocket, no stick. Not Wooper, not Hitmontop. No hearts. No text. No glossy 3D render.
```
