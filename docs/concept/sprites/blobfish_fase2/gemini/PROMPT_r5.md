## Prompt

```
Design a new Pokémon and draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): 64x64 pixel art, at most 16 colours, light from the top left, plain white background, no shadow, no glow, no effects outside the creature.

The Pokémon is based on the BLOBFISH (Psychrolutes marcidus), see the attached photo. It is a large FINAL EVOLUTION: the deep-sea fish after it has been pulled up to the surface and has collapsed. It is BIG and heavy: the sprite fills almost the full width of the 64x64 frame and most of its height.

BODY: shaped like a big PUDDING turned out of its mould and MELTING under its own weight: a ROUND, smooth dome on top (not flat, no edge), and the lower half spreading out wide into a melted, puddle-like base with thick drooping folds. Small limp fins at the sides, a short limp tail at the back.
FACE: on the front of the dome. Small, round, dark beady FISH eyes (no eyelids, no eye whites, nothing human). A big, heavy, drooping blobfish nose hanging far down over the mouth. A very grumpy mouth pulled hard down at the corners. Exaggerated like a caricature.
COLOUR: pale pink to greyish pink of a real blobfish out of water, slightly glossy-wet, darker pink in the folds and the melted base.

POSE: three-quarter view facing LEFT, slumped on itself, centred.

AVOID: a small creature with lots of empty space around it, a flat top, human eyes or eyelids, a cute plush, chibi or Neopets look, a happy face, a plate, a spoon, dessert decorations, water or bubbles, arms or legs, a glossy 3D render.
```

## Tips
- Nog te klein: in hetzelfde gesprek één keer "make it much bigger, it must fill almost the whole 64x64 frame".
