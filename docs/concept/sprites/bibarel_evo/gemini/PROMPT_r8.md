Jordi na r7: "en hij lijkt niet voldoende een bibarel evo" → toelichting: "nee ik bedoel dat het geen fase 3 is nog genoeg"; "het is geen potlood" (r6/r7; r4 wel). Methode: "wat werkt het beste?" → Claude koos: nieuwe startprompt met alle besluiten en het belangrijkste blok bovenaan (vuurwerkslang v14, Dundragoon), daarna wat nog mist samenstellen uit de rondes (mate-kano). Keuzes die blijven: lijf/pose r4, kop r6 zonder lijn onder de rand.
Claude (niet van Jordi): fase 3 vertaald naar concrete kenmerken van Gen 3-eindvormen (vult het hele vak, brede stand, zware romp, dikke nek, kleine kop, ouder gezicht), zonder Pokémon bij naam.

```
Design a brand-new Pokémon. Type Water/Fighting.

FINAL EVOLUTION (most important)
This is the THIRD and LAST stage of a beaver line: the big boss of the beaver construction crew, much bigger and heavier than any normal beaver. It must look like a final evolution, the way Gen 3 final evolutions do:
- it FILLS the whole 64×64 frame,
- a wide, planted stance on thick legs,
- a massive, heavy barrel torso and a thick neck,
- the head is SMALL compared to the huge body,
- an older, serious, determined face (heavy brow), not cute, not round-cheeked,
- thick strong arms, a bit buff, but simple shapes: no detailed muscle definition, no bodybuilder look.

STYLE
An official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): pixel art, 64×64, max 16 colors, dark selout outline, plain white background.

WHAT IT IS
A hard-working builder beaver, a busy boss, working very hard like real beavers do. The construction-worker idea is shown ONLY through the shapes of its fur markings and what it is doing. It wears NOTHING.
Animal anatomy: a real BEAVER, standing upright on its hind legs.
Warm brown fur, dark brown muzzle with a small red nose.
EYES: big, round, white eyes with small black pupils.
TEETH: two HUGE white front teeth, sticking far out of the mouth.
HEAD = HELMET: the head itself is shaped like a hard hat: the top of the skull is a rounded dome with a small ridge of fur over the eyes. Face and dome are ONE continuous piece of fur: NO outline, seam or gap between them. Pale cream fur patches around the eyes continue up over the dome.
OVERALLS = FUR MARKING: the pale cream fur under its chin and on its chest has the shape of bib overalls: a square bib with two cream fur straps over the shoulders. Only a fur pattern: no seams, no pockets, no buttons.
TAIL: a big, broad, flat paddle tail colored like a TRAFFIC CONE: orange with two BROAD white bands.

POSE
Busy at work, mid-stride, determined and in a hurry, carrying ONE heavy log on its shoulder.
THE LOG: one end is gnawed into a long cone ending in a sharp point, EXACTLY like a sharpened pencil: the cone is light bare wood, the rest of the log has dark bark.

VIEW
Three-quarter view facing left: the far arm and leg on the left (partly hidden), the near ones on the right. Compact: everything including the log fits inside the square.

DO NOT
Not a small, chubby, cute beaver; not a first or middle stage. Not a big head on a small body.
No clothing or accessories: no hat, no helmet placed on the head, no fur cap, no cloth overalls, no belt, no gloves, no tools.
No bald smooth head: the dome has fur and the cream patches.
No ground, no water, no shadow. No glow, no effects, no motion lines, no sweat drops.
No small details (wood chips, twigs, rocks). No plush/Neopets look, no chibi, not a realistic painting.
```
