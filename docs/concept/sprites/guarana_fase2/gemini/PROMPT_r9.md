# Guaraná fase 2 – startprompt r9 (26 nov), NIEUW gesprek, geen bijlage
Jordi over r8: "nieuwe prompt dan maar echt duidelijker beschrijven, anders zijn we uren bezig". Nieuw t.o.v. r8: trossen overbuigend/hangend en losser, vrucht peervormig/driezijdig met puntje (echte guaraná; hangen = Claudes geheugen), opbouw stap voor stap beschreven. Eigen invulling van Claude: ±6–8 vruchten per tros, waarvan ±2 dicht; takken buigen over als een herdersstaf.

```
Design a new, original Pokémon.
Draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): pixel art, 64×64, max 16 colors, dark selout outline, plain white background.

WHAT IT IS
A Grass/Ghost Pokémon based on the guaraná plant from the Amazon. It is a living vine that crawls over the ground like an animal. It has no animal or human body parts at all; the ONLY animal-like features are its eyes, and those eyes are its fruits. That is what makes it creepy.

HOW IT IS BUILT (follow this exactly, from bottom to top)
1. BODY: one thick green vine lying low on the ground, made of two or three strands twisted around each other like a rope. A few simple thin tendrils grip the ground like crawling limbs, close to the body. No claws, no hands, no feet, no curls.
2. BRANCHES: three branches grow up out of the body. Each branch rises and then bends over at the top, like a shepherd's crook or a fountain, so its tip points down.
3. FRUIT BUNCHES: from each bent-over tip hangs one bunch of guaraná fruits. The bunch hangs down from the tip, it does not stand up.
   - A bunch is a short central stalk with about six to eight fruits attached to it by very short little stalks, all around and along it.
   - The bunch is loose: every single fruit is clearly separate and fully visible, with a little space between them. It is NOT a tight ball, NOT an upright spike or corn cob, NOT a bunch of grapes.
   - The three hanging bunches fill the space between and beside the branches, so the Pokémon looks full, not bare.
4. LEAVES: a few big real guaraná leaves on the branches. Each leaf is a compound leaf of five long, pointed leaflets with lightly toothed edges. Big enough to read clearly at sprite size.

THE FRUITS AND EYES
- Each fruit has the real guaraná shape: pear-shaped and slightly three-sided, with a short pointed tip. NOT a round berry, NOT a cherry.
- Color: glossy red to orange-red.
- Most fruits in each bunch have split open: the red skin is peeled open around one big eye, like eyelids. The eye is a white ball with a big black pupil, exactly like a real ripe guaraná (black seed in white flesh).
- About two fruits in each bunch are still closed: red, with a seam line, no eye.
- The eyes are big: each eye is about 5-6 pixels wide in the 64×64 sprite, so it clearly reads as a white eye with a black pupil at real sprite size.
- Even though the bunches hang down, every eye looks straight at the viewer.
Mood: unsettling. The eyes stare and follow you, but it is still a Pokémon you would want on your team. Not gory.

COMPOSITION
Compact: the whole Pokémon fits in a square and fills almost the whole 64×64 frame.
Three-quarter view facing left, like every Gen 3 front sprite. It crawls toward the viewer.

DO NOT
No glow, no effects, no ghostly aura, no floating particles.
No ground, no shadow, no pot.
No mouth, no teeth, no blood, no face other than the eyes.
Not a human or child figure, not a snake, not a flower, not a tree.
No plush/Neopets look.
```
