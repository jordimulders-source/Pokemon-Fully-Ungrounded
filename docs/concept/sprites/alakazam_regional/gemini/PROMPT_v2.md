## Prompt

```
Design a regional form of Alakazam and draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style pixel art).

CONCEPT
A Dark/Psychic Alakazam that is a stage illusionist and a swindler: very skilled, but the moment you see it you feel something is not right. Sneaky and untrustworthy, but NOT an over-the-top cartoon villain. Inspired by Alakazam (see the attachment) and recognizable as its regional form, but it may differ more from Alakazam in shape and details.

POSE (most important)
- Turned almost sideways, close to a side view: its chest, face and front point to the RIGHT edge of the image. We see its left side and part of its back. It does NOT face the viewer.
- It has exactly two arms. The far hand is held out in front of it, turned toward the viewer, showing its card nonchalantly, casual and relaxed. The near hand is held behind its back (on the left side of the image), and the viewer can see that this hidden hand holds the same card too.

FACE
- A sneaky look: one eye more open, the other eye half closed.
- ONE long mustache, one strand on each side of the snout, nothing more. The tips curl upward on their own into a twirl, but its hands do NOT touch the mustache.

COLORS
- Black, white and grey, with a red bow tie marking at the collar, part of its body like Rowlet's bow tie.
- [HEAD]
- A tuxedo made from its own body, subtle and elegant: a white chest like a dress shirt, black shoulders and arms like a jacket.

OBJECTS
- In each hand one playing card, and both cards are exactly the same card (the same suit and the same value), which a real deck never has. Big, simple card faces that stay readable at 64x64.

STYLE AND FORMAT
- 64x64 pixel sprite, max 16 colors, 1 px dark outline, light from top left, clean shading like official Game Freak Gen 3 sprites.
- Plain white background. NO floor, rock, shadow, base, motion lines, glow, sparkles or effects. One single creature, no extra views.

AVOID
- No vampire or Dracula look: no high pointed collar, no cape. No top hat, no cloth clothing, no gloves.
- Not facing the viewer, no front view.
- No double mustache.
- Not a human magician, not a person in a costume. No plush toy, chibi or mascot look.
- No extra objects: exactly one card in each hand, nothing else.
```

## Tips
- Draait hij nog te veel naar voren: een posebron (foto/3D-model van iemand in zijaanzicht naar rechts met een hand op de rug) als tweede bijlage werkt beter dan een stickman (GEMINI.md, Hitmongroove).
- Lukt naar rechts niet: dezelfde pose naar links vragen en het plaatje spiegelen.
