# Mr. Prime – startprompt r3 (24 nov, NIEUW gesprek, GEEN bijlage)
Jordi bij r2: "zelfde feedback als eerder". Mr. Rime-bouw in woorden; naam Mr. Mime weg; geen kleding; stok in een wering.

```
Design a brand-new Pokémon: an old English gentleman who fights with his walking cane (bartitsu, the Victorian gentleman's martial art). Fighting/Psychic type. It is a fully evolved Pokémon in the style of Mr. Rime.
Draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): pixel art, 64×64, max 16 colors, dark selout outline, Gen 3 shading with 3–4 tones per color, plain white background.
Draw ONE single Pokémon. It is a Pokémon, NOT a human in clothes.

BODY (built like Mr. Rime)
- A big, round, egg-shaped torso.
- Very long, thin, straight legs ending in small shoes. No trousers.
- Thin arms with white hands.
- A white mime face with a round head; a tall black top hat that is part of its head, like Mr. Rime's hat.
- HAIR: two dark hair spikes slicked flat backwards, lying against the back of the head and pointing backward. They do NOT stick out to the left and right of the head.
- A dark moustache, a bit bigger than a very small pencil moustache.
- A MONOCLE over one eye.
- The gentleman's suit is NOT clothing: no jacket, no tailcoat, no tie, no trousers. It is only a black-and-white color pattern on its own round torso, like Mr. Rime's belly pattern.

COLORS
Black-and-white palette, like an old photo and like a classic mime: white, greys and black.

POSE (most important)
- Standing perfectly upright, straight as a rod: back straight, chin up, legs straight and close together, feet almost touching.
- Fighting guard with the cane: one hand holds the cane by its end, raised to head height, the cane pointing up and forward to the left like a fencer's blade. The other hand is open in front of the chest. The cane does NOT touch the ground and is NOT held across the body.
- COMPACT and NOT WIDE: elbows close to the body.

VIEW
Three-quarter view facing left, like every Gen 3 front sprite. Fits a square.

DO NOT
No ground, no shadow, no glow, no effects, no motion lines.
No cartoon line art, no plush/Neopets look, no chibi mascot, not a realistic human.
```
