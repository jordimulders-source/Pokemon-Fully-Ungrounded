# Gemini-prompt Pachamama r2 (nieuw gesprek, geen bijlage)

```
Design a new legendary Pokémon as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): pixel art, 64x64, max 16 colors, dark outline, light from top-left, plain white background, no ground, no shadow, no effects. Keep it SIMPLE: big clean shapes and flat color areas, very little texture, like a real Gen 3 legendary sprite.

WHAT IT IS: Mother Earth as a living legendary, Ground/Fairy type. A living mountain shaped like a mother wrapped in a wide mantle. It is alive and has eyes, but it is NOT a human: it looks like an idol or a sacred effigy, a stylized carved figure that people worship, more abstract than a person.

BODY: the whole body is one big mountain whose shape is a mantle/cloak, wide at the bottom and rising to a summit. The summit is the head, with a cap of snow like a hood. In the head: a stylized idol face with large open eyes, simple and abstract, not a realistic human face. Two mountain ridges come forward from the sides of the mantle as arms, like a mother holding out her arms. Bold silhouette that fills the square.

COLORS: the colors of Rainbow Mountain (Vinicunca, Peru): the mantle is painted in 3-4 BROAD smooth bands of red/terracotta, pink, ochre yellow and turquoise green, following the slope of the mountain. White snow cap. No brown or grey as main color.

POSE: 3/4 view facing left, upright and imposing like a legendary.

DO NOT: realistic human face, beard, cracks, rock texture, thin stripes, strata lines, plants, grass tufts, snow on the arms, villages, fields, paths, animals, offerings, gems, glowing, background.
```
