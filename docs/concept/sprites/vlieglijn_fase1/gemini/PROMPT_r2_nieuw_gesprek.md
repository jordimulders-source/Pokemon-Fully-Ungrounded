# Vlieglijn fase 1 (fruitvlieg) – r2, nieuw gesprek zonder bijlage (20 nov)

Jordi over r1: "vleugels plat naar achteren en amber kleur, laten we de ogen paars maken. En we doen nog een feature waardoor het meer op een pokemon lijkt, de poten komen schokken vanaf, dus die maken we iets meer zoals stroomkabels"

```
Design a new Pokémon: the first stage of a three-stage line (fruit fly → house fly → mosquito).
Draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): pixel art, 64×64, max 16 colors, dark selout outline, plain white background.

WHAT IT IS
A fruit fly (Drosophila melanogaster), standing low on its legs like the real insect. Anatomy of the real insect: small rounded body; large compound eyes; thorax; abdomen, darker toward the rear; two clear wings (2 wings in total, near and far); six thin legs; short antennae with a feathery bristle (arista).
Colors: the body is amber. The eyes are PURPLE.
Wings: lying flat and folded backwards over its back, like a resting fruit fly, reaching past the end of the abdomen. They do NOT stand up.
Pokémon feature: its legs give off electric shocks, so its six legs look a bit more like electric power cables.
It is a small, early Pokémon: the base stage of its line.

VIEW
Three-quarter view facing left, like every Gen 3 front sprite: the far wing and far legs on the left (narrower, partly hidden), the near ones on the right.
Compact: the whole Pokémon fits inside a square, limbs close to the body.

DO NOT
No sparks, no glow, no electricity effects, no motion lines.
No fruit, no ground, no shadow under it.
No clothing or accessories. No plush/Neopets look, no chibi mascot.
Do not make it look like Beedrill or any bee or wasp: no yellow-and-black stripes, no stinger, no upright wings. Also not Yanma, Venonat, Ninjask or Combee.
```
