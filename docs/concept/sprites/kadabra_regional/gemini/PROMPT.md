## Prompt

```
Design a regional form of Kadabra and draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style pixel art).

The attached image is its evolution: the regional form of Alakazam, a sneaky card-swindler illusionist (Dark/Psychic). Use the attachment ONLY for the design language: the colors (grey body with a light violet sheen in the shadows, black shoulder plates, black back and sides, grey upper arms and black forearms, white chest plate, a small red dewlap under the chin instead of a bow tie, playing cards with black spades) and the style. Do NOT copy its pose or its body: this is Kadabra, with Kadabra's own anatomy (see the original Kadabra: its head shape, its ears, its short mustache, its tail).

CONCEPT
- The pre-evolution: a charlatan's apprentice. It is a card swindler too, but not yet very experienced; it is still learning the trade.
- A red heart mark on its forehead, in the place of the star of the original Kadabra.
- It holds exactly ONE playing card (Alakazam holds two identical cards; Kadabra holds one, like the original Kadabra holds one spoon).

POSE
- An "is this your card?" pose: it holds the one card up toward the viewer, showing its face.
- [FACING: Three-quarter view facing LEFT, like a normal front sprite. / Three-quarter view, turned partly sideways with its front toward the RIGHT side of the image, like its evolution.]

STYLE AND FORMAT
- 64x64 pixel sprite, max 16 colors, 1 px dark outline, light from top left, clean shading like official Game Freak Gen 3 sprites.
- Plain white background. NO floor, rock, shadow, base, motion lines, glow, sparkles or effects. One single creature, no extra views.

AVOID
- No clothing: no jacket, cape, sleeves, top hat or gloves; the tuxedo colors are its own body.
- No bow tie as a separate object; no Dracula collar.
- Not a copy of the attached Alakazam; not a human magician. No plush toy, chibi or mascot look.
- Exactly one card, nothing else in its hands.
```
