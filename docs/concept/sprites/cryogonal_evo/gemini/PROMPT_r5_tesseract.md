# Gemini-prompt r5: tesseract van Cryogonals stukken (25 nov)

Jordi: "maak dan eens een goede prompt". Analyse r2–r4: (1) Gemini tekent bij "edge/bar" altijd gladde balken: elke ribbe moet expliciet een rij korte V-platen zijn; (2) bij "Cryogonal" + "inner cube" zet hij een losse Cryogonal in een kooi: de binnenste kubus ís nu het geslepen gezichtskristal, vast verbonden met 8 armen (zoals Cryogonals armen uit zijn kern komen); (3) "cage/box" expliciet verboden; (4) beschrijving begint met het silhouet, niet met de naam.

Eigen invulling van Claude (niet door Jordi gezegd): binnenste kubus = massief gezichtskristal; elke ribbe = 2–3 V-platen achter elkaar.

```
Draw an original Pokémon as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): 64x64 pixels, max 16 colors, thick black outline, crisp faceted cel shading, plain white background. Compact. No ground, no shadow, no glow, no particles, no effects.

WHAT IT LOOKS LIKE (most important): a floating geometric crystal creature with the silhouette of a tesseract (the "cube inside a cube" figure), but built ENTIRELY out of small, flat, faceted crystal plates. There are NO smooth bars or beams anywhere.

THE BUILDING BLOCK: one short, wide, flat crystal plate with a forked V-notch at both ends (like a chevron or a swallowtail), cut with sharp facets: one bright white facet, one mid blue facet, one dark blue facet. These are exactly the plates that make up the arms of the Pokémon Cryogonal; this Pokémon is Cryogonal's evolution, whose plates have rebuilt themselves into a bigger 3D structure.

HOW THE BLOCKS ARE ARRANGED:
- The 12 edges of the big outer cube are each a row of 2 or 3 of these V-plates, nested tip-into-notch like a chain of arrowheads. You can clearly see the zigzag V-joints along every edge.
- At each of the 8 outer corners, the plate tips meet and interlock in a sharp crystal knot.
- At the center is a SOLID faceted crystal cube, gem-cut, which is the core and carries the face. It is not a separate creature: it is part of the structure.
- 8 arms, each a row of V-plates, run diagonally from the corners of the core cube to the corners of the outer cube, like a crystal radiating its arms outward.
- Open spaces between the plates: you see through the structure.
- 3/4 view facing left, so the 3D depth reads clearly.

FACE (on the front of the core cube): frozen and aggressive; a dark horizontal slit across the upper part with two light cyan eyes in it, and a dark angular mouth below. Large enough to read at 64x64.

COLORS: deep periwinkle and royal blue, white highlights on the top facets, light cyan only in the eyes and a few facet edges, black outline. Saturated and cold, not pale.

DO NOT: draw a cage, a box or a frame with something inside it; draw smooth or round bars; draw a complete snowflake anywhere; draw an existing Pokémon sitting inside the structure; add limbs, a crown or a cape; add stars, space patterns, snow particles or glow.
```
