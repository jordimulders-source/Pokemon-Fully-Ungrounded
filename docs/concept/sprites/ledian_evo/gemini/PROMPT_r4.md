# Ledian-evolutie – Gemini-prompt r4 (27 nov)

Op r3 (`r3.png`), Jordi: "nee nog niet goed genoeg, het mag echt nog meer space robot". Gemini veranderde met de bijlage alleen de rug; daarom nieuwe startprompt zonder bijlage met alle besluiten.

```
Design an original Pokémon: the evolution of Ledian (Gen 2). Draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): 64x64 pixels, at most 16 colors, dark outline, white background, no ground, no shadow, no glow, no energy or special effects.

What it is: a space robot superhero, a tokusatsu hero version of Ledian. Bug type and Cosmic type. It must look much more like a space robot than a bug, while still clearly recognisable as Ledian's evolution: red with black spots, four arms with white round fists, two antennae, Ledian's colors.

Body:
- Back: no big round shell. Small, aerodynamic red wings shaped like space plane wings, almost mechanical.
- Head: aerodynamic, streamlined like a space robot helmet, with Ledian's eyes.
- The whole body looks like a space robot: smooth, hard, mechanical parts, but one creature, not a costume and not loose armor plates.

Pose: flying like a superhero with one arm stretched forward, legs trailing behind. 3/4 view facing left (far arm and far antenna on the left side, near ones on the right). Compact Gen 3 composition that fits a square.

AVOID: a human in a costume, a plush toy, Neopets style, chibi proportions, Gen 5 style, realistic insect rendering, extra limbs beyond four arms and two legs.
```
