# Gemini-prompt r1 (Claude, 15 nov)
Bijlage: `../referenties/gengar_gen3_8x.png` (Gengar, Gen 3 frontsprite).

```
Design a new Pokémon and draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): 64x64 pixel art, at most 16 colours, 1-pixel dark outline, light from the top left, plain white background, no ground, no shadow, no glow, no sparkles, no effects.

The Pokémon is "Gengas", a Cosmic/Poison type based on a BROWN DWARF (a failed star). It is NOT a ghost and NOT related to Gengar; it only looks similar by coincidence (convergent evolution, like Wiglett and Diglett).

Use the attached Gengar sprite as the reference for the face:
- EYES: exactly Gengar's eyes from the attachment: same shape, same angle, same red colour, same position on the body.
- MOUTH: exactly Gengar's wide grin from the attachment: same shape, same white teeth, same size and position.
Do not change the eyes or the mouth in any way.

Everything else is different from Gengar:
- BODY: a round ball of gas, like a small gas planet, with horizontal cloud bands across it (like Jupiter's bands), in magenta-purple with lighter and darker purple bands. Smooth round outline, NO spikes on the back, no fur.
- EARS: two ears in the same place and the same pointed shape as Gengar's ears, but made of gas: each ear is a loop of gas rising from the top of the ball (like the magnetic loops above a star).
- ARMS AND LEGS: exactly four limbs: two hands and two feet, the same stubby rounded shape as Gengar's hands and feet, but NOT attached to the body. They float separately around the ball like small moons, with a gap of background between each limb and the body. Give them a subtle cratered moon texture. Near hand and near foot slightly bigger and in front; far hand and far foot smaller and partly behind.
- POSE: three-quarter view facing left, compact so that the whole Pokémon (ball plus floating limbs) fits in a square, limbs close to the ball.

AVOID: a ghost look, Gengar's spiky back, limbs connected to the body, extra limbs, rings like Saturn, stars or sparkles around it, a cute plush or Neopets look, a planet with a face painted on it.
```
