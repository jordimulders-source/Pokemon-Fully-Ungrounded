# Gemini-prompt Pachamama r3 (nieuw gesprek, geen bijlage)

```
Design a new legendary Pokémon as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): pixel art, 64x64, max 16 colors, dark outline, light from top-left, plain white background, no ground, no shadow, no effects.

WHAT IT IS: Mother Earth as a living legendary, Ground/Fairy type. It must read FIRST as a real mountain, and only second as a mother figure: a huge living mountain whose shape suggests a mother in a wide mantle. It is alive and has eyes, but it is not a human and not a doll: it is like a sacred idol carved out of the mountain itself.

BODY: one big, solid, ANGULAR mountain made of rock. Sharp pointed snowy summit, steep straight slopes, a few large flat rock facets (big planes, not many small cracks). The summit is the head: a stylized idol face carved INTO the rock of the mountain (part of the stone, not a mask stuck on), with large open eyes. Two rocky mountain ridges with jagged edges come forward from the sides as arms, like a mother holding out her arms. Heavy, massive, imposing. Bold silhouette that fills the square.

COLORS: the colors of Rainbow Mountain (Vinicunca, Peru): the rock is colored in 3-4 broad bands of red/terracotta, pink, ochre yellow and turquoise green that follow the slopes like rock layers. White snow on the summit. Shading on the facets so it looks like solid stone.

POSE: 3/4 view facing left, upright like a legendary.

DO NOT: soft rounded blob shape, cloth, coat, fabric, doll, flippers, realistic human face, beard, many small cracks, thin stripes, plants, villages, paths, animals, offerings, gems, glowing, background.
```
