## Prompt

```
Design an original LEGENDARY Pokémon and draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style).

POSE (most important)
- A colossal, heroic ANDEAN CONDOR. Its two wings are raised high and CURVE UP AND AROUND the body until the wing tips almost meet above the head: THE WINGS THEMSELVES FORM ONE ROUND, SUN-LIKE RING around the condor. There is no separate ring or halo, only the wings.
- Three-quarter view facing left, but the WHOLE shape is visible: both wings fully visible, the far wing not hidden behind the body.
- Heroic box-art pose: chest pushed forward, head held high and proud, powerful legs and talons thrust forward.

THE CONDOR (must clearly read as a condor, NOT a turkey)
- Vulture head: bare, wrinkled, DARK skin (charcoal with a deep red tint), a flat dark fleshy comb lying on the forehead and top of the beak, NO dangling wattle, NO blue skin.
- Heavy, sharply hooked beak, pale ivory tip. A fierce, noble eye.
- A big fluffy neck ruff that fans out like the rays of a sun around the neck.
- Long separated finger-like primary feathers at the wing tips, broad powerful wings.
- A massive, heavy body with a broad chest: it feels like a colossus.

LEGENDARY FEEL
- Elegant, stylized ornamental feathers like the official legendary birds: long flowing tail plumes and long sweeping ruff feathers.
- Bold, simple, instantly readable silhouette, like an official Ken Sugimori cover legendary.

COLOR
- GOLD is the main color: large golden areas on the wings, the ruff and the tail plumes.
- Warm fire tones (orange, red) as secondary colors in the feathers, blending into the gold.
- Black and white remain as condor colors (black body base, white wing panels and ruff), but they do not dominate.
- Vivid, bright and dazzling: the most eye-catching legendary of the region.

STYLE AND FORMAT
- Official Gen 3 front sprite, 64x64, max 16 colors, 1 px dark outline, light from top left, flat Gen 3 shading with 3 to 4 tones per color, no dithering.
- A huge legendary that fills almost the whole 64x64 frame.
- Plain white background. NO floor, rock, shadow, clouds, light rays, glow, sparkles or effects. One single creature.

AVOID
- No flames, no fire effects, no glow. No patterns, symbols or geometric markings. No mask, crown, jewelry, sun disc object, halo or armor. No separate ring behind the body. No starfield or galaxy pattern.
- Not a turkey, not Ho-Oh, Moltres, Lugia, Yveltal, Braviary or a phoenix. Not a realistic bird painting, not a plush or western-cartoon look, no glossy 3D render.
```
