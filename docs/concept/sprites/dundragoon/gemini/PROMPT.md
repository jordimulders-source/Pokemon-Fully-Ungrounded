```
Design a brand-new Pokémon: DUNDRAGOON, an evolution of DUNSPARCE. Its type is Dragon/Normal.
Draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): pixel art, 64×64, max 16 colors, dark selout outline, plain white background.

WHAT IT IS
A lazy, goofy dragon that grew out of Dunsparce. It is clearly still a Dunsparce, but more dragon-shaped.
Body: the long, round, chubby body of Dunsparce with its segment-like bands, but bigger and more like a dragon's body.
Tail: the tail flows seamlessly into Dunsparce's drill tip, no seam or break between tail and drill.
Legs: 2 short, stubby legs under the body. No arms.
Wings: 2 big wings on its back (near and far), a much larger version of Dunsparce's small, pale blue-white wings, same shape language and colors.
Head and face: looks like Dunsparce's head and face, but more dragon-shaped. Expression lazy and goofy, not fierce.
Colors: Dunsparce's colors and markings (pale yellow body, blue bands/markings, pale blue-white wings).

VIEW
Three-quarter view facing left, like every Gen 3 front sprite: the far wing and far leg are on the left (narrower, partly hidden), the near ones on the right.
Compact: the whole Pokémon fits inside a square, body coiled or curved close together, not stretched out wide.

DO NOT
No ground, no rock, no shadow under it. No glow, no effects.
No long thin neck, no wing-shaped ears.
No plush/Neopets look, no chibi mascot, not a Western knight dragon, not a realistic reptile.
```
