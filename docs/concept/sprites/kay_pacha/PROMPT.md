# Gemini-prompt Kay Pacha r1 (nieuw gesprek, geen bijlage)

Eigen invulling van Claude (schrappen als Jordi het niet wil): pose (Gen 3-legendary-standaard: staand op vier poten, kop omhoog), "two or three large ornaments" als vertaling van "niet teveel detail", welke Inca-decoratie (Gemini kiest binnen de grenzen), groene accenten als bladeren.

```
Design a new legendary Pokémon as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): pixel art, 64x64, max 16 colors, dark outline, light from top-left, plain white background, no ground, no shadow, no effects.

WHAT IT IS: a Grass/Psychic legendary puma (cougar, Puma concolor), the guardian of "Kay Pacha", the world of the living in Inca belief. It is one of a legendary trio, in the same way Entei and Raikou are legendary beasts: a recognizable big cat with a few striking legendary ornaments. It must not look like Entei, Raikou, Suicune, Leafeon, Meowscarada or any other existing Pokémon.

ANATOMY: a real puma: slim, long, muscular body, small round head, short rounded ears, long thick tail with a darker tip, plain coat WITHOUT spots or stripes (it is not a jaguar or a leopard).

COLORS: the body is the natural tawny/ochre puma coat. The ornaments are Inca gold. Small green accents (leaves) for the Grass type.

ORNAMENTS (Inca-inspired, like Inca goldwork): only two or three LARGE, simple, bold shapes that still read at 64x64. No small patterns, no symbols, no thin lines, no jewelry chains.

SPRITE: keep it simple and readable at 64x64. Big clear shapes, little detail.

POSE: 3/4 view facing left, standing on four legs, head raised, proud and calm like a legendary beast.

DO NOT: spots, stripes, wings, armor, a human, a crown of feathers, glowing aura, magic effects, rocks, grass on the ground, background.
```
