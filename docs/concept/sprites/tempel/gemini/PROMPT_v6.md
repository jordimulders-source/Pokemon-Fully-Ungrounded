# Gemini-prompt v6 (23 nov), naar Jordi's Inca Trail-foto's

Create an original Pokémon as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style), 64×64 pixels, max 16 colors, clean dark outline, plain white background.

The Pokémon IS an ancient Inca temple that has come to life after centuries. It is not a creature wearing a temple, and it is not a creature standing next to one: the building itself is the whole body. Ghost/Rock type. It is huge and heavy, like Snorlax in weight and presence. The body FILLS the 64×64 frame almost edge to edge, in a compact, square composition.

It looks like a REAL Inca ruin on the Inca Trail (like Wiñay Wayna): a stone building on a mountainside terrace. Make it interesting through big, readable shapes (not through small detail):
- The building: one storey of RUBBLE masonry, rough, uneven, roughly stacked fieldstones of different sizes (not polished, not neat polygonal blocks), with walls that lean slightly inward. Its two short side walls rise into TALL, steep triangular stone GABLES, with a few stone pegs sticking out of the gables. The roof is gone, so the gables stand free against the sky.
- Age: moss and short grass growing on top of the walls and gables, lichen spots on the stones. Centuries old.
- Below it, one or two low terrace walls of the same rough stone, the building sits on them.

Face: the front wall has a large Inca doorway with a long flat stone lintel on top, slightly narrower at the top than at the bottom: this doorway is its mouth, dark inside, like an entrance you would walk into. Above it, two small rectangular windows in the wall are its eyes: the eyes are light shining out from inside these holes in the stonework, the only sign that something lives inside. No other facial features. The idea: you think it is just the entrance to a temple, until you notice it is looking at you.

No limbs at all: no arms, no legs, no feet, no hands. Viewed in 3/4 view facing left (front wall and one side wall visible).

Colors: weathered grey stone with some light lichen spots, moss green on top of the walls, darker in the joints and the doorway.

No glow, no aura, no light rays, no particles, no effects. No ground, no rock, no shadow under it.

AVOID: a Maya or Aztec pyramid with a central staircase, a temple on top of a stepped pyramid, a tower of stacked storeys, overhanging ledges or cornices, a ziggurat or fantasy temple, a single box with a door, a regular brick pattern, polished smooth blocks, ears or a cat shape, an igloo or hut shape, Aztec or Maya step pyramids, gold, carvings of gods or faces in the stone, a mummy or ghost figure inside the doorway, cute plush style, a person or creature in a costume, a realistic architectural drawing, lots of tiny detail.
