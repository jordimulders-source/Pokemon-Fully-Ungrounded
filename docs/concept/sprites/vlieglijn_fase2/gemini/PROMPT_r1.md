# Mug (vlieglijn fase 2) – Gemini r1, nieuw gesprek, geen bijlage (21 nov)

Op basis van Jordi (21 nov): nieuwe fase zichtbaar; niet super gedetailleerd; Electric/Poison; robotelement een beetje behouden; snuit en poten niet te dun; LED's behouden met \ / boze look, paars; steeksnuit = robotische naald; vliegend, snuit klaar om aan te vallen; groter, niet dik. Niet genoemd door Jordi (aan Gemini gelaten): voelsprieten, kleuren buiten paars, hoe Poison zichtbaar is, of de kabelpoten terugkomen.

```
Design a new Pokémon: a Poison/Electric robotic mosquito. It is the final evolution of a small robotic fly Pokémon (grey round head with a black LED visor, purple LED eyes, smooth mechanical body). The mosquito must clearly read as a NEW, evolved stage: a mosquito, not the fly with parts added.

Draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): pixel art, 64×64, max 16 colors, dark selout outline, plain white background, three-quarter view facing left.

DESIGN
- A mosquito, partly robotic: keep a little of the robot look, but it is a living Pokémon, not a machine.
- Eyes: a black LED visor with purple LED lights forming an angry expression, like \ / (slanted lines), clearly readable.
- Proboscis: a robotic needle, pointing forward and slightly down, ready to strike.
- Pose: flying, wings spread, body in the air, about to attack with the needle.
- Size: larger than the first stage, filling most of the 64×64 frame, but slender, not thick or bulky.
- Exactly six legs.
- Keep it simple: few large shapes, no small details that would disappear at 64×64. The needle and the legs must not be too thin: at least 2-3 pixels wide, so they still read at sprite size.

DO NOT
No sparks, no glow, no electricity effects, no motion lines, no poison drips or bubbles. No ground, no shadow.
No screws, bolts, panels or gadgets. No plush/Neopets look, no chibi mascot.
Not a bee or wasp, not Beedrill, not Yanmega, not Ninjask.
```
