## Prompt

```
Design an original Pokémon and draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style).

CONCEPT
- Fairy/Rock type, the final stage of a three-stage evolution line. Its name is Gnometrotter: a gnome Pokémon that has walked so far for so long that its body has fully adapted to its environment. This is its Cave Form: it has lived in caves, and its body has partly turned into rough grey rock.
- It is a garden gnome turned into a cartoony Pokémon: an old, seasoned hiker who has been walking for ages. Bigger than its pre-evolution, and clearly older.
- Posture: hunched forward with age, taking a slow, heavy step, leaning on a walking stick.
- Walking stick: a long, rough piece of grey rock, held in one hand. Nothing on top of it.
- Body: a round, pink body on longer legs with sturdy rounded feet (simple rounded feet, a bit big).
- Arms: longer oval arms, each ending in a round, chubby ball-shaped hand.
- A big, round, bulbous nose, the same pink as its skin.
- Cap: still a soft gnome cap in shape (droopy, pointed, the tip bending down behind it, NOT tall and NOT straight up), but made of rough grey rock. The cap comes down over the eyes and rests directly on top of the nose and cheeks: there is NO dark gap, slit or shadow between the cap and the face. The eyes are completely hidden and nothing under the cap looks like an eye.
- Mustache and beard: one big, heavy mass of rough grey rock that hangs from under the nose down over the chest, shaped like a beard. NO mouth is visible.
- Hair: big tufts sticking out sideways from under the cap, also rough grey rock.
- Rocks grow out of its body: a few big, rough grey rock chunks grow out of its shoulders and upper back, part of the body, big enough to change its silhouette. NOT small pebbles stuck onto the skin.
- A simple dark belt around the waist with a small square buckle, between the pink upper body and the blue lower half.
- Colors of the body: pink skin; the lower half of the body including the legs is sky blue, like a garden gnome's trousers, with a thin dark-blue line on the border. This is NOT clothing, just a second body color. All rock parts are plain grey rock.
- Cartoony, but in the Pokémon way: simple bold shapes, a clear readable silhouette, like an official Ken Sugimori design.

STYLE AND FORMAT
- Official Gen 3 front sprite, 64x64, max 16 colors, 1 px dark outline, light from top left, flat Gen 3 shading with 3 to 4 tones per color, no dithering.
- Keep the rock simple: big rough shapes and only a few cracks, no fine texture or speckles.
- Three-quarter view facing left. A final-stage Pokémon, about 60 px tall, compact, centered in the frame.
- Plain white background. NO floor, shadow, loose rocks on the ground, cave background or effects. One single creature.

AVOID
- No visible eyes, no dark gap under the cap, no visible mouth. No crystals, no gems, no stalactites, no glow or sparkles. No human proportions, no little man in a costume. No backpack, no pickaxe, no lantern, no helmet. Not Impidimp, not Hatenna. No western-cartoon or plush look. No glossy 3D render.
```
