```
Create a brand-new POKÉMON (designed like Game Freak would: simplified bold shapes, not a realistic bird with an accessory), the final evolution of a HOATZIN-inspired line. Type: Poison/Dark. Photo of a real hoatzin attached: the silhouette must clearly read as a hoatzin.

Hoatzin silhouette (keep these proportions, simplified):
- small head on a LONG, thin neck
- a heavy, rounded body that leans forward
- a VERY long, broad, fan-like tail hanging down behind it
- the hoatzin's color layout: pale throat/chest, darker wings and back with a light wing edge, pale tip on the tail

Changes from the real bird:
- Keep the bare BLUE face skin and the typical alert bird eye (round, wide open, watchful, red iris). NOT narrowed, NOT scheming.
- Crest: much bigger and uglier than the real one: a tall, ragged, messy crest of stiff, broken FEATHERS (not hair), sticking up and out unevenly.
- Body colors: the typical Poison-type purple, following the hoatzin's color layout (only the face stays blue).
- The belly bulges a little where it ferments leaves: a rounded paunch, not a giant sac or jar.

Pose: perched upright and alert like a real hoatzin, long neck raised, tail hanging down.
Style: official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style), 64x64, max 16 colors, 3/4 view facing left, compact composition, clean dark outline, white background.
No effects: no gas, smoke, glow or stink lines; no ground, shadow or branch.
AVOID: parrot, crow, cockatoo, turkey, chicken, pheasant, hair or dreadlocks on the head, short neck, short tail, cute plush look, jug or pot, transparent sac.
```
