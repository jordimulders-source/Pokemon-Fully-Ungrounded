## Prompt

```
Design an original Pokémon and draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style).

CONCEPT
- Fire/Fairy type, the middle stage of a firework evolution line: a SALAMANDER MADE OF FIRE that spins like a ground-spinner firework.
- Use a real fire salamander only for the shape (see the attached photo): rounded amphibian head, small round eyes, short splayed legs with small toes. Not a lizard, not a dragon.
- THE HEAD, BODY, LEGS AND TAIL ARE MADE OF FIRE. No skin: the whole creature is living flame in salamander shape, with flickering flame tongues along its back and edges and flame streaks running through it. Only the eyes and the mouth are drawn into the flame.
- IT IS THE TOP ITSELF. There is NO separate top, cone, disc or object: it does not lie on, sit on or ride anything. Its own fiery body stands upright and narrows downward into its tail, and the tail tip is the single spinning point that touches the ground. The short legs stick out sideways from the widest part.
- From the tail tip, short solid jets of sparks spray out sideways close to the ground, as if it is spinning right now.
- FIREWORK COLORS WITH GREEN AND BLUE: the flames are vivid emerald green and turquoise blue, with glittering gold-yellow spots (like the spots of a fire salamander) and a few small touches of soft pink. The sparks have the same colors. Saturated and sparkling.
- FAIRY: playful and mischievous, a few small four-pointed twinkling stars in the flames. Not babyish.
- One head, four legs, one tail. No arms, no wings.

STYLE AND FORMAT
- Official Gen 3 front sprite, 64x64, max 16 colors, 1 px dark outline, light from top left, flat Gen 3 shading with 3 to 4 tones per color, no dithering.
- Solid shapes with clean outlines that read as a clear silhouette at 64x64; no thin strands.
- Three-quarter view facing left. Compact composition that fits a square.
- Plain white background. NO floor, shadow, smoke or motion lines. One single creature.

AVOID
- No separate spinning top or cone, nothing it lies or sits on. No smooth skin, no lizard body. No coiled body, no snake shape. No orange. No firework explosions or burst shapes. No firecrackers, no rocket, no stick. No dragon snout, horns or spikes. Not Charmander, not Wooper, not Hitmontop. No hearts. No text. No glossy 3D render.
```
