# Asset 1: hoofd, v2 (17 nov)

Hoofd r1 (`hoofd_r1/`): Jordi "hoe kan die nu weer banette doen". Oorzaak (Claude): de r7-bijlage ís een Banette-hoofd (oor, middenpunt, afhangende punt), en "ghost" + grijns + rode ogen = Gengar.
v2: geen bijlage met een Pokémon-hoofd, geen woord "ghost"; de narrenmuts in woorden. Optioneel: Jordi voegt een foto van een echte narrenmuts toe.

```
Draw ONLY A HEAD, nothing else: no body, no neck. Pixel art in the style of a Gen 3 Pokémon sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald): 1 px dark outline, flat shading with 3 to 4 tones, no dithering, about 30 pixels wide, plain white background, 3/4 view facing left.

It is the head of a sinister jester puppet, an original Pokémon.

HEAD SHAPE: the head itself is shaped like a jester's cap. The top of the head splits into TWO long, soft, floppy points: one bends over to the left, one bends over to the right, each drooping down at the tip. Between them the top of the head is a smooth curve. There is NO spike in the middle, NO ears, NO horns. The rest of the head is a wide rounded shape.

FACE
- A very sinister, wide grin: a zigzag mouth full of sharp teeth. The teeth are the same colour as the head.
- Large, slanted, menacing red eyes.

COLOUR: dark grey with a slight purple tint, smooth, no patches, no stitches, no zipper.

AVOID: the head must not look like any existing Pokémon. No spiky crown, no single point on top, no cat ears.
```
