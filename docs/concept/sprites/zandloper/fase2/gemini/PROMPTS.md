## r1
```
Design an original Pokémon as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style), 64x64 pixels, max 16 colors, white background, facing 3/4 to the left.

Concept: the middle evolution of an hourglass Pokémon (Ground/Psychic). Its final evolution is a sand djinn that has fully escaped from the hourglass; this middle stage is the moment of escaping.
- The hourglass has just broken: it lies on its side, broken open in the middle (at the narrow waist). Sand escapes from that opening.
- Out of the escaping sand comes a relatively large brain, made of sand. Show the brain folds with only a few clear lines.
- The eyes are NOT attached to the brain: they float a little below it, also made of sand.
- Above the brain, also made of sand: a question mark and an exclamation mark.
- Everything is connected to each other by the sand: hourglass, sand stream, eyes, brain, question mark and exclamation mark form one creature.
COLORS (exact): sand in warm ochre gold (#fce296, #e8be62, #cc9c46, #a47034). Glass pale icy blue (#def4fa, #a0cce0, #72a0ba) with a dark slate-blue outline (#405e7a). Wooden end caps smooth dark brown (#8a522e, #5c321e, #603a18).
Keep it simple and readable at 64x64: big clear shapes, like a real Gen 3 sprite. Compact silhouette that fits a square.

No effects, no glow, no sparkles, no shadow; draw no ground under the hourglass. AVOID: a realistic hourglass with a brain on it, a plush toy, Neopets style.
```
