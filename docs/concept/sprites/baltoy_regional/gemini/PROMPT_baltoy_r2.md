# Regional Baltoy – Gemini-startprompt r2 (22 nov, nieuw gesprek, geen bijlage)

```
Design a regional form of the Pokémon BALTOY, type Rock/Psychic, inspired by the ancient stone monoliths of TIWANAKU (Bolivia). It must still clearly be a Baltoy: a small living Pokémon, not a statue.
Draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): pixel art, 64×64, max 16 colors, dark selout outline, plain white background.

BUILD (keep from Baltoy)
Two long thin arms sticking out to the sides.
The body tapers down to a single point at the bottom: it balances and spins on that point like a spinning top.

WHAT CHANGES (Tiwanaku monolith)
The head and the body are more angular, like a Tiwanaku monolith: chiselled carved stone with flat planes and sharp edges, a carved headband, shallow carved geometric patterns.

FACE (most important, keep exactly this)
Two ROUND eyes, each with one horizontal line through the middle.
Above the eyes a RED painted pattern that covers the top of each eye and dips down to a point between the two eyes.

VIEW
Three-quarter view facing left, like every Gen 3 front sprite.
Compact: the whole Pokémon fits inside a square, small (it is a first stage).

DO NOT
Not a rectangular block statue, no human figure, no legs, no feet.
No ground, no pedestal, no shadow. No glow, no aura, no psychic effects, no motion lines.
No starfield or galaxy patterns. No plush/Neopets look.
```
