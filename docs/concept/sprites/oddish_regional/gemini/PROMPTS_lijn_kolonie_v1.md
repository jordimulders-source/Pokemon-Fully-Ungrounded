## Gloom
```
Design a regional form of the Pokémon Gloom and draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style pixel art).

CONCEPT
- Gloom from the deep-sea hydrothermal vents near the Galápagos, based on the giant tube worm (Riftia). Type: Water/Fire. It evolves from an Oddish with five red feathery tube worm plumes on its head.
- Body, face and pose of Gloom's Gen 3 front sprite: the round body, the short arms held out, the short legs, the squinting closed eyes and the wide open drooling mouth.
- Body color: white / cream.
- On top: no bud and no leaves. Instead a dense, drooping cluster of giant tube worms: many long white tubes growing from the top of the head, bending limply over to the left and right under their own weight, each ending in a red feathery plume that hangs down. The cluster looks heavy and tired, like Gloom.
- The drool is dark sulfurous vent water. A faint orange glow deep between the tubes.

POSE
- Three-quarter view facing left, the classic Gloom pose.

STYLE AND FORMAT
- 64x64 canvas, max 16 colors, 1 px dark outline, light from top left, flat Gen 3 shading, no dithering.
- Plain white background. No ground, water, bubbles, smoke or extra effects. One single creature, no extra views.

AVOID
- No leaves, no green, no flower, no round bud, no anemone tentacles, no flames.
```

## Vileplume
```
Design a regional form of the Pokémon Vileplume and draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style pixel art).

CONCEPT
- Vileplume from the deep-sea hydrothermal vents near the Galápagos, based on a whole colony of giant tube worms (Riftia) around a vent, like the "Rose Garden" of the Galápagos Rift. Type: Water/Fire. Its line starts with an Oddish with five red feathery tube worm plumes on its head.
- Body and pose of Vileplume's Gen 3 front sprite: the small body under a large top, short arms held out, short legs, the face mostly hidden under the top.
- Body color: white / cream.
- On top: no flower. Instead a wide ring of upright white tubes, each with a big red feathery plume on top, standing shoulder to shoulder in a crown around a small dark grey-black mineral chimney in the center. The chimney has an opening at the top that glows orange-hot inside.

POSE
- Three-quarter view facing left, the classic Vileplume pose.

STYLE AND FORMAT
- 64x64 canvas, max 16 colors, 1 px dark outline, light from top left, flat Gen 3 shading, no dithering.
- Plain white background. No ground, water, bubbles, smoke or extra effects. One single creature, no extra views.

AVOID
- No petals, no flower shape, no green, no leaves, no anemone tentacles, no flames.
```
