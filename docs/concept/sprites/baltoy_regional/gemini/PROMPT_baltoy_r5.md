# Regional Baltoy – Gemini-startprompt r5 (22 nov, nieuw gesprek, geen bijlage)
Jordi over r4: "ja maak hem levendiger, schrijf r5". = r4-prompt + blok ALIVE met Claudes voorstel (bredere pijler, kortere hals, armen niet stijf opzij).

```
Design a regional form of the Pokémon BALTOY, type Rock/Psychic, inspired by the ancient stone monoliths of TIWANAKU (Bolivia). It must still clearly be a Baltoy: a small LIVING Pokémon creature, not an object or a statue.
Draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): pixel art, 64×64, max 16 colors, dark selout outline, plain white background.

ALIVE (most important)
It must feel like a lively little creature, not a post or a peg with a head on it:
- the body pillar is broad and sturdy, not a thin stick;
- the head sits directly on the body, with almost no neck;
- the arms are not stiffly straight out to the sides: they angle down and slightly forward, in a lively pose.

SHAPE (Tiwanaku: angular, NOT round)
Tiwanaku monoliths are angular: flat faces, straight edges, square corners, cut from stone.
HEAD: a square stone block with a flat top, like the head of a Tiwanaku monolith.
BODY: a rectangular stone pillar with flat sides and flat, shallow carved relief lines on its front, that tapers down to a single point at the bottom (it balances and spins on that point like a spinning top). Not a faceted crystal, not a gem.

BUILD (keep from Baltoy)
Two long thin arms.

HEADBAND
A thick, robust, heavy carved stone headband around the head, deeply cut, bold, clearly readable, with the typical Andean Tiwanaku STEPPED motif: stair-step shapes and the stepped cross (chakana). Not a Greek meander / Greek key pattern.

FACE (keep exactly this)
Two ROUND eyes, each with one horizontal line through the middle.
Above the eyes a RED painted pattern that covers the top of each eye and dips down to a point between the two eyes.

VIEW
Three-quarter view facing left, like every Gen 3 front sprite.
Compact: the whole Pokémon fits inside a square, small (it is a first stage).

DO NOT
No human figure, no legs, no feet.
No ground, no pedestal, no shadow. No glow, no aura, no psychic effects, no motion lines.
No starfield or galaxy patterns. No plush/Neopets look.
```
