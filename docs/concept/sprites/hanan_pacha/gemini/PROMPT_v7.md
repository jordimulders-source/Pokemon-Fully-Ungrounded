## Prompt

```
Design an original LEGENDARY Pokémon and draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style).

IMAGE FORMAT: a SQUARE image (1:1, 1024x1024). The whole creature fits inside the square with a small white margin; nothing is cut off at the edges.

POSE (most important)
- A colossal, proud, heroic ANDEAN CONDOR with BOTH wings raised STEEPLY UPWARD in a narrow V: the wings point mostly UP (about 60 degrees above horizontal), NOT sideways. The figure is about as tall as it is wide.
- SUN SHAPE: the wings curve slightly inward at the top, and their long separated finger-like primary feathers FAN OUT LIKE THE RAYS OF A SUN, pointing outward in all directions from the body. Together with the tail fan below, all feathers radiate from the body like a ROUND SUNBURST: the overall silhouette is almost round. The sun shape comes from these radiating feathers, NOT from a closed ring: the wing tips do NOT meet, there is a gap above the head.
- Exactly TWO wings: the far wing on the left, the near wing on the right.
- Three-quarter view facing left, the WHOLE shape visible: both wings fully visible, the far wing not hidden.
- Heroic box-art pose: chest pushed forward, a LARGE head held HIGH on an upright neck, looking ahead with a fierce, noble eye. The head never hangs down or forward.
- TAIL (important): a long, symmetrical fan of about 9 long, narrow, pointed feathers that hangs STRAIGHT DOWN below the body, between and behind the talons, spreading slightly outward toward the ends like a downward-pointing fringe of a feathered headdress. Each tail feather is clearly separate and lance-shaped. The tail is long: about as long as the body.
- The whole figure fits in a square, NOT a wide horizontal wingspan.

THE CONDOR (must clearly read as a condor, NOT a turkey and NOT a phoenix)
- Vulture head, big and noble: bare wrinkled skin in a deep CRIMSON RED (the same warm red as the wing feathers, like a male condor's red head), a LARGE, stately fleshy comb (crimson red, like the skin) that starts at the top of the beak and rises UP over the forehead like a natural crown, still clearly the real fleshy comb of a male condor, NO wattle. Heavy, sharply hooked beak with a pale ivory tip.
- A smooth, soft collar of white down hugging the base of the neck like a scarf. NOT spiky, NOT fanned out.
- FEW, LARGE, CLEARLY SEPARATE feathers: each wing has 6 to 7 long finger-like primaries with white space between them. Feathers never clump together into one mass. Broad and stiff like a real condor, not flowing ribbons.
- A massive, heavy body with a broad chest.

COLOR
- GOLD is the main color, with warm fire tones (orange, crimson red) as secondary colors.
- Each wing has a broad WHITE BAND across it (the condor's white wing panel) and DARK, almost black finger tips. White and black are accents, never dominant.
- The body is mainly gold and white. This Pokémon is noble and good, not dark or evil.
- Vivid, bright and dazzling: the most eye-catching legendary of the region.

STYLE AND FORMAT
- Official Gen 3 front sprite, 64x64, max 16 colors, 1 px dark outline, light from top left, flat Gen 3 shading with 3 to 4 tones per color, no dithering.
- A huge legendary that fills almost the whole 64x64 frame. Bold, simple, instantly readable silhouette, like an official Ken Sugimori cover legendary.
- Plain white background. NO floor, rock, shadow, clouds, light rays, glow, sparkles or effects. One single creature.

AVOID
- NOT Ho-Oh, NOT a phoenix, no ring of wings above the head, no flowing streamer or ribbon tail, no rainbow. Not a turkey, not Moltres, Lugia, Yveltal or Braviary.
- No flames, no fire effects, no glow. No patterns, symbols or geometric markings. No mask, no crown object, no jewelry, sun disc object, halo or armor. No starfield.
- Not a realistic bird painting, not a plush or western-cartoon look, no glossy 3D render.
```
