# Gemini-prompt r3 Dustly (Claude, 15 nov)
Bijlage: `../referenties/gastly_gen3_8x.png` (alleen Gastly). Nieuw gesprek, r1/r2 niet meesturen.

## Jordi (letterlijk)
- r1: "dit is nog teveel gastly" · "alleen de ogen en de mond hoeven te blijven"
- r2 (`r2_a.png`): "het mag nog wel meer afwijken, dit is nog steeds net gastly"
- Eerder: "ja een wolk, rondachtig zoals gen 1"

## Waarom r2 nog Gastly is (Claude)
- Gezicht vult de hele wolk (Gastly-verhouding); zwarte kern is weer een bol; alles zwart; rand rondom gelijk.

## Keuzes Claude (Jordi heeft hier niets over gezegd; schrappen of aanpassen mag)
- Gezicht klein (±⅓ van de wolkbreedte), niet in het midden: de wolk is het lijf, niet het gezicht.
- Kleur: roestbruin stof i.p.v. zwart; donker alleen aan de schaduwkant (rechtsonder).
- Belichte kant als een halve maan: de linkerbovenkant van de wolk roze-oranje belicht (zoals echte *bright-rimmed* globules), de rest in schaduw.
- Bloemkoolvormige lagen bulten en een paar donkere stofstrepen (filamenten) door de wolk.

## Prompt

```
Design a new Pokémon and draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): 64x64 pixel art, at most 16 colours, light from the top left, plain white background, no ground, no shadow, no glow, no sparkles, no effects outside the creature.

The Pokémon is "Dustly", a Cosmic/Poison type based on a BRIGHT-RIMMED BOK GLOBULE: a small, dense cloud of dust in space in which a star will later be born, lit on one side by a nearby star. It is NOT a ghost and NOT related to Gastly. ONLY its eyes and mouth are the same as Gastly's; everything else must look clearly different from Gastly.

FACE: the attached sprite is GASTLY. Copy ONLY the eyes and the grin with fangs (same shapes), turned into the three-quarter view. The face is SMALL: about one third of the width of the cloud, placed left of the centre. The cloud is the body, not the face.

BODY: one roughly round cloud of dust, built from layers of bulging, cauliflower-like lumps of different sizes, with a bumpy, uneven outline. A few thin, darker streaks of dust curl through the cloud. NO ball or sphere inside it.
COLOUR: rust-brown and reddish-brown dust, NOT black. Only the shadow side (bottom right) is dark brown, almost black.
LIGHT: the upper left part of the cloud is lit by a nearby star, like a crescent moon: the lumps there are rose-pink and pale orange on their lit sides, fading through rust-brown into the shadow side. Drawn as solid pixel shading, not a glow.

POSE: a three-quarter view facing LEFT, floating in space. Compact so it fits in a square; the cloud fills about two thirds of the width of the image, centred.

AVOID: a black body, a black ball or sphere, a face that fills the whole body, a smooth round outline, a halo or ring of gas around the body, floating blobs around it, Gastly's purple, a ghost look, arms, hands, legs, a tail, stars or sparkles, glow effects, a cute plush, chibi or Neopets look, a glossy 3D render.
```
