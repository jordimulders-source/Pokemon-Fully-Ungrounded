```
Design a new form of the Pokémon ORICORIO: SAMBA STYLE, from the carnival of Rio de Janeiro. Its type is Grass/Flying.
It is a new dance style of the Pokémon ORICORIO (the dancing bird from Alola, like its Baile, Pom-Pom, Pa'u and Sensu styles). Keep the Oricorio body exactly: Oricorio STANDS UPRIGHT LIKE A PERSON, not like a normal bird. Its body is a small, vertical egg shape (like a standing penguin or a dancer), with a round head sitting directly on top of it, no visible neck. The body is NOT horizontal and NOT a parrot or pigeon body. A slender, slightly downcurved pink beak; long thin pink legs coming straight down from the bottom of the body, with toes. Only the plumage changes: the feathers themselves ARE the dance costume (like Baile's feathers are a flamenco dress), never clothing or objects worn over the bird.
THE EYE: exactly the happy eye of Pom-Pom Oricorio (the yellow Electric cheerleader style): a big, round, WIDE OPEN solid black eye with one white shine dot, just above the base of the beak. NO eyelid, NO line over the eye, NO white around it. A happy, cheerful, wide-awake look. NOT sleepy, NOT half-closed, NOT cool.

WHAT IT IS
An unmistakable RIO CARNIVAL SAMBA QUEEN made of feathers: glamorous, festive, over the top. MAINLY GREEN (the Grass type color), with bright golden yellow and a little royal blue as accents (the colors of the Brazilian flag). The long plumes are shaped like long pointed leaves because it is Grass type.
THE HEADDRESS (most important): a HUGE, ornate carnival headdress growing from the top of the head, much taller and wider than the head itself: a big half-circle crown of long plumes radiating outward like sun rays, in two layers: an inner ring of short yellow feathers and an outer ring of long green plumes with yellow tips. At the front of the headdress, right above the face, a golden yellow centerpiece shaped like a big ornate leaf, like the jeweled front of a samba headdress. The face stays clearly visible below it.
THE COSTUME ON THE BODY: the body feathers form the samba costume: a golden yellow, sequin-like band across the chest and a golden yellow band around the hips (like a samba dancer's two-piece costume), with a short row of hanging feather tassels from the hip band. The rest of the body is plain green.
THE WINGS: on each side one big wing made of long plumes like a samba dancer's feathered side-wings: green plumes with yellow middle sections and a few blue feather tips.
NO OVERLAP: headdress on top, one wing on each side, body in the middle: these parts do not overlap or hide each other, every part has its own clear outline.
Few, big, simple shapes: it must read clearly at 64×64.

POSE
Samba no pé: the typical samba step, hips swung to one side, standing on the toes of one foot with that knee bent, the other foot lifted, both wings spread wide and high to the sides, chin up, proud and joyful.

Draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): pixel art, 64×64, max 16 colors, dark selout outline, plain white background. Three-quarter view facing left, like every Gen 3 front sprite: far wing and far leg on the left, near ones on the right. 2 wings and 2 legs in total. Compact, fits in a square.
DO NOT
No ground, no shadow, no stage, no glow, no music notes, no effects. No human body parts (no arms, hands, fingers, hair): only feathers. No plush/Neopets look.
```
