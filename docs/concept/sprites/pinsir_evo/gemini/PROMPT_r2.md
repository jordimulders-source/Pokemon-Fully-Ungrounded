# Pinsir-evolutie – Gemini-startprompt r2 (22 nov, nieuw gesprek, geen bijlage)

```
Design a brand-new Pokémon: the evolution of Pinsir. Type Bug/Ground. It must clearly read as Pinsir's evolution, but it is a new creature, not a copy of Pinsir.
Draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): pixel art, 64×64, max 16 colors, dark selout outline, plain white background.

WHAT IT IS
A huge beetle-kaiju hybrid, based on the SABERTOOTH LONGHORN BEETLE (Macrodontia cervicornis) from the Amazon.
Keep Pinsir's identity: the brown beetle body, Pinsir's face with ONE wide mouth lined with teeth (only one mouth: no snout, nose or second mouth above it), and the two big spiked pincers on top of the head. Here those pincers become the sabertooth longhorn's enormous curved mandibles with a FEW big, chunky spikes (big enough to read at 64×64). Head and body are large in the frame; the mandibles are at most about one third of the total height.
From the sabertooth longhorn: a bold bark-like pattern on the back and wing cases. NO antennae.

COLOR
Not monotone: clear contrast between the parts, e.g. a warm reddish-brown/chestnut body, darker wing cases with a bold lighter bark pattern, pale bone-colored mandibles and spikes, a lighter belly.

KAIJU
It stands UPRIGHT ON TWO heavy, thick legs like a classic movie kaiju, with a thick heavy tail dragging behind it, a row of big sharp spikes running down its back and tail, a hunched, massive, towering posture. Short strong arms like Pinsir's. More spikes than Pinsir overall, but fewer and bigger rather than many tiny ones.

VIEW
Three-quarter view facing left, like every Gen 3 front sprite: the far limbs on the left (partly hidden), the near ones on the right.
Compact: the whole Pokémon fits inside a square; tail and limbs close to the body.

DO NOT
Not a dinosaur with a beetle head, not a realistic beetle on its hind legs: one creature that is both Pinsir-beetle and kaiju.
No separate armour plates or robot parts. No ground, no rock, no shadow under it. No glow, no aura, no breath attack, no effects, no motion lines.
No plush/Neopets look, no chibi mascot, not a realistic painting.
```
