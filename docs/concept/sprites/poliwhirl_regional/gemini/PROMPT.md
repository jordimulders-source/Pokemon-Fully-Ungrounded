# Gemini-prompt: regionale Poliwhirl, startprompt v1 (25 nov)
Bijlage: `bijlage_poliwhirl.png` (gewone Poliwhirl, Gen 3, 8×). Besluiten: `../SPEC.md`.

```
Design a regional form of the Pokémon POLIWHIRL and draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style pixel art).

THE ATTACHED SPRITE IS THE NORMAL POLIWHIRL. The new design is a hybrid: it must still be clearly recognizable as Poliwhirl, but slimmer and sharper.

KEEP FROM POLIWHIRL
- Standing on two legs, same view as the attached sprite.
- The gloves on the hands.
- The eyes on top of the head.
- A spiral on the belly.
- No tail.

CONCEPT
- Type Ice/Poison. Based on the harlequin toad (Atelopus), a very poisonous toad from cold mountain streams in the Andes.
- Slimmer and sharper than the normal Poliwhirl: longer, thinner limbs and a more angular, toad-like shape.

COLORS (fixed for this whole evolution line)
- Bright yellow and black in large harlequin-toad patches.
- Pale icy blue for everything made of ice.
- Red for the cinnabar, the spiral and the mouth.

CRYSTALS
- Bigger crystals than a small tadpole would have: clear ice with red cinnabar inside, growing out of the skin. The animal comes first, the crystals second.

STYLE AND FORMAT
- 64x64 pixel sprite, max 16 colors, 1 px dark outline, clean shading like official Game Freak Gen 3 sprites.
- Keep the color count low and the details big enough to read at 64x64.
- Plain white background. No floor, rock, ice block, shadow, water, bubbles, glow, sparkles, frost mist or effects. One single creature, no extra views.

AVOID
- No suction disc on the belly.
- Not like Glimmora: no crystal flower, no petals of cut gemstones, no faceted jewels.
- No glossy 3D render, no plush or mascot look, no human face.
```
