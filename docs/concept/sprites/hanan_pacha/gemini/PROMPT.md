## Prompt

```
Design an original LEGENDARY Pokémon and draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style).

POSE (most important)
- A colossal, heroic ANDEAN CONDOR with its wings spread wide and raised UP and AROUND its body, so that the two wings together form an almost round, sun-like shape with the body and head in the center. Not a flat horizontal wingspan: the whole figure fits in a square.
- Three-quarter view facing left, but the WHOLE shape must be visible: both wings fully visible, the far wing not hidden behind the body.
- Head held high, proud and heroic, looking ahead with a sharp, confident eye.

ANATOMY (real Andean condor)
- Clearly a condor head: bare head with a fleshy comb on top and a small wattle, strong hooked beak (pale ivory tip, darker base).
- A big fluffy WHITE NECK RUFF; on this Pokémon the ruff feathers fan out like the rays of a sun around the neck.
- Black body and black wings with large white panels on the upper wings, and long separated finger-like primary feathers at the wing tips.
- Strong legs with talons under the body.

COLOR AND FEEL
- Black, white and gold. Vivid, bright and striking: it must look like the most dazzling, eye-catching box-art legendary, like a cover legendary of a main Pokémon game.
- Gold accents on the tips of the ruff rays and along the edges of the flight feathers.
- Majestic and powerful, a bold, simple, instantly readable silhouette, like an official Ken Sugimori legendary design.

STYLE AND FORMAT
- Official Gen 3 front sprite, 64x64, max 16 colors, 1 px dark outline, light from top left, flat Gen 3 shading with 3 to 4 tones per color, no dithering.
- A huge legendary that fills almost the whole 64x64 frame.
- Plain white background. NO floor, rock, shadow, clouds, light rays, glow, sparkles or effects. One single creature.

AVOID
- No flames, no fire, no glow. No patterns, symbols, geometric designs or markings on the wings. No mask, crown, jewelry, sun disc object or armor. No starfield or galaxy pattern.
- Not Ho-Oh, Moltres, Lugia, Yveltal, Braviary, Mandibuzz or a phoenix. Not a realistic bird painting, not a plush or western-cartoon look, no glossy 3D render.
```
