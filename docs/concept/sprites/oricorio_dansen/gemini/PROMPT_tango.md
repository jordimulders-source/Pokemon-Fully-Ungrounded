```
Design a new form of the Pokémon ORICORIO: TANGO STYLE, from the night streets of old Buenos Aires. Its type is Dark/Flying. Dark here means night and mystery, not evil.
It is a new dance style of the Pokémon ORICORIO (the dancing bird from Alola, like its Baile, Pom-Pom, Pa'u and Sensu styles). Keep the Oricorio body exactly: Oricorio STANDS UPRIGHT LIKE A PERSON, not like a normal bird. Its body is a small, vertical egg shape (like a standing penguin or a dancer), with a round head sitting directly on top of it, no visible neck. The body is NOT horizontal and NOT a parrot or pigeon body. A slender, slightly downcurved pink beak; very thin pink bird legs with toes. Only the plumage changes: the feathers themselves ARE the dance costume (like Baile's feathers are a flamenco dress), never clothing or objects worn over the bird.
THE EYE: exactly the happy eye of Pom-Pom Oricorio (the yellow Electric cheerleader style): a big, round, WIDE OPEN solid black eye with one white shine dot, just above the base of the beak. NO eyelid, NO line over the eye, NO white around it. A happy, cheerful, wide-awake look. NOT sleepy, NOT half-closed, NOT cool.

WHAT IT IS
A graceful, feminine tango dancer made of feathers, elegant like a woman dancing the Argentine tango at night.
MAINLY BLACK feathers (the Dark type color), glossy; deep red only in the fringe of the hem.
THE DRESS: the body and tail feathers form a typical tango dress: sleek and fitted to the body, long, ending just above the feet in a hem of long, uneven fringe-like feathers that swing. The fringe starts at different heights, some strands reaching higher up into the dress, so the hem is not one straight line. The fringe is deep red; the rest of the dress is black. It is the plumage itself, not clothing.
THE HAIR: the head feathers form the typical tango hairstyle: smooth and glossy, sleeked back tight into a neat round low bun at the back of the head.
It holds nothing, carries nothing.

POSE
Graceful and proud, clearly dancing the tango: standing tall on the toes, chin raised, one wing raised elegantly as if resting on a dance partner's shoulder, the other wing low along the body. The dress swings to one side.
THE LEGS: Oricorio legs, NOT human legs: two very thin, stick-like pink bird legs with small bird feet and toes. Only the lower part of the legs is visible, coming out from under the fringe; the dress covers the rest. One foot is pointed out to the side on its toes, the other stands. No thighs, no knees showing, no slit.

Draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): pixel art, 64×64, max 16 colors, dark selout outline, plain white background. Three-quarter view facing left, like every Gen 3 front sprite: far wing and far leg on the left, near ones on the right. 2 wings and 2 legs in total. Compact, fits in a square.
DO NOT
No ground, no shadow, no stage, no glow, no music notes, no effects. No human body parts (no arms, hands, fingers, hair strands): only feathers. No plush/Neopets look. No hat, no knife, no objects.
```
