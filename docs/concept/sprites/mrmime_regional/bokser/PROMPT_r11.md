# Bokser-Mr. Mime – startprompt r11 (23 nov, NIEUW gesprek, GEEN bijlage)
Pose-tekst van r10 (klopte). Rest = Jordi's punten bij r10.

```
Design a brand-new regional form of Mr. Mime: an old-time GENTLEMAN boxer, like the bare-knuckle boxers in 1880s black-and-white portrait photos. Fighting type.
Draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): pixel art, 64×64, max 16 colors, dark selout outline, Gen 3 shading with 3–4 tones per color, plain white background.
Draw ONE single Pokémon.

WHAT IT IS
It has Mr. Mime's head, face, body shape and proportions (NOT stocky, NOT a heavyweight). It differs from Mr. Mime in these points only:
- TORSO PATTERN: a V-shaped pattern on the torso, as if it has a bare upper body and you see its chest muscles — but clearly NOT a human body, just a simple Pokémon body pattern.
- HANDS: Mr. Mime's five fingers, wrapped in boxing tape. NO boxing gloves.
- HAIR: the two hair spikes keep the same length as Mr. Mime's, but are slicked a bit more back.
- A very small moustache.
- High-waisted old-fashioned dark boxing trunks as a pattern of its own body.
NO balls, spheres or round pads anywhere (not on the shoulders, hands or knees).

COLORS
Black-and-white palette, like an old photo and like a classic mime: white, greys and black.

POSE (most important)
It is NOT punching and NOT in a fighting stance: it stands still and proud, posing for an old portrait photo.
- Legs straight and close together, feet almost touching. No bent knees, no wide stance.
- The far arm is held out forward to the left, the elbow slightly bent, the fist raised a little above shoulder height and held still.
- The near arm is bent with the elbow out to the side, its fist resting against the middle of the chest.
- Chest out, chin up.

VIEW
Three-quarter view facing left, like every Gen 3 front sprite. Compact.

DO NOT
No ground, no ring, no shadow, no glow, no effects, no motion lines.
No cartoon line art, no plush/Neopets look, no chibi mascot, not a realistic human.
```
