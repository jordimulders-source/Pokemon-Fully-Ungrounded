Oorzaak (Claude): v2 veranderde alles tegelijk (kort, gedrongen, max. 2 wielen, geen wagons, "like Gengar") → speelgoedtreintje, en het genoemde gezicht werd gekopieerd. De kracht van r1-b (snelheid, rook die om de trein kolkt, geestenergie door het ijzer, grote silhouet) ging verloren.
Aanpak v3: terug naar de v1-prompt die r1-b gaf (zelfde opbouw, wagonblok b), alleen gericht veranderd: gezicht (geen horror, geen genoemde Pokémon), minder kleine details, kleuren (Jordi). Geen bijlage, nieuw gesprek. Liefst 2–3 keer draaien en de beste kiezen.

## Prompt

```
Design an original Pokémon and draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style pixel art).

TYPE: Steel / Ghost. Final stage of its line. A powerful, impressive final evolution.

CONCEPT: an old steam train that has been taken over by a spirit, a bit like how Rotom possesses machines.
- The locomotive is the head: it has a face in which you can clearly see that it is a ghost (the spirit shows through the locomotive's front).
- There are also hints of the ghost elsewhere on the train: greenish ghost energy running through the iron and swirling around it.
- The metal reads as old iron through texture, not as separate armour plates.
- The train is functional again, not a wreck: warm old iron with at most some tints of rust.
- The ghost has a nice contrasting greenish color against the iron.

FACE
- The face is a living creature's face with its own design: eyes and a mouth with a fierce, determined expression, like a Pokémon, not like a decoration.
- NOT a carved jack-o'-lantern grin, NOT a horror mask, NOT a skull, no jagged rows of teeth.

FULL SPEED (this must be obvious)
- The whole train leans forward, as if charging ahead at full speed.
- The wheels are drawn in motion like in a comic book.
- Show the speed with the train itself, not with effects around it.

WAGONS: behind the locomotive the wagons dissolve into smoke and ghostly mist, so the back of the creature becomes a trail of smoke instead of a row of wagons.

VIEW
- Semi-frontal: slightly more frontal than a three-quarter view, facing left. The locomotive is in front and closest to the viewer.

STYLE AND FORMAT
- 64x64 pixel sprite, the creature filling most of the canvas (final stage), compact silhouette that fits a square, max 16 colors, 1 px dark outline, light from top left, flat Gen 3 shading with 3 to 4 tones per color, no dithering.
- Gen 3 level of detail: big readable shapes, no rows of tiny rivets or tiny windows; small parts only where they read at 64x64.
- Plain white background. NO rails, floor, shadow, glow, speed lines, sparkles or effects outside the creature. One single creature, no extra views.

AVOID
- No skull or demon face on the locomotive, no ghost conductor or separate ghost characters riding the train, no small ghosts in the smoke.
- Not a toy train, not a cartoon / rubber-hose style, not a children's TV train with a friendly human face, not a plush toy, chibi or Neopets mascot. No glossy 3D render.
```

## Tips
- Wordt het weer een speelgoedtrein: "powerful, impressive final evolution, as big and heavy as Aggron" bovenaan.
- Weer horror: "fierce like Tyranitar's face, not scary like a mask".
