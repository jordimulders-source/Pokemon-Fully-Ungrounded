# Mr. Prime – startprompt r4 (24 nov, NIEUW gesprek, GEEN bijlage); basis = r3 (iets beter), alleen Jordi's punten bij r3 veranderd

```
Design a brand-new Pokémon: an old English gentleman who fights with his walking cane (bartitsu, the Victorian gentleman's martial art). Fighting/Psychic type. It is a fully evolved Pokémon in the style of Mr. Rime.
Draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): pixel art, 64×64, max 16 colors, dark selout outline, Gen 3 shading with 3–4 tones per color, plain white background.
Draw ONE single Pokémon. It is a Pokémon, NOT a human in clothes.

BODY (built like Mr. Rime)
- A big, round, egg-shaped torso.
- Thin legs of normal length, about as long as the torso. NOT long stilts. Small shoes.
- Thin arms with white hands.
- A white mime face with a round head.
- HAT AND HAIR ARE ONE: its hat IS its hair, exactly like Mr. Rime's hat. A dark top-hat shape on its head, with two hair spikes slicked backwards that grow out of the hat as one single connected shape. No separate hat sitting on the head, no separate hair spikes.
- A dark moustache, a bit bigger than a very small pencil moustache.
- A MONOCLE over one eye.
- The gentleman look comes ONLY from the shape and color of its own body, like Mr. Mime's body that only resembles a costume. NO clothing details at all: no collar, no bow tie, no buttons, no lapels, no vest, no jacket, no trousers.

COLORS
Black-and-white palette, like an old photo and like a classic mime: white, greys and black.

POSE (most important)
- A FIGHTER'S STANCE, ready to fight with the cane: back straight and upright like a rod, chin up, the body turned toward the opponent on the left. Legs in a short fencer's stance: the front foot a small step forward, knees only slightly bent. Not wide.
- One hand holds the cane by its end, raised, pointing forward to the left like a fencer's blade at the opponent. The other hand raised in front of the chest as a guard.
- COMPACT: elbows close to the body.

VIEW
Three-quarter view facing left, like every Gen 3 front sprite. Fits a square.

DO NOT
No ground, no shadow, no glow, no effects, no motion lines.
No cartoon line art, no plush/Neopets look, no chibi mascot, not a realistic human.
```
