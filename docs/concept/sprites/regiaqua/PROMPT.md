# Gemini-prompt Regiaqua r1 (nieuw gesprek, geen bijlage)

Eigen invulling van Claude (schrappen als Jordi het niet wil): "seven dots", "turquoise/cyan bioluminescent" als stipkleur (Jordi: "zo'n kleur" = diepzee-licht; welke tint niet gezegd).

```
Design a new legendary Pokémon as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): pixel art, 64x64, max 16 colors, dark outline, light from top-left, plain white background, no ground, no shadow, no effects.

WHAT IT IS: a new member of the Regi family of golem legendaries, living deep in an ocean trench. Like every Regi it is a faceless golem with no eyes and no mouth; its only "face" is a pattern of glowing dots in the middle of its body. It must clearly look like it belongs next to the other Regis (blocky golem build, wide shoulders, no separate head), but it is a new design, not a copy of any of them.

BODY: a whirlpool golem. The torso is a swirling whirlpool of deep-sea water. It has NO legs: the bottom of the whirlpool tapers down into the spinning funnel tip, and that is what it stands on. Two arms made of water float next to the body, detached from the torso with a visible gap (like Rayman's floating hands), at shoulder height. Compact, fits in a square.

THE DOTS: real round solid dots, the classic Regi dot pattern, in the middle of the torso. The dots glow like deep-sea bioluminescence (turquoise/cyan) and are NOT the main body color. They are dots, not bubbles and not a spiral.

POSE: 3/4 view facing left, upright and imposing like a Regi.

DO NOT: eyes, mouth, separate head, legs, feet, bubbles, fish, splash effects, water drops flying around, background.
```
