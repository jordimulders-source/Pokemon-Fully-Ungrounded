```
Design an original Pokémon as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style), in the style of Gen 3 Ghost Pokémon like Shuppet, Duskull and Gastly: NOT a cute cartoon or kawaii blob. Type: Steel/Ghost. Middle stage of a line: a pile of sentient train scrap → THIS → a racing ghost steam train.

THE CREATURE: a green ghost that has taken over an old rusty railway wagon and drives it with its own power.
- The wagon: a small old railway freight wagon made of RUSTY IRON/STEEL (not wood, NOT a mine cart, NOT a locomotive). Rickety and half falling apart: bent plates, a missing piece of the side, loose bolts, one side sagging.
- The ghost's power is what keeps the wagon together and moving, and it dominates the design:
  1. thick glowing green ghost energy pours through wide cracks and gaps in the iron, as if the wagon would fall apart without it;
  2. the wheels are made of green ghost vapour, with only a broken iron rim left;
  3. a large green ghost cloud streams out from under and behind the wagon, pushing it forward.
- The ghost rises out of the wagon; its lower body flows down into the wagon and becomes the green energy in the cracks and wheels, so ghost and wagon are one creature. It has exactly two arms: one hand grips the front rim of the wagon, the other arm is raised high in a cheer. Its mouth is wide open with a mischievous, cheeky grin: it is a prankster having the time of its life, full of character, like a real Pokémon with attitude. Not a happy child, not scary, not angry, not cute.

VIEW: semi-frontal, a little more frontal than a 3/4 view, facing left.

COLORS: wagon rust brown (#91745d, darker #5d4b3e) with a little dark grey, outline near-black brown (#292018). Ghost and all ghost parts in soft greens: light #b5f3b6, mid #72d779 / #67b370, dark #467e4b.

AVOID: cute/kawaii/chibi style, closed happy ^^ eyes, a happy-child look, a wooden crate, a mine cart, a locomotive, a face on the wagon, a human-like ghost, extra arms, too much detail.

FORMAT: 64x64 pixel sprite, max 16 colors, 1 px dark outline, flat Gen 3 shading, no dithering, compact silhouette, plain white background, no rails, floor, shadow or effects outside the creature.
```
