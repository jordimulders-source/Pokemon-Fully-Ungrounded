# Diepzee-legendary – Gemini r3, NIEUW gesprek (23 nov)
Jordi stuurde de bekende Shell Perdido-opname (2007, 7828 m diep; `../../referenties/magnapinna_perdido_2007_jordi.webp`): "je moet echt naar deze verwijzen". Geen reactie op Claude's voorstel "armen uit beeld"; dat zit er dus niet in.
Beschrijving van de foto (Claude): klein lijf met twee ronde vinnen bovenaan; armen recht horizontaal opzij als een kleerhanger/dwarsbalk; daaruit ±10 draaddunne armen die evenwijdig en extreem lang recht naar beneden hangen, onderaan iets naar elkaar toe. Hele vorm verticaal, hangend.
Eigen toevoeging Claude (r2 had geen wezen): ogen zichtbaar ("it is a creature, not an object").
Optioneel: de foto als bijlage (Jordi beslist; GEMINI.md zegt normaal geen referentiefoto's).

```
Design a new, original LEGENDARY Pokémon, Water type, from the deepest part of the ocean.
Draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): pixel art, 64×64, max 16 colors, dark selout outline, plain white background.

BASE IT ON THIS EXACT IMAGE
The famous 2007 deep-sea footage of the BIGFIN SQUID (Magnapinna), filmed by an oil-rig robot at 7800 m depth (Shell Perdido). Its silhouette is what makes it frightening: it looks fake, as if it cannot be real.
- At the top: a small body with two ROUND fins, like big rounded ears.
- Below the body, the arms go straight out sideways, perfectly horizontal, like a coat hanger or a crossbar.
- From that crossbar, about ten very thin arms hang STRAIGHT DOWN, parallel to each other, extremely long, many times longer than the body, coming slightly together near the bottom.
- The whole creature is vertical and hanging, like a mobile or a chandelier in the dark.

IT IS A CREATURE
It is a Pokémon, not an object: it has eyes on its small body, looking at the viewer.

COLORS
Like the famous video footage: pale, ghostly, almost translucent white with light tints.

VIEW
Three-quarter view facing left, like every Gen 3 front sprite. It fills the full height of the 64×64 frame: fins at the top edge, arm tips at the bottom edge.

DO NOT
Not a normal squid, not a jellyfish, not Tentacruel, not Nihilego, not Malamar, not an octopus.
Not two legs, not a table or stool shape.
No wavy or curly tentacles.
No effects, no glow, no bubbles, no water, no ground, no shadow.
No plush/Neopets look, no chibi mascot.
```
