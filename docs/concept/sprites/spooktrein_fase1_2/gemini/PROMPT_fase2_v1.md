# Gemini-prompt: spooktrein fase 2 (Steel/Ghost), v1 (16 nov)

Nieuw gesprek, **geen bijlage** (een bijlage wordt overgetrokken, `docs/lessen/GEMINI.md`); kleuren van het fase 3-model in woorden + hex.
Bron: Jordi 16 nov (SPEC.md): roestige wagon, geen mijnkar, krakkemikkig, half uit elkaar; groene geest erin, 1 hand op de rand, 1 hand juichend, mond wijd open; kar zichtbaar aangedreven door de geest (alle drie: groene barsten, spookwielen, geestwolk eronder); "heel blij spook"; semi-frontaal (lijn, 15 nov).

```
Design an original Pokémon as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style). Type: Steel/Ghost. It is the middle stage of a line: a pile of sentient train scrap → THIS → a racing ghost steam train.

THE CREATURE: a very happy green ghost riding in an old rusty railway wagon.
- The wagon: a small old railway freight wagon (NOT a mine cart, NOT a locomotive). Rusty and rickety, half falling apart: bent planks/plates, a missing piece of the side, loose bolts, one side sagging.
- The ghost sits inside the wagon, its upper body sticking out above the rim. It has exactly two arms: one hand rests on the front rim of the wagon, the other arm is raised high in a cheer. Its mouth is wide open in joy. It is overjoyed, not scary, not angry.
- The ghost's power visibly drives the wagon, in three ways at once:
  1. glowing green cracks run through the rusty metal, as if the ghost holds the wagon together;
  2. the wheels are partly made of green ghost vapour;
  3. a green ghost cloud streams out from under the wagon.

VIEW: semi-frontal, a little more frontal than a 3/4 view, facing left.

COLORS: wagon rust brown (#91745d, darker #5d4b3e) with a little dark grey, outline near-black brown (#292018). Ghost and all ghost parts in soft greens: light #b5f3b6, mid #72d779 / #67b370, dark #467e4b.

AVOID: a mine cart, a locomotive, a face on the wagon, a human-like ghost, Thomas-the-tank-engine style faces, extra arms, too much detail.

FORMAT: 64x64 pixel sprite, max 16 colors, 1 px dark outline, flat Gen 3 shading, no dithering, compact silhouette, plain white background, no rails, floor, shadow or effects outside the creature.
```
