```
Design a new Pokémon and draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): 64x64 pixel art, at most 16 colours, 1-pixel dark outline around every part, light from the top left, plain white background, no ground, no shadow, no sparkles.

The Pokémon is "Gengas", a Cosmic/Poison type based on a BROWN DWARF (a failed star): a small ball of gas, like a tiny gas giant planet. It is NOT a ghost and NOT related to Gengar; it only looks similar by coincidence (convergent evolution, like Wiglett and Diglett).

POSE: a true three-quarter view facing to the LEFT, like most Gen 3 front sprites: the face sits on the left part of the ball, the ball's right side curves away from the viewer, the near ear (left) is bigger and the far ear (right) smaller and partly behind the ball. The near hand and near foot (left) are bigger and in front; the far hand and far foot (right) are smaller and higher, partly hidden behind the ball.

FACE: use the attached Gengar sprite. The eyes and the wide grin must be exactly Gengar's (same shape, red eyes, white teeth, size), turned with the ball into the three-quarter view.

BODY: a round ball that clearly reads as GAS: soft swirling cloud bands like Jupiter, wavy flowing edges between the bands, one small swirling storm vortex, soft shading, soft highlight at the top left. No cracks, craters or stone texture on the body.
COLOUR: the real colours of a brown dwarf as the human eye would see it: dark dull red and deep magenta (sodium and potassium in its atmosphere absorb the green and yellow), with darker maroon-purple bands and lighter magenta-red bands. NOT brown, not orange, not pink.
EARS: two solid ears with exactly the shape of Gengar's ears (wide base, sharp point), made of the same swirling gas; only the tips trail off slightly like smoke.
LIMBS: exactly four limbs, two hands and two feet, shaped like Gengar's stubby hands (three blunt fingers) and feet, made as small grey rocky MOONS with craters, the only solid parts. All four float free with a clear gap of white background between each limb and the body; feet below the ball. Nothing touches the ball.
Compact so everything fits in a square.

AVOID: a front-facing pose, brown or orange colours, rock texture on the body, messy smoke strands, Gengar's blue-purple colour, spikes, limbs touching the body, extra limbs, rings like Saturn, stars, a cute plush or candy look.
```
