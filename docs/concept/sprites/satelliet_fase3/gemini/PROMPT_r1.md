# Ruimtestation (fase 3), Gemini-prompt r1 – NIEUW gesprek, geen bijlage
Basis: SPEC.md. Fase 1/2 niet bij naam en niet als bijlage (GEMINI.md: pre-evolutie trekt Gemini terug); hun uiterlijk in woorden (kernbol met oog uit fase 1; capsule met zonnepanelen uit fase 2, afgelezen uit `satelliet_fase2/af/satelliet.png`).
Eigen keuzes van Claude in deze prompt (Jordi niet gevraagd): ring als dikke witte buis met spaken (les Solrock-corona: "ring at an angle" gaf een Saturnusring), 2–3 satellietmodules op de ring, kleuren van fase 2. Jordi daarna: "belangrijk is dat het echt als een evolutie leest en goed te vertalen is naar een sprite ook" → kern = de capsule met lensoog van fase 2 (niet de kale bol), 2 modules, dikke spaken/ring, niets dunner dan de antennes. Ogen op de modules bewust NIET genoemd (Jordi: "eerst de vorm van het ruimtestation zien").

```
Design a Pokémon: an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style), 64×64 pixels, max 16 colors, clean dark outline, plain white background, no ground, no shadow, no effects or glow.

WHAT IT IS: a living space station shaped like a wheel. It is a pure machine with its own personality, the way Magneton, Metagross and Porygon are Pokémon: no animal parts, no arms, no legs, no mouth. The station itself is the creature's body.

POSE AND ANGLE (most important): Gen 3 front-sprite 3/4 view facing the viewer's LEFT, seen slightly from above. The wheel is tilted toward the viewer, so it looks like a wide oval; the near part of the ring is at the bottom right and bigger, the far part at the top left and thinner. Everything is drawn in this one perspective. The creature is big and fills most of the 64×64 frame, but stays compact in a square.

THE HUB (center, must clearly read as the same creature grown bigger): a white capsule body with lavender shading whose front is a big round dome holding one big camera-lens eye: from outside to inside a thin dark line, a white ring, a purple ring, a dark ring, a turquoise iris and a dark pupil, flat colors. Round purple ball joints on the capsule. The hub is the clear focus, but only part of the whole creature.

THE RING: a thick, solid, white tube that forms a closed wheel around the hub, with lavender shading on its underside and a few purple bands where segments join. It is a physical structure, not a line of light. Three thick purple spokes (clearly thicker than the antennae) connect the ring to the hub.

MODULES ON THE RING: exactly two satellites are built into the ring as modules, evenly spaced. Each is a white capsule with purple ball joints and a pair of small dark navy-purple solar panels with a lighter purple grid, sticking out from the ring.

ON TOP: two long purple antennae (not hair-thin) with a round purple ball on each tip, rising from the top of the hub and curving backward.

DETAIL LEVEL: it must translate well to a 64×64 sprite: big readable shapes, thick parts, medium detail, nothing thinner than the antennae. Solar panels: a simple grid of a few large cells. No rivets, no text, no logos, no tiny windows or greebles.

COLORS: pearly white with lavender shading, purple accents (joints, spokes, antennae, seam), dark navy-purple solar panels with lighter purple grid lines, turquoise iris as the only accent. Light from the upper left. Nothing else.

DO NOT: a planet with a Saturn ring, a glowing or energy ring, starfield or galaxy patterns, stars or Earth around it, a flat front view or side view, realistic NASA/ISS hardware, a robot with a face screen, arms or legs, cute plush/Neopets style.
```
