# Dundragoon – Gemini-startprompt r4 (21 nov, nieuw gesprek, geen bijlage)

Na r3: "staart, vleugels, hoofd goed, maar wil geen slang"; lijf: Dunsparce-romp, dik; "al mag de drill naar verhouding nog wat groter". Hoofd, vleugels en staart in woorden zoals ze in r3 waren.

---

```
Design a brand-new Pokémon: a lazy, goofy dragon. Type Dragon/Normal. It is the evolution of Dunsparce, but it is a new creature, not a copy of Dunsparce and NOT a Dragonite.
Draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): pixel art, 64×64, max 16 colors, dark selout outline, plain white background.

WHAT IT IS
Body: Dunsparce's sausage-shaped torso, but much THICKER and HEAVIER, a big chunky body standing on 2 short, stubby legs. No long neck: the head sits on a short, thick neck right at the front of the body. It is NOT a snake and NOT an upright standing biped.
Head: a dragon head with a longer snout and 2 small straight pale blue-white horns pointing backward. The blue ring around the eye stays from Dunsparce; inside it the eye is closed in a lazy line. A blue marking along the jaw. Expression lazy and goofy, not fierce.
Colors: pale yellow body with a few blue bands/patches across the back. No light belly plate, no belly stripes.
Legs: 2 short, stubby legs. NO arms, no hands.
Tail: a short, thick tail that ends in a BIG, CLEAR DRILL: a spiral, cone-shaped drill tip, large in proportion to the body, flowing seamlessly from the tail, no seam between tail and drill.
Wings: 2 wings (near and far) on the back, pale blue-white, based on Dunsparce's small wings but bigger, with soft rounded feather-like lobes; quite bizarre and unusual, NOT typical dragon wings (no bat wings).

POSE
The body runs DIAGONALLY across the sprite: head at the upper left, drill tail at the lower right.

VIEW
Three-quarter view facing left, like every Gen 3 front sprite: the far wing and far leg are on the left (narrower, partly hidden), the near ones on the right.
Compact: the whole Pokémon fits inside a square.

DO NOT
Not a snake: no long thin body, no long neck, no S-curve.
Not Dragonite: no upright standing pose, no arms, no pale striped belly, no curled antennae.
No bat wings, no typical dragon wings. No beard, no whiskers.
No ground, no rock, no shadow under it. No glow, no effects.
No plush/Neopets look, no chibi mascot, not a Western knight dragon, not a realistic reptile.
```
