# Gemini-prompt: Uku Pacha (slang-legendary), v1 (27 nov)

Nieuw gesprek, geen bijlage. Eisen en citaten: `../SPEC.md` (daar ook wat Claude heeft ingevuld).

```
Design an original LEGENDARY Pokémon and draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style). Type: Water/Ghost. It is the serpent of the underworld.

WHAT IT IS (most important)
- A colossal GREEN ANACONDA-like serpent (real anaconda anatomy: very thick, heavy body; eyes and nostrils on top of a broad head; a big, powerful water snake) with TWO HEADS: the neck forks into two necks, and the two heads sit side by side.
- Small wings behind the heads, where the neck splits. Small, not big dragon wings.
- The rear of its body and its tail do not end in a normal tail: they fade and dissolve into a ghostly, watery spirit form, as if the serpent is rising up out of the underworld.

POSE
- The front body and the two heads rise up out of a coil, three-quarter view facing left. The whole figure fits in a square, compact like a Gen 3 sprite, not a long horizontal snake.
- Both heads fully visible. Powerful and imposing.

COLOR AND FEEL
- Deep blues and grays that bring water and spirit together. Clear contrast between the tones: NOT a muddy gray mush.
- It must look like a box-art legendary, a cover legendary of a main Pokémon game. A bold, simple, instantly readable silhouette, like an official Ken Sugimori legendary design.

STYLE AND FORMAT
- Official Gen 3 front sprite, 64x64, max 16 colors, 1 px dark outline, light from top left, flat Gen 3 shading with 3 to 4 tones per color, no dithering.
- A huge legendary that fills almost the whole 64x64 frame.
- Plain white background. NO floor, rock, water surface, shadow, light rays, glow, sparkles or effects. One single creature.

AVOID
- Not Rayquaza, Gyarados, Milotic, Hydreigon, Eternatus, Seviper, Arbok or a Chinese dragon. No legs or arms.
- No starfield or galaxy pattern. No mask, crown, jewelry, armor or symbols.
- Not a realistic painting, not a plush or western-cartoon look, no glossy 3D render.
```
