## Prompt

```
Good start, keep the blobfish face, the pink colours and the pixel style. Now push it much further:
- MORE PUDDING: the body must read clearly as a pudding turned out of its mould: a tall, soft, wobbly mound with a flatter top, sagging and bulging out at the bottom.
- MORE MELTED: it is melting under its own weight: the lower half spreads out wide in thick, drooping, puddle-like folds that flow over the ground line; the nose and cheeks sag down much lower.
- GRUMPIER: a much more sour, fed-up face: heavy drooping eyelids, eyes half closed and looking annoyed, the mouth pulled down hard at the corners.
- MORE EXAGGERATED: exaggerate all of this like a caricature; the drooping nose bigger and heavier, the sagging stronger.
Still a Gen 3 front sprite, 64x64, at most 16 colours, three-quarter view facing left, white background, no shadow, no plate, no spoon, no dessert decorations.
```
