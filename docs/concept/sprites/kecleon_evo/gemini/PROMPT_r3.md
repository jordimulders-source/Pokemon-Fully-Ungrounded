# Kecleon-evolutie – Gemini-startprompt r3 (22 nov, NIEUW gesprek, geen bijlage)

r2, Jordi: "de kleuren zijn echt gruwelijk lelijk, wat vind je van het model verder?". Voorstel Claude: kop + krulstaart van r2, zwaar landleguaanlijf van r1, grote rustige kleurvlakken. Jordi: "akkoord, geel en oranje als hoofdkleur, groen gaat weg". Roze blijft (Jordi, 22 nov: "een van de andere kleuren wordt roze").

```
Design a brand-new Pokémon: the evolution of Kecleon. Type Grass/Fire. It is a new creature, not a copy of Kecleon, but you must clearly recognize Kecleon in it.
Draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): pixel art, 64×64, max 16 colors, dark selout outline, plain white background.

HEAD AND TAIL (from Kecleon)
The whole HEAD SHAPE is comparable to Kecleon's head: Kecleon's EYES, Kecleon's MOUTH and Kecleon's longer CREST on the head (with a rounded top edge, not cut off flat). The tail is Kecleon's CURLED SPIRAL TAIL.

BODY (an evolution: bigger and heavier than Kecleon)
Animal anatomy: a GALÁPAGOS LAND IGUANA (Conolophus subcristatus): a heavy, stocky, thick iguana body, clearly bigger and sturdier than Kecleon, a row of spines along the neck and back, strong thick clawed limbs. It lives on volcanic islands.

COLORS
Main colors: YELLOW and ORANGE, like a real land iguana heated on lava rock. One of the other colors is PINK. NO GREEN anywhere.
Large calm areas of color, not patches or blotches. Less detail: simple and clean, few markings. No actual fire.

POSE
Standing upright on its two hind legs, like Kecleon does, leaning slightly forward. Two arms: the far arm on the left (partly hidden), the near arm on the right, not overlapping in a tangle.

VIEW
Three-quarter view facing left, like every Gen 3 front sprite: the far arm and leg on the left (partly hidden), the near ones on the right.
Compact: the whole Pokémon fits inside a square, limbs close to the body.

DO NOT
No flames, no fire, no glow, no aura, no effects, no motion lines.
No ground, no rock, no cactus prop, no shadow under it.
Not a dragon, not a dinosaur.
No plush/Neopets look, no chibi mascot, not a realistic animal painting.
```
