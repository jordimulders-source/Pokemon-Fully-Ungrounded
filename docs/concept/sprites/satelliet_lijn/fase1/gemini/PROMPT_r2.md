# Satelliet-lijn fase 1 – startprompt r2 (23 nov), NIEUW gesprek, geen bijlage
Jordi over r1: "conceptueel prima idee, maar dit is teveel detail". Zelfde concept als r1, details eruit. Jordi (vóór verzenden): "en het mag nog net iets meer pokemon qua kleur gebruik".

```
Design a new, original Pokémon.
Draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): pixel art, 64×64, max 16 colors, dark selout outline, plain white background.
Keep it SIMPLE, like a first-stage Gen 3 Pokémon (Voltorb, Shuppet, Wurmple): big clean shapes, very few details.
Use colors the way official Pokémon do: clean, bright, a bit more saturated than real metal, with one or two clear accent colors that make it read as a Pokémon, not as a realistic machine. Not just grey.

WHAT IT IS
A small living machine: a tiny early satellite like Sputnik. A smooth, round metal ball with four long, thin, plain antennas sweeping back from it.
It is a pure machine with its own personality, the way Klink, Magnemite and Porygon are Pokémon: no animal parts, no arms, no legs.
It has exactly ONE big eye: a single camera lens in the front of the ball, with one simple ring around it. Curious expression.

KEEP OUT (too much detail)
No panel lines or seams on the ball, no rivets, no patches or spots.
No dishes, knobs or tips on the antennas: just plain thin rods.
Exactly four antennas, all the same.
Only one ring around the eye.

POSE
It floats in the air, tilted slightly as if looking up curiously. The antennas trail behind it.

VIEW
Three-quarter view facing left, like every Gen 3 front sprite.
It is small: it fills about half of the 64×64 frame.

DO NOT
No effects, no glow, no electricity, no stars or planets around it.
No ground, no shadow.
Not Magnemite, not Voltorb, not a Poké Ball, not a robot with a face screen.
No plush/Neopets look.
```
