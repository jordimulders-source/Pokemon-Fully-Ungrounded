# Guaraná fase 2 – startprompt r1 (26 nov), NIEUW gesprek, geen bijlage
Basis: SPEC.md (fase 1 + fase 2) en wat in fase 1 werkte (prompt r2 + r5: groene gladde liaan, simpele ranken, geen krulletjes). Eigen toevoegingen van Claude: compact in het vierkant, ogen ±4–5 px.

```
Design a new, original Pokémon.
Draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): pixel art, 64×64, max 16 colors, dark selout outline, plain white background.

WHAT IT IS
A Grass/Ghost Pokémon based on the guaraná plant from the Amazon.
Its body IS a creeping vine: a living green vine that crawls over the ground like an animal. It has no animal or human body parts at all; the ONLY animal-like features are its eyes. That is what makes it creepy.
The vine branches: from one thick main vine, two or three branches rise up, and each branch carries its own cluster of guaraná fruits, like several heads.

THE FRUITS (must clearly read as guaraná, not a rose, raspberry or grapes)
Every fruit has burst open. Each fruit is a small, round, smooth, glossy red capsule that has split open along its seam; the red skin peels back like two lips around what is inside.
Inside each open fruit sits an eye: a white ball with a big black pupil, exactly like a real ripe guaraná fruit (black seed in white flesh). The red split skin frames every eye.
The eyes are clearly visible at sprite size (about 4-5 pixels wide) and all stare straight at the viewer.
Mood: unsettling. The eyes stare and follow you, but it is still a Pokémon you would want on your team. Not gory.

THE VINE (reads well as a sprite)
Smooth green vine, not woody, not brown. One clear main body with two or three readable branches, not a tangle.
A few simple tendrils grip the ground like crawling limbs, close to the body. Only a few leaves. No decorative curls or spirals.
Compact: the whole Pokémon fits in a square shape and fills almost the whole 64×64 frame.

POSE
It crawls toward the viewer, the fruit clusters raised and turned to you, as if they all just noticed you.

VIEW
Three-quarter view facing left, like every Gen 3 front sprite.

DO NOT
No glow, no effects, no ghostly aura, no floating particles.
No ground, no shadow, no pot.
No mouth, no teeth, no blood, no face other than the eyes.
Not a human or child figure, not a snake, not a flower, not a bunch of grapes, not a tree.
No plush/Neopets look.
```
