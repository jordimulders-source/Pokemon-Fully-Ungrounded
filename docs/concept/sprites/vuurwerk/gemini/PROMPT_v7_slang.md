Nieuw gesprek, geen bijlage. Alleen het hoofd is veranderd t.o.v. v6.

(Oorzaak: Claudes toevoeging "face in the center of the burst" in v4–v6 gaf steeds een kop in een kraag.)

## Jordi (letterlijk, 16 nov)
- Over v5: "het is wel oke, maar zo saaitjes"
- Uit Claudes voorstellen: "1, 2 en 4, maak v6" = (1) spiraal omhoog als een lancerende vuurpijl, barst op het hoogste punt; (2) twee à drie kleinere barsten langs de rug (meertrapsvuurwerk); (4) lijf met de print van vuurpijlpapier (banden/strepen rood/roze/geel).
- Blijft: "de staart 1 vuurpijl, die richt naar boven, of half naar boven"; hoofd = veelkleurige vuurwerkexplosie; geel/rood/roze; Fire/Fairy; verjaagt boze geesten.

## Keuzes Claude (schrappen mag)

## Prompt

```
Design an original Pokémon and draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style).

CONCEPT
- Fire/Fairy type. A SERPENT MADE OF FIREWORKS, caught at the moment of launch: its body SPIRALS UPWARD in a corkscrew, like a firework rocket shooting into the sky.
- ITS HEAD IS MADE OF FIRE at the top of the spiral: the serpent head itself is shaped out of flames and firework burst, with flame points forming the snout, the jaws and the back of the head. There is NO separate head sticking out of a fiery collar or star; the fire IS the head. Only the eyes and the open mouth are drawn into the flame.
- ALONG ITS BACK sit two or three SMALLER FIREWORK BURSTS, like a multi-break firework shell: small star-shaped bursts growing from the body as ornaments.
- ITS BODY IS PATTERNED LIKE FIREWORK ROCKET WRAPPING PAPER: bold bands and diagonal stripes in red, pink and yellow, so you can see it is a firework.
- THE TAIL IS ONE FIREWORK ROCKET, pointing upward or half upward: the end of the body becomes a rocket casing with a pointed tip.
- No arms and no legs: a true serpent.
- A few separate teardrop-shaped sparks fly outward from the body, close to it.
- Colors: YELLOW, RED AND PINK only, with bright yellow-white at the cores of the bursts.
- It chases away evil spirits: a bold, defiant, protective expression.
- Final stage of a three-stage line (sparkler -> ground spinner -> firework rocket).

STYLE AND FORMAT
- Official Gen 3 front sprite, 64x64, max 16 colors, 1 px dark outline, light from top left, flat Gen 3 shading with 3 to 4 tones per color, no dithering.
- Three-quarter view facing left. Compact composition that fits a square; the spiral is tight.
- Plain white background. NO floor, shadow or smoke. One single creature.

AVOID
- No collar, no mane, no star or halo behind the head. Not a Chinese dragon, no horns, no whiskers. Not Rayquaza, not Gyarados. Not a rainbow, no green or blue. No text. No glossy 3D render.
```

## Tips
- Spiraal wordt een warboel: "one clear corkscrew with two and a half turns".
- Barsten op de rug worden vleugels: "the small bursts are round star shapes, not wings or fins".
