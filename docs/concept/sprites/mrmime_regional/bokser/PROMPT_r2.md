# Bokser-Mr. Mime – Gemini-startprompt r2 (22 nov, nieuw gesprek, geen bijlage)
Alleen Jordi's punten bij r1: meer gentlemanly, kleur meer afwijkend van Mr. Mime, zwart-wit palet, oren meer achterovergekamd. Houding: Jordi stuurde een 19e-eeuwse boksersfoto ("deze houding"), in woorden overgenomen. Snor/accent: niet beantwoord, dus niet erin.

```
Design a brand-new regional form of Mr. Mime: an old-time GENTLEMAN boxer, like the heavyweights in black-and-white boxing photos. Fighting type.
Draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): pixel art, 64×64, max 16 colors, dark selout outline, plain white background.
Draw ONE single Pokémon.

WHAT IT IS
A mime that became a boxer: shadowboxing is miming a fight against an invisible opponent. It has the dignified, gentlemanly bearing of a boxer from the old days.
The BODY SHAPE is completely different from Kanto Mr. Mime (just like Galarian Mr. Mime has its own body): not slim with thin limbs, but short and stocky, with a thick neck, heavy shoulders, a barrel chest and short, thick forearms.
On top of that body: Mr. Mime's head and face, with the two hair spikes slicked far back along the head.
It wears NO clothes: like Mr. Rime's tuxedo, its high-waisted old-fashioned boxing trunks are a pattern of its own body.
Hands: NO boxing gloves. Bare fists in white hands (or wrapped in tape).
NO pink balls anywhere.

COLORS
A BLACK-AND-WHITE palette, like an old boxing photo and like a classic mime: white, greys and black. Clearly different colors from Kanto Mr. Mime (no blue, no pink).

POSE
The classic 1880s boxer photo pose: standing UPRIGHT and still, feet planted, chest out, chin up, proud.
One arm stretched forward, fist out in front of it at shoulder height; the other fist held close against its chest.

VIEW
Three-quarter view facing left, like every Gen 3 front sprite. Compact: fits inside a square.

DO NOT
No boxing gloves, no skirt, no tunic, no robe, no fabric.
No ground, no ring, no shadow, no glow, no effects, no motion lines. Only one Pokémon.
No plush/Neopets look, no chibi mascot, not a realistic human.
```
