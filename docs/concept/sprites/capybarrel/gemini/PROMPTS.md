# Capybarrel (fase 1, Normal): Gemini-prompts r1 (23 nov)
Besluiten en citaten: `docs/concept/IDEEEN.md` → "Capybarrel". Vast: puur Normal, 2 fases (evolutie = houten kart, ook puur Normal), geen water.
Open (niet door Jordi gezegd): lijf ís de ton (A) of capibara zit in de ton (B). Daarom twee varianten; elk in een eigen nieuw gesprek, zonder bijlage.

## Variant A: het lijf is de ton
```
Design an original Pokemon called CAPYBARREL. Type: Normal. First stage of a two-stage line.

Concept: a capybara whose barrel-shaped body IS a wooden barrel. The anatomy is a real capybara (Hydrochoerus hydrochaeris): large blunt square head, small rounded ears high on the head, small eyes high on the head, big flat nose, short sturdy legs with small feet. The body between head and legs is not fur but a wooden barrel: vertical wooden staves, two dark iron hoops around the belly, a small round bung hole with a wooden plug on the side. The head and legs grow out of the barrel naturally; it is one creature, not an animal wearing or sitting in a barrel.

Fur: warm reddish-brown capybara fur on head and legs. Barrel: warm brown wood with darker grain lines.

Expression: the typical calm capybara face.

Style: official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style), 64x64, max 16 colors, dark outline, facing 3/4 to the left, compact composition with legs close to the body. White background, no ground, no shadow, no effects.

AVOID: plush toy / Neopets look, beaver or Bidoof/Bibarel look, a cartoon barrel with a face, Donkey Kong barrel, water, hot spring, fruit on its head.
```

## Variant B: de capibara zit in de ton
```
Design an original Pokemon called CAPYBARREL. Type: Normal. First stage of a two-stage line.

Concept: a capybara (Hydrochoerus hydrochaeris) that lives in a wooden barrel. The barrel is part of the Pokemon, like a shell. The capybara's head and front legs stick out of the open top of an upright wooden barrel; the barrel has vertical wooden staves and two dark iron hoops. Real capybara anatomy: large blunt square head, small rounded ears high on the head, small eyes high on the head, big flat nose, short sturdy front legs with small feet.

Fur: warm reddish-brown capybara fur. Barrel: warm brown wood with darker grain lines.

Expression: the typical calm capybara face.

Style: official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style), 64x64, max 16 colors, dark outline, facing 3/4 to the left, compact composition. White background, no ground, no shadow, no effects.

AVOID: plush toy / Neopets look, beaver or Bidoof/Bibarel look, a cartoon barrel with a face, Donkey Kong barrel, water, hot spring, fruit on its head.
```

## r2 (nieuw gesprek, zonder bijlage)
```
Design an original Pokemon called CAPYBARREL. Type: Normal. First stage of a two-stage line.

Concept: just a capybara in Pokemon style, with a wooden barrel around its body, but it can still walk.

The capybara stands on all four legs in a natural walking stance. A wooden barrel lies horizontally around its torso, like a tube: the head sticks out of the front opening, the rump out of the back opening, and the four short legs come out underneath. The barrel has vertical wooden staves and two dark iron hoops.

Anatomy: a real capybara (Hydrochoerus hydrochaeris): large blunt square head, small rounded ears high on the head, small eyes high on the head, big flat nose, short sturdy legs with small feet. Drawn as a Pokemon designed by Ken Sugimori: stylized, simplified shapes and clean forms, NOT a realistic capybara.

Fur: warm reddish-brown capybara fur. Barrel: warm brown wood with darker grain lines.

Style: official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style), 64x64, max 16 colors, dark outline, facing 3/4 to the left, compact composition. White background, no ground, no shadow, no effects.

AVOID: plush toy / Neopets look, beaver or Bidoof/Bibarel look, standing upright, sitting inside an upright barrel, a cartoon barrel with a face, water, hot spring, fruit on its head.
```

## r3 (nieuw gesprek, zonder bijlage)
Uit de early-route-knagers (Rattata, Sentret, Zigzagoon, Bidoof, Patrat, Skwovet) gehaald: grotere kop t.o.v. lijf, grote ogen met glimlicht, zichtbare voortanden, simpele vormen, stevige pootjes. Geen namen bij het gezicht (Gemini kopieert dan).
```
Design an original Pokemon called CAPYBARREL. Type: Normal. First stage of a two-stage line, an early-route rodent Pokemon.

Concept: a capybara Pokemon with a wooden barrel around its body; it can still walk.

Pose: standing on all four legs in a walking stance. A wooden barrel lies horizontally around its torso, like a tube: the head sticks out of the front opening, the rump out of the back opening, and the four short legs come out underneath. The barrel has vertical wooden staves and two dark iron hoops.

Make it look like a real Pokemon, at the same level of stylization as the early-route rodent Pokemon of the main games, but do not copy any of them:
- the head is big compared to the body (about as tall as the barrel);
- big, expressive eyes with a white highlight, set high on the head;
- two front teeth visible under the big flat nose;
- small rounded ears, short chunky legs with small rounded feet;
- simple, clean shapes with few details; no realistic fur texture.
It must still read as a capybara: blunt square muzzle, heavy calm build.

Fur: warm reddish-brown. Barrel: warm brown wood with darker grain lines.

Style: official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style), 64x64, max 16 colors, dark outline, facing 3/4 to the left, compact composition. White background, no ground, no shadow, no effects.

AVOID: realistic animal look, plush toy / Neopets look, beaver look, standing upright, sitting inside an upright barrel, a cartoon barrel with a face, water, hot spring, fruit on its head.
```
