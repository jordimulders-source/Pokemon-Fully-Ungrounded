# Ruimtestation (fase 3), Gemini-prompt r5 – NIEUW gesprek, geen bijlage
r4 (`r4.png`), Jordi: "dat is al wat beter, maar ik vind hem nog iets te simpel". Inspiratie van Jordi (`../inspiratie/`: Tiangong-render, ISS-foto, ISS-boekomslag): "Deze komen dichter in de buurt, maar we moeten het wel zo maken dat het goed naar een sprite vertaalt".
Basis = prompt r4 (werkte bijna). Wijzigingen (uit de inspiratie gelezen door Claude): kruisvorm met een centraal knooppunt i.p.v. één rechte balk; modules van verschillende maten met donkerpaarse banden; meer panelen (3 paren, lang en smal zoals op de foto's). Sprite-eisen: panelen ≥ 3 px breed op 64 px, weinig grote cellen, geen kleine onderdelen. Foto's niet als bijlage (GEMINI.md: geen referentiefoto's), in woorden.

```
Design a Pokémon: an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style), 64×64 pixels, max 16 colors, clean dark outline, plain white background, no ground, no shadow, no effects or glow.

WHAT IT IS: a big living space station, the final evolution of a small one-eyed satellite. It is a pure machine with its own personality, the way Magneton, Metagross and Porygon are Pokémon: no animal parts, no arms, no legs, no mouth.

SIZE AND COMPLEXITY (most important): this is a LARGE, heavy, final-stage Pokémon, clearly more complex than a single satellite or a straight line of modules. It fills the WHOLE 64×64 frame from edge to edge. Its eye-sphere is only a SMALL part of the whole creature (about one fifth of its width).

POSE AND ANGLE: Gen 3 front-sprite 3/4 view facing the viewer's LEFT, seen slightly from above. Everything in this one perspective. Compact in a square.

BUILD (a cross-shaped station): in the middle is a round white node sphere. From it, white cylinder modules branch out in FOUR directions like a cross: a long main arm toward the front-left, a long arm toward the back-right, and two shorter side arms. The modules have different sizes (some wide and short, some long and slim) and each has one or two dark purple bands around it. At the end of the side arms sits a smaller white sphere. All joints are purple ring-shaped docking collars. Nothing floats loose: it is one solid connected machine.
THE EYE-SPHERE (at the end of the front-left arm, nearest to the viewer, the focus point): one big camera-lens eye: from outside to inside a thin dark line, a white ring, a purple ring, a dark ring, a turquoise iris and a dark pupil, flat colors. Two long purple antennae (not hair-thin) with a round purple ball on each tip rise from it and curve backward.

SOLAR PANELS: THREE pairs of long, narrow, flat rectangular solar panel wings sticking out at right angles from the arms (one pair near the front, one pair at the node, one pair at the back), dark navy-purple with a lighter purple grid of a few large cells. Long like on a real space station, but each panel still clearly wide (never thin strips). All in the same 3/4 perspective; the far ones smaller and partly hidden.

DETAIL LEVEL: it must translate well to a 64×64 sprite: big readable shapes, thick parts, medium detail, nothing thinner than the antennae. No rivets, no text, no logos, no flags, no tiny windows, dishes or greebles.

COLORS: pearly white with lavender shading, purple accents (collars, bands, rods, antennae), dark navy-purple solar panels with lighter purple grid lines, turquoise iris as the only accent. Light from the upper left. Nothing else.

DO NOT: a single satellite with two panels, one straight line of modules, a small creature with empty space around it, a ring or wheel, thin orbit lines, starfield or galaxy patterns, stars or Earth around it, a space shuttle, a flat front view or side view, realistic NASA/ISS hardware, a robot with a face screen, arms or legs, cute plush/Neopets style.
```
