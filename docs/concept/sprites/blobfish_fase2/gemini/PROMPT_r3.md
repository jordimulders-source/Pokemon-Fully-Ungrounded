## Prompt

```
Better, keep the melted puddle base, the drooping nose, the grumpy mouth, the colours and the pixel style. Only two changes:
- EYES: the eyes look too human. Make them fish eyes: small, round, dark beady eyes like a real blobfish, without human eyelids, eyelashes or eye whites.
- TOP: the top must stay ROUND: a smooth rounded dome, not a flat top and no sharp edge around it.
Everything else stays the same. Gen 3 front sprite, 64x64, at most 16 colours, three-quarter view facing left, white background.
```
