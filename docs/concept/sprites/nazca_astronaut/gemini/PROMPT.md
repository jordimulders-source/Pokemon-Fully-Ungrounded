# Nazca-astronaut – Gemini-startprompt r2 (nieuw gesprek, geen bijlage)

r1 (`r1/`): Jordi: "vreselijk. Lijkt totaal niet op de nazca lijnen, plus het lijkt niet op een counterpart van sigilyph". Oorzaak (Claude): "small humanoid", "owl-like", "waving", "short body" en driekwart gaven een schattige mascotte (chibi uil-kind met snavel en vingers).
r2 (voorstellen Claude, niet van Jordi): de geoglief letterlijk als bouwplan (cirkelkop, cirkelogen, geen mond, stijve lijnledematen); Sigilyph's bouw (stijf, frontaal, symmetrisch, zwevend, totem) in woorden; frontaal zoals Lunatone/Solrock/Claydol in Gen 3; kleuren nog open, alleen "niet die van Sigilyph".
Werkt dit niet: r3 met Sigilyph's sprite als bijlage ("this is SIGILYPH, the counterpart").

```
Design a new Pokémon that is the geoglyph "El Astronauta" (the Owlman) of Nazca, Peru, come to life. Type: Cosmic/Normal.
It is the counterpart of SIGILYPH (the Nazca bird geoglyph Pokémon), the way LUNATONE is the counterpart of SOLROCK: clearly made by the same hand and in the same style, but contrasting. It is its own creature, not Sigilyph.
Draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): pixel art, 64×64, max 16 colors, dark selout outline, plain white background.

BUILD IT FROM THE GEOGLYPH
The geoglyph is made of simple thick lines: a big perfectly round head; two big round eyes drawn as circles inside the head; NO mouth, no beak, no nose; a simple straight body; one arm raised up, one arm pointing down, both bent at sharp angles like lines; two straight legs with simple feet.
Keep that look: geometric, flat-feeling shapes, stiff straight limbs made of line segments, not soft round flesh.

BUILD IT LIKE SIGILYPH
Sigilyph is not an animal: it is a stiff, symmetrical, floating totem-like figure built from geometric parts, with ancient patterns (zigzag bands, eye-like spots) and limbs that look like ornaments. This creature is built the same way: stiff, ancient, otherworldly, a living artifact, not a cute animal.
It floats above the ground, like Sigilyph.
Use DIFFERENT colors than Sigilyph (not black with yellow, red, blue and green).

VIEW
Facing the viewer, symmetrical apart from the arms, like the Gen 3 sprites of Lunatone, Solrock and Claydol. Compact, fits a square.

DO NOT
Not cute, not a mascot, not a child, not chibi, no big shiny anime eyes, no smile.
Not an owl or bird: no beak, no feathers.
No fingers or hands with fingers; no human in a space suit, no helmet, no robot.
No ground, no lines on the ground, no shadow, no glow, no stars, no effects.
```
