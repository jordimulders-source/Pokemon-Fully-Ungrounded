# Gemini-prompt: Banette-evolutie, v3 (17 nov)

Nieuw gesprek; bijlagen `bijlage_banette.png` en `bijlage_shuppet.png` (niet r1/r2).
Wijzigingen (Jordi over r2): narrenmutspunten, imposanter, enger/sinisterder, lijf sluit aan op het kledingstuk, Shuppets groter, kruizen sluiten aan op de armen. Kledingstuk-tekst van v2 blijft (Jordi: "nu wel beter").

## Keuzes Claude (niet besproken; schrappen mag)
- Imposant: volwassen verhoudingen (lang lijf, lange armen, hoofd relatief kleiner dan bij Banette) en hij buigt over de kijker heen.
- Sinister: ogen half dichtgeknepen met zware wenkbrauwrand, grijns breed tot aan de wangen.
- Shuppets ongeveer een kwart van zijn hoogte, eenvoudige vorm.

```
Design an original Pokémon: the evolution of BANETTE (Ghost/Dark). Attached: the Gen 3 sprite of BANETTE (its pre-evolution) and the Gen 3 sprite of SHUPPET (Banette's own pre-evolution).

CONCEPT
- A truly evil, scary ghost puppet master: imposing and sinister, a villain. NOT cute.
- The zipper mouth of Banette has been opened and this new creature has climbed out of it. He is much bigger than Banette.
- Proportions: adult, not chibi. A tall body with long arms; the head is smaller relative to the body than Banette's. He looms and leans forward over the viewer.
- Banette's former body hangs around his waist like a garment, with the open zipper around his waist. The garment clearly reads as Banette's old body (its shape, its little arms, its tail with the yellow tip), worn like an empty costume. Banette's eyes and face are NOT on it.
- His torso grows directly out of the open zipper: body and garment connect seamlessly, no gap, no floating parts.

HEAD
- Two long points on his head like the points of a jester's hat, so he looks like an evil puppet master / joker.
- A very sinister grin, wide up to the cheeks. The mouth is NOT a zipper: a zigzag mouth with teeth, the teeth the same colour as his head.
- Eyes similar to Banette's eyes, but larger, narrowed and menacing under a heavy brow.

ARMS AND HANDS
- He holds both arms up high.
- His hands ARE marionette control crosses (the crossbars a puppeteer holds): each arm flows directly into its cross, in one continuous shape, like the cross grows out of the arm. NOT a cross held in a hand, not a separate object.
- The crosses are NOT wood: they are made of the same dark ghostly substance as his body.

PUPPETS
- From each hand-cross hang threads, each thread only 1 pixel thick.
- On the threads hang two SHUPPETs (like the attached Shuppet), one under each hand, controlled as puppets.
- Each Shuppet is about a quarter of his height, with a simple clear shape that still reads at sprite size.

COLOURS
- Like Banette, but a bit darker.

STYLE AND FORMAT
- Official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style), 64x64, max 16 colours, 1 px dark outline, light from top left, flat Gen 3 shading with 3 to 4 tones per colour, no dithering.
- 3/4 view facing left, with a perspective as if seen slightly from above.
- Large: the figure, including the two Shuppets, fills almost the whole 64x64 square.
- Plain white background. NO floor, shadow, glow, aura or effects.

AVOID
- No zipper mouth. No wooden crosses. No cross held in a separate hand. Banette's face must not appear on the garment. Not a small Banette with a costume, not cute, not chibi. Not a plush toy, no glossy 3D render.
```
