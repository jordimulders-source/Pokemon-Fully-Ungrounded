Colors: keep the head's colors; the flame shifts from pink near the head to red where it leaves the rocket. Rocket stays pink, magenta and yellow.

64x64, max 16 colors, 1 px dark outline, flat Gen 3 shading, no dithering, three-quarter view facing left, plain white background, no shadow. No sparkles, no smoke, no text.
