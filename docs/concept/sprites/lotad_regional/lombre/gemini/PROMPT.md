# Gemini-prompt: gaucho-Lombre, startprompt v1 (25 nov)

Regels: `docs/lessen/GEMINI.md`. Geen bijlage (Jordi, 22–25 nov). Besluiten Jordi: `../../SPEC.md`.
Opzet = Lotad-prompt v7 (gaf r8, het gekozen model), aangepast naar fase 2.
Uit Jordi's woorden: plant die gaucho is, geen kleding/accessoires; vanaf Lombre Fighting; "steeds meer gaucho hoed" per evolutie (de Lotad-hoeden "lijken op cowboy hoeden").
Keuzes Claude (schrappen bij twijfel): (1) gauchohoed beschreven als lage platte bol + vlakke rechte rand (de *chambergo*), tegenover de omgekrulde rand van de Lotad; (2) kleuren van de Lotad-sprite in woorden, zodat de lijn bij elkaar past; (3) Ground in het type weggelaten (Ground/Fighting of puur Fighting is open).

```
Design a regional form of the Pokémon LOMBRE and draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style pixel art).

CONCEPT
- A plant that IS a gaucho (the cowboy of the South American pampas, known for his wide hat).
- Type: Fighting. It is NOT a Water or Grass type and does not live in water.
- The gaucho look comes only from the plant's own body parts. The leaf on its head forms the gaucho hat.
- This is the middle stage of its line: bigger and tougher than the first stage, more gaucho than before.

THE HAT
- More clearly a real gaucho hat than a cowboy hat: a low crown with a FLAT top and a wide, FLAT, straight brim. Not a curled-up cowboy brim, not a tall pointed sombrero.
- It is the plant's own leaf, part of its body, not a hat put on top.

DESIGN
- Keep Lombre's basic build.
- Colors: the leaf-hat in terracotta / clay-brown, the body warm red-brown, a yellow bill, darker olive-brown plant parts.
- Design the rest freely so that it reads as a plant-gaucho.

POSE
- Three-quarter view facing left, like official Gen 3 front sprites.

STYLE AND FORMAT
- 64x64 pixel sprite, max 16 colors, 1 px dark outline, clean shading like official Game Freak Gen 3 sprites.
- Keep the details few and bold, like a real Gen 3 sprite: big readable shapes, no fine patterns or tiny textures.
- Compact silhouette, limbs close to the body.
- Plain white background. No floor, rock, soil, shadow, water, glow, sparkles or effects. One single creature, no extra views.

AVOID
- NO clothing and NO accessories: no poncho, scarf, belt, boots, spurs, knife, whip, lasso, bolas, mate cup, no real hat on top of the leaf.
- Not a human, not a person in a plant costume.
- Not green and blue like the normal Lombre.
- No glossy 3D render, no plush or mascot look.
```
