# Guaraná fase 2 – startprompt r8 (26 nov), NIEUW gesprek, geen bijlage
Jordi over r7: "het zijn niet echt trosjes en hij heeft het nu druk gemaakt, zonder de open ruimte op de takken in te vullen"; koos route B (Claude): echte guaraná-tros, vruchten aan korte steeltjes langs het laatste stuk van de tak. Rest = prompt r6 (groot blad, getwijnde liaan, ogen 5–6 px, een paar dicht). "Vanaf halverwege de tak" = eigen invulling van Claude.

```
Design a new, original Pokémon.
Draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): pixel art, 64×64, max 16 colors, dark selout outline, plain white background.

WHAT IT IS
A Grass/Ghost Pokémon based on the guaraná plant from the Amazon.
Its body IS a creeping vine: a living green vine that crawls over the ground like an animal. It has no animal or human body parts at all; the ONLY animal-like features are its eyes. That is what makes it creepy.
The vine branches: from one thick main vine, three branches rise up. The last part of each branch is covered in a long bunch of guaraná fruits, like several heads.

THE FRUIT BUNCHES AND EYES (must clearly read as guaraná, not a rose, raspberry, cherries or grapes)
Each bunch grows like a real guaraná bunch: many fruits hang on very short little stalks all along the last part of the branch, from halfway up the branch to its tip. The fruits sit close together, so the end of each branch is fully covered in fruit and no bare branch shows there. Each bunch is an elongated, full cluster, not one ball on top of a bare stick.
In every bunch most fruits are open with an eye, and a few are still closed. The closed fruits are round, glossy red, with a seam line and no eye.
Each open fruit is a round, smooth, glossy red capsule that has split open along its seam; the red skin peels back like two lips around what is inside.
Inside each open fruit sits one big eye: a white ball with a big black pupil, exactly like a real ripe guaraná fruit (black seed in white flesh). The red split skin frames every eye.
The eyes are big: each eye is about 5-6 pixels wide in the 64×64 sprite, so every eye clearly reads as a white eye with a black pupil at real sprite size. They all stare straight at the viewer.
Mood: unsettling. The eyes stare and follow you, but it is still a Pokémon you would want on your team. Not gory.

THE VINE (reads well as a sprite)
Smooth green vine, not woody, not brown. The main vine is two or three vine strands twisted around each other like a rope, as lianas do in the jungle. At the top the strands separate into the three branches that carry the fruit bunches. One clear body, not a tangle.
A few simple tendrils grip the ground like crawling limbs, close to the body. No decorative curls or spirals.
Leaves: real guaraná leaves, each a compound leaf of five long, pointed leaflets with lightly toothed edges. Only a few leaves, but each leaf is big: the leaves are clearly visible next to the fruit bunches and read clearly at sprite size.
Compact: the whole Pokémon fits in a square shape and fills almost the whole 64×64 frame.

POSE
It crawls toward the viewer, the fruit bunches raised and turned to you, as if they all just noticed you.

VIEW
Three-quarter view facing left, like every Gen 3 front sprite.

DO NOT
No glow, no effects, no ghostly aura, no floating particles.
No ground, no shadow, no pot.
No mouth, no teeth, no blood, no face other than the eyes.
Not a human or child figure, not a snake, not a flower, not a bunch of grapes, not a tree.
No plush/Neopets look.
```
