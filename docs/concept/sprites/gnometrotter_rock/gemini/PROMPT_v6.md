# Gemini-prompt: Gnometrotter (Rock-vorm), v6 (23 nov)

Nieuw gesprek, geen bijlage. Jordi (r5): "we kunnen ook doen dat hij helemaal van stenen is geworden, maar dat we de kleurenpatronen wel behouden". v5 blijft de andere richting.

## Ingevuld door Claude
- Steenkleuren per vlak: neus/lijf/arm roze steen, onderhelft/voeten hemelsblauwe steen, muts/riem/rug/stok donkergrijs, baard/haar lichtgrijs (zoals r5).
- Zolen grijs (zoals r5).

## Prompt

```
Design an original Pokémon and draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style).

CONCEPT
- Fairy/Rock type, the final stage of a three-stage evolution line. Its name is Gnometrotter: a gnome Pokémon that has walked so far for so long that its body has fully adapted to its environment. This is its Cave Form: after ages in caves, its whole body has turned to stone. A rock gnome: an old garden gnome that has become a living stone figure.
- Its ENTIRE body is made of stone, but it keeps the exact color pattern of a cartoony garden gnome: every part is stone in its own color.

COLOR PATTERN (all stone)
- Nose: a big, perfectly ROUND, ball-shaped nose of smooth PINK stone, like a round pebble. Not oval, not long.
- Body: a round, chubby, ball-shaped body of PINK stone. NOT muscular, no broad chest.
- Lower half of the body and legs: SKY-BLUE stone, like a garden gnome's trousers.
- Feet: rounded SKY-BLUE stone feet, a bit big, standing on a thick, chunky slab of grey rock as a sole that sticks out a little at the front and back. No toes.
- Free arm: a thin, short arm of PINK stone ending in a round, chubby ball-shaped pink stone hand.
- Cap: DARK GREY rock.
- Beard, mustache and hair tufts: LIGHT GREY rock.
- Belt ring and back ridge: DARK GREY rock.
- The stone parts have a few cracks and chipped edges, so you can see it is stone, but keep the shapes simple and smooth enough to read; the colors stay clearly pink, sky blue, dark grey and light grey.

SHAPE
- Posture: hunched forward with age, taking a slow, heavy step, leaning on its stick. An old, seasoned hiker.
- Stick arm: one arm, from the shoulder down, has grown into a long rock walking stick of dark grey rock. Fist and stick are ONE single piece: no hand gripping a stick, no fingers, no border between fist and stick; the forearm simply continues and grows down into the stick.
- Cap: the droopy pointed shape of a soft gnome cap (NOT tall, NOT straight up); its tip hangs down behind and fuses with a rocky ridge on its shoulders and back.
- The cap comes down low over the face: its lower edge overlaps and covers the top of the nose. The cap touches the nose directly, so there is NO dark band, gap, slit or shadow between cap and face, and nothing that could read as eyes.
- Belt section: a thick ring of dark grey rock around the waist, between the pink body and the blue lower half, connected to the rock ridge on its back. Buckle included in the stone.
- Beard: one heavy block of light grey rock with hard, angular, chipped edges, like a beard carved out of stone, from under the nose down to the belt ring. Hair tufts sticking out sideways from under the cap: hard, angular light grey rock chunks. NO soft strands, NO fluff. NO mouth visible.

STYLE AND FORMAT
- Official Gen 3 front sprite, 64x64, max 16 colors, 1 px dark outline, light from top left, flat Gen 3 shading with 3 to 4 tones per color, no dithering.
- Keep the stone readable at sprite size: big shapes and a few cracks, no fine texture or speckles.
- Three-quarter view facing left. A final-stage Pokémon, about 60 px tall, compact, centered in the frame.
- Plain white background. NO floor, shadow, loose rocks on the ground, cave background or effects. One single creature.
- Cartoony, but in the Pokémon way: bold shapes, a clear and cool silhouette, like an official Ken Sugimori design.

AVOID
- No visible eyes, no dark band under the cap, no visible mouth. No soft skin, no fluffy beard or hair. No muscles. No toes. No hand holding a separate stick. No oval or long nose. No crystals, no glow. No all-grey statue: the pink and blue must stay clearly pink and blue. No human proportions. Not Golem, not Graveler, not Impidimp, not a garden statue. No western-cartoon or plush look. No glossy 3D render.
```
