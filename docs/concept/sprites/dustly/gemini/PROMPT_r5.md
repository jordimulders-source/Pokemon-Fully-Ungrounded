```
Design a new Pokémon and draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): 64x64 pixel art, at most 16 colours, light from the top left, plain white background, no ground, no shadow, no glow, no sparkles, no effects outside the creature.

The Pokémon is "Dustly", a Cosmic/Poison type: a living DUST CLOUD from space, a small dark cloud of gas and dust in which a star will one day be born (a Bok globule). It has no solid body at all: it is only a cloud with a face.

BODY: a loose, soft cloud of gas and dust, shaped like a drifting puff of smoke or a small storm cloud. There is NO ball, NO sphere, NO head and NO round shape inside it; the face floats directly in the smoke. The cloud is densest around the face and thins out towards the edges: an irregular outline that breaks up into wisps, drifting strands and curling tendrils, with small gaps where the white background shows through. Light and airy, like smoke, not solid.
COLOUR: dark grey-brown dust, NOT black and NOT rust-brown. Densest part (around the face) the darkest grey-brown; thinner parts and wisps lighter, softer grey-brown.
LIGHT: only a THIN rim along the upper left edge of the cloud is lit by a nearby star: a narrow line of rose-pink with a little pale orange, one or two pixels wide. Solid pixels, not a glow.
FACE: small, about one third of the width of the cloud, left of the centre: two big, angry, slanted white eyes with small black pupils, and a wide, mischievous grin with two small pointed white fangs at the top. No nose, no outline around the face.

POSE: a three-quarter view facing LEFT, floating. Compact so it fits in a square; the cloud fills about two thirds of the width of the image, centred.

AVOID: a ball, sphere, orb or round head of any kind, a dark circle behind the face, a smooth outline, cauliflower or brain-like lumps, rust-brown or orange colours, a pink body, a pitch-black body, a halo or ring of gas, purple, a ghost look, arms, hands, legs, stars or sparkles, glow effects, a cute plush, chibi or Neopets look, a glossy 3D render.
```
