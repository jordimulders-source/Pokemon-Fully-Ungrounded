# Gemini-prompt Kay Pacha r4 (twee nieuwe gesprekken, geen bijlage)

Jordi na r3: "nu overdrijft die, hier is geen sprite van te maken." "nieuwe prompt from scratch, hij krijgt wat grotere bladeren op bepaalde plekken óf we maken de inca versiering mossiger, ik wil dat die een masker krijgt en dat de rugversiering blijft, we verwerken er iets van paars in".
Eigen invulling van Claude (schrappen als Jordi het niet wil): masker = gouden Inca-masker over het gezicht (ogen zichtbaar); waar de bladeren zitten en waar het paars komt laat ik aan Gemini; "only a few ornaments" tegen het overdrijven; rugkam beschreven zoals in r2.

Variant A (grotere bladeren):
```
Design a new legendary Pokémon as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): pixel art, 64x64, max 16 colors, dark outline, light from top-left, plain white background, no ground, no shadow, no effects.

WHAT IT IS: a Grass/Psychic legendary beast based on a puma, the guardian of "Kay Pacha", the world of the living in Inca belief. It belongs to a legendary trio, like Entei, Raikou and Suicune: it must read as a legendary Pokémon, not as a normal animal wearing accessories. It must not look like Entei, Raikou, Suicune, Leafeon, Meowscarada or any other existing Pokémon.

BODY: puma build (slim, muscular, long thick tail, short rounded ears), stylized like a Pokémon, with a BIG head. GREEN coat, PURPLE eyes. No spots, no stripes.

DECORATION (Inca-inspired gold, bold and simple, must read at 64x64):
- MASK: it wears an Inca-style gold mask on its face; the purple eyes stay visible.
- BACK: a large gold crest along the back in the stepped zigzag shape of the Inca walls of Sacsayhuamán.
- LEAVES: a few BIG leaves at certain places on the body (large, clear leaf shapes, not tiny sprigs).
- PURPLE: work some purple into the design.
Keep it to these elements. No engravings, no spirals, no small patterns, no faces or symbols on the gold, no extra plates.

POSE: 3/4 view facing left, standing on four legs, head raised, proud and calm like a legendary beast.

DO NOT: a collar, bracelets, jewelry chains, armor covering the body, spots, stripes, wings, glowing aura, magic effects, rocks, grass on the ground, background.
```
