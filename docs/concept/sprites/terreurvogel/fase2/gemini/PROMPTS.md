## r1 (27 nov)
Concept + sprintpose, enorme snavel, fossiel à la Archeops (oertrekjes, geen steen). Kleuren niet besproken, dus niet gegeven.
```
Design an original Pokémon as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style), 64x64 pixels, max 16 colors, white background, facing 3/4 to the left.

Concept: a fully evolved fossil Pokémon (Rock/Fighting) based on the terror bird (Phorusrhacos / Kelenken), the extinct flightless predator bird of South America. It is a fast physical attacker.
Anatomy: a flightless bird on long, powerful running legs with large clawed feet, small wings, and an ENORMOUS, tall hooked beak shaped like an axe blade. The beak is its most recognizable feature and is huge compared to the head.
It reads as a revived prehistoric creature the way Archen and Archeops do: primitive, reptile-like touches such as scaly legs and claws, NOT made of stone.
Pose: in a full sprint, body stretched forward low, one leg far forward and one far back, the huge beak leading.
Compact silhouette that fits a square.

No effects, no glow, no sparkles, no speed lines, no dust, no ground or shadow under it. AVOID: copying Archeops, an ostrich, an emu, a chicken, a modern eagle, a dinosaur, a plush toy, Neopets style.
```
