## Prompt

```
Design a new Pokémon and draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): 64x64 pixel art, at most 16 colours, light from the top left, plain white background, no ground, no shadow, no glow, no sparkles, no effects outside the creature.

The Pokémon is "Dustly", a Cosmic/Poison type based on a BOK GLOBULE: a small, dark, dense cloud of gas and dust in space in which a star will later be born. On space photos it is a black, roughly round cloud in front of glowing pink hydrogen gas. It is NOT a ghost and NOT related to Gastly; it only looks similar by coincidence (convergent evolution, like Wiglett and Diglett).

FACE: the attached sprite is GASTLY. The eyes and the grin with fangs must be exactly Gastly's from this attachment (same shape, same size), turned into the three-quarter view.

BODY: NOT a ball and NOT a solid sphere: a roughly round CLOUD of dark dust, like the original Gen 1 Gastly, which was a cloud with a face. The centre of the cloud is pitch black (thickest dust) and it gets thinner towards the edge: dark reddish-brown, then ragged, frayed, uneven wisps where it fades into the gas around it. No smooth outline around the cloud as a whole; the edge is soft and ragged, made of small clumps of dust.
GAS: around the dark cloud floats pink and rose-red hydrogen gas, in the same place where Gastly has its purple gas: soft clumps of gas drawn as solid pixel clouds, thicker near the dark cloud and breaking up into small loose puffs further out. Pink and rose-red, not purple.

POSE: a three-quarter view facing LEFT, floating in space. Compact so everything fits in a square; the dark cloud fills about half the width of the image, centred.

AVOID: a solid ball or sphere with a hard round outline, a shiny or glossy surface, Gastly's purple, a ghost look, arms, hands, legs, a tail, a body shape other than a roughly round cloud, stars or sparkles around it, glow effects, a cute plush, chibi or Neopets look, a glossy 3D render.
```

## Tips
- Wordt het toch een harde bal: nieuw gesprek, "the body is a dark cloud of dust with ragged edges, like Gen 1 Gastly, not a ball" bovenaan.
- Niet langer dan 1–2 keer bijsturen in hetzelfde gesprek (GEMINI.md).
