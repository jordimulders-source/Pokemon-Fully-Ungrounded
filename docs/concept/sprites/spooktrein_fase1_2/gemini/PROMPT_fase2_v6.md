```
Design an original Pokémon as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style). Type: Steel/Ghost. Middle stage of a line: a pile of sentient train scrap → THIS → a racing ghost steam train.

THE CREATURE: a green steam ghost that has taken over an old rusty railway wagon and drives it with its own power, extremely fast.

THE GHOST:
- Its body is a thick plume of green steam, like steam from a locomotive chimney: the outline is made of rounded steam puffs, and the top of its head ends in a curl of steam, blown flat backwards by the speed. NOT a round ball head, not a blob.
- The wagon walls hide its lower body. Exactly two arms made of steam puffs: one hand grips the front rim of the wagon, the other arm is raised high with a fist in a cheer.
- FACE (most important): the mouth is exaggeratedly huge, a gigantic open grin that takes up most of the face, open so wide you see the dark inside of the mouth; no row of teeth. The eyes are small merry eyes, little twinkling dots full of fun, NOT slanted, NOT narrowed, NOT evil. It must not look like Gengar in any way.

THE WAGON:
- A railway freight wagon, NOT a mine cart: rectangular with straight vertical sides (no tapered bucket shape), on an iron railway chassis with two round buffers at the front.
- Made of rusty STEEL plates with rivets, NOT wood: no planks, no wood grain. Rickety and half falling apart.
- Short and compact, so the whole creature fits in a square.

GHOST POWER (clearly visible, it drives the wagon):
- many wide, bright green cracks across the whole wagon, green energy glowing out of every seam and gap;
- the wheels are almost completely green ghost vapour;
- a big green ghost cloud streams from under and behind the wagon.

EXTREME SPEED: the whole creature leans back, the wagon tilts with its front wheels lifted a little, the ghost cloud streams straight back in long streaks, loose bolts and small rusty plates fly off the back.

VIEW: semi-frontal, a little more frontal than a 3/4 view, facing left: you see the front of the wagon with its buffers, the side runs back at an angle. NOT a side view.

COLORS: wagon rust brown (#91745d, darker #5d4b3e) with a little dark grey, outline near-black brown (#292018). Ghost and all ghost parts in soft greens: light #b5f3b6, mid #72d779 / #67b370, dark #467e4b.

AVOID: Gengar's face (slanted eyes, toothy grin), a round ball head, cute/kawaii/chibi style, tentacles, wood, a mine cart, a locomotive, a face on the wagon, a long side view, extra arms, too much detail.

FORMAT: 64x64 pixel sprite, max 16 colors, 1 px dark outline, flat Gen 3 shading, no dithering, compact silhouette, plain white background, no rails, floor, shadow or speed lines outside the creature.
```
