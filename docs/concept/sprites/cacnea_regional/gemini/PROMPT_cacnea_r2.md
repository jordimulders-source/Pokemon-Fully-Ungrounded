# Regionale Cacnea – Gemini-startprompt r2 (23 nov, nieuw gesprek, geen bijlage)

```
Design a regional form of CACNEA: a small first-stage Pokémon, type Grass/Ground. It must stay clearly recognisable as Cacnea.
Draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): pixel art, 64×64, max 16 colors, dark selout outline, plain white background, 3/4 view facing left.

WHAT IT IS
Its body is a young COPIAPOA cactus from the Atacama desert (Chile): a round cactus ball with shallow vertical ribs and small clusters of dark, almost black spines along the ribs. The skin is grey-green with a chalky, whitish waxy bloom, so it looks like a pale stone among stones, but some green still shows through (it is young). On top of the head a small woolly tuft from which its yellow crown grows.

KEEP FROM CACNEA
Round ball body, the small yellow crown on top of the head, two round eyes with black pupils, the dark spots on the face that form its mouth, two thick arms held out to the sides, short stubby feet.

COMPOSITION
Compact, the whole Pokémon fits inside a square, small (it is a first stage), limbs close to the body.

DO NOT
No ground, no sand, no rocks around it, no shadow. No glow, no effects, no motion lines.
Not a plush/Neopets look, not a realistic photo, not a potted plant.
```

Notitie (voorstellen Claude, niet van Jordi): Cacnea-bouw behouden; ribben, donkere stekels, grijsgroen met witte waslaag, wollige kruin met de gele kroon (Copiapoa bloeit geel). Schrappen of aanpassen wat niet klopt.
