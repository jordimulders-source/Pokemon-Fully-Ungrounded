# Regional Claydol – Gemini-startprompt r1 (23 nov, nieuw gesprek, geen bijlage)

```
Design a regional form of the Pokémon CLAYDOL, type Rock/Psychic, inspired by the STAFF GOD (Viracocha) carved on the Gateway of the Sun at TIWANAKU (Bolivia). It is a big final-stage Pokémon: a living, floating carved stone idol, not a statue on a pedestal.
Draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): pixel art, 64×64, max 16 colors, dark selout outline, plain white background.

WHAT IT IS (Staff God)
A square, flat-faced carved stone head and a short blocky stone body, in the angular Tiwanaku style: flat planes, straight edges, square corners, shallow carved stepped patterns.
Around the head a large RAYED HEADDRESS: stone rays sticking out all around the head like a crown of sun rays.
Instead of two arms it has two carved stone STAFFS (sceptres) floating on either side of the body, one on each side, held by psychic power, not attached.

EYES (most important, this is what makes it a Claydol)
Each ray of the headdress ends in one ROUND EYE: together they form a ring of round eyes all around the head.
The eyes are open: a round light eye with a small dark pupil (a dot or a short line).
The flat face itself has no extra eyes; the only eyes are the ring of eyes at the ends of the rays.

COLORS
Beige-grey carved stone, with a few red painted accents.

VIEW
Three-quarter view facing left, like every Gen 3 front sprite: the far side on the left, the near side on the right.
It floats: no legs, no feet.
Compact: the whole Pokémon fits inside a square and fills it (final stage).

DO NOT
No ground, no pedestal, no gate, no shadow. No glow, no aura, no psychic effects, no motion lines.
No starfield or galaxy patterns. Not a human in a costume, no plush/Neopets look, not a realistic statue photo.
```
