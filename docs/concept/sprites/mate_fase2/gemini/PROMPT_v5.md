## Prompt

```
Design an original Pokémon as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style). Image 1 is its pre-evolution, a yerba mate gourd. Image 2 shows the little leaf man who rides in it; use ONLY the leaf man from image 2, not its canoe.

THE POKÉMON
- A canoe that is at the same time a giant, perfectly made yerba mate gourd, as if this Pokémon is the world champion mate maker: polished brown calabash, a neat silver metal rim (virola) along the whole top edge, like the rim on the gourd in image 1.
- The canoe shape is sharper, like a real canoe: a clean pointed bow at the front and a pointed stern. The bow is a sharp point, NOT a pursed or tube-shaped mouth, no opening at the tip.
- On the side near the bow: two eyes and a small mouth CARVED into the gourd, in the same carved style as the face of image 1.
- The front part is covered and filled with a heap of green yerba mate leaves and powder. The back part holds hot water; a little spills over the stern.
- In the middle sits the little leaf man from image 2 (same head, face and colors). He is clearly rowing.
- His paddles are bombillas: a long thin straight silver straw as the shaft, with the flat, round, spoon-shaped bombilla filter at the lower end as the paddle blade, in the water beside the canoe. One in each hand, held like real paddles.

STYLE AND FORMAT
- 64x64, max 16 colors, 1 px dark outline, light from top left, flat Gen 3 shading, no dithering.
- Compact silhouette that fills the frame, 3/4 facing left.
- Plain white background. NO river, NO floor, NO shadow, NO effects.

AVOID
- No pursed lips or snout on the canoe, no thick or bulky rim, no forks, no normal wooden oars, no Octillery look.
```
