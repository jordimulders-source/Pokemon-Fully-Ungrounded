## Prompt

```
Design an original Pokémon and draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style pixel art).

TYPE: Steel / Ghost. Final stage of its line.

CONCEPT: a ghost that has possessed an old steam locomotive and turned it into its body. It is a creature, not a locomotive with a face painted on it.

THE TRAIN PARTS ARE ITS BODY
- The front of the locomotive is its head. The cowcatcher is its lower jaw: the mouth is built from the cowcatcher.
- Two identical eyes on the front of the head. The headlight is NOT an eye.
- Only a few big, simple train parts: a long low boiler, a short chimney, the cowcatcher jaw, two big front wheels. Nothing else: no cab, no windows, no rods, no pipes, no rows of rivets.
- Long and low silhouette, with the head pushed forward like a snout.

FACE
- A mischievous, playful grin: it is visibly having fun being a ghost and enjoys going so fast. Cheeky, not angry, not scary.

GHOST SMOKE
- Behind the front wheels, the back of the train dissolves into greenish ghost smoke. Inside the smoke, the back wheels appear as faint ghostly wheels.

COLORS (only these)
- Rust brown iron with a bit of dark grey.
- Green for the ghost (eyes, smoke, ghostly wheels).

FULL SPEED (this must be obvious)
- The whole body leans forward, charging ahead.
- The front wheels are drawn in motion like in a comic book.
- Show the speed with the creature itself, not with effects around it.

VIEW
- Semi-frontal: slightly more frontal than a three-quarter view, facing left. The head is closest to the viewer.

STYLE AND FORMAT
- 64x64 pixel sprite, the creature filling most of the canvas (final stage), max 16 colors, 1 px dark outline, light from top left, flat Gen 3 shading with 3 to 4 tones per color, no dithering.
- Big readable shapes, few small details.
- Plain white background. NO rails, floor, shadow, glow, speed lines, sparkles or effects outside the creature. One single creature, no extra views.

AVOID
- No realistic locomotive, no wagons, no cab with windows.
- No angry face, no skull, no horror mask, no ghost conductor, no small ghosts in the smoke.
- Not a children's TV train with a human face, not a plush toy, chibi or Neopets mascot. No glossy 3D render.
```

## Tips
- Weer een gewone loc: "THE TRAIN PARTS ARE ITS BODY" als eerste regel, nieuw gesprek.
- Te simpel/kaal (zoals Clover): één onderdeel terug toevoegen, niet alles.
