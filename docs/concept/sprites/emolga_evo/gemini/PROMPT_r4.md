# Emolga-evolutie – startprompt r4 (23 nov), NIEUW gesprek, geen bijlage
Opgebouwd zoals Skinguin v2 (Jordi: "kijk naar skinguin hoe we dat beter gedaan hebben"): het dier vanuit de echte anatomie
en de sport beschrijven, de pre-evolutie alleen voor kleuren en artstijl; vaste blokken; alle besluiten uit SPEC.

```
Design a new Pokémon: the evolution of EMOLGA.
Draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): pixel art, 64×64, max 16 colors, dark selout outline, plain white background.

WHAT IT IS
An adrenaline-junkie flying squirrel whose body IS a wingsuit (the suit that people wear to jump from cliffs and fly down, with big fabric wings between arms and body and between the legs).
Anatomy: a flying squirrel, but clearly bigger and longer than Emolga: a long, elongated, streamlined body, long legs, a long flat tail.
The wingsuit is part of its body: a SUPER LARGE gliding membrane, stretched taut like wingsuit fabric, from the wrists down to the ankles on both sides, and a second panel between the two legs. The membrane grows directly out of the whole side of the body, so body and wings form one aerodynamic shape. It is not a suit it wears.
Head: long, pointed ears. Around the eyes it has a visor-colored marking in its fur, like the tinted visor of a helmet. It is a fur marking, NOT glasses, NOT goggles, NOT a mask. It has NO cheek spots.
Belly: a cool aerodynamic pattern on the belly.

COLORS (Emolga's pattern)
White face and white belly. Black on top of the head, on the back and on the upper side of the wing membrane. Bright yellow on the underside of the wing membrane. Not mostly yellow.

POSE
It is diving downward like a real wingsuit flyer: head first, the body pointing steeply down-left, arms and legs spread wide so the whole membrane is stretched open, tail straight behind. Exactly two arms and two legs.
All the speed comes from the pose itself.

VIEW
Three-quarter view facing left, like every Gen 3 front sprite: the far wing panel is on the left (narrower, partly hidden), the near one on the right.
It is big: it fills most of the 64×64 frame. Compact: it fits inside a square.

DO NOT
No speed lines, no wind, no motion blur, no electricity, no glow or effects.
No ground, no clouds, no shadow.
No helmet, no goggles, no glasses, no clothing or accessories.
Not simply Emolga with longer wings. No bat or dragon wings, no feathers, no plush/Neopets look, not a human in a costume, no parachute.
```

Daarna (net als Skinguin r3): wat bijna goed is in **hetzelfde gesprek** bijsturen met max. 2 wijzigingen ("keep everything else exactly as it is").
