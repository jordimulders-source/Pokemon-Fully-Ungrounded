# Regional Baltoy – Gemini-startprompt r1 (22 nov, nieuw gesprek, geen bijlage)

```
Design a brand-new Pokémon: a small first-stage Pokémon, type Rock/Psychic. It is a living carved stone figure based on the ancient stone monoliths of TIWANAKU (Bolivia): a small idol come to life.
Draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): pixel art, 64×64, max 16 colors, dark selout outline, plain white background.

WHAT IT IS
A small, squat, rectangular stone block figure, like a miniature Tiwanaku monolith: a square head with a carved headband, a blocky body, both hands held flat against its chest (one holding a small tall beaker, the other a short staff), shallow carved geometric patterns on the body, legs barely separated from the block.
It is carved stone: chiselled edges, flat planes, no soft clay.

EYES (most important, keep exactly this)
Two narrow horizontal slit eyes, squinting/closed, set inside one RED painted band that runs across the face.
On the chest/belly: one round orange "eye" with a red rim.

VIEW
Frontal, like a carved statue, symmetric.
Compact: the whole Pokémon fits inside a square, small (it is a first stage).

DO NOT
No ground, no pedestal, no rock under it, no shadow. No glow, no aura, no psychic effects, no motion lines.
No starfield or galaxy patterns.
Not a human in a costume, no plush/Neopets look, not a realistic statue photo.
```
