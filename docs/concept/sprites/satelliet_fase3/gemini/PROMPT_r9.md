# Ruimtestation (fase 3), Gemini-prompt r9 – NIEUW gesprek, geen bijlage
r8 (`r8.png`), Jordi: "nee dit is geen ruimtestation meer" · "dan ligt het aan de prompt, de zijkanten moeten niet zo groot zijn als de voor en achterkant, doe nog even een sterke prompt maken".
Basis = prompt r5 (Jordi: "qua vorm prima"), met alleen de besluiten erna: antennes weg; ogen op alle vier de uiteinden (achterste niet zichtbaar), iris en pupil blijven, minder detail dan fase 2; zijogen gespiegeld; panelen per paar gespiegeld (r6 "kloppen niet", r7 "teveel panelen weg"); pixel art, geen Sugimori-art; NIEUW: zijarmen en zijbollen duidelijk kleiner dan voor- en achterkant. De lange vierkante paneelvorm en de verdeling van r5 blijven (3 paren).

```
Design a Pokémon: an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style), 64×64 pixels, max 16 colors, clean dark outline, plain white background, no ground, no shadow, no effects or glow.
IMPORTANT: the result must be a real PIXEL ART SPRITE as it appears in the game: visible square pixels, a small flat palette, hard pixel edges, no smooth gradients, no anti-aliasing. NOT official artwork, NOT a smooth illustration.

WHAT IT IS: a big living space station, the final evolution of a small one-eyed satellite. It is a pure machine with its own personality, the way Magneton, Metagross and Porygon are Pokémon: no animal parts, no arms, no legs, no mouth. At a glance it must read as a SPACE STATION, like a compact cartoon version of a real modular space station: modules docked together in a cross, with long solar panel wings.

SIZE AND COMPLEXITY (most important): this is a LARGE, heavy, final-stage Pokémon, clearly more complex than a single satellite. It fills the WHOLE 64×64 frame from edge to edge. Each eye-sphere is only a small part of the whole creature.

POSE AND ANGLE: Gen 3 front-sprite 3/4 view facing the viewer's LEFT, seen slightly from above (not from high above, not lying flat). Everything in this one perspective. Compact in a square.

BUILD (a cross-shaped station with a clear main axis):
- In the middle is a round white node sphere.
- THE MAIN AXIS is BIG: a long, thick arm of white cylinder modules toward the front-left and an equally long, thick arm toward the back-right. These two arms are the backbone of the station.
- THE SIDE ARMS are SMALL: two short, thinner arms to the left and right of the node, clearly shorter (about half as long) and thinner than the main axis.
- The modules have different sizes (some wide and short, some long and slim) and each has one or two dark purple bands around it. All joints are purple ring-shaped docking collars. Nothing floats loose: it is one solid connected machine.

FOUR EYE-SPHERES: at the end of EACH of the four arms sits a round white sphere with one eye pointing outward along its arm.
- The front sphere (end of the front-left main arm, nearest to the viewer) is the largest; we see its eye from the front. It is the focus point.
- The back sphere (end of the back-right main arm) is seen only from behind; its eye is hidden.
- The two side spheres are SMALLER (matching their thinner arms) and are exact mirror images of each other: each side eye points straight outward along its own arm (left one to the left, right one to the right), seen at the same angle, half turned toward the viewer.
- Each eye is SIMPLE so it reads at small size: one purple ring, a turquoise iris and a dark pupil, flat colors, no extra rings, no shine.
NO ANTENNAE anywhere.

SOLAR PANELS: THREE pairs of long, narrow, flat rectangular solar panel wings sticking out at right angles from the main axis (one pair near the front, one pair at the node, one pair near the back). Each pair is mirrored: its two panels have the same size and the same angle, one on each side, attached with a short purple rod. Dark navy-purple with a lighter purple grid of only a few large cells (about 2 by 3). Long like on a real space station, but each panel still clearly wide (never thin strips). All in the same 3/4 perspective; the far ones smaller and partly hidden. No panel floats loose.

DETAIL LEVEL: it must translate well to a 64×64 sprite: big readable shapes, thick parts, medium detail, no thin parts (every part at least a few pixels wide). No rivets, no text, no logos, no flags, no tiny windows, dishes or greebles.

COLORS: pearly white with lavender shading, purple accents (collars, bands, rods, eye rings), dark navy-purple solar panels with lighter purple grid lines, turquoise iris as the only accent. Light from the upper left. Nothing else.

DO NOT: Sugimori-style official artwork or any smooth/vector illustration, side arms as big or long as the main axis, a symmetrical plus-sign layout, a flat lying station seen from high above, small square panels, antennae, more or fewer than four eye-spheres, eyes with many rings, a single satellite with two panels, a small creature with empty space around it, a ring or wheel, thin orbit lines, starfield or galaxy patterns, stars or Earth around it, a space shuttle, a flat front view or side view, realistic NASA/ISS hardware, a robot with a face screen, arms or legs, cute plush/Neopets style.
```
