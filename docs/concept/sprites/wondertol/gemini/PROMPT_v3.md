## Prompt

```
Design an original Pokémon and draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style).

CONCEPT
- Fire/Fairy type, the middle stage of a firework evolution line: a SALAMANDER WHOSE BODY IS A SPINNING GROUND-SPINNER FIREWORK, A WHIRLING BALL OF FIRE.
- Attachment 1 (fire salamander) is only for the head and legs. Attachment 2 (a burning ground-spinner firework) is only for the body.
- THE BODY IS THE FIREWORK: one round, spinning FIREBALL. Swirling flame bands curve around the ball, showing that it spins. There is no separate body behind or under it: the fireball IS the body.
- THE HEAD sticks out of the front of the fireball: a FLAT, WIDE salamander head, held low and forward, with small eyes sitting on top of the head and a wide mouth line. The head is made of fire too; only the eyes and the mouth are drawn into the flame.
- FOUR SHORT SALAMANDER LEGS made of fire stick out sideways from the ball, with small splayed toes. They are legs, not arms.
- A short tail of flame curls along the side of the ball, following the spin.
- Short solid jets of sparks fly off the spinning ball to the sides.
- FIREWORK COLORS WITH GREEN AND BLUE: emerald green and turquoise blue flames, flat solid gold-yellow spots like a fire salamander (no glitter inside them), a few small touches of soft pink. Sparks in the same colors.
- FAIRY: playful and mischievous expression.

STYLE AND FORMAT
- Official Gen 3 front sprite, 64x64, max 16 colors, 1 px dark outline, light from top left, flat Gen 3 shading with 3 to 4 tones per color, no dithering.
- Solid shapes with clean outlines that read as a clear silhouette at 64x64; the round fireball body must be obvious in the silhouette.
- Three-quarter view facing left. Compact composition that fits a square.
- Plain white background. NO floor, shadow, smoke or motion lines. One single creature.

AVOID
- NOT CHARMANDER: no big round head, no big cute eyes, no upright standing body, no belly, no arms, no flame on the tail tip. No dinosaur or dragon look, no horns or spikes.
- No cone, no separate top, disc or object; it does not lie or sit on anything. No smooth skin. No coiled body, no snake shape. No glitter, no sparkle specks, no twinkling stars. No orange. No firework explosions or burst shapes. No rocket, no stick. Not Wooper, not Hitmontop. No hearts. No text. No glossy 3D render.
```
