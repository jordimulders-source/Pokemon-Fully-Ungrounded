Draw ONLY A HEAD, nothing else: no body, no neck. Pixel art in the style of a Gen 3 Pokémon sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald): 1 px dark outline, flat shading with 3 to 4 tones, no dithering, about 30 pixels wide, plain white background, 3/4 view facing left.

It is the head of a sinister jester puppet, an original Pokémon.

HEAD SHAPE: the head itself is shaped like a jester's cap. The top of the head splits into TWO long, thin, floppy points like cloth: one bends over to the left, one bends over to the right, each drooping down at the tip. They are soft like fabric, NOT horns. Between them the top of the head is a smooth curve. There is NO spike in the middle, NO ears.

FACE SHAPE: NOT a round ball. A long jester's face, taller than wide, narrowing to a pointed chin.

FACE
- A very sinister, wide grin: a zigzag mouth full of sharp teeth. The teeth are the same colour as the head.
- Large, slanted, menacing red eyes.

COLOUR: dark grey with a slight purple tint, smooth, no patches, no stitches, no zipper.

AVOID: the head must not look like any existing Pokémon. Not a round ball head. No spiky crown, no single point on top, no cat ears, no ram horns.
