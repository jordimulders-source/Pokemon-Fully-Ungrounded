# Bibarel-evolutie – Gemini-startprompt r3 (23 nov, NIEUW gesprek, geen bijlage)

Jordi bij r2: "ok de tanden vertalen duidelijk niet, de houten log mag een punt hebben alsof die er goed aan heeft geknaagd, hij mag wel wat groter en meer buff denk ik, op zich is het bouwvakkerstuinbroek idee als lichaamspatroon wel een goede, evenals een helm die onderdeel van het hoofd lijkt te zijn, grotere ogen zoals bibarel".
Jordi (23 nov, tijdens het schrijven): "tanden weer gewoon wit".
Vijf wijzigingen → nieuwe startprompt (GEMINI.md: max 1–2 per bijlageronde). "Not muscular" vervalt; "no detailed muscle definition" blijft (Jordi eerder: gespierd is vaak lelijk). Bibarel niet bij naam: ogen in woorden. Kleur van tuinbroek en helm niet voorgeschreven (Jordi zei geen kleur).

```
Design a brand-new Pokémon. Type Water/Fighting. It is a hard-working builder beaver: a busy boss, working very hard, like real beavers do. The "construction worker" idea is shown through its body, its fur pattern and what it is doing; it wears NO actual clothing.
Draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): pixel art, 64×64, max 16 colors, dark selout outline, plain white background.

FINAL EVOLUTION (most important)
This is the THIRD and final stage of its line. It must look big, heavy, mature and imposing, like a fully-grown boss.
Bigger and a bit more buff: a large, strong, heavy frame with thick strong arms. But keep the shapes simple: no detailed muscle definition, no bodybuilder look.
Adult proportions: the head is smaller compared to the large body.

WHAT IT IS
Animal anatomy: a real BEAVER, standing upright on its hind legs.
Brown fur with a lighter tan muzzle.
FUR PATTERN LIKE BIB OVERALLS: its fur is patterned like construction-worker dungarees (a bib on the chest/belly with two shoulder straps, continuing over the legs) in a different fur color. It is fur, part of its body, not cloth.
HELMET THAT IS PART OF ITS HEAD: the top of its head is shaped like a hard hat (a rounded dome with a small brim), but it grows from its head, like a hard natural cap. Not a separate hat.
EYES: big, round, dark eyes with a white shine, clearly visible.
TEETH: two HUGE white front teeth, sticking far out of the mouth, very big and very clear, the most noticeable feature of the face.
A big, broad, flat paddle tail, shaped a bit like a mason's trowel, with one or two BROAD bands in a bright warning color (safety yellow or orange) like a road-work sign.

POSE
Busy at work: carrying a heavy log on its shoulder, mid-stride, determined and in a hurry.
THE LOG: one end is gnawed to a sharp POINT (like a pencil), the way real beavers gnaw through a tree trunk.

VIEW
Three-quarter view facing left, like every Gen 3 front sprite: the far arm and leg on the left (partly hidden), the near ones on the right.
Compact: the whole Pokémon including the log fits inside a square.

KEEP IT SIMPLE
Large, clear shapes that read at 64×64. No small details: no wood chips, no twigs, no rocks, no tool belts, no claws drawn in detail, no thin stripes.

DO NOT
Not a small, cute, baby-like beaver; not a first-stage Pokémon.
No real clothing or objects worn: no separate hat or helmet, no safety vest, no cloth overalls, no belt, no gloves, no tools held in hand.
No ground, no water, no rock, no shadow under it. No glow, no aura, no effects, no motion lines, no sweat drops.
No plush/Neopets look, no chibi mascot, not a realistic animal painting.
```
