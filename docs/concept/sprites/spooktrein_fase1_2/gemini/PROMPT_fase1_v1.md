```
Design an original Pokémon as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style). Type: pure Steel. First stage of a line: THIS → a rusty railway wagon driven by a green steam ghost → a racing ghost steam train.

THE CREATURE: a small pile of rusty train scrap that has become sentient, the way other Steel Pokémon are living metal. There is NO ghost in it yet: no green, no glow, no vapour. It is a weak, humble Pokémon.

THE PILE:
- A low, compact heap of old rusty railway parts: a small spoked train wheel, a short bent piece of rail, a few loose bolts and rivets, small bent steel plates, a bit of chain.
- Out of the heap, a short locomotive chimney sticks up half upright, tilted at an angle. It is the "head": the biggest, clearest part of the creature.
- EYES: exactly two eyes, inside the dark opening of the chimney, looking out of it. Only the chimney has eyes; no other part has a face.

VIEW: 3/4 view facing left.

COLORS: rust browns (light #a0846a, mid #765a43, dark #363122) with a little dark grey steel (#4e4a44, #7b7c7e, highlight #aba09a), outline near-black brown (#292018).

AVOID: any ghost, green or glow, a full locomotive or wagon, a mine cart, wood, a robot, arms or legs, a mouth full of teeth, cute/kawaii/chibi style, Trubbish or Garbodor look, too many tiny parts, too much detail.

FORMAT: 64x64 pixel sprite, small creature (fills about half the canvas), max 16 colors, 1 px dark outline, flat Gen 3 shading, no dithering, compact silhouette that fits in a square, plain white background, no ground, floor, shadow or rails under it.
