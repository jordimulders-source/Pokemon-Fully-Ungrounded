# Gemini-prompt v1: regionale Oddish (fase 1), 20 nov
Plakken in een **nieuw gesprek**, zonder bijlagen.

```
Design a regional form of the Pokémon Oddish and draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style pixel art).

CONCEPT
- Oddish from a deep-sea region near the Galápagos, based on the giant tube worm (Riftia) of hydrothermal vents. Type: Water.
- Keep Oddish's body and face EXACTLY as in the original: the same round bulb body, the same two small stubby feet, the same face (eyes, mouth, placement). Only the color and what grows on top change.
- Body color: white / cream, like the white tube of a giant tube worm.
- On top of the head, instead of Oddish's leaves: the bright red, feathery plume of a giant tube worm, in the same place and roughly the same size as Oddish's leaves.

POSE
- Three-quarter view facing left, the classic Oddish standing pose.

STYLE AND FORMAT
- Gen 3 composition: compact. Small first stage, about 40x40 px of a 64x64 canvas.
- Max 16 colors, 1 px dark outline, light from top left, flat Gen 3 shading with 3 to 4 tones per color, no dithering.
- Plain white background. No ground, water, bubbles, shadow or extra effects. One single creature, no extra views.

AVOID
- No leaves, no flower, no anemone tentacles, no fire or smoke.
- Do not change the body shape, the feet or the face of Oddish.
- Not a plush toy, chibi or glossy 3D render.
```
