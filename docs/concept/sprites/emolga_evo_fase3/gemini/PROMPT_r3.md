```
Design a new, original Pokémon.
Draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): pixel art, 64×64, max 16 colors, dark selout outline, plain white background.

WHAT IT IS
A fruit vampire: a vampire bat that feeds on fruit.
Anatomy: based on a real South American FRUIT BAT (Artibeus): short broad snout with a leaf-shaped nose, big dark eyes, large wings made of skin membrane. Large, adult, not cute.
PROPORTIONS (important): it is LONG and slender, not compact. Hanging, it is about 3 times as tall as it is wide. The body is about 3 times as long as the head. The closed wings are long and narrow, wrapped tightly around the body like a folded umbrella.
Ears: very long and pointed, each ear as long as the whole head, black on the outside.
Face: the fur around its eyes has a different color, a cool blue-grey, like the tint of a helmet visor. This is only a FUR PATTERN with soft, fur-like edges that follow the shape of the face. The eyes are fully visible inside it, drawn normally. It must NOT read as glasses, goggles or a mask: no lenses, no frame, no band across the face. It has NO cheek spots.

COLORS
White face and white belly. Black on top of the head, on the ears, on the back and on the outer side of the wing membrane. Bright yellow on the inner side of the wing membrane. Blue-grey fur around the eyes.

POSE
It hangs upside down by its feet, head pointing down. The HEAD IS UPSIDE DOWN TOO, like a real hanging bat: the ears point down, the chin and mouth point up, the eyes are below the nose. Not a face turned the right way up. Its wings are closed and wrapped around its body. Its feet grip at the top as if hanging from a branch, but do NOT draw the branch.

VIEW
Three-quarter view facing left, like every Gen 3 front sprite.
It is big: it fills the full height of the 64×64 frame, from the feet at the top to the head at the bottom.

DO NOT
No effects, no glow, no electricity.
No ground, no branch, no shadow.
No cape, no clothing or accessories, no fruit held or drawn.
No plush/Neopets look, not a human in a costume.
```
