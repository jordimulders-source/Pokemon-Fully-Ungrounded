# Regional Claydol – Gemini r8b (nieuw gesprek, zelfde bijlage)
r8 tekende een heel nieuw (geen pixel-art) wezen; alleen de gezichtsindeling was bruikbaar. r8b: sterker als pixelbewerking geformuleerd, gezicht in dezelfde woorden.
Bijlage: `claydol_gemini_r3/r5/claydol_g3r5_paletB_8x.png`.

```
This is a PIXEL EDIT task, not a new drawing.
The attached image is a 64×64 pixel-art sprite, enlarged 8× (every sprite pixel is an 8×8 block). Return the SAME 64×64 sprite at the same 8× size, with the same blocks. Do not redraw it, do not change its style, do not make it smooth or high resolution, do not change its pose, body, arms, feet, staffs, crown of small eyes or the point on top. Only repaint the pixels inside the big round head, using only colors that are already in the sprite.

Inside the head, the new face:
- TWO big eyes instead of three: round, cream white, dark ring, one short horizontal line in the middle (no pupils, no mouth).
- A stone nose ridge in the middle between the two eyes.
- Three-quarter view facing left: the two eyes and nose sit a little to the left of the middle of the head; the right part of the head is the side of the head.
- The red line: one arch above each eye, meeting in a V above the nose; after the right arch it curves into a horizontal line around the side of the head.
- The rest of the head: smooth round stone, light top left, darker bottom right, a few thin cracks, no other patterns.
```
