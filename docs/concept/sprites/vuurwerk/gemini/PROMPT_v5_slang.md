## Prompt

```
Design an original Pokémon and draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style).

CONCEPT
- Fire/Fairy type. A SERPENT WHOSE SHAPE IS MADE OF FIRE AND FIREWORKS: the long body is smooth, glowing flame, like the light trail of a firework, coiled in an S-shape.
- ITS HEAD IS A FIREWORK EXPLOSION: a crown of large flame points bursting outward like a star, with a small readable serpent face (eyes and mouth) in the center.
- THE TAIL IS ONE FIREWORK ROCKET, pointing upward or half upward: the end of the body becomes a rocket casing with a pointed tip.
- [REGEL A OF B HIER]
- A few separate teardrop-shaped sparks fly outward from the body, close to it.
- Colors: YELLOW, RED AND PINK, ordered like a real firework: bright yellow-white near the face and body core, red further out, pink at the tips.
- It chases away evil spirits: a bold, defiant, protective pose, head raised.
- Final stage of a three-stage line (sparkler -> ground spinner -> firework rocket).

STYLE AND FORMAT
- Official Gen 3 front sprite, 64x64, max 16 colors, 1 px dark outline, light from top left, flat Gen 3 shading with 3 to 4 tones per color, no dithering.
- Three-quarter view facing left. Compact composition that fits a square; the coils stay close together.
- Plain white background. NO floor, shadow or smoke. One single creature.

AVOID
- Not a Chinese dragon, no horns, no whiskers. Not Rayquaza, not Gyarados. Not a rainbow, no green or blue. No text. No glossy 3D render.
```
