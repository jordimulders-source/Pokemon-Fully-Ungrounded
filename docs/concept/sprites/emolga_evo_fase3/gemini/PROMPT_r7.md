```
Design a new, original Pokémon.
Draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): pixel art, 64×64, max 16 colors, dark selout outline, plain white background.

WHAT IT IS
A fruit vampire: a vampire bat that feeds on fruit.
Anatomy: based on a real South American FRUIT BAT (Artibeus): short broad snout with a small dark leaf-shaped nose, big dark eyes, large wings made of skin membrane. Large, adult, not cute.
PROPORTIONS (important): it is long, but not thin. Hanging, it is about 2.3 times as tall as it is wide. The body is about 3 times as long as the head.
Ears: very long and pointed, each ear as long as the whole head, black on the outside.
Head: black on top, like a cap, and the black comes down onto the forehead in a pointed V. The rest of the face is white.
Face: the fur around its eyes is a dark slate grey, like the tint of a helmet visor. This is only a FUR PATTERN with soft, fur-like edges that follow the shape of the face. The eyes are fully visible inside it, drawn normally. It must NOT read as glasses, goggles or a mask: no lenses, no frame, no band across the face. It has NO cheek spots.
The snout and chin are white fur, not yellow: nothing that looks like a bird's beak.

WINGS
The wings are wrapped around the body, but loosely: near the head they fall open a little, so a clear, large area of the bright yellow inner membrane is visible on the front. The outer side of the membrane is black. Because the wings fall open, it is wider than a tightly wrapped bat.

COLORS
White face and white belly. Black on the head cap with its V on the forehead, on the ears, on the back and on the outer side of the wing membrane. Bright yellow on the inner side of the wing membrane, clearly visible as a large yellow area. Dark slate grey fur around the eyes.

POSE
It hangs upside down by its feet, head pointing down. The HEAD IS UPSIDE DOWN TOO, like a real hanging bat: the ears point down, the chin and mouth point up, the eyes are below the nose. Not a face turned the right way up. Its feet grip at the top as if hanging from a branch, but do NOT draw the branch.

VIEW
Three-quarter view facing left, like every Gen 3 front sprite.
It is big: it fills the full height of the 64×64 frame, from the feet at the top to the head at the bottom.

DO NOT
No effects, no glow, no electricity.
No ground, no branch, no rope, nothing above the feet, no shadow.
No cape, no clothing or accessories, no fruit held or drawn.
No plush/Neopets look, not a human in a costume.
```
