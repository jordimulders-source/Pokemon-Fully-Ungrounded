# Gemini-prompt: Gnometrotter (Grass-vorm (bos)), v1 (23 nov)

Nieuw gesprek, geen bijlage. Jordi: "nu gaan we ook de grass en ice vorm maken, zelfde idee, alleen dan wordt gras bladeren en hout en ijs wordt allerlei vormen van ijs". Basis = Rock-prompt v8 (gaf r8), materiaal vervangen.

## Ingevuld door Claude (voorleggen)
- Verdeling per onderdeel (zie COLOR PATTERN); roze en blauw blijven, de rest in de kleuren van het materiaal.

## Prompt

```
Design an original Pokémon and draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style).

CONCEPT
- Fairy/Grass type, the final stage of a three-stage evolution line. Its name is Gnometrotter: a gnome Pokémon that has walked so far for so long that its body has fully adapted to its environment. This is its Forest Form: after ages in forests, its whole body has turned into wood and leaves. A forest gnome: an old garden gnome that has become a living wooden figure, but every part keeps its original color.
- It must still clearly be the evolution of a cartoony garden-gnome Pokémon: a big round pink nose, a droopy gnome cap over the eyes, a round pink belly, a sky-blue lower half and rounded blue feet.

WOOD AND LEAVES IN THE SHAPES (most important)
- Show the material through its SHAPES. The body is built out of separate chunky pieces of wood fitted together, like carved wooden blocks, with visible seams between the pieces. Arms and legs are made of wooden segments stacked on each other. A little wood grain is fine, but the shapes do the work.
- COLOR PATTERN, all wood or leaves:
  - Nose: one big, perfectly ROUND, ball-shaped nose of PINK wood, like a polished round knot. Not oval, not long.
  - Body and free arm: PINK wood pieces. The free hand is a round ball of pink wood.
  - Lower half of the body and legs: SKY-BLUE wood pieces, like garden gnome trousers carved from blue wood.
  - Feet: sky-blue wood, on thick soles of brown bark.
  - Cap: made of big, overlapping DARK GREEN leaves forming the droopy pointed cap shape.
  - Beard, mustache and hair tufts: LIGHTER GREEN leaves hanging in layers, like a beard of leaves.
  - Belt ring and back ridge: rough BROWN bark, with a few short twigs or small leafy sprouts growing from the back ridge.
  - Stick arm: one arm, from the shoulder down, has grown into a long brown branch used as a walking stick; the fist and the branch are one piece of wood.

SHAPE AND POSE
- Posture: hunched forward with age, taking a slow, heavy step, leaning on its stick. An old, seasoned hiker.
- A round, chubby body, like a ball. NOT muscular: no big biceps, no broad chest.
- Its free arm (the one without the stick) is a short arm ending in a round, chubby ball-shaped hand.
- Rounded feet, a bit big, each on a thick, chunky sole that sticks out a little at the front and back. No toes.
- Cap: the droopy pointed shape of a soft gnome cap (NOT tall, NOT straight up); its tip hangs down behind and fuses with a ridge growing out of its shoulders and back.
- The cap comes down low over the face: its lower edge overlaps and covers the top of the nose. The cap touches the nose directly, so there is NO dark band, gap, slit or shadow between cap and face, and nothing that could read as eyes.
- Belt section: a thick ring around the waist, between the pink body and the blue lower half, connected to the ridge on its back: back and belt are one mass. Buckle included in the same material.
- A heavy beard with the mustache, from under the nose down to the belt ring, and tufts of hair sticking out sideways from under the cap. NO mouth visible.
- Everything is one body of the same material, nothing is stuck on.

STYLE AND FORMAT
- Official Gen 3 front sprite, 64x64, max 16 colors, 1 px dark outline, light from top left, flat Gen 3 shading with 3 to 4 tones per color, no dithering.
- Keep it readable at sprite size: big shapes and seams between the pieces, no fine texture or speckles.
- Three-quarter view facing left. A final-stage Pokémon, about 60 px tall, compact, centered in the frame.
- Plain white background. NO floor, shadow, ground, background or effects. One single creature.
- Cartoony, but in the Pokémon way: bold shapes, a clear and cool silhouette, like an official Ken Sugimori design.

AVOID
- No visible eyes, no dark band under the cap, no visible mouth. No soft skin anywhere. No flat cap or beret: it must be a long, droopy, pointed gnome cap. No leather belt. No muscles. No toes. No oval or long nose. No glow, no sparkles. No human proportions, no little man in a costume. Not Impidimp. No flowers, no mushrooms, no fruit. Not Trevenant, not Sudowoodo, not Shiftry. No moss carpet. No western-cartoon or plush look. No glossy 3D render.
```
