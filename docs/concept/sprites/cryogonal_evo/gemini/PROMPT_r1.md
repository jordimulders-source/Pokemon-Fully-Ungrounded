# Gemini-prompt r1 (compositie A)

Plakken in een nieuw Gemini-gesprek, zonder bijlage.

Notitie (eigen toevoegingen van Claude, schrappen bij twijfel): ketting uit de mond, achterste plaat zonder gezicht, holle uiteinden van de zuil.

```
Design an original Pokémon and draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): 64x64 pixels, max 16 colors, clean dark outline, flat cel shading, plain white background. No ground, no shadow, no glow, no sparkles, no special effects.

WHAT IT IS (most important): a living ice crystal shaped like a real "capped column" snow crystal. It is NOT an animal and NOT a humanoid: no limbs, no tail, no hair, no body parts at all. It is a floating geometric object with a face.

SHAPE:
- A thick hexagonal ice column (a six-sided prism) with a large flat hexagonal snowflake plate at each end, like a spool of thread.
- The column points toward the viewer, seen in 3/4 view facing left: the FRONT plate is large and faces us, the column goes back in perspective, and the BACK plate is visible behind it, partly hidden, a bit smaller because it is farther away.
- Both plates are flat six-pointed hexagonal snowflakes with sharp, straight, crisp edges and short pointed arms. Strict geometry: straight lines, sharp angles, perfect six-fold symmetry. Nothing rounded or organic.
- The column's six side faces are clearly separated by hard light and dark planes, so the 3D prism reads clearly at 64x64.
- The ends of the column are slightly hollow.
- It floats in the air.

FACE (only on the front plate, nowhere else): the face is made of two horizontal cracks in the ice. The upper crack contains two small glowing eyes, the lower, wider crack is a large open mouth. The expression is frozen, still and aggressive. From the mouth hangs one chain made of ice links.

COLORS: icy pale blue and white with shiny highlights, darker steel-blue on the shaded faces of the column, eyes in a single solid color. Icy and cold, glossy like polished ice.

DO NOT: add arms, legs, wings, a body, a crown, horns or a cape; draw a realistic photo crystal or a gem; add a starfield or galaxy pattern; add snow particles or a glow aura; make it round, cute or plush.
```
