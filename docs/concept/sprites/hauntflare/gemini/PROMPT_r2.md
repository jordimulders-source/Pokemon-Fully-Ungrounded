## Prompt

```
Design a new Pokémon and draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): 64x64 pixel art, at most 16 colours, 1-pixel dark outline around every part, light from the top left, plain white background, no ground, no shadow, no glow, no sparkles, no effects outside the creature.

The Pokémon is "Hauntflare", a Cosmic/Poison type based on a PROTOSTAR: a newborn star inside a cloud of dust that shoots out two jets of gas from its opposite poles (Herbig-Haro jets). It is NOT a ghost and NOT related to Haunter; it only looks similar by coincidence (convergent evolution, like Wiglett and Diglett). Its shape must clearly differ from Haunter.

FACE: the attached sprite is HAUNTER. The eyes and the wide grin must be exactly Haunter's from this attachment (same shape, same size, same teeth), not Gengar's. The face sits on the LEFT part of the ball, because the Pokémon looks to the left.

BODY: a round ball of hot glowing gas (the young star), soft and matte, like a cloud of light, NOT glossy, no shiny plastic highlight. Around its middle a THICK, DARK DISC of dust that hugs the ball closely: it crosses only the lower half of the ball and sticks out only a little at the sides. It is NOT a thin ring like Saturn's and it does not stick out far from the ball. The face sits above the disc.
JETS: two jets of gas shoot out of the ball in OPPOSITE directions, drawn as solid, clean shapes:
- the upper jet comes out of the top of the ball, pointing up and slightly back, as two or three sharp flame-like spikes that give the head its pointed shape;
- the lower jet comes out of the bottom of the ball, pointing down, as one long, tapering, wavy tail that ends in a point.
The two jets are on one straight line through the ball (top and bottom), not both trailing to the same side like a comet.
HANDS: exactly two hands, shaped like Haunter's hands (three fingers), made of bright knots of glowing gas. They float free on either side of the ball, with a clear gap of white background between each hand and the body. Nothing else floats.
COLOUR: the real colours of Herbig-Haro jets: the ball pinkish-white in the centre, the jets and hands red and rose-pink with orange edges, the dust disc dark reddish-brown. NOT Haunter's purple, not blue.

POSE: a true three-quarter view facing LEFT, like most Gen 3 front sprites: the face on the left part of the ball, the right side of the ball curving away. Floating in space. The near hand (left) bigger and in front, the far hand (right) smaller and partly behind. Compact so everything fits in a square.

AVOID: Gengar's face, facing right, a thin Saturn ring, a glossy plastic ball, both jets trailing to one side like a comet or fireball, a ghost look, a purple colour, legs or feet, arms connected to the body, extra hands, stars or sparkles around it, glow effects, messy smoke strands, a cute plush, chibi or Neopets look, a glossy 3D render.
```
