# Regionale Mr. Mime (jiujitsu) – Gemini-startprompt r1 (22 nov, nieuw gesprek, geen bijlage)

```
Design a brand-new regional form of Mr. Mime: a Brazilian jiu-jitsu fighter. Type Fighting/Psychic.
Draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): pixel art, 64×64, max 16 colors, dark selout outline, plain white background.

WHAT IT IS
Mr. Mime as a jiu-jitsu fighter, lying with its back on the ground and inviting the opponent into its guard ("get into my guard").
Keep from Mr. Mime: its face with the red cheek circles, the blue hair spikes on its head, and the round pink balls (on hands, shoulders, knees).
It wears a BLUE jiu-jitsu gi with a belt.
Keep Mr. Mime's Pokémon proportions (big round head, round body, short limbs): it is a Pokémon, not a human in a gi.

POSE
Lying on its back, head and shoulders slightly raised, legs up in an open guard, inviting the opponent in.

VIEW
Three-quarter view facing left, like every Gen 3 front sprite: the far limbs on the left (partly hidden), the near ones on the right.
Compact: the whole Pokémon fits inside a square.

DO NOT
No ground, no mat, no rock, no shadow under it. No glow, no aura, no effects, no motion lines.
No opponent, nothing else in the image.
No plush/Neopets look, no chibi mascot, not a realistic human.
```
