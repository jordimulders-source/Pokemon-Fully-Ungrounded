# Dundragoon – Gemini-startprompt r7 (21 nov, nieuw gesprek, geen bijlage)

Na r6: "best prima, maar hij mag nog iets dikker". Prompt r6 met alleen het lijf dikker (en "exactly 2 legs": r6 kreeg er 4).

---

```
Design a brand-new Pokémon: a lazy, goofy dragon in mid-flight. Type Dragon/Normal. It is the evolution of Dunsparce, but it is a new creature, not a copy of Dunsparce and NOT a Dragonite.
Draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): pixel art, 64×64, max 16 colors, dark selout outline, plain white background.

POSE (most important)
It is FLYING, lazily rising up through the air on a steep DIAGONAL: the head is high at the upper left, the body slopes down behind it, and the drill tail hangs low at the lower right. Seen as a whole, its body is a diagonal line from upper left to lower right, NOT a horizontal line. The 2 short legs dangle loosely under the body. The wings are raised on its back.

WHAT IT IS
Body: a short, compact, VERY THICK, fat and heavy sausage-shaped torso (like Dunsparce's, but much chunkier): the body is clearly thicker than the head is tall, a round, bulging belly and back. Not long: the body is about as long as the head plus the drill together. No long neck: the head sits on a short, thick neck.
Head: a dragon head with a longer snout and 2 small straight pale blue-white horns pointing backward. A blue ring around the eye; inside it the eye is closed in a lazy line. A blue marking along the jaw. Expression lazy and goofy, not fierce.
Colors: pale yellow body with a few blue bands across the back. No light belly plate, no belly stripes.
Legs: exactly 2 short, stubby legs (not 4). NO arms, no hands.
Tail: a thick tail that ends in a BIG, CLEAR DRILL: a spiral, cone-shaped drill tip, large in proportion to the body, flowing seamlessly from the tail.
Wings: 2 wings (near and far) on the back, pale blue-white, based on Dunsparce's small wings but bigger, with soft rounded feather-like lobes; quite bizarre and unusual, NOT typical dragon wings (no bat wings).

VIEW
Three-quarter view facing left, like every Gen 3 front sprite: the far wing and far leg are on the left (narrower, partly hidden), the near ones on the right.
Compact: the whole Pokémon fits inside a square.

DO NOT
Not horizontal, not lying or walking on the ground, not a long stretched body.
Not a snake: no long thin body, no long neck, no S-curve.
Not Dragonite: no upright standing pose, no arms, no pale striped belly, no curled antennae.
No bat wings, no typical dragon wings. No beard, no whiskers.
No ground, no rock, no shadow under it. No glow, no effects, no motion lines.
No plush/Neopets look, no chibi mascot, not a Western knight dragon, not a realistic reptile.
```
