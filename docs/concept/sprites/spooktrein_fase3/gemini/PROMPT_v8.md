# Gemini-prompt: spooktrein fase 3 (Steel/Ghost), v8 (15 nov)

Jordi: "het moet echt een stuk schroot zijn dat zonder de geest van ellende uit elkaar zou vallen, maar nu is het een razendsnel voertuig geworden, en de geest heeft er ontzettend veel lol in, een beetje zoals rotom" / "we moeten duidelijker de vorm gaan beschrijven" / "de wielen mogen best onderdeel van de geest zijn".
Vormbeschrijving: voorstel Claude (druppelvorm, bolle kop, wigkaak, schoorsteen naar achteren, gerafeld einde); Jordi reageerde met de schroot-kern, niet op de losse punten.
Sugimori-ontwerp eerst; daarna stap 2 (sprite) zoals in `PROMPT_v5.md`.

```
Design an original Pokémon in the official Ken Sugimori Pokémon artwork style (clean lines, flat cel shading, plain white background).

TYPE: Steel / Ghost. Final stage of its line.

CONCEPT: a piece of rusty railway scrap that would miserably fall apart without the ghost inside it. The ghost holds it together and has turned it into a blazing fast vehicle, and it is having an enormous amount of fun, a bit like Rotom. It is ONE creature, not a train with a ghost added.

HELD TOGETHER BY THE GHOST
- The body is made of loose, battered pieces of rusty iron (bent plates, a dented chimney, a broken cowcatcher) that do not really fit together.
- Green ghost energy fills the cracks and gaps between the pieces and holds them in place, like glue made of ghost.

SHAPE (follow this closely)
- Overall: a teardrop or tadpole shape lying on its side, thick and round at the front, narrowing towards the back. Long and low, fitting a square. The whole body tilts forward and down, as if diving ahead.
- Head (front half): one big rounded iron block with a bulging, bullet-like nose. No flat round door, no plate with a face on it.
- Eyes: two identical, big green eyes high on the front, slightly squinted and mischievous, glowing out of a crack in the iron.
- Mouth: the cowcatcher is a wedge-shaped lower jaw sticking forward under the head; its slanted bars are the teeth. It is open in a wide, cheeky grin, green inside.
- Chimney: short, wide and dented, bent backwards as if by the wind, with a wisp of green ghost smoke.
- Body (back half): the iron narrows and ends in a ragged, broken edge. Out of the open back, the ghost streams out as a green smoke tail.
- Wheels: no iron wheels. Two big wheels made of green ghost energy under the head, like front legs, spinning in motion like in a comic book. One or two faint ghostly wheels in the smoke tail.

COLORS: rusty iron (rust brown with a bit of dark grey); everything of the ghost is green.

EXPRESSION: cheeky and mischievous, clearly enjoying the speed. Not angry, not scary.

VIEW: slightly more frontal than three-quarter, facing left.

AVOID: not a realistic or complete locomotive, no cab, no windows, no rods, no pipes, no wagons, no headlight, no ghost sitting inside or looking out, no human face (no nose, no eyebrows, no lips), not Thomas the Tank Engine, no skull, no conductor, no other ghosts, no rails, no speed lines.
```
