# Kudu (fase 3 impala-lijn, Normal/Steel): Gemini-prompts
Besluiten: `../SPEC.md`. Lessen meegenomen: geen bijlage, pre-evolutie niet noemen, Steel = materiaal van een lichaamsdeel (geen platen), driekwart naar links (verre hoorn/oor/poten links), Pokémon-verhoudingen bovenaan (dikdik r3, impala).
Eigen invulling Claude (niet door Jordi gezegd, schrappen bij twijfel):
- Soort: mannetjeskudu (grote kudu, *Tragelaphus strepsiceros*), want alleen die heeft hoorns.
- Aantal strepen niet genoemd ("several"); V als chevron tussen de ogen zoals bij een echte kudu.
- Gouden vacht mat en warm, hoorns koel en spiegelend, zodat ze niet samenvloeien; manen en keelfranje iets donkerder goud.
- Ogen/kop: zelfde Pokémon-eisen als bij de impala (groot hoofd, grote ogen, grote oren; echte kudu heeft ook grote ronde oren).

## r1 (nieuw gesprek, zonder bijlage)
```
Design an original Pokemon. Type: Normal/Steel. Final stage of a three-stage line.

Concept: a male greater kudu, the African antelope, in Pokemon style. Its horns are real metal: mirror-polished steel, growing from its head as part of its body. They must have the real kudu horn shape: two long open corkscrew spirals, each with 2 to 2.5 wide twists, rising up and back from the head and diverging outward, with a ridge (keel) running along the spiral. The horns are clearly exaggerated: very large, about as tall as the body, so they dominate the silhouette like a crown. The design is stylized, but it must still be recognizable as a kudu. It does not hold or carry anything.

PROPORTIONS AND PERSONALITY (most important): this is a Pokemon, not a realistic animal and not a recolored animal. Drawn as a Pokemon designed by Ken Sugimori: stylized, simplified, bold shapes. Big head with big expressive eyes, large rounded ears (like a real kudu's, a bit bigger), strong neck, powerful body with a deep chest, legs strong but not long and stilt-like. Final stage: the most powerful and impressive of the line, but clearly a Pokemon. Personality: regal and calm. Majestic, serene, dignified expression. Pose: standing still and proud, head held high, the horns carried like a crown.

Anatomy: based on a real male greater kudu (Tragelaphus strepsiceros): large antelope body with a slightly humped shoulder, deep chest, small hooves, large rounded ears, big dark eyes. A mane of longer fur runs down the back of the neck, and a fringe of long hair hangs under the throat, both as clear shapes with their own outline.

Colors and pattern: the fur is gold (normal fur, matte and warm, not metal); the mane and throat fringe are a slightly darker gold. The real kudu pattern made sharper and bolder: several thin, clean white vertical stripes down the sides of the body, clearly visible (they are a key feature of the design), and a white chevron (V) between the eyes. Horns: mirror-polished steel, clearly different from the fur: cool silver-white, with very hard shine, strong white highlights, dark reflections, and a bright white sharpened edge along the keel of the spiral.

Style: official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style), 64x64, max 16 colors, dark outline, facing 3/4 to the left (the far horn, far ear and far legs are on the left, the near ones on the right), a big sprite: the Pokemon fills almost the whole 64x64 frame, compact composition that fills a square. White background, no ground, no shadow, no effects, no glow.

AVOID: deer, antlers, realistic animal proportions, a small head on a long neck, long stilt legs, a recolored real animal, straight horns, ram horns curling around the ears, tightly coiled screw horns without open twists, Deerling, Sawsbuck, Stantler, Wyrdeer, armor plates, a metal body, a gold metal body, robot parts, a crown or jewelry, holding or carrying weapons, glowing metal, sparkles, horns in the same color as the fur.
```
