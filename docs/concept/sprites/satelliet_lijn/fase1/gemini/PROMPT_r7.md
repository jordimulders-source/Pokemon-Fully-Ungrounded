# Satelliet-lijn fase 1 – startprompt r7 (23 nov), NIEUW gesprek, geen bijlage
= r6 + langere antennes (precies 4) + groter oog. r5/r6-prompts stonden alleen in de chat; r6 is r7 zonder de punten ANTENNAS-lengte en EYE-grootte.

```
Design a new, original Pokémon.
Draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): pixel art, 64×64, max 16 colors, dark selout outline all around, plain white background.

WHAT IT IS
A small living machine based on Sputnik 1, the first satellite: a polished white sphere with four long purple antennas. The Sputnik inspiration must be clearly recognizable.
It is a pure machine with its own personality, the way Klink, Magnemite and Porygon are Pokémon: no animal parts, no arms, no legs.

THE EYE (important)
Exactly ONE big eye that clearly reads as the creature's EYE, not as a small camera on a machine. It is large: about one third of the sphere's width. Robotic: a few concentric purple rings around a teal iris with a dark pupil. Flat pixel-art colors: no glossy reflection on the lens.

DETAIL LEVEL
Medium: one clearly visible PURPLE seam line around the middle of the sphere (like Sputnik's two halves), 1–2 pixels thick. A PURPLE ball-socket where each antenna attaches. Nothing else: no rivets, no panel grid, no spots, no dishes or knobs on the antennas.

ANTENNAS
EXACTLY FOUR, identical: same purple, same purple socket. They attach at the back of the sphere and sweep backward in two pairs, like Sputnik.
LONG: each antenna is about as long as the sphere is wide, or longer, like the real Sputnik's long whip antennas.
THICK: a solid purple rod at least 3 pixels wide in the 64×64 sprite, with a darker purple shadow side. Never thin lines.

COLORS
Pearly white sphere with purple accents, close to Mewtwo's colors. Smooth, sleek, cyber look. Light from the upper left; the shadow side of the sphere in a clearly deeper lavender-purple.

VIEW (most important)
A true three-quarter view facing LEFT, seen slightly from ABOVE, like Gen 3 sprites of round Pokémon (Voltorb, Shuppet).
- The purple seam is NOT a straight line: it curves as an arc across the sphere.
- The eye sits on the front-left of the sphere, about one third in from the left edge, seen slightly from the side (a slightly oval eye).
- The antennas go back to the right and away from the viewer; the far ones are partly hidden behind the sphere.

POSE
It floats in the air, tilted slightly as if looking up curiously.
With the antennas it fills about two thirds of the 64×64 frame; the sphere itself is about half the frame wide.

DO NOT
No effects, no glow, no electricity, no stars or planets around it.
No ground, no shadow.
Not Magnemite, not Voltorb, not a robot with a face screen.
No plush/Neopets look.
```
