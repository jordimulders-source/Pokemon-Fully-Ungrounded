# Gemini-prompt Pachamama r1 (nieuw gesprek, geen bijlage)

Eén regel verschilt: kies OGEN A (open) of B (dicht/slapend). Eigen invulling Claude: zie SPEC.md.

```
Design a new legendary Pokémon as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): pixel art, 64x64, max 16 colors, dark outline, light from top-left, plain white background, no ground, no shadow, no effects.

WHAT IT IS: Mother Earth as a living legendary, Ground/Fairy type. An abstract creature: a living mountain shaped like a mother wrapped in a wide mantle. It is clearly alive, a creature with a face, not a landscape and not a statue.

BODY: the whole body is one big mountain whose shape is a mantle/cloak, wide at the bottom and rising to a summit. The summit is the head: a face built into the top of the mountain, with large eyes. A cap of snow on the summit works like a hood or hair. Two mountain ridges come forward from the sides of the mantle as arms, like a mother holding out her arms. Simple, bold silhouette that fills the square.

EYES (A): large, open, calm eyes.
EYES (B): large eyes, closed, as if sleeping.

COLORS: earthy mountain tones.

POSE: 3/4 view facing left, upright and imposing like a legendary.

DO NOT: human woman, villages, houses, fields, paths, trees, animals, feathers, scales, offerings, gems, glowing, background.
```
