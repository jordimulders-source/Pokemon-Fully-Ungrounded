Design a new Pokémon: the final evolution of Emolga (Electric/Flying flying-squirrel Pokémon). It must look like a real evolution — a clearly bigger, more mature creature with its own design, not Emolga with a new pose.

Concept: an adrenaline-junkie flying squirrel based on WINGSUIT FLYING (people jumping from cliffs in a suit with large fabric wings between arms and body and between the legs). Its gliding membrane is a SUPER LARGE wingsuit: huge taut panels from wrist to ankle on both sides plus a big panel between the legs, wider than the body itself, with clean seam-like edges. Aerodynamic look: streamlined, sleek head swept back, ears flattened backwards by the wind, long narrow body, pointed shapes.

Colour pattern (Emolga's real pattern): white face and white belly/underside of the body; black on top of the head, the back and the upper side of the wing membrane; bright yellow on the underside of the wing membrane and on the cheeks. Not mostly yellow.

Pose: diving steeply downward like a real wingsuit flyer — head down and forward, body at about 45–60 degrees pointing down-left, arms and legs spread wide so the whole wingsuit is stretched open, tail trailing straight behind. Exactly two arms and two legs; the near wing panel is fully visible, the far wing panel partly behind the body. Confident daredevil expression. No helmet, no goggles, no clothing or accessories.

Style: official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style), 64×64 pixels, max 16 colours, dark outline, 3/4 view facing left, compact composition filling the square. No electricity, sparks, speed lines, glow or effects; no ground, cloud, rock or shadow. Plain white background.

AVOID: plush/Neopets look, bat or dragon wings, bird feathers, a human in a costume, a parachute, a simply bigger Emolga.
