# Diepzee-legendary – Gemini r2, NIEUW gesprek, geen bijlage (23 nov)
Jordi over r1: "hij lijkt gewoon op een water pokemon, niet op een legendary cryptid" · "en geen mooi design". Op de vraag wat hem legendary maakt: "nee het silhouet, die echte zien er eng uit omdat ze bizar lang zijn, de hele vorm van het dier lijkt nep". Kleur: "zoals de bekende foto/video".
Uit r1 geschrapt (eigen toevoegingen Claude): vliegervorm, "few arms", pixelbreedtes. Gehouden: recht met ellebogen (echte dier), anti-Tentacruel (Jordi's vraag).

```
Design a new, original LEGENDARY Pokémon, Water type, from the deepest part of the ocean.
Draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): pixel art, 64×64, max 16 colors, dark selout outline, plain white background.

THE SILHOUETTE (most important)
It is based on the real BIGFIN SQUID (Magnapinna), the cryptid squid only ever seen on deep-sea robot cameras. What makes the real animal frightening is its silhouette: it is BIZARRELY LONG. The whole shape of the animal looks fake, as if it cannot be real.
- A small body with wide fins at the top.
- From it, extremely long, thin arms: they first spread out sideways, bend sharply at an "elbow", then hang straight down, far longer than the body, as if they never end.
- The body is tiny compared to the arms. The proportions must feel unnatural and unsettling.

COLORS
Like the famous deep-sea video footage of the bigfin squid: pale, ghostly, almost translucent white with light blue tints.

VIEW
Three-quarter view facing left, like every Gen 3 front sprite. It fills the full height of the 64×64 frame.

DO NOT
Not a normal squid with normal proportions, not a jellyfish, not Tentacruel, not Nihilego, not Malamar, not an octopus.
No wavy or curly tentacles.
No effects, no glow, no bubbles, no water, no ground, no shadow.
No plush/Neopets look, no chibi mascot.
```

Jordi over r2: "haha dit ziet er niet uit" (twee armen als tafelpoten, geen gezicht/ogen, geen wezen).
