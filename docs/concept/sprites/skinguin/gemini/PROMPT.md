# Skinguin – Gemini-startprompt (r1, 19 nov)

Besluiten (Jordi, 19 nov; citaten in `docs/concept/IDEEEN.md` → Delibird-evolutie → Skinguin):
Delibird-evolutie · voeten zijn ski's · rood/wit · ski-/kerstmuts als hoofdvorm, geen accessoire ·
koningspinguïn met snavel en wenkbrauwen van de macaroni (hybride) · kuif "delibird stijl" · pose: L-vorm bergafwaarts,
vleugels strak naar achteren, ski's schuin naar voren, muts/hoofd naar achter · "de zak verdwijnt".

Bijlagen:
1. `bijlage_delibird.png` (Delibird, Gen 3-frontsprite)
2. Een foto van een koningspinguïn (zelf erbij zoeken, liefst van opzij of driekwart)
3. Eventueel een foto van een macaronipinguïn (kop, voor snavel en wenkbrauwen)

---

```
Design a new Pokémon: SKINGUIN, the evolution of DELIBIRD.
Draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): pixel art, 64×64, max 16 colors, dark selout outline, plain white background.

WHAT IT IS
A king penguin that races downhill. It does NOT stand on skis: its feet themselves have the shape of skis (long, flat, rigid feet that grow out of its legs as one body part). There are no separate skis anywhere.
Anatomy: a king penguin (see attached photo): tall, upright, streamlined body, long neck.
Two features come from the MACARONI PENGUIN instead: its aerodynamic beak and its eyebrows (the plumes above the eyes). They have the shape of the macaroni penguin's, drawn in the same art style as Delibird.
It is a Delibird evolution (attached sprite is DELIBIRD): it keeps Delibird's red-and-white color scheme and Delibird's art style.
Its head is shaped like a ski cap / Santa hat: the hat is not an accessory, it is the actual shape of its head (red and white).
It has NO sack and no sack-shaped tail.

POSE
It is shooting down a ski slope at extreme speed. The body forms an L shape: torso upright, the ski-shaped feet pointing forward and diagonally down. Both wings (2 wings in total, near and far) are pressed tightly backwards along the body. The hat-shaped head is swept backwards by the speed.
All the speed comes from the pose itself.

VIEW
Three-quarter view facing left, like every Gen 3 front sprite: the far wing and far ski-shaped foot are on the left (narrower, partly hidden), the near ones on the right.
Compact: the whole Pokémon fits inside a square, limbs close to the body.

DO NOT
No speed lines, no snow spray, no motion blur, no glow or effects.
No slope, no ground, no snow, no shadow under it.
No separate skis, no ski poles, no goggles, no scarf, no clothing or accessories.
No sack. No plush/Neopets look, no chibi mascot, not a human in a penguin costume.
```
