- Uit fase 3 (16 nov): geen rotjes; Gemini maakt geen vuurwerkexplosies (barsten/spikkels pas in de spritefase).

## Lezing en keuzes Claude (niet besproken; schrappen mag)
- **Lezing:** het vuur stroomt uit de onderkant van de pijl, krult omhoog om de pijl en vormt het bovenlijf; het kopje kijkt boven de pijl uit; de armen krullen om de pijl en de handjes zitten daaronder vast aan de huls. Geen benen, geen staart.
- Pijl zoals in fase 3: puntige top, gestreepte huls in roze, magenta en geel (zodat de lijn herkenbaar is).
- Kleur zoals fase 3: kleurverloop van goud-wit bij het kopje via felroze naar magenta onderaan; glitterspikkels, een paar vierpuntige twinkelsterretjes; geen oranje.
- Cute: groot kopje, grote ogen, klein lijfje (verhoudingen van een basisvorm).

## Prompt (A: salamander; voor B "SALAMANDER" → "GECKO" en de anatomieregel vervangen, zie onder)

```
Design an original Pokémon and draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style).

CONCEPT
- Fire/Fairy type, the small, cute first stage of an evolution line.
- A FIREWORK ROCKET STANDS UPRIGHT, pointed tip at the top. Fire pours out of the BOTTOM of the rocket, and that fire IS the Pokémon: a baby SALAMANDER made entirely of fire.
- Only the UPPER BODY of the salamander exists: head, neck, chest and two arms. No legs, no tail. The whole creature comes out of the rocket; nothing else of it is anywhere else.
- The fiery body streams out of the bottom of the rocket and CURLS UP AROUND the rocket, so the little face peeks out ABOVE the rocket.
- The two arms CURL AROUND the rocket first, and only below that curl do you see the small hands, clinging tightly to the rocket.
- Salamander anatomy for the head and hands: broad, rounded, flat head, small round eyes set wide apart, wide gentle smile, short arms with four small rounded fingers.
- CUTE: big head, large shining eyes, small body, happy and curious expression, clinging to its rocket.
- Made of fire: flame edges and flame streaks, not smooth skin. Only the eyes and mouth are drawn into the flame.
- THE ROCKET: pointed tip, striped casing in pink, magenta and yellow. It stands perfectly upright.
- FIREWORK COLORS, NOT CAMPFIRE COLORS: vivid, saturated, glittering. The color shifts along the body like a firework changing color: glittering gold-white at the face, hot pink in the middle, magenta where the fire leaves the rocket. Glitter specks and a few small four-pointed twinkling stars. No orange.
- No firecrackers. NO firework explosions or burst shapes anywhere.

STYLE AND FORMAT
- Official Gen 3 front sprite, 64x64, max 16 colors, 1 px dark outline, light from top left, flat Gen 3 shading with 3 to 4 tones per color, no dithering.
- Solid shapes with clean outlines that read as a clear silhouette at 64x64; no thin strands.
- Three-quarter view facing left. Compact composition that fits a square; the creature is small, well under 64 px tall.
- Plain white background. NO floor, shadow or smoke. One single creature.

AVOID
- No legs, no tail, no full salamander. No creature sitting on or riding the rocket. No smooth skin, no flat single color. No dragon features, no horns. No green, blue or orange. No plush or Neopets look. Not Charmander. No text. No glossy 3D render.
```

### Variant B (gekko)
- `a baby SALAMANDER made entirely of fire` → `a baby GECKO made entirely of fire`; `the salamander` → `the gecko`.
- Anatomieregel vervangen door: `- Gecko anatomy for the head and hands: rounded triangular head, very large round eyes on the sides of the head, wide smile, short arms with sticky toe pads (round tips on the fingers).`
- In AVOID: `no full salamander` → `no full gecko`.
