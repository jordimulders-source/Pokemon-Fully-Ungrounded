# Regionale Cacnea – Gemini-startprompt r1 (23 nov, nieuw gesprek, geen bijlage)

```
Design a regional form of CACNEA: a small first-stage Pokémon, type Rock/Grass. It must stay clearly recognisable as Cacnea.
Draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): pixel art, 64×64, max 16 colors, dark selout outline, plain white background, 3/4 view facing left.

WHAT IT IS
Its whole body IS stone: a living stone in the shape and texture of a YARETA (Azorella compacta), the rock-hard cushion plant of the high Andes. The surface is a dense mass of tiny, tightly packed rosettes, like a lumpy hard cushion that looks like a moss-covered rock. It is not a plant growing on a rock; the creature itself is the stone.

KEEP FROM CACNEA
Round ball body, the small crown on top of the head, two round eyes with black pupils, the dark spots on the face that form its mouth, two thick arms held out to the sides, short stubby feet.

COMPOSITION
Compact, the whole Pokémon fits inside a square, small (it is a first stage), limbs close to the body.

DO NOT
No ground, no pedestal, no rock under it, no shadow. No glow, no effects, no motion lines.
No separate armour plates. Not a plush/Neopets look, not a realistic photo.
```

Notitie: "Keep from Cacnea" (bouw en herkenningspunten) is een voorstel van Claude, zie SPEC.
