```

Design only the HEAD of the MIDDLE stage, as official Gen 3 pixel art (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style). Draw FOUR different variants of this head side by side on one sheet. Only heads, no bodies, no rocket.

- Half-lidded eyes: a cool, confident, slightly cocky look, with a grin.
- No gills. Instead ONE crest of flame that sweeps BACKWARD over the back of the head.
- A small swollen bulge behind each eye, like a real salamander.
- Colors from the attachments: cream-white face, pink and red flames, dark outline.
- Three-quarter view facing left. Not Charmander, no dragon.

Each head about 32x32 pixels, max 16 colors, 1 px dark outline, flat Gen 3 shading, no dithering, plain white background. No sparkles, no text.
```
