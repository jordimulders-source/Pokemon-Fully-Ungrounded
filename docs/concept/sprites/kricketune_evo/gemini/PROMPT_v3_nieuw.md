# Kricketune-evolutie – startprompt v3 (22 nov, nieuw gesprek, geen bijlage)
Jordi: "ja het is vooral de outfit/het lichaam die wat meer moet hebben, het silhouet, kom maar met een prompt" (akkoord met Claudes maestro-definitie).

```
Design a brand-new Pokémon: the final evolution of Kricketune (Kricketot → Kricketune → this). Type Bug/Fairy.
Draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): pixel art, 64×64, max 16 colors, dark selout outline, plain white background.

WHAT IT IS
A cricket that is a world-famous orchestra conductor: a true maestro who masterfully commands a concert hall and an orchestra. An IMPRESSIVE look.
It must clearly read as the third and final stage, bigger and grander than Kricketune. Grander does NOT mean bulkier or more muscular: the grandeur comes from the outfit, the body shape and the SILHOUETTE.
It must clearly read as Kricketune's evolution (red body, curled moustache, dark arms), but it is a new creature, not a copy.

OUTFIT (white tie, the conductor's tailcoat, made of the cricket's own body parts)
- Tailcoat tails: the wing covers hang down behind the body as two long tails, almost to the ground.
- A black jacket: dark shoulders and back, with Kricketune's red as the waistcoat/body underneath.
- A white shirt front on the chest with a white bow tie at the throat (Kricketune's cream color can be this white).
- Maestro hair: the antennae sweep back in long flowing waves, like the famous mane of a great conductor.
- A bigger, neatly curled moustache than Kricketune: groomed and elegant.

PRESENCE
- Tall and perfectly upright, chest out, chin up: proud, confident, in charge.
- A stern, piercing gaze that silences the orchestra with one look.
- Baton arm raised high and precise; the other hand open, directing ("now, you").
- Standing firmly and still on two straight legs; all the movement is in the arms.

BATON
One finger of one hand is a long, thin, pointed baton that grows out of the hand, in the same dark color as the hand. It is the baton and also a weapon, like a crab that has one claw bigger than the other. There is no separate stick.

VIEW
Three-quarter view facing LEFT, like every Gen 3 front sprite: not straight to the front, not facing right.
Compact: the whole Pokémon fits inside a square.

DO NOT
No separate baton held in a hand. Not bulky, not muscular, no lobster or crab claws for arms. No tangled or dangling legs, no floating.
No music notes, no sheet music, no podium, no ground, no shadow. No glow, no aura, no effects, no motion lines.
No plush/Neopets look, no chibi mascot, not a realistic insect painting.
```
