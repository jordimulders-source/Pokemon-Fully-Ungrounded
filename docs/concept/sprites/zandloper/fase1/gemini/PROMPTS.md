# Zandloper-lijn fase 1 – Gemini-prompts

Jordi (26 nov): "fase 1 is gewoon een zandloper met levend zand erin, nog niks eruit of vorm, dus alleen ogen"; "fase 1 is een zandloper met ogen". Fase 2: "de zandloper net gebroken", dus fase 1 is nog heel. Type Ground/Psychic (lijn).
Invulling Claude (geen besluit): de zandloper staat rechtop; glas lichtblauw en houten uiteinden zoals de zandloper onder de djinn van fase 3 (`../fase3/af/`); geen bijlage (les GEMINI.md: bijlage = kopie, en de fase 3-sprite zou een djinn opleveren). In welke bol de ogen zitten laat de prompt aan Gemini.

## r1
```
Design an original Pokémon as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style), 64x64 pixels, max 16 colors, white background, facing 3/4 to the left.

Concept: the first stage of an hourglass Pokémon (Ground/Psychic). It is simply an hourglass with living sand inside. The sand has no shape yet and nothing comes out of the glass: the only sign that it is alive are the eyes in the sand. Only eyes, no mouth, no arms, no legs.
The hourglass is whole and standing upright: pale blue glass with two wooden end caps (top and bottom).
Small first-stage Pokémon. Keep it simple and readable at 64x64: big clear shapes, like a real Gen 3 sprite.

No effects, no glow, no sparkles, no ground or shadow under it. AVOID: a plush toy, Neopets style.
```

**Jordi over r1:** "nee dit is te saai en te basic, we moeten er een pokemon van maken, niet een zandloper mete ogen"; "we moeten er alvast naar hinten dat er een soort djinn in opgesloten zit"; "en de pose moet meer zweven, niet gewoon rechtop staan".

## r2
Hoe het een Pokémon wordt en hoe de djinn-hint eruitziet laat de prompt aan Gemini (geen eigen invulling; "no mouth/arms/legs" uit r1 geschrapt, dat was Claude).
```
Design an original Pokémon as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style), 64x64 pixels, max 16 colors, white background, facing 3/4 to the left.

Concept: the first stage of an hourglass Pokémon (Ground/Psychic). Its final evolution is a sand djinn that escapes from the hourglass. This first stage is still a closed hourglass with living sand inside, but the design must already hint that a kind of djinn is locked up inside it.
It must be a real Pokémon with character, not a plain hourglass with eyes stuck on it: the hourglass itself is the creature's body, designed with personality like official object Pokémon.
The hourglass is still whole: pale blue glass with wooden end caps. Nothing comes out of the glass yet.
POSE: it floats in the air at a tilt, it does not stand upright on the ground.
Small first-stage Pokémon. Keep it simple and readable at 64x64: big clear shapes, like a real Gen 3 sprite.

No effects, no glow, no sparkles, no ground or shadow under it. AVOID: a plain realistic hourglass with eyes, a plush toy, Neopets style.
```
