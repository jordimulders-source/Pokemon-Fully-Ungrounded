# Gemini-prompt: Hanan Pacha, v3 (27 nov)

Nieuw gesprek, geen bijlage. Basis = v2 (deed wat we vroegen), alleen Jordi's punten na r2 veranderd.

## Wijzigingen t.o.v. v2
- Minder Ho-Oh: veren in condorvorm (breed, stijf, vingerpennen) i.p.v. sierlijke feniksslierten; "phoenix" en Ho-Oh expliciet verboden.
- Veel minder zwart (Jordi: duidt op evil).
- Kop groot, hoog en trots; heroïsch.
- Kraag niet meer als uitwaaierende stralen (kalkoen).
- Staart waaiert naar links, rechts en tussen de klauwen (Jordi: "misschien").
- Ingevuld door Claude: lijf vooral wit en goud; kraag als gladde, zachte witte donskraag (zoals de echte condor); zwart alleen nog als klein accent.

## Prompt

```
Design an original LEGENDARY Pokémon and draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style).

POSE (most important)
- A colossal, proud, heroic ANDEAN CONDOR. Its two wings are raised high and CURVE UP AND AROUND the body until the wing tips almost meet above the head: THE WINGS THEMSELVES FORM ONE ROUND, SUN-LIKE RING around the condor. No separate ring or halo, only the wings.
- Three-quarter view facing left, the WHOLE shape visible: both wings fully visible, the far wing not hidden.
- Heroic box-art pose: chest pushed forward, a LARGE head held HIGH on an upright neck, above the body, looking ahead with a fierce, noble eye. The head never hangs down or forward.
- The tail feathers fan out symmetrically: to the left, to the right and down between the talons.

THE CONDOR (must clearly read as a condor, NOT a turkey and NOT a phoenix)
- Vulture head, big and noble: bare wrinkled dark skin (charcoal with a deep red tint), a flat fleshy comb lying on the forehead and top of the beak, NO wattle, NO blue skin. Heavy, sharply hooked beak with a pale ivory tip.
- A smooth, soft collar of white down hugging the base of the neck like a scarf. NOT spiky, NOT fanned out, NOT a turkey fan.
- Broad, strong condor wings with long separated finger-like primary feathers at the tips. Feathers are broad and stiff like a real condor, not flowing ribbons.
- A massive, heavy body with a broad chest.

COLOR
- GOLD is the main color, with warm fire tones (orange, red) as secondary colors in the feathers.
- The body is mainly white and gold. Black is only a small accent (a few feather edges), never a large area: this Pokémon is noble and good, not dark or evil.
- Vivid, bright and dazzling: the most eye-catching legendary of the region.

STYLE AND FORMAT
- Official Gen 3 front sprite, 64x64, max 16 colors, 1 px dark outline, light from top left, flat Gen 3 shading with 3 to 4 tones per color, no dithering.
- A huge legendary that fills almost the whole 64x64 frame. Bold, simple, instantly readable silhouette, like an official Ken Sugimori cover legendary.
- Plain white background. NO floor, rock, shadow, clouds, light rays, glow, sparkles or effects. One single creature.

AVOID
- NOT Ho-Oh, NOT a phoenix, no long flowing streamer tail, no rainbow. Not a turkey, not Moltres, Lugia, Yveltal or Braviary.
- No flames, no fire effects, no glow. No patterns, symbols or geometric markings. No mask, crown, jewelry, sun disc object, halo or armor. No starfield.
- Not a realistic bird painting, not a plush or western-cartoon look, no glossy 3D render.
```
