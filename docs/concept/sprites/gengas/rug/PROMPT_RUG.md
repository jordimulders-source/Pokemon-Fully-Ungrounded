## Prompt
> The first image is a Pokémon battle sprite of my creature (front view): a round planet of gas with cloud bands, ears made of gas, and four detached limbs floating around it. The second image is the official Gen 3 (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald) back sprite of Gengar, only as an example of how back sprites are drawn.
>
> Draw the back sprite of MY creature (first image) in exactly the same way as the Gengar example: seen from behind and slightly above on the left, so we mostly see the back of the round gas body; only the edge of its face is visible at the right side, one eye and the corner of its grin, as in the example. Zoomed in like the example: the body is larger than in the front sprite and the bottom is cut off by a straight horizontal line, with nothing below it.
>
> Keep my creature exactly as it is: same colors, same cloud bands, same gas ears, same four floating limbs (the ones further away are partly hidden behind the body), same art style and outline as the first image. No spikes on its back. Do not add anything new, do not redesign it, and do not copy Gengar's shape or colors.
>
> Gen 3 pixel art, plain white background, no ground, no shadow, no effects, no text.
