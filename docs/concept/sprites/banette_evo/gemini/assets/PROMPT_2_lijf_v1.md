# Lijf op het hoofd, v1 (17 nov)

Hoofd r7 (`hoofd_r7/`), Jordi: "ok de kleuren kloppen niet, maar de vorm iig wel, ze lijken nu wel wat meer uit de zijkant te komen, maar dat is kleurgebruik. Nu gaan we dit hoofd op de rest van het lichaam plakken, doe een prompt voor de rest van de pokemon".
Kleuren van de muts later. Alle besluiten uit SPEC.md, zonder het woord Banette behalve het verbod.
Nieuw gesprek, bijlage `hoofd_r7/hoofd_r7.png`.

```
Attached is the finished HEAD of my original Pokémon, a Ghost/Dark evil puppet master / jester. Keep this head EXACTLY as it is (shape, points, bells, eyes, mouth) and draw the rest of the Pokémon below it. DO NOT DRAW BANETTE.

BODY
- Big, broad and imposing, but NOT muscular: a heavy, wide body shape, smooth, in the same dark colour as the face. No muscles.
- The head sits directly on the body.
- He floats. Under the body, two short stubby legs without detail.

GARMENT
- Around his waist hangs an empty grey cloth doll body, worn like clothing: it has patches and stitches, two limp little arms and a limp tail ending in a yellow tip. It has no face.
- An opened zipper runs around his waist: his own body rises directly out of the opened zipper of this empty doll.

ARMS AND HANDS
- Both arms raised high.
- His hands ARE marionette control crosses: each arm flows directly into its cross in one continuous shape, same dark substance as his body. Not wood, not held in a hand.

PUPPETS
- From each cross hang threads, 1 pixel thick.
- On the threads hangs one Shuppet under each hand, controlled as a puppet, big enough to read clearly.

STYLE AND FORMAT
- Official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style), 64x64, max 16 colours, 1 px dark outline, flat shading, no dithering, plain white background, 3/4 view facing left, seen slightly from above. The whole Pokémon fills almost the whole 64x64 square.
```
