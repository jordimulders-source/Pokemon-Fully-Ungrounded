# Ledian-evolutie – Gemini-prompt r1 (27 nov)

Besluiten (Jordi, 27 nov): Bug/Cosmic, fase 3 (Ledyba → Ledian → deze). "zelfde inspiratie maar dan nog meer superheld", "soort van space super hero. We trekken eigenlijk de designrichting die Ledian heeft nog meer door".
Niet in de prompt: de naam Ledian (les GEMINI.md: anders kopieert Gemini de basisvorm); de kenmerken staan er in woorden in.

```
Design an original Pokémon and draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): 64x64 pixels, at most 16 colors, dark outline, white background, no ground, no shadow, no glow, no energy or special effects.

What it is: a ladybug that is a space superhero. It is the final evolution of a line of upright ladybug Pokémon whose design is based on ladybugs and superheroes; this final form pushes that superhero design further. It is a superhero that can fly through space.

Anatomy (keep these from the line): an upright ladybug body standing on two legs, a round red ladybug shell on its back with black spots, four arms with white round hands like gloves, two long antennae on the head, large eyes, dark body underneath the shell. Bug type and Cosmic type.

Pose and framing: 3/4 view facing left (far arm and far antenna on the left side, near ones on the right), heroic superhero stance. Compact Gen 3 composition that fits a square, limbs close to the body.

AVOID: a human in a costume, a plush toy, Neopets style, cartoon chibi proportions, Gen 5 style, realistic insect rendering, extra limbs beyond four arms and two legs.
```
