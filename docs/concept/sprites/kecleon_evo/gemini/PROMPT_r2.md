# Kecleon-evolutie – Gemini-startprompt r2 (22 nov, NIEUW gesprek, geen bijlage)

r1 (`r1.png`), Jordi: "leuk maar ik zie hier geen kecleon in terug, oog mond, de langere kuif en krulstaart mogen bewaard blijven"; "hij heeft ook iets teveel detail"; "of misschien gewoon de hele hoofdvorm moet vergelijkbaar zijn".

```
Design a brand-new Pokémon: the evolution of Kecleon. Type Grass/Fire. It is a new creature, not a copy of Kecleon, but you must clearly recognize Kecleon in it. The whole HEAD SHAPE is comparable to Kecleon's head. KEEP these Kecleon traits: Kecleon's EYES, Kecleon's MOUTH, Kecleon's longer CREST on the head, and Kecleon's CURLED SPIRAL TAIL.
Less detail: keep it simple and clean, few markings.
Draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): pixel art, 64×64, max 16 colors, dark selout outline, plain white background.

WHAT IT IS
Animal anatomy: a GALÁPAGOS LAND IGUANA (Conolophus subcristatus): a heavy, stocky iguana body, a row of spines along the neck and back, scaly skin, strong clawed limbs and a thick tail (curled into a spiral like Kecleon's). It lives on volcanic islands and eats prickly-pear cactus.
Fire type shown ONLY through its skin colors: warm yellow, orange and red scales like a real land iguana heated on lava rock; one of the other colors is PINK. No actual fire.

POSE
Standing upright on its two hind legs, like Kecleon does, leaning slightly forward.

VIEW
Three-quarter view facing left, like every Gen 3 front sprite: the far arm and leg on the left (partly hidden), the near ones on the right.
Compact: the whole Pokémon fits inside a square, limbs close to the body.

DO NOT
No flames, no fire, no glow, no aura, no effects, no motion lines.
No ground, no rock, no cactus prop, no shadow under it.
Not a chameleon, not a dragon, not a dinosaur.
No plush/Neopets look, no chibi mascot, not a realistic animal painting.
```
