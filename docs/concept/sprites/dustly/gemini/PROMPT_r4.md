## Prompt

```
Design a new Pokémon and draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): 64x64 pixel art, at most 16 colours, light from the top left, plain white background, no ground, no shadow, no glow, no sparkles, no effects outside the creature.

The Pokémon is "Dustly", a Cosmic/Poison type based on a BOK GLOBULE: a small, dark cloud of gas and dust in space in which a star will later be born, lit at one edge by a nearby star. It is NOT a ghost and NOT related to Gastly. ONLY its eyes and mouth are the same as Gastly's; everything else must look clearly different from Gastly.

FACE: the attached sprite is GASTLY. Copy ONLY the eyes and the grin with fangs (same shapes), turned into the three-quarter view. The face is SMALL: about one third of the width of the cloud, placed left of the centre, in the densest part of the cloud.

BODY: a loose, soft CLOUD OF GAS AND DUST, not a ball and not a solid lump. It is densest around the face and gets thinner towards the edges: the outline is irregular and breaks up into wisps, drifting strands and curling tendrils of dust, with a few small gaps where the white background shows through. It must read as smoke-like gas, light and airy, not as something solid or heavy.
COLOUR: dark grey-brown dust, NOT black and NOT rust-brown or orange-brown. Densest part (around the face) the darkest grey-brown, thinner parts and wisps a lighter, softer grey-brown.
LIGHT: only a THIN rim along the upper left edge of the cloud is lit by a nearby star: a narrow line of rose-pink with a little pale orange, one or two pixels wide. The rest of the cloud is not lit. Drawn as solid pixels, not a glow.

POSE: a three-quarter view facing LEFT, floating in space. Compact so it fits in a square; the cloud fills about two thirds of the width of the image, centred.

AVOID: a ball or sphere, a solid heavy lump, cauliflower or brain-like lumps, rust-brown or orange-brown colours, a pink body, a pitch-black body, a face that fills the whole body, a smooth round outline, a halo or ring of gas around the body, Gastly's purple, a ghost look, arms, hands, legs, stars or sparkles, glow effects, a cute plush, chibi or Neopets look, a glossy 3D render.
```
