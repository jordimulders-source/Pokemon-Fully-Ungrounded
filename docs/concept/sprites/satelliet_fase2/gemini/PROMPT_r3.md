Daarom r3 = r1 zo nauwkeurig mogelijk beschreven (onderdelen, aantallen, maten en kleuren uit r1 afgelezen), alleen de hoek wordt een echte Gen 3-driekwart naar links (r1 kijkt naar rechts). Nieuw gesprek, zonder bijlage.

```
Design a Pokémon: an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style), 64×64 pixels, max 16 colors, clean dark outline, plain white background, no ground, no shadow, no effects or glow.

WHAT IT IS: a living satellite. The satellite itself is the creature's body, not a costume or a machine with a face stuck on.

ANGLE (most important): a true Gen 3 front-sprite 3/4 view facing the viewer's LEFT, like every Pokémon front sprite in Ruby/Sapphire. The lens-eye points toward the viewer and slightly to the left. Its far side is on the left of the image and is foreshortened; its near side is on the right. The body and both panels share this ONE consistent perspective.

BODY: a white bullet/acorn-shaped capsule with soft lavender shading. The back is narrower and rounded; the front is wider and ends in a big white dome.
- THE EYE: in the middle of that front dome sits a small camera-lens eye: a lavender ring, a turquoise iris, a dark pupil. Around the dome runs a thin dark line and one lavender ring, like the rim of a lens housing.
- JOINTS: four round purple ball joints on the body: two on top (each is the base of an antenna), one at the upper back, one at the lower back where the far panel is attached.
- ANTENNAE: two long, thin purple antennae with a purple ball on each tip, rising from the two top joints and curving upward and backward like insect feelers. One short purple rod connects the upper back of the body to the far panel.

SOLAR PANELS: two large flat rectangular solar panels, dark navy-purple with a lighter purple grid and a dark outline; each has a grid of 2 columns by 4 rows of cells, and a faint lighter shine band across the cells. Together they are much bigger than the body; each panel is taller than the body. They stick out from the back half of the body, one on each side. Because of the 3/4 view, the near panel (right) looks large and the far panel (left) is foreshortened and partly behind the body.

COLORS: white body with lavender shading, purple joints, antennae and rod, dark navy-purple panels with lighter purple grid lines, turquoise iris as the only accent. Nothing else.

DO NOT: facing right, a side view or a flat front view, panels in different perspectives, a bigger or cartoon eye, a mouth, arms or legs, starfield or galaxy patterns, realistic NASA hardware, cute plush/Neopets style.
```
