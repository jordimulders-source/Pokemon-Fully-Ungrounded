Nieuw gesprek, geen bijlage. Basis: v7 (dichte vormen, sprite-baar), niet v8 (losse strengen).

## Prompt

```
Design an original Pokémon and draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style).

CONCEPT
- Fire/Fairy type. A FURIOUS SERPENT MADE OF FIRE that streams out of a firework rocket: the rocket is its tail, and everything else, body and head, is fire coming out of that rocket.
- THE BODY MAKES SEVERAL RINGS: it loops around itself in multiple coils and rings, so the silhouette is a set of loops, not a single line. The body stays slender and elegant: no muscles, not stocky.
- A FEW LOOSE FLAMES break away from the coils and lick outward, giving the shape motion and dynamism.
- THE FIREWORK GROWS OUT OF THE FIRE ITSELF: here and there the flames of its body burst open into star-shaped firework explosions, as if the fire turns into fireworks.
- ITS HEAD IS MADE OF FIRE: the head itself is shaped out of flames, with flame points forming the snout, jaws and the back of the head. No separate head, no collar or halo. Only the eyes and the wide-open, roaring mouth are drawn into it.
- THE TAIL IS ONE FIREWORK ROCKET with a pointed tip and a striped casing in red, pink and yellow; it may point diagonally down.
- Dynamic, furious and strong, like a fully evolved Pokémon that chases away evil spirits; narrowed, angry eyes.
- FAIRY, BUT NOT CUTE: a wild, fierce fae spirit in the old folklore sense. A few small four-pointed twinkling stars, and a soft pink glow in the core of the body and bursts.
- No arms and no legs.
- Colors: YELLOW, RED AND PINK, with soft pink and bright yellow-white in the cores.

STYLE AND FORMAT
- Official Gen 3 front sprite, 64x64, max 16 colors, 1 px dark outline, light from top left, flat Gen 3 shading with 3 to 4 tones per color, no dithering.
- SOLID SHAPES WITH CLEAN OUTLINES that read as a clear silhouette at 64x64. Flames are solid shapes, not thin strands or loose lines.
- Three-quarter view facing left. Compact composition that fits a square.
- Plain white background. NO floor, shadow or smoke. One single creature.

AVOID
- No thin spark strands or hairlike lines. No firecrackers. No muscles. No solid skin or scales. No collar, mane or halo behind the head. Not a Chinese dragon, no horns, no whiskers. Not Rayquaza, not Gyarados. No green or blue. Nothing cute, no hearts. No text. No glossy 3D render.
```
