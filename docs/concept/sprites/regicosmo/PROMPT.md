# Gemini-prompt Regicosmo r1 (nieuw gesprek, geen bijlage)

Eigen invulling van Claude (schrappen als Jordi het niet wil): aantal bollen (7), rangschikking per lichaamsdeel, "zwakke paarse/blauwe tinten" niet genoemd (kleur aan Gemini gelaten).

```
Design a new legendary Pokémon as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): pixel art, 64x64, max 16 colors, dark outline, light from top-left, plain white background, no ground, no shadow, no effects.

WHAT IT IS: a new member of the Regi family of golem legendaries. Like every Regi it is a faceless golem with no eyes and no mouth; its only "face" is a pattern of glowing dots in the middle of its body. It must clearly look like it belongs next to the other Regis (blocky golem build, wide shoulders, arms, no separate head), but it is a new design, not a copy of any of them.

BODY: the golem is built from separate planets and celestial bodies (small rocky planets, a moon, a ringed planet, etc.) that do NOT touch each other: there are visible gaps between every part. Together they still clearly form a golem silhouette: a big central sphere is the torso, smaller bodies float where the shoulders, arms, hands and lower body would be. Compact, fits in a square, limbs close to the body.

THE DOTS (most important detail): the central sphere has the Regi dot pattern. The dots are a map of its own body: each dot sits in the position that matches one of the floating planets around it, so the dot pattern looks like a tiny star chart / orrery diagram of the golem itself. The dots glow.

POSE: 3/4 view facing left, standing upright and imposing like a Regi.

DO NOT: starfield or galaxy texture on the body, nebula, sparkles, eyes, mouth, separate head, planets touching each other, orbit lines, effects.
```

# r2 (nieuw gesprek, geen bijlage)
Na Jordi op r1: te veel planeten, veel te veel stippen, max 8.
```
Design a new legendary Pokémon as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): pixel art, 64x64, max 16 colors, dark outline, light from top-left, plain white background, no ground, no shadow, no effects. It must read clearly at 64x64, so keep it simple: few, big shapes.

WHAT IT IS: a new member of the Regi family of golem legendaries. Like every Regi it is a faceless golem with no eyes and no mouth; its only "face" is a small pattern of glowing dots in the middle of its body. It must clearly look like it belongs next to the other Regis (blocky golem build, wide shoulders, arms, no separate head), but it is a new design, not a copy of any of them.

BODY: exactly 7 parts, nothing more:
- 1 big central planet = the torso (takes up most of the sprite).
- 2 medium celestial bodies = the shoulders/upper arms, one on each side.
- 2 smaller celestial bodies = the hands, below the shoulders.
- 2 small celestial bodies = the feet, under the torso.
The parts do NOT touch: a small visible gap between each part. Together they clearly form a golem silhouette. Each part is a different kind of celestial body (e.g. a moon, a ringed planet, a rocky planet). No extra moons, pebbles or debris.

THE DOTS: exactly 7 big glowing dots on the central planet, no lines connecting them. 6 dots form a tiny map of the 6 floating parts (each dot in the same relative position as its part: two for the shoulders, two for the hands, two for the feet), plus 1 dot in the center for the torso itself. Each dot is 2x2 or 3x3 pixels at 64x64.

POSE: 3/4 view facing left, standing upright and imposing like a Regi.

DO NOT: more than 7 parts, more than 7 dots, lines between dots, starfield or galaxy texture, nebula, sparkles, eyes, mouth, separate head, orbit lines, effects.
```
