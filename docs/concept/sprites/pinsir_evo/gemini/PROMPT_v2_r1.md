# Pinsir-evolutie – ontwerp v2, startprompt r1 (23 nov, nieuw gesprek, geen bijlage)
Na 11 sprite-rondes op model r4B (te gedetailleerd voor 64 px). Jordi: "jij kan het beste inschatten na zoveel werk wat het best vertaalt, maak een prompt daarvoor". Voorstellen A–F van Claude verwerkt; besluiten van Jordi blijven.

```
Design a brand-new Pokémon: the evolution of Pinsir. Type Bug/Ground. It must clearly read as Pinsir's evolution (much bigger, spikier, more monstrous), but it is a new creature, not a recolored Pinsir.
Draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): pixel art, 64×64, max 16 colors, plain white background. Design it FOR 64×64: big, simple, chunky shapes that stay readable at that size, like Tyranitar, Aggron or Pinsir in Gen 3. No tiny details.

WHAT IT IS
A beetle-kaiju based on the SABERTOOTH LONGHORN BEETLE (Macrodontia cervicornis) from the Amazon, standing UPRIGHT ON TWO legs like a classic movie kaiju, fairly upright (like Tyranitar), not hunched forward.
- Head: a broad, flat, slightly domed head shield on top with 3 big spikes. Under it Pinsir's own mouth (the vertical mouth with rows of teeth on both sides), somewhat bigger; only that one mouth, no snout or nose. Two angry, slanted, light eyes under the edge of the shield, forming an angry V.
- Mandibles: the sabertooth longhorn's mandibles GROW OUT OF THE HEAD (thick at the base, rooted at the two front corners of the head beside the mouth), point forward and curve toward each other at the tips. Medium length: they stay close to the body so the whole Pokémon fits compactly in a square. Each has only 2 or 3 BIG teeth on the inside edge. Dark reddish-brown with darker tips.
- Body: big, heavy, simple. Medium brown with a lighter cream-tan belly plate in a few large segments (like Pinsir's belly). NOT black: there must be clear light and shadow on every part.
- Arms: two short, thick arms held close to the body, each ending in 3 big claws. Simple shapes, no separate plates per joint.
- Legs: two heavy, thick kaiju legs, simple big shapes with 3 big claws per foot.
- Back: two hard wing cases (elytra) lying closed on the back, each one big smooth shape with a few (4 or 5) large dark bark-like blotches on ochre/tan.
- Tail: a thick tail with a row of 4 or 5 BIG spikes.
- Spikes overall: few and big (head, back, tail), each a clean triangle. No rows of tiny spikes.

VIEW
Three-quarter view facing left, like every Gen 3 front sprite: the far limbs on the left (partly hidden), the near ones on the right.
Compact: the whole Pokémon fits inside a square; body large in the frame, mandibles, tail and limbs close to the body.

DO NOT
No tiny spikes, saw-teeth, cones or textures. No thick black lines dividing the body into many small plates. No antennae. Not a dinosaur with a beetle head, not a realistic beetle.
No ground, no rock, no shadow under it. No glow, no aura, no effects, no motion lines.
No plush/Neopets look, no chibi mascot, not a realistic painting.
```
