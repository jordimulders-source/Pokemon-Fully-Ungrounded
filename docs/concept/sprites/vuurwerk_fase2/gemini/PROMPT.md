## Prompt

```
Design an original Pokémon as an official Gen 3 pixel-art front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style).

The two attached sprites are the FIRST stage (a small cute fire axolotl clinging to an upright firework rocket) and the FINAL stage (a large firework fire-serpent whose tail is the rocket) of one evolution line. Draw the MIDDLE stage. Use the attachments only for the colors, the striped rocket and the sprite style. Do NOT copy either pose or body.

WHAT IT IS
A confident fire salamander made entirely of fire, that comes out of a firework rocket and holds that rocket like a bazooka. It is the moment of LAUNCH: the rocket is about to fire at the opponent.

HOW IT IS BUILT, IN THIS ORDER
1. The rocket lies on the creature's shoulder like a bazooka, pointing diagonally FORWARD AND UP (toward the left of the image, toward the opponent).
2. A short, wide jet of fire blasts out of the open BACK end of the rocket (bottom right).
3. That fire becomes the creature's body: one thick wavy flame that leaves the back of the rocket and winds ONCE around the rocket.
4. Out of this flame body come two short arms with small hands. The rear hand grips the rocket near the back like a handle. The front hand holds it under the pointed tip.
5. The head sits beside the rocket, cheek against it, looking along the rocket as if aiming through a sight: one eye squeezed shut, a confident grin.
The creature has NO legs, NO feet, NO tail and no second body: the flame from the rocket is its whole body.

DETAILS
- Head of a fire salamander: flat, broad head, rounded snout, black bulging eyes, swollen glands behind the eyes, a few flame points at the back of the head. No gills. Not Charmander, no dragon.
- Made of fire: flame edges along the body. Colors shift along the body like a firework changing color: cream-white at the head, hot pink in the middle, red where the fire leaves the rocket.
- Rocket: cardboard tube with diagonal pink, magenta and yellow stripes, pointed pink tip.
- Size between the two attachments: bigger and bolder than the first stage, smaller than the final stage.

FORMAT
64x64, max 16 colors, 1 px dark outline, flat Gen 3 shading, no dithering, three-quarter view facing left, compact composition, plain white background, no shadow. No smoke, no speed lines, no sparkles, no firework explosions. No text, no 3D render.
```
