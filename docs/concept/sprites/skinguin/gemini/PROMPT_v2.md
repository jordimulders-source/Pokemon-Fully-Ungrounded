# Skinguin – Gemini-startprompt v2 (19 nov), voor een NIEUW gesprek

Feedback op r1 (`r1.png`), Jordi: "hij moet echt rood wit zijn zoals delibird, de ski's en snavel als uitzondering, die zijn delibird geel, de wenkbrauwen wit. De muts moet onderdeel van het lichaam zijn, het lichaam dat rood is met een witte buik. Daarnaast is die niet voorover genoeg geleund"

```
Design a new Pokémon: SKINGUIN, the evolution of DELIBIRD.
Draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): pixel art, 64×64, max 16 colors, dark selout outline, plain white background.

WHAT IT IS
A king penguin that races downhill. It does NOT stand on skis: its feet themselves have the shape of skis (long, flat, rigid feet that grow directly out of its legs as one body part, not boards under the feet). There are no separate skis anywhere.
Anatomy: a king penguin: tall, streamlined body, long neck.
Two features come from the MACARONI PENGUIN: its aerodynamic beak and its eyebrows (the plumes above the eyes), in the shape of the macaroni penguin's, drawn in Delibird's art style.
Its head is shaped like a ski cap / Santa hat, and this hat shape is part of its body: one continuous red body from the tip of the hat down, no seam between hat and head. It is not a hat it wears.

COLORS (exactly like Delibird)
The body is red with a white belly, like Delibird. NO black, NO dark blue, it is not colored like a real penguin.
Exceptions: the beak and the ski-shaped feet are Delibird's yellow. The eyebrows are white.
It has NO sack and no sack-shaped tail.

POSE
It is shooting down a ski slope at extreme speed. The body forms an L shape with the ski-shaped feet pointing forward and diagonally down, and the whole body leans far forward into the speed. Both wings (2 wings in total, near and far) are pressed tightly backwards along the body. The hat-shaped head is swept backwards by the speed.
All the speed comes from the pose itself.

VIEW
Three-quarter view facing left, like every Gen 3 front sprite: the far wing and far ski-shaped foot are on the left (narrower, partly hidden), the near ones on the right.
Compact: the whole Pokémon fits inside a square, limbs close to the body.

DO NOT
No speed lines, no snow spray, no motion blur, no glow or effects.
No slope, no ground, no snow, no shadow under it.
No separate skis, no sled, no ski poles, no goggles, no scarf, no clothing or accessories.
No sack. No plush/Neopets look, no chibi mascot, not a human in a penguin costume.
```
