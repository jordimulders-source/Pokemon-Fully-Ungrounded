# Mightyena-evolutie – Gemini-startprompt r1 (22 nov, nieuw gesprek, geen bijlage)

```
Design a brand-new Pokémon: the evolution of Mightyena. Type Dark/Fighting. It is the female clan leader (matriarch) of a hyena pack. It must clearly read as Mightyena's evolution, but it is a new creature, not a copy of Mightyena.
Draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): pixel art, 64×64, max 16 colors, dark selout outline, plain white background.

WHAT IT IS
Much bigger and more powerful than Mightyena: heavy, muscular, imposing.
Animal anatomy: a SPOTTED HYENA. A real, typical hyena head: broad and blunt, short wide muzzle, massive bone-crushing jaws, big ROUND ears, a very thick neck and heavy shoulders, front legs longer than the hind legs so the back slopes down toward the rear.
Colors and markings from Mightyena: black and grey fur, the shaggy black mane along the neck and back, red eyes with yellow sclera.

POSE
Standing, with ONE front paw raised, as if it is sneakily, gleefully snickering (a sly hyena laugh).

VIEW
Three-quarter view facing left, like every Gen 3 front sprite: the far legs on the left (partly hidden), the near ones on the right.
Compact: the whole Pokémon fits inside a square.

DO NOT
Not a wolf, not a dog, not just a bigger Mightyena: the head must be unmistakably a hyena.
No ground, no rock, no shadow under it. No glow, no aura, no effects, no motion lines.
No plush/Neopets look, no chibi mascot, not a realistic animal painting.
```
