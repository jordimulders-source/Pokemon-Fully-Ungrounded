Design a brand-new Pokémon: the evolution of Sunflora, Grass/Cosmic type, drawn as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style).

What it is: a Sunflora that went so hard on photosynthesis that it gained cosmic powers. It is truly halfway between a plant and a star. It may look very cosmic and "unlocked", but its body still clearly looks like a plant. It floats in the air instead of standing.

Face: Sunflora's content, happy face stays (or becomes only slightly different), and it is very, very happy.

Sprite rules:
- 64x64 pixels, max 16 colors, 1 px dark outline in Gen 3 style, soft Gen 3 shading.
- 3/4 view facing left, compact so it fills a square.
- Plain white background; no ground, no shadow, no rock.
- No glow, no sparkles, no light rays, no loose effects: everything cosmic must be part of its own body.
AVOID: plush/toy look, Neopets style, chibi mascot, realistic sunflower painting, a human in a costume.
