# Gemini-prompt v5, 20 nov (zonder bijlagen)
Jordi over v4: "niet zo goed he"; "misschien de referenties weglaten". Nieuw gesprek, geen bijlagen.

```
Design a regional form of the Pokémon Oddish and draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style pixel art).

CONCEPT
- Oddish from the deep sea near the Galápagos, based on the giant tube worm (Riftia) of hydrothermal vents. Type: Water.
- Body, feet, face and pose are exactly those of Oddish's own Gen 3 front sprite: the small round body, the two stubby feet in a walking pose, tiny red dot eyes and a small open red mouth. No big eyes, no smile.
- Body color: white / cream, like the white tube of a giant tube worm.
- On top: instead of Oddish's five leaves, five separate giant tube worm plumes in exactly the places, sizes and directions of Oddish's leaves, fanning out from one point on top of the head, mostly up and to the right. Together the crown is about as large as the body.
- Each plume is bright red and feathery: a tuft made of many fine red filaments, like a feather duster, with frayed tips. Not a leaf, not a smooth bud.

POSE
- Three-quarter view facing left, the classic Oddish walking pose.

STYLE AND FORMAT
- 64x64 canvas, max 16 colors, 1 px dark outline, light from top left, flat Gen 3 shading, no dithering.
- Plain white background. No ground, water, bubbles, shadow or extra effects. One single creature, no extra views.

AVOID
- No leaves, no green, no flower, no anemone tentacles, no fire or smoke.
```
