# Satelliet-lijn fase 1 – startprompt r1 (23 nov), NIEUW gesprek, geen bijlage
Basis: SPEC.md (Spoetnik-bolletje met antennes, één oog, pure machine). Kleuren niet besproken → Gemini kiest.

```
Design a new, original Pokémon.
Draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): pixel art, 64×64, max 16 colors, dark selout outline, plain white background.

WHAT IT IS
A small living machine: a tiny early satellite like Sputnik. A round metal ball with long thin antennas sweeping back from it.
It is a pure machine with its own personality, the way Klink, Magnemite and Porygon are Pokémon: no animal parts, no arms, no legs.
It has exactly ONE eye: a single camera lens set in the front of the ball, with a clear, curious expression.
It is the first stage of an evolution line: it will later grow into a big satellite and then a space station, so keep it simple and small.

POSE
It floats in the air, tilted slightly as if looking up curiously. The antennas trail behind it.

VIEW
Three-quarter view facing left, like every Gen 3 front sprite.
It is small: it fills about half of the 64×64 frame.

DO NOT
No effects, no glow, no electricity, no stars or planets around it.
No ground, no shadow.
Not Magnemite, not Voltorb, not a Poké Ball, not a robot with a face screen.
No plush/Neopets look.
```
