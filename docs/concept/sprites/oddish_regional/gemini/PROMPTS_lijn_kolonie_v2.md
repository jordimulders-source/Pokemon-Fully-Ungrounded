# Gemini-prompts Gloom en Vileplume, kolonie-richting v2 (20 nov)
Jordi: "ja, doe die twee prompts opnieuw, voordeel van de twijfel". Na kolonie r1: Gloom had Gloom's oranje bladeren erbij, een waaier/schelp in het midden en kleine kwastjes; Vileplume werd een bloempot met handjes. Nieuw gesprek per prompt, geen bijlagen.

## Gloom
```
Design a regional form of the Pokémon Gloom and draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style pixel art).

CONCEPT
- Gloom from the deep-sea hydrothermal vents near the Galápagos, based on the giant tube worm (Riftia). Type: Water/Fire. It evolves from an Oddish with five red feathery tube worm plumes on its head.
- Body, face and pose of Gloom's Gen 3 front sprite: the round body, the short arms held out, the short legs, the squinting closed eyes and the wide open drooling mouth.
- Body color: white / cream.
- On top, the ONLY thing is a cluster of four thick, white giant tube worm tubes growing from the top of the head. They are too long and heavy to stand: they bend over limply, two flopping down to the left and two to the right, like wilted stems. From the end of each tube hangs a big bright red feathery plume, a tuft of many fine red filaments like a feather duster, drooping downward.
- The drool is dark sulfurous vent water. A faint orange glow at the base of the tubes, on top of the head.

POSE
- Three-quarter view facing left, the classic Gloom pose.

STYLE AND FORMAT
- 64x64 canvas, max 16 colors, 1 px dark outline, light from top left, flat Gen 3 shading, no dithering.
- Plain white background. No ground, water, bubbles, smoke or extra effects. One single creature, no extra views.

AVOID
- No orange or brown leaves, no bud, no fan or shell shape, no upright tubes, no small tassels, no green, no flower, no flames.
```

## Vileplume
```
Design a regional form of the Pokémon Vileplume and draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style pixel art).

CONCEPT
- Vileplume from the deep-sea hydrothermal vents near the Galápagos, based on a colony of giant tube worms (Riftia) around a black smoker vent. Type: Water/Fire. Its line starts with an Oddish with five red feathery tube worm plumes on its head.
- Body and pose of Vileplume's Gen 3 front sprite: the small body under a large, wide top, short arms held out, short legs, the face mostly hidden under the top.
- Body color: white / cream.
- On top: a tall, narrow, dark grey-black mineral chimney rises from the center of the head, clearly the tallest part, with an opening at the top that glows orange-hot inside. Around its base, about seven separate white tubes stand in a ring and lean outward, each clearly apart with gaps between them (not joined into one wall). Each tube ends in a big bright red feathery plume, a tuft of many fine red filaments like a feather duster, the same kind of plume as Oddish has. Together the plumes form a wide red crown, as wide as Vileplume's flower.

POSE
- Three-quarter view facing left, the classic Vileplume pose.

STYLE AND FORMAT
- 64x64 canvas, max 16 colors, 1 px dark outline, light from top left, flat Gen 3 shading, no dithering.
- Plain white background. No ground, water, bubbles, smoke or extra effects. One single creature, no extra views.

AVOID
- No pot, vase, basket or bouquet shape, no tubes fused into a wall, no small hand-shaped plumes, no petals, no flower shape, no green, no leaves, no flames.
```
