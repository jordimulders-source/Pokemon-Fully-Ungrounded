# Gemini-prompts: Gnometrotter (Rock-vorm), v1 (22 nov)

Jordi: "doe maar meerdere prompts, ik moet het eerst voor me zien". Drie varianten (A amethist/geode, B lavendel steen, C druipsteen) voor de steenonderdelen; de basis (alles uit SPEC) is in alle drie gelijk. Elke prompt in een **nieuw gesprek, zonder bijlage**.

## Ingevuld door Claude (voorleggen)
- Steenmateriaal per variant (Jordi koos nog niet).
- Riem met gesp blijft zoals bij Gnomad (onveranderd).
- Pose: wandelend midden in een stap (zoals Gnomad).
- Formaat ±60 px hoog (eindfase).
- Haarplukjes, snor en baard: grijs steengruis in A/B, crème/oker in C.

## Prompt A: amethist/geode

```
Design an original Pokémon and draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style).

CONCEPT
- Fairy/Rock type, the final stage of a three-stage evolution line. Its name is Gnometrotter: a gnome Pokémon that has walked so far for so long that its body has fully adapted to its environment. This is its Cave Form: it has lived in deep caves, and parts of its body have turned to stone.
- It is a garden gnome turned into a cartoony Pokémon, a seasoned, experienced hiker, logically grown up from its pre-evolution: bigger than before, same proportions, just more grown.
- Body: a round, pink body, standing on longer legs with the same sturdy rounded feet, only a bit bigger.
- Arms: a bit longer oval arms, each ending in a round, chubby ball-shaped hand (same shape as before, a bit bigger).
- A big, round, bulbous nose, the same pink as its skin.
- Its huge pointed cap droops down over its eyes so the eyes are completely hidden. The cap is big and long, and its tip trails behind it.
- Below the nose: a big mustache, and now also a beard that grows down from under the mustache. The beard covers where the mouth would be: NO mouth is visible.
- More hair than before: bigger tufts sticking out sideways from under the cap on both sides of the head.
- A simple dark belt around the waist with a small square buckle, between the pink upper body and the blue lower half.
- Colors of the body stay: pink skin; the lower half of the body including the legs is sky blue, like a garden gnome's trousers, with a thin dark-blue line on the border. This is NOT clothing, just a second body color.
- It is walking: caught mid-stride, a steady, experienced walk.
- Cartoony, but in the Pokémon way: simple bold shapes, a clear readable silhouette, like an official Ken Sugimori design.

CAVE FORM (what turned to stone)
- The cap is now rough grey stone, with a few cracks and angular facets instead of soft fabric; same shape and length as a soft gnome cap, still over the eyes.
- The tip of the cap ends in a purple amethyst crystal.
- The mustache, beard and hair tufts are still fluffy in shape but grey like stone dust and gravel, with here and there a small amethyst crystal point.
- A few small purple amethyst crystals grow out of its shoulders and upper back, like inside a geode.
- Colors of the stone parts: stone grey with purple amethyst accents.

STYLE AND FORMAT
- Official Gen 3 front sprite, 64x64, max 16 colors, 1 px dark outline, light from top left, flat Gen 3 shading with 3 to 4 tones per color, no dithering.
- Three-quarter view facing left. A final-stage Pokémon, about 60 px tall, compact, limbs close to the body, centered in the frame.
- Plain white background. NO floor, shadow, rocks on the ground, cave background or effects. One single creature.

AVOID
- No visible eyes, no visible mouth, no human proportions, no little man in a costume. No props: no backpack, no walking stick, no pickaxe, no lantern, no helmet. No glow or sparkles. Not Impidimp, not Hatenna. No western-cartoon or plush look. No glossy 3D render.
```
