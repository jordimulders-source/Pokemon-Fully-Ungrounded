## Prompt

```
Design an original Pokémon and draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style pixel art).

CONCEPT
- The final (third) stage of a pseudo-legendary dragon line based on the pink dragon fruit (pitahaya: hot pink skin with green-tipped scale flaps, white flesh with black seeds) and its climbing cactus stem (green, three-ribbed). The attached image is the first stage: a pitahaya head on a cactus-stem body. Stage 2 is a tegu lizard.
- Stage 3 is a Komodo dragon: heavy monitor-lizard body, long neck and head, thick tail, rough scaly skin. It stands on two legs but leans forward (body angled forward, not upright like a human), the tail as counterweight.
- The pitahaya must still be clearly recognizable: pink skin with green-tipped scale flaps on the body, green cactus-stem parts.

DESIGN
- Big, heavy and powerful, a fully grown pseudo-legendary. Reads as a dragon first, not as a fruit with legs.
- Colors from the fruit and the stem: hot pink, green, with a bit of white. Keep the palette limited.
- Readable silhouette: forward-leaning two-legged lizard with a long heavy tail.

POSE
- Three-quarter view facing left, threatening.

STYLE AND FORMAT
- 64x64 pixel sprite, the creature filling most of the canvas (it is a final stage), max 16 colors, 1 px dark outline, light from top left, clean shading like official Game Freak Gen 3 sprites.
- Plain white background. NO floor, shadow, base platform, leaves, fire, glow or effects. One single creature, no extra views.

AVOID
- No wings. Not a snake. Not standing straight up like a human or a kaiju.
- Not Garchomp, Salamence, Tyranitar or Krookodile. Not a dinosaur (no T-rex).
- Not a lizard wearing or holding a separate fruit. No glossy 3D render, no plush or mascot style.
```
