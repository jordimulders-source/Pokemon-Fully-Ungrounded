# Guaraná fase 2 – bijsturen r5 (26 nov), in HETZELFDE gesprek als r4
Jordi over r4: "waar ik nu over twijfel is dat het fruit is ook is afgenomen. Misschien iets meer fruit maar niet alle ogen geopend, zoals fase 1?" Voorstel Claude: gesloten vruchten kleiner, achter/tussen de open vruchten, ogen even groot houden.

```
Keep everything exactly the same: every open fruit and every eye at the same size, the vine, the leaves, the pose and the colors. Change ONLY this:

Add more guaraná fruits to each cluster: two or three extra fruits per cluster that are still closed (round, glossy red, with a seam line, no eye). The closed fruits are a bit smaller and sit behind and between the open ones, so the clusters look fuller. Do not make the open fruits or the eyes smaller.
```
