## Prompt

```
Design an original Pokémon and draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style pixel art).

CONCEPT
- The evolved, second stage of a blobfish Pokémon (pure Water type). It is now a real blobfish, like the famous collapsed blobfish in the attached photo.
- Even grumpier than a normal blobfish, with drool hanging from its mouth.
- It is made of that sticky, slimy jelly toy material children play with: wet, glossy, gross and slippery to touch.
- It looks even more melted than a normal blobfish: sagging and drooping.

DESIGN
- Color: pink like a real blobfish. Show the glossy jelly with a few sharp white highlights and darker edges, NOT with see-through effects.
- Readable silhouette: the big droopy nose and sagging mouth clearly visible.

POSE
- Three-quarter view facing left, collapsed and sagging.

STYLE AND FORMAT
- 64x64 pixel sprite, the creature filling most of the canvas (final stage), max 16 colors, 1 px dark outline, light from top left, flat Gen 3 shading with 3 to 4 tones per color, no dithering.
- Plain white background. NO floor, puddle, shadow, water, drips on the ground or effects. One single creature, no extra views.

AVOID
- No clay, no fingerprints.
- Not a plush toy, chibi or Neopets mascot. No glossy 3D render.
```

## Tips
- Te weinig gesmolten: "melting like a candle, drooping even more than a real blobfish".
- Kwijl wordt een plas op de grond: "drool hangs from the lip, nothing on the ground".
