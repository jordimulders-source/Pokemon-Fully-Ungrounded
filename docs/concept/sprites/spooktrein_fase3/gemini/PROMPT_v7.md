# Gemini-prompt: spooktrein fase 3 (Steel/Ghost), v7 (15 nov)

Jordi na ronde 5: "de wielen mogen best onderdeel van de geest zijn, alsof de trein alleen gepowered wordt door een geest" / "ik ga niet schetsen, dat kan ik niet".
Aanpak (Claude): het onderwerp van de prompt is een geest-Pokémon, niet een locomotief; het woord "locomotive" komt er niet als onderwerp in (les GEMINI.md). De geest is één lijf: ogen voorin, wielen eronder, rookstaart achter; het ijzer is zijn schulp. Gemini wordt gevraagd zelf een ontwerp in Pokémon-vormtaal te maken; Sugimori-stijl, daarna stap 2 (sprite) zoals in `PROMPT_v5.md`.

```
Design an original Pokémon in the official Ken Sugimori Pokémon artwork style (clean lines, flat cel shading, plain white background).

TYPE: Steel / Ghost. Final stage of its line.

It is ONE creature: a green ghost whose body is a hollow iron shell. The shell is shaped after the front of an old steam engine, but redesigned in Pokémon shape language (like Revavroom or Klinklang): a few bold, rounded shapes, nothing realistic.

THE GHOST IS WHAT MAKES IT MOVE
- It has no iron wheels. Underneath the shell, the green ghost body itself swirls into big spinning wheels of ghost energy that carry it forward, drawn in motion like in a comic book.
- Behind, the ghost body trails off into green ghost smoke.
- At the front, two identical green ghost eyes shine out of the iron head. The cowcatcher at the front is its jaw, with a cheeky, mischievous grin.
- On top, one short chimney. Nothing else: no cab, no windows, no rods, no pipes, no wagons, no headlight.

LOOK
- Long and low, leaning forward, racing ahead: it is visibly having fun being a ghost and going so fast. Not angry, not scary.
- The iron shell is dark grey, with rust patches and rust streaks. The ghost (eyes, wheels, smoke) is green.
- View: slightly more frontal than three-quarter, facing left.

AVOID: not a realistic train or locomotive with something added, no ghost sitting inside or looking out, no human face (no nose, no eyebrows, no lips), not Thomas the Tank Engine, no skull, no conductor, no other ghosts, no rails, no speed lines.
```

## Tips
