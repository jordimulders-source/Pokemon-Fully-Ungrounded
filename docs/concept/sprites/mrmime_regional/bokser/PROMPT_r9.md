# Bokser-Mr. Mime – startprompt r9 (23 nov, NIEUW gesprek, GEEN bijlage)
Basis = tekst van r3 (beste sprite); kop = Mr. Mime-kop (r7), lijf nieuw; pose zoals r6 hem goed had.

```
Design a brand-new regional form of Mr. Mime: an old-time GENTLEMAN boxer, like the bare-knuckle boxers in 1880s black-and-white portrait photos. Fighting type.
Draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): pixel art, 64×64, max 16 colors, dark selout outline, Gen 3 shading with 3–4 tones per color, plain white background.
Draw ONE single Pokémon.

WHAT IT IS
The HEAD is Mr. Mime's head: its head shape and its face, with its two hair spikes slicked far back.
The BODY is a new design, not Mr. Mime's body: short and stocky, thick neck, heavy shoulders, barrel chest, short thick forearms, big fists. Dignified and gentlemanly.
It wears NO clothes: its high-waisted old-fashioned boxing trunks are a pattern of its own body.
Bare fists, no boxing gloves. NO balls or round pads anywhere (not on the shoulders, hands or knees).

COLORS
A BLACK-AND-WHITE palette, like an old photo and like a classic mime: white, greys and black.

POSE (most important)
It is NOT punching and NOT in a fighting stance: it stands still and proud, posing for an old portrait photo.
- Legs straight and close together, feet almost touching. No bent knees, no wide stance.
- The far arm is held out forward to the left, almost straight, the fist still at shoulder height.
- The near arm is bent with the elbow out to the side, its fist resting against the chest.
- Chest out, chin up.

VIEW
Three-quarter view facing left, like every Gen 3 front sprite. Compact.

DO NOT
No boxing gloves, no skirt, no tunic, no fabric.
No ground, no ring, no shadow, no glow, no effects, no motion lines.
No cartoon line art, no plush/Neopets look, no chibi mascot, not a realistic human.
```
