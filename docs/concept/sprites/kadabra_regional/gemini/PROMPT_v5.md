```
Design a regional form of Kadabra and draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style pixel art).

ATTACHMENT
- The attached image is its evolution: the regional form of Alakazam, a card-swindler illusionist (Dark/Psychic). Use it for the COLOR SCHEME, the markings and the pixel-art style: grey body with a light violet sheen in the shadows, black shoulder plates, black back and sides, grey upper arms and black forearms, a white chest plate, black knee pads, a white playing card with a black spade. Same grey, same black, same white. Do NOT copy its shapes or its pose.

BODY (Kadabra, not Alakazam)
- The body is the official Kadabra: a fox-like head with a pointed snout and two big pointed ears standing up, a short mustache (two short strands, not long and drooping), a long thick tail, a slim body, and smaller shoulder plates than Alakazam. It must read as Kadabra at first glance.
- A red club mark (♣) on its forehead, in the place of Kadabra's star. This is the ONLY red on the whole sprite: no red under the chin, no bow tie.

CONCEPT
- The pre-evolution: a charlatan's apprentice. A card swindler too, but not yet very experienced; still learning the trade.

POSE (most important)
- Kadabra stands and looks in a three-quarter view facing LEFT, like a normal Gen 3 front sprite: not a straight front view, not a side view. Legs apart, tail behind it.
- A magician's "ta-dah! is this your card?" pose with both arms:
  - The arm on the left side of the image is stretched out and holds exactly ONE playing card up, pinched between its fingers. The card is shown completely frontal: a flat upright rectangle facing the viewer, not tilted, with the black spade visible.
  - The arm on the right side of the image is stretched out to the other side with an open, spread hand, palm toward the viewer: the classic "voilà" flourish.

HANDS (very important, Pokémon hands, not human hands)
- Kadabra's hands are built like its feet: a small round palm with one thumb and two fingers, and nothing more. Look at the hands of the attached Alakazam: the same kind of hand.
- Every finger and the thumb are thick and short and end in a small, sharp white claw, like the white claws on its toes. No human fingers, no fingernails, no four- or five-fingered hands.
- On the open hand the thumb and the two fingers are spread apart like a fan, so each one can be seen separately.

STYLE AND FORMAT
- 64x64 pixel sprite, max 16 colors, 1 px dark outline, light from top left, clean shading like official Game Freak Gen 3 sprites.
- Plain white background. NO floor, rock, shadow, base, motion lines, glow, sparkles or effects. One single creature, no extra views.

AVOID
- Not Alakazam: no huge round shoulder pads, no long drooping mustache, no Alakazam pose turned to the right, no hand behind the back.
- No straight front view of the body. No tilted card. No single raised finger.
- No human hands. No brown; no dark-all-over body. No red except the club mark.
- No clothing: no jacket, cape, sleeves, top hat or gloves.
- Not a human magician. No plush toy, chibi or mascot look.
- Exactly one card; the other hand is empty.
```
