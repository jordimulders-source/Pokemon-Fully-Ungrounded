De rest van r1 blijft gelijk. Nieuw gesprek, zonder bijlage.

```
Design a Pokémon: an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style), 64×64 pixels, max 16 colors, clean dark outline, plain white background, no ground, no shadow, no effects or glow.

WHAT IT IS: a small living satellite. The satellite itself is the creature's body, not a costume or a machine with a face stuck on.

BODY (the focus, must fill most of the sprite): a rounded white capsule body with soft lavender shading, its front is one big camera lens that is the creature's single eye: lavender rim, turquoise iris, dark pupil. Small purple ball joints on the body. Two thin purple antennae with ball tips on top, curving like insect feelers.

SOLAR PANELS: two dark navy-purple solar panels with a lighter purple grid, one on each side. They are SMALL compared to the body (each panel at most about as tall as the body), attached close to the body like short wings, not long sails. Both panels are seen in the SAME 3/4 perspective as the body: the near panel (right) slightly larger, the far panel (left) slightly smaller and partly behind the body.

POSE: 3/4 view facing left, the lens-eye looking toward the viewer-left. Compact composition that fits in a square, everything close to the body.

COLORS: white/lavender body, purple joints and antennae, dark navy-purple panels, turquoise iris as the only accent.

DO NOT: huge panels that dominate the image, panels in different perspectives, starfield or galaxy patterns, extra eyes, mouth, arms or legs, realistic NASA hardware, cute plush/Neopets style.
```
