## Prompt

```
Three attached images. Combine them into ONE Pokémon, an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style).

- THE CANOE comes from image 1: the same round, bulbous brown mate gourd shape, size, colors and texture. Only change: the front tip is a closed, slightly sharper point (no opening, no pursed lips).
- THE RIM: a thin, neat silver metal rim along the whole top edge, like in image 2.
- THE EYES: two eyes on the side near the front, made exactly like the eyes of image 3: holes cut into the gourd, through which you see the green yerba mate leaves inside. Round and clearly readable. No mouth on the canoe.
- Front part covered with a heap of green yerba mate leaves, back part with hot water that spills a little over the back edge (as in image 1).
- THE LEAF MAN and his two bombilla paddles come from image 2: same head, face, colors and rowing pose; thin silver straws with the round spoon-shaped filter as the paddle blade. No straw crossing his mouth; his mouth is a small simple Gen 3 mouth.

STYLE AND FORMAT
- 64x64, max 16 colors, 1 px dark outline, light from top left, flat Gen 3 shading, no dithering. 3/4 facing left, compact.
- Plain white background. NO river, NO floor, NO shadow, NO effects.

AVOID
- Not the long angular canoe of image 2. No slit or scratch eyes, no snout, no thick rim, no Octillery look.
```
