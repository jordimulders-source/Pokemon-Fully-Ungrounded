# Regionale Staryu/Starmie – Gemini-prompts v1 (27 nov)

Jordi (27 nov): "serieus/grap geen idee, laten we allebei anders even proberen, maak prompts voor een carambola staryu en starmie en een chocolate chip staryu en starmie. Mochten beide vet zijn, prefereer ik carambola".

Type, serieus of grap: open. Daarom staat er geen type in de prompts.

Bijlagen (bovenste frame van de Gen 3-sprite, 4×): `gemini/bijlage_staryu_gen3.png`, `gemini/bijlage_starmie_gen3.png`. Elke prompt in een **nieuw gesprek**, met alleen de bijlage die erbij staat.

Eigen invulling Claude (niet door Jordi gekozen, schrappen/aanpassen mag):
- Opbouw zoals bij de Jynx v2: silhouet en kern van Staryu/Starmie houden, alleen het materiaal en de kleuren worden carambola of chocolate chip-zeester. De kern (het juweel) blijft zoals in de bijlage.
- Carambola: het lijf als dwarsdoorsnede van de vrucht (vijfpuntige ster): glanzend geel vruchtvlees, wasachtige geelgroene schil met bruingroene randjes langs de punten, een paar bruine pitjes rond de kern.
- Chocolate chip-zeester (*Protoreaster nodosus*): beige/crème lijf met rijen donkerbruine tot zwarte kegelvormige knobbels op de armen.
- Let op immersie: de chocolate chip-zeester leeft in de Indische en de westelijke Stille Oceaan, niet rond Zuid-Amerika.

## 1. Carambola-Staryu (bijlage `gemini/bijlage_staryu_gen3.png`)

```
The attached image is STARYU, a Gen 3 Pokémon sprite. Design a REGIONAL FORM of Staryu: it must still be instantly recognizable as Staryu, but clearly different. Its theme: carambola (star fruit).

Output: a simple, clean official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style), 64x64 pixels, max 16 colors, flat colors with 2-3 shades per color, the same low level of detail as the attached Staryu. White background, no ground, no shadow, no effects, no glow.

KEEP FROM STARYU (most important): the five-pointed star silhouette, the same proportions and angle as the attachment, and the round gem core in the center exactly as in the attachment. No face, no eyes, no mouth, no limbs.

CHANGE INTO CARAMBOLA: the whole star body is a cross-section slice of a carambola, which is naturally a five-pointed star. Glossy, juicy yellow fruit flesh; a thin waxy yellow-green skin along the outer edges with brownish-green tips on the five points; a few small brown seeds around the gem core.

DO NOT: a realistic photo of fruit, a whole uncut fruit, a plate or knife, leaves or a stem, starfield or galaxy patterns, sparkles, plush style, a face.
```

## 2. Carambola-Starmie (bijlage `gemini/bijlage_starmie_gen3.png`)

```
The attached image is STARMIE, a Gen 3 Pokémon sprite. Design a REGIONAL FORM of Starmie: it must still be instantly recognizable as Starmie, but clearly different. Its theme: carambola (star fruit).

Output: a simple, clean official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style), 64x64 pixels, max 16 colors, flat colors with 2-3 shades per color, the same low level of detail as the attached Starmie. White background, no ground, no shadow, no effects, no glow.

KEEP FROM STARMIE (most important): the silhouette of two overlapping five-pointed stars (the back star rotated behind the front star), the same proportions and angle as the attachment, and the round gem core in the center exactly as in the attachment. No face, no eyes, no mouth, no limbs.

CHANGE INTO CARAMBOLA: both stars are cross-section slices of a carambola, which is naturally a five-pointed star. Glossy, juicy yellow fruit flesh; a thin waxy yellow-green skin along the outer edges with brownish-green tips on the points; a few small brown seeds around the gem core. The back star is a slightly riper, deeper yellow-orange so the two stars stay readable.

DO NOT: a realistic photo of fruit, a whole uncut fruit, a plate or knife, leaves or a stem, starfield or galaxy patterns, sparkles, plush style, a face.
```

## 3. Chocolate chip-Staryu (bijlage `gemini/bijlage_staryu_gen3.png`)

```
The attached image is STARYU, a Gen 3 Pokémon sprite. Design a REGIONAL FORM of Staryu: it must still be instantly recognizable as Staryu, but clearly different. Its theme: the chocolate chip sea star (Protoreaster nodosus).

Output: a simple, clean official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style), 64x64 pixels, max 16 colors, flat colors with 2-3 shades per color, the same low level of detail as the attached Staryu. White background, no ground, no shadow, no effects, no glow.

KEEP FROM STARYU (most important): the five-pointed star silhouette, the same proportions and angle as the attachment, and the round gem core in the center exactly as in the attachment. No face, no eyes, no mouth, no limbs.

CHANGE INTO A CHOCOLATE CHIP SEA STAR: the body is cream-beige like that sea star, thicker and puffier than normal Staryu, with rows of dark brown to black cone-shaped knobs along the top of each of the five arms, like chocolate chips. The knobs are few and big enough to read at sprite size.

DO NOT: a realistic photo, real chocolate or a cookie, sand or rocks, starfield or galaxy patterns, sparkles, plush style, a face.
```
