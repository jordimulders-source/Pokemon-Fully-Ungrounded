Nieuw gesprek. Bijlage: ALLEEN `bijlage_kadabra_origineel.png`. De Alakazam niet meer meesturen: met die bijlage bleef het resultaat te veel op Alakazam lijken; de kleuren staan nu in woorden in de prompt.

Jordi (11 nov): "ik wil toch voor een klaver gaan ipv een hart ook". Kleur van de klaver: nog open.

Jordi over ronde 2 (letterlijk): "dit is te frontaal, hij moet meer driekwart. Plus hij lijkt nog steeds teveel op alakazam en het lijkt alsof die zn middelvinger opsteekt"

```
Design a regional form of Kadabra and draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style pixel art).

The attached image is the original Kadabra. Keep Kadabra's own body and anatomy: its head shape, big pointed ears, short mustache, its long thick tail clearly visible, its slim hunched posture, and its own armor and markings on chest and belly. It must clearly read as Kadabra, NOT as Alakazam.

CONCEPT
- A Dark/Psychic regional form: a charlatan's apprentice, a card swindler who is not yet very experienced and is still learning the trade.
- A [BLACK / RED] club mark (♣, the card suit "clubs") on its forehead, in the place of the star.
- Colors: recolor Kadabra's own markings. Its yellow body becomes grey with a light violet sheen in the shadows; its brown armor parts become black; a white chest; a small red dewlap under the chin. No new body parts.
- It holds exactly ONE playing card (white with a black spade).

POSE (most important)
- Three-quarter view facing LEFT, like a normal Gen 3 front sprite (not a straight front view, not a side view). Its head and eyes look toward the left.
- An "is this your card?" pose: it holds the card out at the side, at arm's length, with the card face turned toward the viewer.
- It pinches the card at its edge between thumb and fingers, with all fingers visible and curled around the card. No single raised finger.

STYLE AND FORMAT
- 64x64 pixel sprite, max 16 colors, 1 px dark outline, light from top left, clean shading like official Game Freak Gen 3 sprites.
- Plain white background. NO floor, rock, shadow, base, motion lines, glow, sparkles or effects. One single creature, no extra views.

AVOID
- Not Alakazam: no big round shoulder pads, no long drooping mustache, no Alakazam body.
- No straight front view. No single raised finger.
- No clothing: no jacket, cape, sleeves, top hat or gloves; no bow tie as a separate object.
- Not a human magician. No plush toy, chibi or mascot look.
- Exactly one card, nothing else in its hands.
```
