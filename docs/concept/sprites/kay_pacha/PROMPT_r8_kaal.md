# Gemini-prompt Kay Pacha r8 (nieuw gesprek, geen bijlage)

Jordi: "laten we een grass/psychic poema maken die daar tussen zou passen" (= tussen Entei, Suicune en Raikou).
Eigen invulling van Claude: de beschrijving van de ontwerptaal van de beesten (natuurkracht als lichaamsdelen); groen, paarse ogen en Inca staan er bewust niet in (niet genoemd in deze opdracht).

```
Design a new legendary Pokémon as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): pixel art, 64x64, max 16 colors, dark outline, light from top-left, plain white background, no ground, no shadow, no effects.

WHAT IT IS: a Grass/Psychic legendary puma that fits right next to Entei, Suicune and Raikou, as if it were a fourth legendary beast made by the same designers. Like them, it is a force of nature in the body of a big cat, not an animal wearing accessories: its special features (mane, crest, face plate, markings, tail) are part of its body and express its Grass and Psychic nature. It must be its own design and not a recolor or copy of Entei, Suicune or Raikou, or of any other existing Pokémon.

POSE: 3/4 view facing left, standing on four legs, proud and powerful, like the Gen 3 sprites of the legendary beasts.

DO NOT: jewelry, armor, collars, bracelets, clothing, glowing aura, magic effects, rocks, ground, background.
```
