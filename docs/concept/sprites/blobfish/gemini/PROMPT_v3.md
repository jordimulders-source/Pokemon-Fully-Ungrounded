## Prompt

```
Design an original Pokémon and draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style pixel art).

CONCEPT
- A blobfish that is a carved and painted wooden fishing decoy (like the wooden fish decoys used in ice fishing). Pure Water type, the first stage of a two-stage line.
- The attached picture shows the blobfish as it looks deep under water. Use it ONLY for the anatomy, do NOT copy or trace the picture: design a Pokémon. Anatomy: a big, bulbous head, a tapering body, big rounded pectoral fins, a small dorsal fin, a fan-shaped tail fin, small eyes and big, thick lips.
- THE NOSE DOES NOT HANG: no drooping, sagging nose over the mouth. The face is firm. This is the big difference with its evolution.

IT MUST CLEARLY READ AS A WOODEN DECOY
- Because it is carved out of wood, it has LESS of a fish shape: simplified, carved shapes with a few flat cut planes, not a realistic fish body.
- A few clear wood grain lines.
- Painted in blobfish pink, matte, slightly worn so the wood shows on some edges. No varnish shine.
- Fins are carved wood with cut grooves as fin rays.
- A small metal line-tie ring on its back.
- No hooks.

PERSONALITY
- Slightly derpy and happy, as if it is glad to see you. A goofy smile with those big lips.

POSE
- Three-quarter view facing left, lying flat on its belly as if resting on the sea floor, pectoral fins spread to the sides, looking up at the viewer.

STYLE AND FORMAT
- 64x64 pixel sprite, the creature not filling the whole canvas (first stage), max 16 colors, 1 px dark outline, light from top left, flat Gen 3 shading with 3 to 4 tones per color, no dithering.
- Plain white background. NO floor, sea bed, rocks, shadow, bubbles, water, fishing line or effects. One single creature, no extra views.

AVOID
- Not a copy of the attached picture. No hanging or drooping nose. Not the collapsed, saggy blobfish; no slime, drool, jelly or gloss.
- Not a plush toy, chibi or Neopets mascot. No glossy 3D render, no photo.
```

## Tips
- Leest niet als hout: "made of carved wood, like a folk-art fishing decoy" bovenaan.
- Er verschijnt een vislijn of haak: noem in AVOID "no line, no hook" en begin een nieuw gesprek.
