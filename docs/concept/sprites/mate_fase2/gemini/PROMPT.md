## Prompt A (kano met ogen, punt = neus)

```
Design an original Pokémon, shown as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style). It is the evolution of a small yerba mate gourd creature (attached image, for colors only; do NOT copy its body).

THE POKÉMON
- A small canoe shaped like a yerba mate gourd (brown dried calabash), seen 3/4 facing left.
- The front of the canoe is closed (decked over) and carries a heap of green yerba mate leaves and powder.
- The back of the canoe is open and holds hot water; some of the water spills over the back edge.
- In the middle sits a little man made of yerba mate leaves (green). He has the face: simple eyes and mouth, like a Gen 3 Pokémon face.
- He paddles with two bombillas (the silver metal drinking straws of a mate gourd, with the flat filter end used as the paddle blade), one in each hand.
- The canoe itself has two eyes near the front, and the pointed tip of the canoe works as its nose. No mouth on the canoe.

STYLE AND FORMAT
- 64x64, max 16 colors, 1 px dark outline, light from top left, flat Gen 3 shading, no dithering.
- Plain white background. NO river, NO floor, NO shadow, NO splash effects or sparkles around it; only the water that spills from the canoe itself.

AVOID
- No human sailor, no hat, no Lotad/Lombre/Ludicolo look, no Sinistcha/Poltchageist look, no Neopets/plush style.
```

## Prompt B (zelfde, plus mond op de kano)
Vervang in prompt A de laatste regel van THE POKÉMON door:
```
- The canoe itself has two eyes near the front, the pointed tip of the canoe works as its nose, and below the tip the canoe has a mouth.
```
