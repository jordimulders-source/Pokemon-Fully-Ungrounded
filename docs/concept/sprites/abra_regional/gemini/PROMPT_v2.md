## v2A: playing possum

```
Design a regional form of Abra and draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style pixel art).

It must stay clearly recognizable as Abra: Abra's fox-like head with big pointed ears, closed eyes, no mouth, segmented body plates on the shoulders, three-fingered hands and feet, a tail.
The attached image is its evolution, the regional Kadabra. Use it ONLY for the skin color of this evolutionary line (grey with a light violet sheen in the shadows) and the style. Do NOT copy its pose, its markings, its card or its black-and-white tuxedo pattern.
Its body language is that of an opossum "playing dead".

CONCEPT
- Pure Dark type. A shady little street urchin who wants to become a magician but is not one yet. It carries nothing.
- More opossum than the original Abra: a long, thin, bare, rat-like tail; a slightly more pointed snout.
- One ear has a notch (a small V-shaped nick in the edge) from life on the street.
- Body plates: dark charcoal grey. No red anywhere.

POSE
- Playing possum: lying on its side, half rolled onto its back, belly plates turned toward the viewer.
- The head is the limpest part: it has rolled to one side and droops toward the bottom of the image.
- Both arms bent and loose, hands curled and limp; legs slightly drawn up and floppy; the long bare tail lies in a slack curl.
- It must NOT look like it is crawling, walking, sneaking or pouncing: no limb is supporting its weight, nothing points forward.
- Compact: the whole body fits in a square, not a long horizontal shape.
- Three-quarter view facing left.
- Eyes stay CLOSED, exactly like Abra. NO mouth, like Abra.

STYLE AND FORMAT
- 64x64 pixel sprite, max 16 colors, 1 px dark outline, light from top left, clean shading like official Game Freak Gen 3 sprites.
- Plain white background. NO floor, rock, shadow, base, motion lines, glow, sparkles, "Z" sleep letters or effects. One single creature, no extra views.

AVOID
- No clothing, rags, scarf, hat, bag or any object.
- No open eyes, no mouth, no fangs, no tongue, no red.
- No fur tufts or hair.
- Not a dead-looking corpse (no X eyes, no wounds besides the ear notch). Not a plush toy, chibi or mascot. Not a plain recolor of Abra.
```
