# Gemini-prompt: Banette-evolutie, v4 (17 nov)

Nieuw gesprek. **Bijlage: alleen `bijlage_shuppet.png`** (geen Banette: die werd steeds gekopieerd). Banette alleen genoemd voor het kledingstuk; zijn ogen in woorden.
Verder dezelfde besluiten als v3, maar zonder Claudes extra woorden over "leans forward" en de wenkbrauwrand (r3 slechter dan r2).

```
Design an original Pokémon: a sinister ghost puppet master, Ghost/Dark type. Attached: the Gen 3 sprite of SHUPPET, a small ghost; he controls two of them as puppets.

ORIGIN (important for the design)
- He was once a cursed grey-black cloth doll with a zipper mouth. The zipper opened and he climbed out of it. The empty doll is now a garment hanging around his waist: an empty grey-black cloth costume with two limp little stubby arms and a limp tail ending in a yellow tip, the opened zipper running around his waist. The doll's face is NOT on the garment.
- His own torso rises directly out of the open zipper, one connected shape with the garment.

HIS OWN HEAD AND BODY (new, much bigger than the doll was)
- Imposing and genuinely scary: a villain, not cute.
- His head has two long points like the points of a jester's hat, so he looks like an evil puppet master / joker. No single horn.
- A very sinister wide grin: a zigzag mouth with sharp teeth. The teeth are the same dark grey as his head. No zipper, no yellow in the mouth.
- Large slanted eyes: red with a yellow centre.
- Floating, with two short stubby undefined legs under the garment.

ARMS AND HANDS
- Both arms held up high.
- His hands ARE marionette control crosses: each arm flows directly into its cross in one continuous shape. Not a cross held in a hand. Not wood: the same dark ghostly substance as his body.

PUPPETS
- From each cross hang threads, each thread only 1 pixel thick.
- One SHUPPET (like the attached sprite) hangs under each hand, big enough to read clearly at sprite size.

COLOURS
- Dark grey-black, a bit darker than the attached Shuppet; red-yellow eyes; yellow tip on the garment's tail.

STYLE AND FORMAT
- Official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style), 64x64, max 16 colours, 1 px dark outline, light from top left, flat Gen 3 shading with 3 to 4 tones per colour, no dithering.
- 3/4 view facing left, with a perspective as if seen slightly from above.
- Large: the figure, including the two Shuppets, fills almost the whole 64x64 square.
- Plain white background. NO floor, shadow, glow, aura or effects.

AVOID
- No zipper mouth, no yellow mouth. No single horn on the head. No wooden crosses. Not cute, not chibi. Not a plush toy, no glossy 3D render.
```
