# Gemini-prompt Kay Pacha r5 (nieuw gesprek, geen bijlage)

Basis = de versiering van r2 (Jordi: "veel beter, dit mag nog iets meer zelfs"), in woorden; plus Jordi na r3: masker, rugversiering blijft, iets van paars, grotere bladeren op bepaalde plekken. r4B was "veel te kaal", r3 "overdrijft".
Eigen invulling van Claude: masker = gouden Inca-masker; welke plekken voor de bladeren; "a bit more than a few, less than full armor" als maat.

```
Design a new legendary Pokémon as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): pixel art, 64x64, max 16 colors, dark outline, light from top-left, plain white background, no ground, no shadow, no effects.

WHAT IT IS: a Grass/Psychic legendary beast based on a puma, the guardian of "Kay Pacha", the world of the living in Inca belief. It belongs to a legendary trio, like Entei, Raikou and Suicune: richly decorated so it clearly reads as a legendary, but still clean enough to become a 64x64 sprite. It must not look like Entei, Raikou, Suicune, Leafeon, Meowscarada or any other existing Pokémon.

BODY: puma build (slim, muscular, long thick tail, short rounded ears), stylized like a Pokémon, with a BIG head. GREEN coat, PURPLE eyes. No spots, no stripes.

DECORATION (Inca-inspired gold, every part a bold, clear shape):
- MASK: an Inca-style gold mask on its face; the purple eyes stay visible.
- HEAD: a tall gold headpiece shaped like a tumi (Inca ceremonial knife with a half-moon blade) on top of the head.
- BACK: a large gold crest along the back in the stepped zigzag shape of the Inca walls of Sacsayhuamán.
- BODY: several large plain gold plates on the shoulders, chest and hips, shaped like fitted Inca stones and trapezoid Inca doorways, connected by gold bands; green vines wind along the gold.
- LEAVES: BIG leaves at a few places (behind the headpiece, at the shoulders, along the tail), large clear leaf shapes, not tiny sprigs.
- PURPLE: work some purple into the gold decoration.
Rich decoration, but no engravings, no spirals, no tiny patterns, no speckles or noisy texture (no moss specks), no faces or symbols on the gold. Gold and coat are clean flat areas with simple shading.

POSE: 3/4 view facing left, standing on four legs, head raised, proud and calm like a legendary beast.

DO NOT: a collar, bracelets, jewelry chains, full body armor, spots, stripes, wings, glowing aura, magic effects, rocks, grass on the ground, background.
```
