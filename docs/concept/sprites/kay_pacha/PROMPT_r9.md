# Gemini-prompt Kay Pacha r9 (nieuw gesprek, geen bijlage)

Basis r8 + Claudes punten (Jordi: "prima") + mantel goudgeel ichu-gras ("goudgeel is wel geinig").

```
Design a new legendary Pokémon as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): pixel art, 64x64, max 16 colors, dark outline, light from top-left, plain white background, no ground, no shadow, no effects. Big, simple shapes that read at 64x64.

WHAT IT IS: a Grass/Psychic legendary puma that fits right next to Entei, Suicune and Raikou, as if it were a fourth legendary beast by the same designers. It is the living land of the Andes in the body of a big cat: NOT an animal wearing accessories or armor. All its special features are part of its body, the way Raikou's cloud, Entei's mane and Suicune's ribbons are. Its own design, not a copy of any existing Pokémon.

COLORS: three main colors with strong contrast, like the legendary beasts: GREEN body, GOLDEN YELLOW mantle, CREAM face plate and crest, plus PURPLE eyes and markings.

BODY: stylized fantasy big cat with puma features: slim, powerful, long legs, short rounded ears, and a BIG head. Green coat.
FACE PLATE: like Entei's and Raikou's face plates, a hard cream-colored plate that is part of its face, shaped like a stylized Inca puma head. Purple eyes.
CREST: one big, clearly stepped (staircase-shaped) Inca crest rising from the forehead, the same cream color as the face plate, so plate and crest form one shape. Like Suicune's crest.
MANTLE: its biggest feature, like Raikou's cloud or Entei's mane: a SOFT, flowing mantle of large overlapping golden-yellow grass blades (like the golden ichu grass of the Andes highlands), from the neck over the back to the hips, falling in broad stepped layers like farming terraces on a mountainside. Soft and alive, not hard plates, not a shell, not armor.
MARKINGS: bold purple stepped stripes on the legs and flanks, like Raikou's stripes but in an Inca staircase shape.
TAILS: two broad, long tails like flowing ribbons of leaf, like Suicune's ribbon tails: a big part of the silhouette, not thin strings.

POSE: 3/4 view facing left, standing on four legs, strong and powerful, head held high, mantle and tails flowing back, like the Gen 3 sprites of the legendary beasts.

DO NOT: armor, shell, hard plates on the body, jewelry, gold metal ornaments, collars, clothing, glowing aura, magic effects, rocks, ground, background.
```
