# Regionale Rufflet – Gemini-startprompt (r1, nieuw gesprek)

Bijlage: Jordi's inspiratiefoto van het harpijkuiken (die van 20 nov). Besluiten: `../SPEC.md`.
Jordi: regional form moet aan Rufflet herinneren ("dat staat buiten kijf"). Voorstellen Claude (niet van Jordi): pose zoals op de foto; staalloos (Steel komt pas bij de evolutie); kuif als zachte versie van de dubbele kuif van de evolutie.

---

```
Design a new regional form of the Pokémon RUFFLET, based on a HARPY EAGLE CHICK from the Amazon rainforest. Its type is pure Flying. It evolves into a Steel/Flying harpy eagle Pokémon, so it is the baby version of that bird.
It is a regional form: it must still clearly be RUFFLET, the way Galarian Meowth is still clearly Meowth. Keep Rufflet recognizable, but as a harpy eagle chick.
The attached photo is a real harpy eagle chick: use it as the source for its look.
Draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): pixel art, 64×64, max 16 colors, dark selout outline, plain white background.

WHAT IT IS
A fluffy harpy eagle chick, like in the photo: a white downy head with a messy, upright tuft of white down at the back of the head; a dark hooked beak; a dark eye close to the beak; a white downy body (chest and belly); half-spread, hanging grey wings with white edges; thick yellow legs with big dark talons that are much too big for the chick.
No metal anywhere: it is not Steel type yet.

POSE
It stands, like in the photo: body upright, wings half-spread and hanging down, the big feet planted. 2 wings in total (near and far).

VIEW
Three-quarter view facing left, like every Gen 3 front sprite: the far wing and far foot are on the left (narrower, partly hidden), the near ones on the right. The head turns a bit more toward the viewer than the body.

STYLE
Stylized like an official Pokémon design, not a realistic bird drawn as pixel art.

DO NOT
No nest, no branch, no twigs, no ground, no shadow under it.
No glow, no effects. No metal or armor.
No plush/Neopets look, not a realistic photo-like bird: the photo is only a source, the result is a Pokémon sprite.
```
