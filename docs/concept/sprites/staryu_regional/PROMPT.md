## 1. Carambola-Staryu (bijlage `gemini/bijlage_staryu_gen3.png`)

```
The attached image is STARYU, a Gen 3 Pokémon sprite. Design a REGIONAL FORM of Staryu: it must still be instantly recognizable as Staryu, but clearly different. Its theme: carambola (star fruit).

Output: a simple, clean official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style), 64x64 pixels, max 16 colors, flat colors with 2-3 shades per color, the same low level of detail as the attached Staryu. White background, no ground, no shadow, no effects, no glow.

KEEP FROM STARYU (most important): the five-pointed star silhouette, the same proportions and angle as the attachment, and the round gem core in the center exactly as in the attachment. No face, no eyes, no mouth, no limbs.

CHANGE INTO CARAMBOLA: the whole star body is a cross-section slice of a carambola, which is naturally a five-pointed star. Glossy, juicy yellow fruit flesh; a thin waxy yellow-green skin along the outer edges with brownish-green tips on the five points; a few small brown seeds around the gem core.

DO NOT: a realistic photo of fruit, a whole uncut fruit, a plate or knife, leaves or a stem, starfield or galaxy patterns, sparkles, plush style, a face.
```
