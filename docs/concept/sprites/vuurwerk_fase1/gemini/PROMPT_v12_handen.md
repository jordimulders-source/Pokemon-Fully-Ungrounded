# Gemini-prompt: vuurwerklijn fase 1, v12 (19 nov): handen netter, r11 als bron

Nieuw gesprek. **Bijlage: `../r11/gemini_sprieten.png`** (Gemini r11; ligt exact op het 64×64-raster, 16 px per spritepixel).

## Jordi over r11
"Kijk dat gaat goed. Doe nu nog een 2e verbeterprompt om de handen nog wat netter af te werken, zijn er nog verdere dingen die gemini kan oppoetsen denk je".

## Prompt (max. 2 wijzigingen: handen + overgang mond/kieuwen)

```
The attached image is a finished Gen 3 Pokémon front sprite (64x64 pixel art, enlarged): a small fire axolotl whose pink flame body winds around a firework rocket, cream head with pink gills to the right of the rocket tip.

Keep EVERYTHING exactly as it is, pixel for pixel: the rocket, the winding body, the head, the eyes, the gills, the colors, the outline and the size. Make only these two changes:

1. Clean up the two small hands that grip the rocket just below its pointed tip. Each hand should read clearly as a little hand with fingers wrapped around the rocket, with a clean dark outline and clear finger lines. The two hands are not mirror images: they grip the rocket at slightly different heights and angles, like in a natural pose. Keep them the same size and position.

2. Clean up where the mouth meets the pink gills on the right side of the head: the mouth line, the pink lower lip and the gills should read as separate parts, not as one dark blob.

Same pixel-art style, same pixel size, same palette. Plain white background. Return the whole sprite.
```
