# r12: nieuw gesprek, geen bijlage. Doel: een lijf dat logisch aan een enorm hoofd vastzit; het hoofd wordt later vervangen door het r5-hoofd.

Design a brand-new Pokémon, drawn as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style). Grass/Cosmic type, the evolution of Sunflora.

What it is: a sunflower Pokémon that went so hard on photosynthesis that it gained cosmic powers. Its flower head has grown ENORMOUS: a huge star-shaped flower head with many points (not five) and a very happy face in the middle. The head is much bigger than the whole body.

The body is a plant body, a flower body like Sunflora's: a small green leaf-shaped torso, two leaf arms and two leaf feet. Because the head is so big, the head covers the upper part of the body: the neck and the top of the torso are hidden behind the head, like a flower that sits on its stem. Below and beside the head we see the rest of the torso, the two leaf arms and the two leaf feet. It floats, so the leaf feet hang down.

Sprite rules:
- 64x64 pixels, max 16 colors, 1 px dark outline in Gen 3 style, soft Gen 3 shading.
- 3/4 view facing left, compact so it fills a square.
- Plain white background; no ground, no shadow.
- No glow, no sparkles, no effects, no loose leaves, no vines.
AVOID: plush/toy look, swamp creature with skin, fingers or toes, a human in a costume.
