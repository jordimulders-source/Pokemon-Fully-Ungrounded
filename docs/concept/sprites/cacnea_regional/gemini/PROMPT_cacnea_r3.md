# Regionale Cacnea – Gemini-startprompt r3 (23 nov, nieuw gesprek, geen bijlage)

Na r2 (`cacnea_r2.png`), Jordi: "vind het nog niet herkenbaar genoeg de copiapoa, plus die bloem hoort niet?"

```
Design a regional form of CACNEA: a small first-stage Pokémon, type Grass/Ground. Its build stays Cacnea's, but its body must read instantly as a COPIAPOA CINEREA cactus from the Atacama desert (Chile).
Draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): pixel art, 64×64, max 16 colors, dark selout outline, plain white background, 3/4 view facing left.

COPIAPOA (most important)
- Chalky, almost white, pale blue-grey skin, like a stone covered in white powder; only a faint hint of green in the shadows.
- Clear, deep vertical RIBS running from the top of the head down to the feet, like a segmented ball.
- Along the edge of every rib, a row of LONG, straight, jet-black needle spines (one or two per spot) that stick out sharply; black on white is the key contrast.
- On top of the head, instead of a crown: a flat WOOLLY CAP of brownish-grey felt, where the ribs meet.

KEEP FROM CACNEA
Round ball body, two round eyes with black pupils, the dark spots on the face that form its mouth, two thick arms held out to the sides (also ribbed with black spines), short stubby feet.

COMPOSITION
Compact, the whole Pokémon fits inside a square, small (it is a first stage), limbs close to the body.

DO NOT
No flower, no yellow crown, no yellow at all.
No ground, no sand, no rocks around it, no shadow. No glow, no effects, no motion lines.
Not a plush/Neopets look, not a realistic photo, not a potted plant.
```

Notitie (Claude): de wollige kruin vervangt de kroon op dezelfde plek (Copiapoa-kenmerk); lange zwarte naalden op witte ribben en de krijtwitte huid zijn wat Copiapoa cinerea herkenbaar maakt.
