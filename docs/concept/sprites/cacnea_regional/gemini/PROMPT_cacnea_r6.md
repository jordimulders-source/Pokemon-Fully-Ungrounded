# Regionale Cacnea – Gemini-startprompt r6 (23 nov, nieuw gesprek, geen bijlage)

Na r5. Jordi: "stekels raar, mond raar, en echt teveel nog de normale cacnea"; "is dit wel een design wat kan?"; wat van Cacnea blijft: "ogen, mondpatroon en iets op zijn hoofd". Nieuwe opzet: begint bij de Copiapoa, de naam Cacnea staat niet meer in de prompt (les Dundragoon: basisnaam = kopie). Stekels: Jordi gaf geen keuze; geen maten in de prompt.

```
Design a brand-new small first-stage Pokémon, type Grass/Ground: a young COPIAPOA CINEREA cactus from the Atacama desert (Chile) that has come alive.
Draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): pixel art, 64×64, max 16 colors, dark selout outline, plain white background, 3/4 view facing left.

WHAT IT IS
A young Copiapoa cinerea: a chalky, almost white, pale blue-grey cactus with a white powdery bloom, broad vertical ribs, black spines, and a woolly felt cap on top of its head where the ribs meet. Design its body from the cactus itself; decide yourself whether and how it has limbs.

FACE
- Two round white eyes with a thick dark outline and one round black pupil each.
- Below the eyes, the mouth: one large dark round mouth spot in the middle, with small dark diamond-shaped spots beside it on both sides.

COMPOSITION
Compact, the whole Pokémon fits inside a square, small (it is a first stage), limbs close to the body.

DO NOT
No flower, no yellow, no crown.
No ground, no sand, no rocks around it, no shadow. No glow, no effects, no motion lines.
Not a plush/Neopets look, not a realistic photo, not a potted plant.
```
