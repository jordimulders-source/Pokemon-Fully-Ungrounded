# Satelliet-lijn fase 1 – startprompt r3 (23 nov), NIEUW gesprek, geen bijlage
Op basis van Jordi's feedback op r2 (zie SPEC).

```
Design a new, original Pokémon.
Draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): pixel art, 64×64, max 16 colors, dark selout outline, plain white background.

WHAT IT IS
A small living machine based on Sputnik 1, the first satellite: a polished metal sphere with four long antennas sweeping back from it in two pairs. The Sputnik inspiration must be clearly recognizable.
It is a pure machine with its own personality, the way Klink, Magnemite and Porygon are Pokémon: no animal parts, no arms, no legs.
It has exactly ONE big eye: a single camera lens in the front of the sphere with a ring around it. Curious expression.

DETAIL LEVEL
Medium: more than a plain ball, less than a realistic machine. Allowed details: one seam around the middle of the sphere (like Sputnik's two halves), and a small socket where each antenna attaches. Nothing else: no rivets, no panel grid, no spots or patches, no dishes or knobs on the antennas.

ANTENNAS
Exactly four, all the same. They must be THICK enough to read clearly in the sprite: at least 2–3 pixels wide, tapering slightly toward the tip. Never 1-pixel lines.

COLORS
White and purple, close to Mewtwo's colors: a pearly white sphere with soft lavender-purple shading, purple accents (seam, sockets, eye ring). Smooth, sleek, cyber/high-tech look, like polished plastic and chrome. The eye may glow-colored (e.g. purple or cyan iris) but no glow effect around it.

POSE
It floats in the air, clearly turned in three-quarter view: we see the front of the sphere with the eye AND part of its left side, with the antennas trailing behind it at an angle. Tilted slightly as if looking up curiously.

VIEW
Three-quarter view facing LEFT, like every Gen 3 front sprite: the eye is on the left half of the sphere, the antennas point back to the right.
It is small: it fills about half of the 64×64 frame.

DO NOT
No effects, no glow, no electricity, no stars or planets around it.
No ground, no shadow.
Not Magnemite, not Voltorb, not a Poké Ball, not a robot with a face screen.
No plush/Neopets look.
```
