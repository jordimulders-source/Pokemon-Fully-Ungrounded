# Vlieglijn fase 1 (vlieg) – r6, nieuw gesprek zonder bijlage (20 nov)

Besluiten: Bug/Electric; robotischer, gladder/mechanischer (Jordi, 20 nov); verder alles uit r5 (grijze kop, LED-oogschermen ^, zwart borststuk, grijs achterlijf met zwarte vlekken, kabelpoten, lichte vleugels plat naar achteren zoals r3/r4); precies 6 poten.

```
Design a new Pokémon: a Bug/Electric type fly, the first stage of a two-stage line (fly → mosquito).
Draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): pixel art, 64×64, max 16 colors, dark selout outline, plain white background.

WHAT IT IS
A house fly that is a bit robotic: the body is smooth and mechanical, like a small machine, while its shape is still clearly a fly (large eyes, thorax, rounded abdomen, short antennae).
Head: GREY, no mouth. Two separate, very large eyes (near and far) cover most of the head. Each eye is a black LED screen; in each eye the lit purple LEDs form one happy upside-down V (^), with thick lines.
Thorax: black.
Abdomen: grey with a few large black patches (a dark line down the middle and 2–3 big black spots on each side).
Wings: two wings (near and far), almost white with a cool light-blue tint, clear, thin veins. They lie flat and folded backwards over the back, reaching past the end of the abdomen. They do NOT stand up.
Legs: EXACTLY SIX legs, all attached to the thorax: three on the near side, three on the far side (the far ones partly hidden). They are thick black legs with yellow lines, shaped like electric power cables.

VIEW
Three-quarter view facing left, like every Gen 3 front sprite: the far wing and far legs on the left (narrower, partly hidden), the near ones on the right.
Compact: the whole Pokémon fits inside a square, limbs close to the body.

DO NOT
No sparks, no glow, no electricity effects, no motion lines. No ground, no shadow.
No mouth, no face drawn on the head. No screws, bolts, antennas with balls, or human-made gadgets added on top.
No plush/Neopets look, no chibi mascot.
Not Beedrill, not a bee or wasp: no stinger, no upright wings, no yellow-and-black stripes on the body. Also not Yanma, Ninjask, Joltik, Galvantula or Magnemite.
```
