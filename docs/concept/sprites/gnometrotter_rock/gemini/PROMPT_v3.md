# Gemini-prompt: Gnometrotter (Rock-vorm), v3 (23 nov)

Nieuw gesprek, geen bijlage. Jordi: "probeer allemaal maar" (suggesties 1–7) + "meer rock", vuist doorgegroeid tot wandelstok, "niet alle rotsen hetzelfde", rugrotsen niet geplakt.

## Ingevuld door Claude
- Stokarm = de verre/voorste kant zoals Gemini kiest; andere arm blijft roze.
- Oog: als het terugkomt, oplossen in de sprite.

## Prompt

```
Design an original Pokémon and draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style).

CONCEPT
- Fairy/Rock type, the final stage of a three-stage evolution line. Its name is Gnometrotter: a gnome Pokémon that has walked so far for so long that its body has fully adapted to its environment. This is its Cave Form: after ages in caves, rock has become part of its body. It is a rock gnome, half gnome and half rock golem.
- It must still clearly be the evolution of a cartoony garden-gnome Pokémon: a big round pink nose, a droopy gnome cap over the eyes, a round pink belly, a sky-blue lower half and a belt.

THE GNOME PART (keep)
- A big, round, bulbous pink nose: the only soft, pink thing in its face.
- A round pink belly. The lower half of the body including the legs is sky blue, like a garden gnome's trousers, with a thin dark-blue line on the border. This is NOT clothing, just a second body color.
- A simple dark belt around the waist with a small square buckle, between the pink belly and the blue lower half.
- Its other arm (the one without the stick) is still a pink oval arm ending in a round, chubby ball-shaped pink hand.

THE ROCK PART (new)
- Heavy golem posture: very broad, massive shoulders, no neck, the head sitting low between the shoulders, hunched forward with age, taking a slow, heavy step. An old, seasoned hiker.
- Stick arm: one arm has turned completely to stone from the shoulder down, and its fist has grown out into a long rock walking stick. The stick is part of the arm, not held. It leans on it.
- Cap and back are one rock mass: the cap still has the droopy pointed shape of a soft gnome cap (NOT tall, NOT straight up), but it is made of rock; its tip hangs down behind and fuses with a rocky ridge that covers its shoulders and runs down its spine, like a mountain ridge growing out of its back.
- The cap comes down over the eyes and rests directly on the nose: NO dark gap, slit or shadow between cap and face. The eyes are completely hidden.
- Rock breaks through the skin: where pink skin meets rock (shoulders, upper back, the stone arm) the border is jagged, with cracks running into the pink skin. The rock grows out of it, nothing is stuck on.
- Beard like a rockslide: one heavy beard of angular rock layers, from under the nose down to the belt, with a broken, angular bottom edge. Mustache part of it. NO mouth visible.
- Hair: rough rock tufts sticking out sideways from under the cap.
- Feet: same simple rounded feet, but with rock soles and blunt rock toes, so every step looks heavy.
- Two kinds of rock: the cap, the back ridge and the stone arm are dark, angular grey rock with big flat break surfaces. The beard and hair tufts are a lighter, grainy grey rock made of smaller chunks.

STYLE AND FORMAT
- Official Gen 3 front sprite, 64x64, max 16 colors, 1 px dark outline, light from top left, flat Gen 3 shading with 3 to 4 tones per color, no dithering.
- Keep the rock readable at sprite size: big shapes and a few cracks, no fine texture or speckles.
- Three-quarter view facing left. A final-stage Pokémon, about 60 px tall, compact, centered in the frame.
- Plain white background. NO floor, shadow, loose rocks on the ground, cave background or effects. One single creature.
- Cartoony, but in the Pokémon way: bold shapes, a clear and cool silhouette, like an official Ken Sugimori design.

AVOID
- No visible eyes, no dark gap under the cap, no visible mouth. No crystals, no gems, no stalactites, no glow. No backpack-like slab on the back, no pebbles stuck onto the skin. No separate walking stick held in a hand. No human proportions, no little man in a costume. Not Golem, not Graveler, not Impidimp. No western-cartoon or plush look. No glossy 3D render.
```
