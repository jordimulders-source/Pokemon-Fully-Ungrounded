# Ledian-evolutie – Gemini-prompt r6 (27 nov), from scratch

Op r5, Jordi: "ok dat is niet beter, hij heeft nu helemaal geen mond meer, terwijl ledian dat wel heeft, de armen zitten verder ook gek, zijn te kort, nieuwe prompt from scratch graag".
Alle besluiten tot nu toe: fase 3, Bug/Cosmic, nog meer superheld/tokusatsu, space superhero/space robot ("het mag echt nog meer space robot"), rug kleiner en aerodynamisch met spacevliegtuigvleugels bijna mechanisch, hoofd aerodynamischer, hoofd volledige stervorm en rood, vliegen met arm voorwaarts, mond zoals Ledian, armen niet te kort, iets langer. Nieuw gesprek, geen bijlage.

```
Design an original Pokémon: the final evolution of Ledian (Gen 2). Draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): 64x64 pixels, at most 16 colors, dark outline, white background, no ground, no shadow, no glow, no energy or special effects.

What it is: Ledian evolved into a space robot superhero, a tokusatsu hero version of itself that flies through space. Bug type and Cosmic type. It looks much more like a space robot than a bug, but is clearly recognisable as Ledian's evolution: red with black spots, Ledian's eyes, Ledian's mouth, two antennae, four arms with white round fists.

Body:
- Head: a complete five-pointed star shape, red, streamlined like a space robot helmet. Ledian's eyes and a small mouth like Ledian's.
- Back: no big round shell. Small, aerodynamic red wings shaped like space plane wings, almost mechanical.
- Torso: a bit longer and slimmer than Ledian's, smooth hard mechanical parts, one creature, not a costume and not loose armor plates.
- Arms: four arms, as long as Ledian's arms, attached in pairs at the sides of the upper torso, each ending in a white round fist.

Pose: flying like a superhero with one arm stretched forward. 3/4 view facing left (far arms and far antenna on the left side, near ones on the right). Compact Gen 3 composition that fits a square.

AVOID: a human in a costume, a plush toy, Neopets style, chibi proportions, Gen 5 style, realistic insect rendering, short stubby arms, no mouth, extra limbs beyond four arms and two legs.
```
