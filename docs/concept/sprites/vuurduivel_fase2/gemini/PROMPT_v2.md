## Prompt

```
Design an original Pokémon and draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style).

CONCEPT
- Fire/Dark type, the final (second) stage of a two-stage evolution line.
- The first stage is a small, cute, laughing satyr devil imp: black body, hair made of flames (dark red at the roots, orange, yellow at the tips), two small red devil horns, big pointed ears, shaggy brown goat legs with cloven hooves, a thin black tail with a fire-colored tip.
- This evolution is the grown-up version: a mature GOAT DEVIL. Bigger and stronger, with powerful shaggy goat legs with cloven hooves. The goat side is now much stronger: a goat-like devil head.
- HORNS: two very long goat horns that curl much more than a normal goat's, each curling around into a big spiral. They are the most striking part of the silhouette.
- POSE: the classic Baphomet pose. It sits upright with its shaggy goat legs crossed in front of it, hooves visible. Exactly two arms: one arm raised straight up with the hand pointing upward, the other arm lowered with the hand pointing downward. Upright, calm and confident, like an idol.
- It keeps the same family features: black body, the flame hair (now a bigger mane of fire), the shaggy brown goat fur on the legs, the tail with the fire-colored tip.
- Still a trickster at heart: a sly, confident grin. Imposing, but still a Pokémon, not a horror monster.
- Cartoony, but in the Pokémon way: simple bold shapes, a clear readable silhouette, expressive face, like an official Ken Sugimori design.

STYLE AND FORMAT
- Official Gen 3 front sprite, 64x64, max 16 colors, 1 px dark outline, light from top left, flat Gen 3 shading with 3 to 4 tones per color, no dithering.
- Three-quarter view facing left. A large final-stage Pokémon that fills most of the 64x64 frame.
- Plain white background. NO floor, rock, throne, shadow, smoke or effects. One single creature.

AVOID
- No pentagram, no symbols, no text, no occult signs, no torch between the horns. No clothing, no props, no pitchfork, no wings. Not Houndoom, Grimmsnarl, Infernape or Krampus. No muscles or abs. No red body. No western-cartoon or plush look. No glossy 3D render.
```
