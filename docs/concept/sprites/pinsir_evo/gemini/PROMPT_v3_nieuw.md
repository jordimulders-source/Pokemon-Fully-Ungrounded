```
Design a brand-new Pokémon: a huge stag-beetle kaiju. Type Bug/Ground.
Draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): pixel art, 64×64, max 16 colors, dark selout outline, plain white background.

WHAT IT IS
A massive, heavy beetle monster that leans forward, like a classic kaiju: a strong, simple hybrid of a beetle and a monster.
It stands on two thick legs and has two strong arms (four limbs in total). The legs and arms are beetle legs, but thick and sturdy: longer and more jointed than stubby monster legs, but NOT long and thin like a real beetle's legs.
The body is one big, simple, solid shape with a clear silhouette. Simple shapes, not detailed.

HORNS
Two big horns grow out of the top of its HEAD. Each horn is one smooth, tapering curve, like a sickle or a curved tusk: it rises upward and the tip curls FORWARD into a single sharp point. Smooth edges, one point per horn, both horns the same shape. A fringe of short orange-brown hair runs along the back edge of each horn.

COLORS
The body is a warm, rich brown with clear light and shadow, so it looks like a hard, shiny shell. The horns are grey-white (dark brown only at the base), so they stand out against the body.

POSE
Leaning forward, heavy and powerful, arms slightly raised, ready to grab and crush. It looks much stronger and heavier than an ordinary beetle Pokémon.

VIEW
Three-quarter view facing LEFT, like every Gen 3 front sprite: not straight to the front, not facing right.
Compact: the whole Pokémon fits inside a square, limbs close to the body.

DO NOT
No complex patterns, no spikes all over the body, no separate armor plates, no extra horns.
Not a realistic insect painting.
No ground, no rocks, no shadow, no dust. No glow, no aura, no effects, no motion lines.
No plush/Neopets look, no chibi mascot.
```
