# Mate-lijn: Gemini-prompts en rondes

Besloten (Jordi, 15 nov): **nieuwe lijn** (geen vorm van Sinistea of Poltchageist), **Grass/Water**, **2 fases**; "het gaat me puur om ontwerp, de rest bedenk ik zelf wel". Drie richtingen visualiseren: A (kalebas-schildpad), B (hoornkikker), D (kalebas-vogel). Eerst fase 2 (Jordi: "de 1e doen we daarna").

Voorstellen van Claude in de prompts (niet van Jordi): fase 2 = gecureerde, bewerkte kalebas met zilver, fase 1 = groene, ongerijpte kalebas; de soort bij A (Chaco-schildpad, *Chelonoidis chilensis*) en B (Argentijnse hoornkikker, *Ceratophrys ornata*); bij D is de vogelsoort nog open.

Bijlagen (aanbevolen, GEMINI.md): een foto van een mate-kalebas met zilveren virola en bombilla, en een foto van het dier.

## Gemeenschappelijk blok (zit al in elke prompt)
Gen 3-frontsprite, 64×64, ≤ 16 kleuren, 3/4 naar links, witte achtergrond, geen ondergrond/schaduw/effecten.

## Prompt A: kalebas-schildpad (fase 2)
```
Attached: (1) a photo of a traditional yerba mate gourd with a silver rim and a bombilla, (2) a photo of a Chaco tortoise.

Design an original Pokémon: a Grass/Water-type, the fully evolved (second and final) stage of a two-stage line. It is based on yerba mate, the drink shared in Argentina, Uruguay, Paraguay and southern Brazil, combined with a Chaco tortoise (Chelonoidis chilensis). Its shell is a cured mate gourd: rounded and brown, decorated with burned or engraved patterns, with a silver rim (the "virola") along the edge of the shell. On top of its back grows a sloped little mound of green yerba leaves (the "montañita"), and a silver bombilla (a metal drinking straw with a flattened bend and a gold mouthpiece) sticks out of it at an angle, like a little flag. Its feet look like the small silver feet of a decorated mate. Anatomy of a real tortoise: one head, four legs, a shell. As the final stage it looks cured, decorated and silver-trimmed (its pre-evolution will be a raw, green, unripe gourd).

Pose: standing on all four legs, body in 3/4 view facing left, head turned slightly towards the viewer. Compact composition that fits in a square, legs close to the body.

Style: an official Gen 3 Pokémon front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style), 64x64 pixels, at most 16 colours, 1px dark outline, light from the top left. Plain white background. No ground, no shadow, no water splashes, no steam, no glow or effects.

AVOID: a teacup or teapot ghost (Sinistea/Polteageist look), an object with a face, plush/Neopets look, realistic painting, extra props (thermos, kettle, cup).
```
