# Gemini-prompt v7 (23 nov), gelaagd tegen een bergwand

Create an original Pokémon as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style), 64×64 pixels, max 16 colors, clean dark outline, plain white background.

The Pokémon IS an ancient Inca temple that has come to life after centuries. It is not a creature wearing a temple, and it is not a creature standing next to one: the building itself is the whole body. Ghost/Rock type. It is huge and heavy, like Snorlax in weight and presence. The body FILLS the 64×64 frame almost edge to edge, in a compact, square composition.

The whole body is a LAYERED Inca temple built against a steep mountainside, like the ruins of Wiñay Wayna on the Inca Trail: the temple climbs the slope in a few terrace levels. Big, readable shapes, not small detail:
- Bottom level (front, lowest): a stone wall with the entrance. This is where the face is.
- Above and behind it: two or three more terrace levels stepping back and UP, each a retaining wall of rough stone with a small temple building standing on it. The upper buildings are roofless ruins with steep triangular stone gables. The highest level is at the back, so the whole silhouette rises like a mountainside covered with temple buildings.
- The levels are not symmetrical: they follow the slope of the mountain, slightly irregular, like real terraces on a hillside. NO central staircase.
- Rough, roughly stacked fieldstones (rubble masonry), walls leaning slightly inward. Moss and short grass on top of the walls and gables, lichen spots on the stones. Centuries old.

Face: the bottom wall has a large Inca doorway with a long flat stone lintel on top, slightly narrower at the top than at the bottom: this doorway is its mouth, dark inside, like an entrance you would walk into. Above it, two small rectangular windows in the wall are its eyes: the eyes are light shining out from inside these holes in the stonework, the only sign that something lives inside. No other facial features. The idea: you think it is just the entrance to a temple, until you notice it is looking at you.

No limbs at all: no arms, no legs, no feet, no hands. Viewed in 3/4 view facing left (front wall and one side wall visible).

Colors: weathered grey stone with some light lichen spots, moss green on top of the walls, darker in the joints and the doorway.

No glow, no aura, no light rays, no particles, no effects. No ground, no rock, no shadow under it.

AVOID: a Maya or Aztec pyramid with a central staircase, a temple on top of a stepped pyramid, a tower of stacked storeys, a single house, a symmetrical pyramid, overhanging ledges or cornices, a ziggurat or fantasy temple, a single box with a door, a regular brick pattern, polished smooth blocks, ears or a cat shape, an igloo or hut shape, Aztec or Maya step pyramids, gold, carvings of gods or faces in the stone, a mummy or ghost figure inside the doorway, cute plush style, a person or creature in a costume, a realistic architectural drawing, lots of tiny detail.
