# Bibarel-evolutie – Gemini-startprompt r4 (23 nov, NIEUW gesprek, geen bijlage)

Jordi bij r3 (met de officiële Bibarel-art erbij, `referenties/bibarel_officieel.png`): "ok dat is al wat beter, maar het is kleding en geen lichaampatroon, de ogen zijn te klein, wat bibarel hier om de ogen heeft die vacht, dat mag doorlopen en een helm worden. De vacht onder zijn kin dat mag de vorm van de tuinbroek worden. De staart mag dan een soort van pylonkleur krijgen, oranje met wit gestreept".
Bibarel niet bij naam en niet als bijlage (GEMINI.md: basisnaam/bijlage = kopie); zijn vachtvlakken en ogen in woorden. Rest gelijk aan r3.

```
Design a brand-new Pokémon. Type Water/Fighting. It is a hard-working builder beaver: a busy boss, working very hard, like real beavers do. The "construction worker" idea is shown ONLY through the SHAPES OF ITS FUR MARKINGS and what it is doing. It wears NOTHING: no hat, no overalls, no clothing of any kind.
Draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): pixel art, 64×64, max 16 colors, dark selout outline, plain white background.

FINAL EVOLUTION (most important)
This is the THIRD and final stage of its line. It must look big, heavy, mature and imposing, like a fully-grown boss.
Bigger and a bit more buff: a large, strong, heavy frame with thick strong arms. But keep the shapes simple: no detailed muscle definition, no bodybuilder look.

WHAT IT IS
Animal anatomy: a real BEAVER, standing upright on its hind legs.
Warm brown fur, dark brown muzzle with a small red nose.
EYES: BIG, round, white eyes with small black pupils, clearly visible.
HELMET = FUR MARKING: around its eyes it has large patches of pale cream fur. This cream fur continues upward over the top of its head and forms the shape of a hard hat (a rounded dome with a small brim over the eyes). It is its own fur, the same cream fur as around the eyes, not a separate hat.
OVERALLS = FUR MARKING: the pale cream fur under its chin and on its chest has the shape of bib overalls: a square bib on the chest with two straps of cream fur going up over the shoulders. It is a fur pattern on its body, not cloth: no seams, no pockets, no buttons.
TEETH: two HUGE white front teeth, sticking far out of the mouth, very big and very clear.
TAIL: a big, broad, flat paddle tail colored like a TRAFFIC CONE: orange with white stripes.

POSE
Busy at work: carrying a heavy log on its shoulder, mid-stride, determined and in a hurry.
THE LOG: one end is gnawed to a sharp POINT (like a pencil), the way real beavers gnaw through a tree trunk.

VIEW
Three-quarter view facing left, like every Gen 3 front sprite: the far arm and leg on the left (partly hidden), the near ones on the right.
Compact: the whole Pokémon including the log fits inside a square.

KEEP IT SIMPLE
Large, clear shapes that read at 64×64. No small details: no wood chips, no twigs, no rocks, no tool belts, no claws drawn in detail, no thin stripes.

DO NOT
NO CLOTHING: no hard hat, no helmet object, no overalls, no straps of fabric, no vest, no belt, no gloves, no tools held in hand. Only fur.
Not a small, cute, baby-like beaver; not a first-stage Pokémon.
No ground, no water, no rock, no shadow under it. No glow, no aura, no effects, no motion lines, no sweat drops.
No plush/Neopets look, no chibi mascot, not a realistic animal painting.
```
