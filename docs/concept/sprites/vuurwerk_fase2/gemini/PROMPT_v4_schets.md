```
Design an original Pokémon as an official Gen 3 pixel-art front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style).

The attached image is a rough SHAPE SKETCH. Follow its composition and proportions exactly, but not its flat drawing style.

WHAT IT IS
A confident fire salamander made entirely of fire, that comes out of a firework rocket and holds that rocket like a bazooka. The creature is clearly BIGGER than the rocket: it is in charge of the rocket now.

HOW IT IS BUILT (as in the sketch)
1. The rocket is held diagonally, its pointed tip forward and up (left), its open back end down-right.
2. Fire comes out of the open back end. That fire IS the creature's body: thin and red where it leaves the rocket, it curls down and around in one big loop, passes in FRONT of the rocket, and then rises up behind it into a thick, upright, S-shaped body like a rearing cobra, with a chest.
3. Two short arms come out of the chest and hold the rocket like a bazooka.
4. On top: the head, looking forward over the rocket, with a confident grin.
The creature has NO legs, NO feet and no second body or tail: the flame from the rocket is its whole body.

DETAILS
- Head of a fire salamander: flat, broad, cream-white head, rounded snout, black eyes, swollen glands behind the eyes. Behind the head, three pointed flames like gills turned into fire. Not Charmander, no dragon.
- Made of fire: flame edges and a few flame tongues along the body. Colors shift along the body like a firework changing color: cream-white head, hot pink chest, red where the fire leaves the rocket.
- Rocket: cardboard tube with diagonal pink, magenta and yellow stripes, pointed pink tip.

FORMAT
64x64, max 16 colors, 1 px dark outline, flat Gen 3 shading, no dithering, three-quarter view facing left, compact composition, plain white background, no shadow. No smoke, no speed lines, no sparkles, no firework explosions. No text, no 3D render.
```
