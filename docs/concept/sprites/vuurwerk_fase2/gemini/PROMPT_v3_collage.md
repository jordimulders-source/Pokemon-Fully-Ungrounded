## Prompt

```
The attached image is a rough collage: a firework rocket held at an angle, pointing diagonally forward and up, with one flame that comes out of its open back end, winds around the rocket and ends in a cream-white salamander head above the tip. Redraw it as ONE clean creature. Keep the rocket, its angle, the winding flame, the head and the pixel-art style. Make only these two changes:

1. Blend the head smoothly into the end of the flame, so the flame becomes its neck. The head is pasted on and cut off straight at the bottom: fix that.

2. TWO SMALL HANDS grow out of the flame just below the head and grip the rocket under its pointed tip, like holding a bazooka. The hands must be clearly part of the flame.

The creature has no legs, no tail and no other body: the winding flame is its body. Gen 3 sprite, 64x64, plain white background, no sparkles.
```
