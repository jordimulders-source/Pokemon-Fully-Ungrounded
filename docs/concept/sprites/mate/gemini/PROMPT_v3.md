## Prompt

```
The attached image is a Pokémon sprite draft of a yerba mate gourd creature. KEEP EVERYTHING EXACTLY AS IT IS: the same body, shape, colors, face, green eye holes, silver rim, bombilla, hands, feet and pose.

ADD ONLY THIS ONE THING
- Two short, slightly curved horns on top of the gourd, one on each side next to the silver rim, like the small horns on the carved aña-aña festival masks of the Chané and Guaraní (Gran Chaco).
- The horns are part of the gourd itself: the same brown dried calabash, same texture and outline. Not metal, not bone.
- Small: each horn about as tall as the silver rim is high. They must not touch or cover the bombilla.

STYLE AND FORMAT
- Official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style), 64x64, max 16 colors, 1 px dark outline, light from top left, flat Gen 3 shading, no dithering.
- Plain white background. NO floor, shadow, effects.

AVOID
- No other changes. No devil horns, no extra face details, no extra limbs.
```

## Tips
- Gemini verandert ook het gezicht of de pose: "pixel for pixel the same as the attachment, only add the horns" bovenaan.
- Hoorns te groot of duivels: "tiny stubby horns, like two little bumps with a point".
- Volgende ronde (apart): "move the far hand off the cheek, so it rests against the side of the body".
