```
Design a new form of the Pokémon ORICORIO: SAMBA STYLE, from the carnival of Rio de Janeiro. Its type is Grass/Flying.
It is a new dance style of the Pokémon ORICORIO (the dancing bird from Alola, like its Baile, Pom-Pom, Pa'u and Sensu styles). Keep the Oricorio body exactly: Oricorio STANDS UPRIGHT LIKE A PERSON, not like a normal bird. Its body is a small, vertical egg shape (like a standing penguin or a dancer), with a round head sitting directly on top of it, no visible neck. The body is NOT horizontal and NOT a parrot or pigeon body. A slender, slightly downcurved pink beak; long thin pink legs coming straight down from the bottom of the body, with toes. Only the plumage changes: the feathers themselves ARE the dance costume (like Baile's feathers are a flamenco dress), never clothing or objects worn over the bird.
THE EYE: exactly the happy eye of Pom-Pom Oricorio (the yellow Electric cheerleader style): a big, round, WIDE OPEN solid black eye with one white shine dot, just above the base of the beak. NO eyelid, NO line over the eye, NO white around it. A happy, cheerful, wide-awake look. NOT sleepy, NOT half-closed, NOT cool.

WHAT IT IS
A samba carnival dancer made of feathers, dressed like a Rio carnival samba dancer: MAINLY GREEN (the Grass type color), with yellow as accent. The plumes are long carnival feathers, green with yellow sections, shaped like long pointed leaves because it is Grass type.
THE HEADDRESS: the head feathers form a tall carnival headdress: long plumes rising up and fanning out from the TOP OF THE HEAD like a crown, the face clearly visible underneath it. The headdress grows from the head, it is not a fan behind the body.
THE WINGS: the wings are the carnival backpiece: on each side of the body one big wing of long plumes, hanging and spreading out to the sides at body height, like the feathered side-wings of a samba dancer. Each wing is one clear wing shape.
NO OVERLAP: headdress on top, one wing on each side, body in the middle: these parts do not overlap each other and do not hide each other, so every part has its own clear outline.
The body has smooth, plain green feathers (no sparkle or speckle pattern). Few, big, simple shapes: it must read clearly at 64×64.

POSE
A proud samba dancer pose: standing on the toes of one foot, the other foot lifted in a samba step, one wing tip raised up near the head, the other wing tip on the hip.

Draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): pixel art, 64×64, max 16 colors, dark selout outline, plain white background. Three-quarter view facing left, like every Gen 3 front sprite: far wing and far leg on the left, near ones on the right. 2 wings and 2 legs in total. Compact, fits in a square.
DO NOT
No ground, no shadow, no stage, no glow, no music notes, no effects. No human body parts (no arms, hands, fingers, hair): only feathers. No plush/Neopets look.
```
