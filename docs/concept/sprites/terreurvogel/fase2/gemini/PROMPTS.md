Alleen wat Jordi zei (Rock/Fighting, snel, physical sweeper, grote snavel) + echte anatomie. Pose, kleuren en steenmate niet besproken, dus niet gegeven.
```
Design an original Pokémon as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style), 64x64 pixels, max 16 colors, white background, facing 3/4 to the left.

Concept: a fully evolved fossil Pokémon (Rock/Fighting) based on the terror bird (Phorusrhacos / Kelenken), the extinct flightless predator bird of South America. It is a fast physical attacker.
Anatomy: a tall, flightless bird on long, powerful running legs with large clawed feet, small wings, and a huge, tall hooked beak shaped like an axe blade. The beak is its most recognizable feature and is very large compared to the head.
Compact silhouette that fits a square.

No effects, no glow, no sparkles, no ground or shadow under it. AVOID: an ostrich, an emu, a chicken, a modern eagle, a dinosaur, a plush toy, Neopets style.
```
