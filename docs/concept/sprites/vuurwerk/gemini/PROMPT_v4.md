## Prompt

```
Design an original Pokémon and draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style).

CONCEPT
- Fire/Fairy type. A PORCUPINE WHOSE SHAPE IS MADE OF FIRE AND FIREWORKS. You recognize a porcupine (rounded body, short snout, small feet), but the body is smooth, glowing flame, not fur or skin.
- The quills are only a SUGGESTION: a halo of separate teardrop-shaped sparks and thin spark needles that fly outward from its back and head, like the moment a firework shell bursts. Each spark is a clean, simple shape with a bright highlight. No straight colored lines, no bristles.
- ITS HEAD IS A FIREWORK EXPLOSION: a crown of large flame points bursting outward like a star, with a small readable face (eyes and snout) in the center.
- Colors: YELLOW, RED AND PINK, ordered like a real firework: bright yellow-white near the face and body core, red further out, pink at the tips of the sparks and flame points.
- Exactly TWO FRONT LEGS; from the elbow down each front leg IS a firework rocket (casing in red and pink with yellow, pointed tip forward like a fist, a small lit fuse at the back). Its arms are rockets; it does not hold them.
- It chases away evil spirits: a bold, defiant, protective stance.

STYLE AND FORMAT
- Official Gen 3 front sprite, 64x64, max 16 colors, 1 px dark outline, light from top left, flat Gen 3 shading with 3 to 4 tones per color, no dithering.
- Three-quarter view facing left. Compact composition that fits a square; the sparks stay close to the body.
- Plain white background. NO floor, shadow or smoke. One single creature.

AVOID
- Not a rainbow, no green or blue. Not a broom of straight lines. Not a humanoid, no hat. Not a dragon or serpent. Not Sonic. No text. No glossy 3D render.
```
