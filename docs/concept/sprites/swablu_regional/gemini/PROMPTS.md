## Swablu (Electric/Flying)
Bijlage: `../referenties/jordi_bijlage_swablu_slapend.png`

```
Design a regional form of SWABLU (the attached image is official art of Swablu sleeping inside its cloud). Like the Alolan forms (Alolan Diglett, Alolan Geodude, Alolan Grimer): clearly different, but instantly recognizable as Swablu.

Theme: a thunderstorm cloud in the making. Type: Electric/Flying.

Pose: the same pose as the attachment, Swablu sitting inside its cloud with only its head and upper body showing, but AWAKE: eyes open, a curious look, unaware of any danger. Keep Swablu's head shape, round white beak and face.

The cloud: not soft round cotton puffs, but a growing cumulus congestus cloud: cauliflower-like towers bulging upward, with a flat, darker grey base. Slate grey, lighter on top where the sun hits it.

Body color: deeper than normal Swablu, a greyish blue.

The two antennae-like crest feathers on its head are shaped like yellow sparks.

No other sparks, no lightning, no glow, no rain, no effects.

Style: official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style), 64x64, max 16 colors, dark outline, facing 3/4 to the left, compact composition. White background, no ground, no shadow underneath (ignore the shadow in the attachment).

AVOID: plush toy / Neopets look, sleeping eyes, cotton-candy cloud, lightning bolts flying around it.
```

## Altaria (Electric/Dragon)
Bijlage (optioneel): officiële art van Altaria.

```
Design a regional form of ALTARIA (attached: official art of normal Altaria). Like the Alolan forms (Alolan Diglett, Alolan Geodude, Alolan Grimer): clearly different, but instantly recognizable as Altaria.

Theme: a wild thunderstorm cloud. Type: Electric/Dragon.

Keep Altaria's anatomy: bird body, long neck, head with its crest feathers, beak, tail feathers, exactly two wings (near and far).

The cloud: not soft cotton, but a fierce cumulonimbus thundercloud:
- the top spreads out flat like an anvil, and this anvil cloud forms the wings;
- along the bottom, a dark rolling shelf cloud.
Slate grey, lighter on top where the sun hits it, darkest at the bottom.

Body color: deep indigo / night blue, like the sky during a storm.

Static electricity: small blue-white sparks crackling IN and ON the cloud, part of the cloud itself. Not yellow. No lightning bolts shooting out, no glow around the Pokemon, no rain.

Style: official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style), 64x64, max 16 colors, dark outline, facing 3/4 to the left, compact composition with wings close to the body. White background, no ground, no shadow.

AVOID: plush toy / Neopets look, cotton-candy cloud, yellow electricity, lightning bolts flying around it, looking like Zekrom or a generic thunder dragon.
```
