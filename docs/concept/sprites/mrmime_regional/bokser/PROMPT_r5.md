# Bokser-Mr. Mime – startprompt r5 (NIEUW gesprek, bijlage: Jordi's bronfoto). Niet laten spiegelen: Claude spiegelt de sprite later zelf naar links.

```
Design a brand-new regional form of Mr. Mime: an old-time GENTLEMAN boxer, like the bare-knuckle boxers in 1880s black-and-white portrait photos. Fighting type.
Draw it as an official Gen 3 front sprite (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald style): pixel art, 64×64, max 16 colors, dark selout outline, plain white background.
Draw ONE single Pokémon.

WHAT IT IS
Its own new Pokémon. Like Galarian Mr. Mime, only a few hints show it is related to Mr. Mime: a mime-like white face and two hair spikes slicked far back along the head. Everything else is a new design, NOT Mr. Mime's body, head shape or details.
POKÉMON PROPORTIONS, NOT A HUMAN BODY
- The head is BIG: about one third of the total height.
- A round, barrel-shaped torso with heavy shoulders and almost no neck line.
- SHORT stubby legs: about one quarter of the total height, with simple rounded feet (no toes, no human knees or calves).
- Thick, simple arms with big round fists; no human muscle anatomy, no six-pack, no realistic shading.
- Overall a compact Pokémon shape that fits in a square, like a Gen 3 Fighting-type, not a tall human figure.
Dignified and gentlemanly.
It wears NO clothes: its high-waisted old-fashioned boxing trunks are a pattern of its own body.
Bare fists, no boxing gloves. NO balls or round pads anywhere (not on the shoulders, hands or knees).

COLORS
A BLACK-AND-WHITE palette, like an old photo and like a classic mime: white, greys and black.

POSE (most important)
The ATTACHED PHOTO is a real human boxer from the 1880s. Copy ONLY the pose of his arms and his upright stance as in the photo, facing the SAME direction as in the photo (do not mirror it):
- standing stiffly upright, legs straight and close together;
- the front arm reaches FAR forward, away from the body, almost straight, fist at shoulder height (not a fist held up near the face);
- the other fist rests against the chest;
- chest out, chin up.
Do NOT copy his human body, face, moustache, clothes, boots or the background.

VIEW
Three-quarter view, the same direction as the photo. Compact.

DO NOT
No boxing gloves, no skirt, no tunic, no fabric.
No ground, no ring, no shadow, no glow, no effects, no motion lines. Only one Pokémon.
No plush/Neopets look, no chibi mascot, not a realistic human.
```
